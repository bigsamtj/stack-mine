In short

What is Stack Capital?

Stack Capital is Stack18’s strategic investment arm. It invests in early-stage startups, usually at pre-seed and seed, and backs each one with Stack18’s team, platform and enterprise network as well as capital.

Each investment combines cash with optional build capacity on the Agentic SDLC, and is made from Stack18’s own balance sheet. Stack Capital is the investing side of Stack18. Stack Ventures is the building side, which creates companies inside Stack18.

Fit

What we look for, and what we pass on.

We would rather tell you quickly that we are not the right investor than take weeks to say the same thing.

✓We look for

  • Founders who know the problem from the inside
  • A product live, or in build with real users close by
  • B2B or B2B2C, with a path to enterprise customers
  • A clear way Stack18 can help in the next 18 months
  • India-first, with a route to global markets
  • Teams that build with AI from day one

✕We pass on

  • Consumer social, gaming and media
  • Crypto tokens and lending balance sheets
  • Companies where the only thing we would add is money
  • Terms that would give us control of the company
  • Rounds that need an answer in days, with no diligence

FAQ

Stack Capital: common questions

Pitch to us
What is Stack Capital?

Stack Capital is Stack18’s strategic investment arm. It invests in early-stage startups, usually at pre-seed and seed, and backs them with Stack18’s team, platform and enterprise network as well as capital.

How is Stack Capital different from Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures builds companies inside Stack18, with Stack18’s own team, and brings in a CEO once each one reaches ₹1 crore in revenue. Stack Capital invests in companies that outside founders start and lead from day one.

What stage do you invest at?

Mostly pre-seed and seed. We sometimes follow on in later rounds for companies we have already backed.

How much does Stack Capital invest?

An indicative first cheque is ₹25 lakh to ₹2 crore, depending on the stage, the track and the size of the round. Every amount is agreed in writing after diligence.

What is build-for-equity?

Part of the investment is delivered as a Stack18 build pod on the Agentic SDLC, working to your roadmap, instead of cash. Up to half of the cheque can be delivered this way. The code and IP belong to your company.

Do I have to use Stack18 for engineering or services?

No. Build capacity is an option, not a condition. Many founders take a strategic cheque and use Stack18 only for advice and introductions.

Do you lead rounds?

We can lead, co-lead or join a round led by another investor. Strategic cheques are most often made alongside a lead; build-for-equity investments are usually led by Stack Capital.

Which sectors do you invest in?

AI-native vertical software, agentic enterprise automation, MSME fintech and compliance, commerce infrastructure, supply chain and manufacturing, and developer and AI infrastructure.

Do you invest outside India?

Stack Capital is India-first. We also look at companies with Indian founders or Indian operations building for global markets.

Is Stack Capital a fund?

No. Stack Capital invests from Stack18’s own balance sheet. It does not manage money for outside investors and does not offer investment products.
