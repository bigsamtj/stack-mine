In short

What is Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures is Stack18’s company-building arm. It builds new companies inside Stack18 first, using Stack18’s own team and its AI-native delivery platform, the Agentic SDLC.

Every venture follows one five-stage model: build the product, reach product-market fit, reach ₹1 crore in revenue, hire a dedicated CEO (or bring in a co-founder), then spin off as a separate legal entity. Stack Ventures is not a venture studio and not an investment fund.

Why build first

Most startups ask people to bet before there is proof.

The usual path asks a founder to quit a job, raise money, hire a team and build a product, all before anyone knows whether customers will pay. Most of the time and money in year one goes into assembling things, not learning from customers.

Stack Ventures turns that order around. Stack18 already has the team, the platform and the operations to build products. So the company is built and proven first, and the leader joins a business that works.

  • The hard, uncertain part is done inside Stack18Product, fit and first revenue are built by a team that ships software every two weeks.
  • The leader joins a business, not an ideaA live product, paying customers and ₹1 crore in revenue on day one.
  • The company becomes independentA separate legal entity, with its own board and cap table, ready to scale.

Principles

What the model is built on.

Evidence before equityNobody is asked to bet a career or a cheque on an idea. Each stage is earned with evidence.
Revenue before raisingVentures prove the business with paying customers before outside capital becomes part of the story.
Leaders join proof, not pitch decksA CEO takes over a live product with customers and ₹1 crore in revenue, not a blank page.
One builder until it worksStack18 carries the product, team and early operations, so there is no hiring scramble in year one.
A clean separationAt spin-off the venture becomes its own legal entity with its own IP, contracts, board and cap table.
Partners after spin-offStack18 stays involved as a shareholder and technology partner, not as the boss.

FAQ

Stack Ventures: common questions

Ask us anything
What is Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures is Stack18’s company-building arm. It builds new companies inside Stack18 first, takes them to product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, hires a dedicated CEO or brings in a co-founder, and then spins each one off as a separate company.

Is Stack Ventures a venture studio?

No. Stack Ventures does not co-found companies with outside founders from day one. Stack18 builds each venture internally until it has a product, product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, and only then puts a dedicated leader in charge.

Is Stack Ventures an investor or a VC fund?

No. Stack Ventures does not invest in outside startups. It builds its own companies with Stack18’s team and platform. Stack18’s strategic investments in early-stage startups are made through Stack Capital.

What are the five stages of the Stack Ventures model?

1. Build the product. 2. Reach product-market fit. 3. Reach ₹1 crore in revenue. 4. Hire a dedicated CEO, or bring in a co-founder. 5. Spin off as a separate legal entity.

Why wait for ₹1 crore in revenue before hiring a CEO?

Because it proves the business model works and customers will pay. The CEO then joins a company with a live product, real customers and revenue, and spends their time scaling it rather than searching for it.

What does a Stack Ventures CEO get?

A company with a working product, proven demand and revenue; a team already in place; meaningful equity agreed in writing before they join; and Stack18 as a shareholder and technology partner.

Can I join a venture as a co-founder?

Yes. Domain experts and operators can join a venture as a co-founder, including before stage 4 where their expertise helps reach product-market fit or revenue. Apply through the form on this page.

What happens at spin-off?

The venture is incorporated as a separate legal entity. Its product, intellectual property, customers, contracts and team move into the new company, which gets its own board and cap table and is ready to raise capital.

Does Stack18 stay involved after spin-off?

Yes. Stack18 remains a shareholder and the venture’s technology partner, while the CEO and leadership team run the company.

Can I pitch my startup idea to Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures builds its own ventures rather than taking outside ideas through a studio. If you have deep expertise in a problem area, apply to join a venture as a co-founder.

How can investors get involved?

Investors can back Stack Ventures companies at or after spin-off, when each venture has a product, paying customers and revenue behind it. Select the investor option in the form.

What has Stack Ventures built so far?

Stack Ventures is building its first venture, Venture 01, inside Stack18 right now. It is in Stage 1, building the product, and will be named on this page once it is live with real users.
