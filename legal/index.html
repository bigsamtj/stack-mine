Your rights under the DPDP Act, at a glance.

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other data protection laws such as the GDPR where they apply, you can ask Stack18 to act on your personal data. One email address handles every request.

Nominate someoneName another person to exercise your rights in the circumstances the law specifies.

Governance in practice

Five gates between an idea and production.

How Stack18 keeps people in charge of AI-assisted work. Gates are configurable by project, team and risk level, and every decision is recorded to the audit trail.

  1. 1IntentScope, goals and constraints are confirmed before anything is built.

FAQ

Legal and policies: common questions

Email legal
Which policies does Stack18 have?

The Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy (which includes how Stack18 uses cookies) and the Security & AI Governance page. All are linked from this page.

Which law governs Stack18’s terms?

The Terms of Service are governed by the laws of India, with good-faith resolution first. A signed order may set out its own dispute process.

How do I make a request about my personal data?

Email privacy@stack18.com. The Privacy Policy explains your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including access, correction, erasure and withdrawing consent.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Follow the steps in the “Found a vulnerability?” section of the Security & AI Governance page. Please do not access, change or keep data beyond what is needed to show the issue.

What if my contract says something different from a policy?

Your signed order form, statement of work or agreement with Stack18 governs your engagement and takes precedence where it differs from the website policies.

How will I know when a policy changes?

Each policy shows its effective date and the date it was last updated. This page lists the latest dates for every document.
