Why the Layer exists

The intelligence deficit.

Many organisations lack behavioural analysis, threat prediction and dark-ecosystem mapping, and so cannot stay ahead of capable adversaries.

Security operations see alerts. Intelligence explains the actor behind them.

The Intelligence Layer studies the human and machine behaviours that drive threats: how actors choose targets, how criminal ecosystems organise, how stolen credentials and exploits move from underground markets into active use, and where the next campaign is likely to emerge.

Its outputs are designed to change decisions: which threats to prioritise, where to deploy detection, what scenarios to exercise and what leadership should know.

Questions, answered

Intelligence Layer: common questions

Is the Intelligence Layer operational?

The capability is part of the HELIOS SYSTEMS architecture. Specific intelligence mandates are agreed individually, and supporting platforms have disclosed development statuses. The site does not claim operational services that have not been confirmed.

How is intelligence different from threat feeds?

Feeds provide indicators. Intelligence explains actors, intent, capability and likely behaviour, and states confidence. It is analysis, not a data subscription.

Can intelligence support legal proceedings?

Intelligence can support investigations and briefings, but evidence for proceedings must meet forensic and legal standards. Attribution and admissibility are treated separately.

How do I commission a briefing?

Use the contact page and select Corporate engagement. Describe the decision, audience and confidentiality context.
