Quick answer

What is AI agent development?

AI agent development means building software agents that take on a defined task, such as qualifying leads, answering support tickets or reconciling payments, using AI to read, decide and act across your systems. Stack18 builds governed agents with defined limits, human approval for critical actions and a full audit trail, starting with one workflow.

FAQ

AI Agents & Automation questions.

Ask us anything
What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent also takes actions, such as updating a CRM record, sending a reminder or preparing a document, within limits you define. Many of our agents have a chat interface, but their value is in the work they complete.

Are agents safe to connect to our systems?

Agents get the minimum access they need, act within written limits and ask a person before critical steps. Every action is logged, and nothing reaches production without passing our five governance gates.

Which models and tools do you use?

We choose per task, balancing accuracy, cost, speed and data sensitivity, including private deployments where data must stay in your environment.

How do you measure whether an agent is working?

Before the build we agree a baseline and target for the workflow, such as time saved, response time or accuracy. Agents are tested against reviewed cases before launch and monitored afterwards.

Can agents work with WhatsApp, Tally or our ERP?

Yes. We connect agents to WhatsApp Business, email, CRMs, ERPs and accounting tools including Tally and Zoho, using official APIs wherever they exist.

Do you provide AI agent development and automation in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers AI agent development and automation for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
