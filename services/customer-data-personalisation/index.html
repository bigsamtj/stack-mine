Quick answer

What are customer data platform and personalisation services?

A customer data platform (CDP) joins the records a business holds about each customer, from purchases and website visits to support tickets and WhatsApp chats, into one profile. Personalisation uses that profile to send the right message, offer or experience to each customer. Stack18 audits your customer data, resolves identities across systems, builds consent-aware segments and journeys on WhatsApp, email, SMS, app and web, and measures each one against a holdout group.

FAQ

Customer Data & Personalisation questions.

Ask us anything
Do we need to buy a customer data platform?

Not always. Many businesses can build a single customer view on their existing warehouse and CRM, while others benefit from a packaged CDP. The Customer Data Audit compares build, buy and extend for your size and budget, and we have no commission from any vendor.

Which tools do you work with?

We work with the CRM, messaging and analytics tools you already have, such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, WhatsApp Business Platform, email and SMS providers and GA4, and with warehouses such as BigQuery, Snowflake and PostgreSQL. Stack18’s Customer OS is an option when you need a ready-made base.

How do you handle consent under the DPDP Act?

Consent and notices are designed in from the start. We record what each customer agreed to, honour opt-outs on every channel, support access, correction and deletion requests, and limit each team to the data its purpose needs.

How is this different from CRM & Marketing Automation?

CRM & Marketing Automation sets up the CRM and the follow-ups your team runs every day. Customer Data & Personalisation joins data from every system into one profile and uses it to decide who gets what, then proves the effect with holdout groups.

How do you prove personalisation works?

Every journey keeps a random holdout group that does not receive it. We report the difference in purchases or conversions between the two groups, so you see incremental revenue rather than opens and clicks.

Do you provide customer data and personalisation services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and works with retail, D2C, education, healthcare and B2B businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price audit and weekly read-outs, wherever you are.
