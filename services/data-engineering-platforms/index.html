Quick answer

What are data engineering and data platform services?

Data engineering builds the systems that collect data from your applications, clean and organise it, and store it in a warehouse where reports and AI models can use it. Stack18 designs the architecture, builds pipelines from sources such as Tally, ERPs, CRMs and marketplaces, adds data quality and access controls, and documents everything so your team can run it.

FAQ

Data Engineering & Platforms questions.

Ask us anything
Do we need a data warehouse, or are dashboards enough?

Dashboards on top of spreadsheets work for a while. A warehouse becomes worthwhile when several systems must agree on the same numbers, or when data volumes and users grow. The audit tells you which stage you are at.

Which tools and cloud do you use?

We choose per project among warehouses such as BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake and PostgreSQL, with open or managed pipeline tools, based on your size, budget and skills.

Can you pull data from Tally and Indian marketplaces?

Yes. We have dedicated integration capabilities for Tally, Zoho, Shopify, and Amazon and Flipkart, among others, using official APIs or supported exports.

How is this different from Data & Analytics?

Data & Analytics covers the full journey from data strategy to dashboards and advanced analytics. Data Engineering & Platforms goes deep on the pipelines, warehouse and quality layer that everything else depends on.

How do you protect personal data?

We design access controls, masking and retention with the DPDP Act in mind, document data flows and limit access to what each role needs.

Do you provide data engineering and data warehouse services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers data engineering and data warehouse services for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
