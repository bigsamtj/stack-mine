Quick answer

What is a fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is an experienced technology leader who works with a company part-time, usually a few days a month, instead of as a full-time employee. Stack18's fractional CTOs set technology direction, guide architecture, help hire and organise engineering teams, oversee delivery and report to the board, and can bring in Stack18 engineers when you need to build.

FAQ

Fractional CTO Services questions.

Ask us anything
How much time does a fractional CTO give us?

Typically 2 to 6 days a month, set in writing. Some months are heavier, such as during hiring or a big decision. We agree the rhythm up front and review it every quarter.

Fractional CTO or full-time CTO?

A fractional CTO suits companies that need senior judgement but not yet a full-time executive. If you are heading towards a full-time hire, we help define the role and interview candidates, then hand over.

Will you manage our engineers or agency?

We set direction, review architecture and delivery, and coach your team or hold an agency to account. Day-to-day line management stays with you unless we agree otherwise.

Can the fractional CTO also build for us?

Yes, if you want that. A fractional CTO can bring in a Stack18 team. We keep advice and delivery clearly separate so recommendations stay honest, and your plans stay usable with any partner.

How do you protect our confidential information?

An NDA is available on request, and access to code and systems is limited to what the role needs.

Do you provide fractional CTO services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers fractional CTO services for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
