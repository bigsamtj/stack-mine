What is legacy application modernization?

Legacy application modernization is upgrading ageing applications, databases and integrations without stopping the business. Stack18 works in safe, reversible steps, using AI to map and document old code so nothing is lost, and proves parity before every switch-over.

FAQ

Legacy Modernization questions.

Ask us anything
Do we have to rewrite everything?

No. We assess each component and choose to retain, wrap with APIs, refactor or rebuild, based on value and risk.

How do you avoid downtime?

New modules run alongside the old system and traffic moves over gradually, with parity tests and rollback plans.

What if nobody understands the old code?

AI-assisted analysis maps the code, data and business rules, and our engineers verify the findings with your team.

How long does modernisation take?

Assessments take 3–4 weeks. Programmes run in phases of 6–12 weeks, each delivering something live.

Can you modernise to cloud at the same time?

Yes, where it makes sense. We design the target architecture and hosting together.

Do you provide legacy modernization in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers legacy modernization for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
