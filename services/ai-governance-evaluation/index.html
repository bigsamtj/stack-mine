Quick answer

What is AI governance?

AI governance is the set of policies, roles, controls and tests that make AI systems safe, accountable and compliant. Stack18 helps organisations define their AI policy and risk register, evaluate models and agents against test sets, add human approval gates, and prepare for obligations such as India's DPDP Act.

FAQ

AI Governance & Evaluation questions.

Ask us anything
Do we need AI governance if we only use off-the-shelf tools?

Yes. Risks such as data leakage, wrong outputs and unclear accountability apply to bought tools as much as built ones. Governance is lighter for low-risk use, and we scale it to match.

Is this about regulation or about quality?

Both. We help you meet obligations such as the DPDP Act and customer or regulator expectations, and we test whether systems actually perform, because compliance without quality protects no one. This is not legal advice.

What is an evaluation set?

A collection of real or realistic cases with known good answers, used to score an AI system before launch and after every change. It turns "it seems fine" into a measurable result.

How do human approval gates work?

For each AI action we decide the risk level and whether a person must approve it first. Low-risk actions can run automatically with logging. High-risk ones require sign-off, which we record.

Can you govern AI that another vendor built?

Yes. We can assess, test and set controls around systems built by other teams or bought from vendors, and we stay vendor-neutral in those reviews.

Do you provide AI governance and evaluation services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers AI governance and evaluation services for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
