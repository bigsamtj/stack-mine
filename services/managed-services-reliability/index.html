Quick answer

What are managed services and application support?

Managed services means a partner runs the day-to-day operation of your applications and cloud: monitoring, incident response, backups, patching, releases and improvements. Stack18 provides this under a written service level agreement, with monthly reports and a plan to reduce incidents over time, not just fix them.

FAQ

Managed Services & Reliability questions.

Ask us anything
Do you support systems you did not build?

Yes. We start with an assessment, document the system and stabilise it before taking on support, so we know what we are accepting responsibility for.

What does the SLA cover?

The SLA sets support hours, severity levels and response and resolution targets, and it names what is in and out of scope. We agree it in writing before the run phase starts.

Can you manage cloud costs too?

Yes. Cost reviews, rightsizing and budget alerts are part of cloud operations, and we report the effect every month.

Is support available outside office hours?

Coverage hours are part of the SLA you choose. Critical systems can have extended or on-call coverage, priced accordingly.

Will we keep access to everything you manage?

Yes. You keep ownership of accounts, code and infrastructure. We work inside your environment with named access, and everything is documented so you can change partners.

Do you provide application support and managed services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers application support and managed services for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
