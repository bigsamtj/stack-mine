Quick answer

What are predictive analytics and machine learning services?

Predictive analytics uses your historical data to estimate what is likely to happen next: how much of each product will sell, which customers are about to leave, which leads will convert or which invoices will be paid late. Stack18 checks whether your data can support a reliable model, builds and validates it against a simple baseline, deploys it into the systems your teams use and monitors it so accuracy does not quietly decay.

FAQ

Predictive Analytics & ML questions.

Ask us anything
How much data do we need for a predictive model?

It depends on the question. Demand forecasting usually needs at least 18 to 24 months of sales history to capture seasonality, while churn and lead scoring need enough past examples of the outcome you want to predict. The feasibility study tells you plainly whether your data is enough, before you pay for a build.

Will the model be a black box?

No. We prefer the simplest method that beats the baseline, show which factors drive each prediction and give every score reason codes your team can read. Every model ships with a model card that explains what it does, how it was tested and where it should not be used.

Which tools and platforms do you use?

We work in Python with standard open-source libraries for forecasting and machine learning, and deploy on your cloud (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud) or on-premise. Scores are written back to your ERP, CRM or BI tool rather than living in a separate system.

How is this different from Data & Analytics?

Data & Analytics tells you what has happened, through a trusted warehouse and dashboards. Predictive Analytics & Machine Learning estimates what is likely to happen next and puts that prediction into a daily decision, such as a reorder, a call list or a collections queue.

How do you handle personal data and the DPDP Act?

We use the minimum personal data the model needs, mask or pseudonymise it where possible, document the purpose and keep access role-based, in line with the DPDP Act. Your data is not used to train models for anyone else.

Do you provide predictive analytics and machine learning services in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and builds predictive models for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price feasibility study and weekly read-outs, wherever you are.
