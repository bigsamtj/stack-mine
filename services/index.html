FAQ

Questions, answered.

Ask us anything
How do engagements usually start?

With a free consultation, then a short, fixed-price first step such as a discovery sprint or assessment. You see the plan and the team before committing to more.

How do you price services?

Fixed price for defined scope, or a transparent monthly cost for a dedicated team. You get an indicative estimate after the first call.

What does AI-native delivery mean for us?

Your project runs on the Agentic SDLC. AI agents draft specs, code, tests and documentation, and senior people review and approve. You get more working software, sooner, with full traceability.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes. We work with teams in India and abroad, across time zones, with English as the working language.

Should we choose services, a solution or the Enterprise Pod?

Choose a solution when a ready-made system fits, services when you need something built or changed, and the Enterprise Pod when you want one team for a multi-year transformation. We will tell you honestly which fits.
