Quick answer

What is SaaS product development?

SaaS product development is building software that customers subscribe to, covering the MVP, multi-tenant architecture, subscription billing, onboarding and analytics. Stack18's product team takes an idea from MVP to scale-up, with as much focus on activation and retention as on code.

FAQ

SaaS Product Development questions.

Ask us anything
Can you build the MVP and also help us scale?

Yes. Most clients start with an MVP and move to a scale-up pod as traction grows.

Do you help with pricing and packaging?

Yes. We help design plans and limits, and build them into the product with feature flags and metering.

Do you support both Indian and global billing?

Yes. Razorpay for India and Stripe or Chargebee for global customers, with GST-compliant invoices.

Will investors be comfortable with the codebase?

Yes. Clean architecture, tests and documentation are standard, which matters in due diligence.

Can you take over from another agency?

Yes. We start with a code review and a stabilisation plan before adding features.

Do you provide SaaS product development in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers SaaS product development for businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
