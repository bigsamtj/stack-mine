Stack School is the practical learning initiative of Stack18, an AI-native technology company headquartered in Jaipur, India. What is Stack School?

Learning that starts from the work, not the syllabus.

Stack School is a learning initiative by Stack18, designed to bridge the gap between traditional education and what real technology and business work demands.

It is not a university and it isn’t trying to look like one. It is a place to pick up skills in AI, software, product and entrepreneurship by working on real problems, with guidance from people who build for a living.

How learning works

Learn it, build it, show it.

Every Stack School programme follows the same loop that Stack18’s own teams use to ship software: short cycles, working output at the end of each one, and review from someone more experienced before moving on.

  1. Learn a concept

    From someone who uses it at work, with the context of why it matters and where it breaks.

  2. Build something that runs

    A small, real project: an agent, an API, a prototype or a business model you can test.

  3. Get practitioner review

    Feedback from an engineer, product lead or founder on what works and what to change.

  4. Ship and repeat

    Finish something you can show, then take on a bigger problem in the next round.

FAQ

Stack School: common questions

Ask us anything
What is Stack School?

Stack School is the practical learning initiative of Stack18. It teaches AI, software engineering, product, entrepreneurship and digital transformation through hands-on projects guided by practitioners. It is not a university.

Who is Stack School for?

Students and fresh graduates, working professionals, startup founders and entrepreneurs, and businesses that want to build AI and technology capability across a team.

What can I learn at Stack School?

Seven tracks: Artificial Intelligence & AI Agents; Software Development & Engineering; Entrepreneurship & Startup Building; Product Management & Innovation; Digital Transformation & Business Technology; Automation & Future of Work; and Cloud, Data & Emerging Technologies.

Are Stack School programmes open now?

Programmes are being shaped now. Register your interest to get early notice when they open and a say in which tracks launch first.

Are classes online or in person?

Both are planned. Formats include online learning programmes, live workshops, masterclasses, bootcamps, industry and founder sessions, practical projects and corporate training. Each programme states its format when it opens.

How much does Stack School cost?

Fees, duration and any eligibility requirements are published with each programme when it opens. Registering interest is free.

Does Stack School guarantee a job or placement?

No. Stack School makes no placement promises. The focus is practical skills and projects you can show and explain.

Do I need a technical background?

Not for every track. Tracks start from fundamentals and move towards applying them, and you can tell us your experience level when you register interest.

Does Stack School offer corporate training?

Yes. Corporate training is one of the formats: learning tailored to a team’s tools and context, focused on outcomes the business cares about. Stack18 also runs AI transformation programmes for organisations.

Can I teach or mentor at Stack School?

Yes. Experienced founders, engineers, product leaders and technology leaders can apply through the Become a Mentor form to teach a session, mentor learners or help shape a track.
