Quick answer

How does Stack18 keep client data and AI work secure?

Stack18 delivers on the Agentic SDLC, where AI agents execute work but people approve requirements, architecture, releases and production changes at governance gates. Solutions and Enterprise work runs in environments you own or control, and every decision is recorded in an audit trail. We do not use your source code or business data to train AI models without your prior written permission.

Governed autonomy

Five gates between an idea and production.

Gates are configurable per project, team and risk level, and every decision is recorded to the audit trail. AI does the repetitive work; your people decide what moves forward.

1

Intent gate

Scope, goals and constraints are confirmed before anything is built.

2

Requirements gate

Acceptance criteria are approved by you before design starts.

3

Architecture gate

Design and data decisions are signed off before code is generated.

4

Release gate

Quality and security evidence is reviewed before a release candidate moves on.

5

Production gate

Promotion to production is approved by the right owner.

Who does what

AI executes. Your people govern.

Human approval is required for production releases, architecture changes and data access.

Your data

Your code, your data, your environment.

Ownership, access and AI handling are written into our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and into your agreement.

Subject to payment of the fees due, you own the source code, configurations, designs and documentation created for you, and you retain ownership of your data.

  • Deliverables created for you
  • Your data and content

Your environment

Solutions and Enterprise deliverables are deployed on servers, cloud accounts or environments that you own or control. The Agentic SDLC platform can run on your infrastructure.

  • Access only as you grant it
  • Only for the work engaged
  • Only for as long as needed

AI and model training

We do not use your source code or business data to train AI models without your prior written permission.

  • Approved AI providers agreed in advance
  • Data handling agreed in advance
  • Your data stays in your environment

Data protection and residency

Data handling is aligned with India’s DPDP Act and your own policies, with data residency in Indian regions where needed.

  • Role-based access to data
  • Policies enforced per workflow
  • Retention set in your agreement

Access and confidentiality

On Enterprise pods, every member works under SSO, least-privilege access and joiner-mover-leaver controls, with NDAs, IP assignment and background-verified team members.

  • SSO and least-privilege access
  • NDAs and IP assignment
  • Confidentiality continues after the engagement

Our delivery controls and offboarding

Our delivery workflows use access controls, least-privilege access, encryption in transit and audit logging. At the end of an engagement we return or delete engagement materials and revoke access.

  • Audit logging of delivery workflows
  • Encryption in transit
  • Access revoked at the end
Governed AI for your business

If you run AI agents of your own, run them under control.

The same governance ideas apply to the AI you put to work in sales, support, finance and operations. AI Enterprise OS includes the controls below, so agents reach production with oversight from the first day.

Permissions and approvals

Define what each AI agent can read, write and do alone, and what needs a person to approve.

  • Per-agent permissions
  • Human approval for what matters

Audit trail and observability

Every prompt, tool call and decision is logged and searchable.

  • Searchable history
  • Review workflows

Guardrails and PII redaction

Unsafe outputs are blocked, and Aadhaar, PAN, phone and account numbers are masked, in line with DPDP expectations.

  • Budgets and guardrails on requests
  • PII masked before it reaches a model

Private by design

AI Enterprise OS gives your company a private AI workspace on your own data. Models are accessed through enterprise APIs or hosted in your cloud, and your data is not used for training.

  • Respects access rights in the knowledge base
  • Runs in your environment

FAQ

Security and AI governance questions.

Ask us anything
Do you use our data or code to train AI models?

No, not without your prior written permission. Where the Agentic SDLC uses third-party AI services during an engagement, we agree the approved providers and data handling with you in advance.

Where does our data live?

In environments you own or control. Solutions and Enterprise deliverables are deployed on your servers or cloud accounts, and we can support data residency in Indian regions where needed.

Can AI push changes to production without approval?

No. Promotion to production is approved by the right owner at the production gate, and human approval is required for production releases, architecture changes and data access.

Who owns the code you build for us?

Subject to payment of the fees due, you own the source code, configurations, designs, documentation and other deliverables created specifically for you. Stack18 retains ownership of its own platform, tools, frameworks and templates.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. NDAs are available on request, and our confidentiality duties continue after the engagement ends. Enterprise pods also include IP assignment and background-verified team members.

Can the Agentic SDLC run on our own infrastructure?

Yes. The platform can be deployed on your own infrastructure and connects to your Git, cloud, CI/CD, identity and observability tools.

Do you hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 or similar certifications?

This page does not list certifications. If your procurement requires a specific one, tell us on the first call and we will answer plainly, including what we can offer in its place, such as security reviews scoped with your team.

How do I raise a security concern or a privacy request?

Write to privacy@stack18.com or use our contact form. Our Privacy Policy sets out your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where they apply, other data protection laws.

How do I report a security vulnerability to Stack18?

Email privacy@stack18.com with a description of the issue, the affected URL or system and steps to reproduce it. Please do not access, change or keep data beyond what is needed to show the issue.

Is Stack18’s data handling aligned with India’s DPDP Act?

Yes. Data handling is aligned with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and your own policies, with data residency in Indian regions where needed. Your rights are set out in the Privacy Policy.

Can Stack18 help our company prepare for ISO 27001, SOC 2 or a VAPT?

Yes. Stack18’s Security & Compliance Advisory practice helps organisations prepare for ISO 27001, SOC 2, DPDP Act requirements and VAPT.

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Tell us.

If you believe you have found a security issue in the Stack18 website or in something Stack18 built, please report it privately so it can be investigated and fixed.

  • Where to send itprivacy@stack18.com
  • What to includeA description of the issue, the affected URL or system, steps to reproduce it and your contact details.
