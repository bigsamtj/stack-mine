Company overview

Infrastructure for the Agentic Enterprise.

Stack18 is an AI-native technology company founded in January 2018 by Prateek Mathur and headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It builds digital products, custom and enterprise software, ready-made business systems and AI agents, and runs enterprise transformation programmes for businesses in India and abroad. Everything is delivered on its own method, the Agentic SDLC, where AI agents execute the work and senior people approve the decisions that matter.
