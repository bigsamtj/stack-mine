Why Stack18?

A different way to build.

Stack18 is not another software development company. It is building an AI-native way of creating and transforming businesses, and it needs people who want to build that with it.

  • An AI-native environment

    Ten specialised AI agents work across seven lifecycle stages of every project. Using AI well is the job here, not a side experiment.

  • Real ownership and responsibility

    Work is assigned to people, not committees. If you scope it, you stand behind it until it ships, and you are trusted to.

  • Exposure to global projects

    Stack18 builds for businesses in India and abroad, so the problems you meet come from different markets, industries and scales.

  • Many technology domains

    Thirty service practices, six business systems and in-house ventures under one roof. Go deep in one domain or stretch across several.

  • A fast-moving, entrepreneurial culture

    Stack18 was started by an engineer and has kept the appetite for building. Decisions happen close to the work.

  • Continuous learning and experimentation

    The best way to build changes every few months. Trying new tools and methods is expected, and so is sharing what worked.

Life at Stack18

The people, the rooms and the moments in between.

Working sessions, whiteboards, learning days and celebrations. Most of the job is solving problems with other people, and making time to mark it when something ships.

How we work

An AI-native team, by default.

Stack18 delivers through the Agentic SDLC. It changes what a working day looks like: less repetitive work, more decisions, and a clear record of who approved what.

  • Ten AI agents, seven stagesSpecialised agents draft specifications, code, tests and documentation across the lifecycle. You direct them, review their output and make the calls.
  • Five governance gatesCritical decisions are approved by people at defined gates, with an audit trail, so speed never comes at the cost of judgement.
  • Two-week sprintsEach sprint ends in a demo someone can click. Progress is shown, not reported.
  • Senior accountabilityWhoever scopes the work stands behind it until it ships, and is trusted to make decisions close to the work.

Our culture

What we choose when two good things compete.

  • Ownership over hierarchy

    Anyone can pick up a problem. A title does not decide who gets to fix it.

  • Curiosity over assumptions

    We ask, test and check before we decide, and we change our minds when the evidence does.

  • Execution over excuses

    Working software at the end of every sprint is the standard we hold ourselves to.

  • Collaboration over silos

    Engineers, designers and strategists share one backlog and one outcome.

  • Continuous learning

    No finished skill sets. Time spent learning is part of the work, not a reward for finishing it.

  • Ambition with accountability

    Aim high, and own the result either way.
