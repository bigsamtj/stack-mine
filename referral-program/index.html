In short

What is the Stack18 Referral Program?

The Stack18 Referral Program rewards people who introduce new clients to Stack18. If a business you refer signs an engagement, you receive a share of what it pays Stack18 in its first twelve months: 5% for clients and individuals, and 10% for registered referral partners, up to ₹5 lakh per referral.

There is nothing to buy and no sign-up fee. You make the introduction; Stack18 does the scoping, selling and delivery, and keeps you informed as the conversation develops.

The rules

Simple, fair and written down.

These summarise the program. A short written referral agreement confirms them before any reward is paid.

01

New clients only

The business must not already be a Stack18 client or in active conversation with us when you introduce it.

02

First introduction counts

If two people refer the same business, the first complete introduction we receive is credited.

03

Six-month window

The referred business needs to sign within six months of your introduction for the reward to apply.

04

Paid as they pay

Rewards are calculated on payments received in the first twelve months, excluding GST, and paid within 30 days of each.

05

Tax handled properly

Rewards are subject to tax deduction at source where it applies. We will need your PAN, and a GST invoice if you are registered.

06

Consent first

Only share a contact's details if they are happy to hear from us. We handle all data under our privacy policy.

Not eligible: Stack18 employees, self-referrals, and engagements where the client's own rules do not allow referral payments. Stack Ventures and Stack Capital are outside the program. Stack18 may update the program; rewards for introductions already made follow the terms at the time.

FAQ

Referral Program: common questions

How much can I earn from a referral?

Clients and individuals earn 5%, and registered referral partners earn 10%, of what the referred business pays Stack18 in its first twelve months, excluding GST, up to ₹5 lakh per referred client.

When is the reward paid?

Within 30 days of each payment Stack18 receives from the referred client during its first twelve months.

Do I need to be a Stack18 client to refer?

No. Anyone can refer a business. Advisors and consultants who refer regularly can register as referral partners for the higher reward.

What if the business is already talking to Stack18?

The program covers new clients only. If the business is already a client or in active conversation with Stack18, the referral is not eligible, and we will tell you so.

How long is a referral valid?

The referred business needs to sign within six months of your introduction.

Is tax deducted from rewards?

Rewards are subject to tax deduction at source where it applies. We will ask for your PAN, and for a GST invoice if you are GST-registered.

How is this different from the Partner Programme?

The Referral Program is for introductions. The Partner Programme is for companies that co-sell, co-deliver or integrate with Stack18, with commercials agreed for each partnership.
