In short

How does Stack18 work?

Stack18 starts every engagement with a free 30-minute call and a short, fixed-price first step, then delivers in two-week sprints that each end in working software. All work runs on the Agentic SDLC: seven lifecycle stages, ten specialised AI agents that do the repetitive work, and five human governance gates where senior people approve what moves forward.

You can buy a ready-made system, a fixed-price services project, or a dedicated Enterprise Transformation Pod. Whichever you choose, the standards are the same: senior people accountable, progress you can click, and a record of who approved what.

Inside a sprint

Progress you can click, not a status report.

Two-week sprints keep work small, visible and easy to change. You see working software at least every week, and decisions are made with the people doing the work.

Because AI agents draft specs, code, tests and documentation, senior people spend their time reviewing, deciding and talking to you, rather than on repetitive work.

  • Plan togetherAgree the sprint goal and the stories that serve it, with acceptance criteria you approved.
  • Build with agentsSpecialised agents draft code, tests and documentation; engineers review and refine.
  • Weekly working demoA build you can click every week, on a link you can share inside your company.
  • Review and approveCritical changes pass a human gate, and every decision is recorded to the audit trail.
  • Ship and learnRelease through the pipeline, measure what happened and feed it into the next sprint.

FAQ

How we work: common questions

Something else? Ask us directly.

How does a project with Stack18 start?

With a free 30-minute call with a senior specialist. We reply within one business day with an indicative estimate, and the work begins with a short, fixed-price discovery of one to four weeks.

What is the Agentic SDLC?

Stack18's AI-native delivery method: seven lifecycle stages from discover to operate, ten specialised AI agents that execute the work, and five human governance gates where people approve what moves forward. Every decision is recorded to an audit trail.

Will AI agents make decisions about my project?

No. Agents draft specifications, code, tests and documentation. Senior people review their output, and human approval is required at the five gates, including for production releases, architecture changes and data access.

How often will I see progress?

Work runs in two-week sprints, with a working demo you can click at least every week.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Once the fees due are paid, you own the code, configurations, designs and documentation created for you, as set out in the Terms of Service and your agreement.

Can we start small?

Yes. The fixed-price discovery is designed so you can judge the fit before committing to more, and its output is yours to keep.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. NDAs are available on request before anything sensitive is shared.
