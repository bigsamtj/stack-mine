Before you write

What helps us reply faster.

You don’t need a brief or a specification to get in touch. These four things help the right specialist come to the first call prepared.

  • What you want to build or changeA sentence or two is enough: a new product, a system to replace, an AI use case.
  • Where you are todayCurrent tools or systems, team size and anything already tried.
  • Timing and constraintsDeadlines, budget range if you have one, and compliance needs such as DPDP.
  • Who to talk toYour role, and whether others should join the first call.
