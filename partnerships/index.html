What is the Stack18 Partner Programme?

Stack18 partnerships let technology companies, consultants, system integrators, agencies, independent advisors, enterprises and founders build, deliver and grow together with Stack18, an AI-native software company headquartered in Jaipur, India.

There are six partnership paths: technology, implementation, agency, referral, strategic & enterprise and venture. Each begins with a conversation, not paperwork, and the roles, client ownership and commercial model are agreed in writing before any shared work starts.

Why partnerships matter

Customers want one accountable team. Partnerships make that possible.

Most technology projects now cross more than one company’s strengths: a SaaS platform, an integration, an AI layer, change management and someone accountable for the outcome. Stack18 partners so each of those pieces is done by the people best placed to do it, while the customer sees one plan and one standard of delivery.

  • One delivery standardShared work runs on the same two-week sprints and working-software demos Stack18 uses on its own projects.
  • Clear ownershipWho owns the client relationship, and how revenue and delivery are shared, is agreed up front.
  • Long-term intentWe would rather build a few partnerships that compound than collect logos.
Why partner with Stack18?

A partnership works when each side makes the other stronger.

Here is what you can expect to gain by building with us, and what we hope to gain from building with you.

01

AI-native technology infrastructure

The Agentic SDLC is the platform Stack18 builds on: AI agents draft specs, code, tests and documentation while people review and decide. Partners get a delivery engine that lets a small team move like a much larger one.

02

Enterprise-grade engineering and implementation

Our engineers build software for organisations that cannot afford fragile systems. Partners can lean on that depth for architecture, integration, security and delivery instead of stretching their own bench.

03

Ready-made business solutions

Commerce OS, Customer OS, FinOps OS, AI Enterprise OS, Business OS and Supply Chain OS give partners something concrete to put in front of a customer, rather than starting every conversation from a blank page.

04

Joint business development

We prefer working an opportunity together: shared discovery, co-written proposals and introductions where there is a genuine fit. A lead thrown over the wall helps nobody.

05

Technical and delivery support

Architecture conversations, delivery guidance and a clear point of escalation, so partners are never left alone halfway through a customer project.

06

Long-term strategic collaboration

A few partnerships that compound beat a long list of logos. We invest in relationships where both sides expect to still be working together in a few years.

07

Opportunities to build new products and ventures

Through Stack Ventures, partners can go beyond reselling or referring: lead or co-found a company Stack18 has built, back it at spin-off, or become its first customer.

How partnerships work

Four steps from first conversation to growing together.

No lengthy onboarding or heavy paperwork before we have talked. We begin with a conversation and move at the pace of the opportunity.

  1. Connect

    Tell us about your organisation: what you do, who you serve and what you hope to build with a partner.

  2. Explore

    We look for the overlap together: customers, capabilities and opportunities that make sense for both of us.

  3. Collaborate

    Define the partnership model: roles, how work is shared, how customers are introduced and how success is measured.

  4. Grow Together

    Execute, deliver and expand. Start with one shared opportunity, learn from it and widen the collaboration from there.

FAQ

Partnering with Stack18: common questions

Ask us anything
Who can partner with Stack18?

SaaS and AI companies, cloud providers and technology vendors; IT consultants and system integrators; digital, marketing and development agencies; independent consultants and advisors; enterprises and institutions; and founders, operators and investors.

What types of partnership does Stack18 offer?

Six: technology (integrations and joint solutions), implementation (deliver with Stack18), agency (extend your capability), referral (introduce and collaborate), strategic and enterprise (joint initiatives) and venture (lead, co-found or back Stack Ventures companies).

What do partners get from working with Stack18?

Access to the Agentic SDLC, where AI agents draft specs, code, tests and documentation while people review and decide; enterprise-grade engineering; six ready-made business systems; 30 service practices; joint business development; and technical and delivery support.

How do I become a Stack18 partner?

Send a partnership enquiry. The process has four steps: connect, explore where the overlap is, agree the partnership model, then deliver together and grow from the first shared opportunity.

Who owns the client relationship in a partnership?

It is agreed up front for each partnership, together with how delivery and revenue are shared, before any shared customer work begins.

Does enquiring commit me to anything?

No. Enquiring is free and creates no commitment for either side. A person on the Stack18 team reads every enquiry and replies by email.

Can companies outside India partner with Stack18?

Yes. Stack18 builds for businesses in India and abroad, and partners can bring local knowledge and delivery capacity to work that crosses regions.

What can partners sell or deliver?

Stack18’s six business systems (Commerce OS, Business OS, Customer OS, Supply Chain OS, FinOps OS and AI Enterprise OS), its 30 service practices and, for larger organisations, the Enterprise Transformation Pod.

How is a venture partnership different from other partnerships?

Venture partnerships go beyond delivery or referral: operators can lead or co-found companies that Stack Ventures has built inside Stack18, and investors can back them at spin-off.
