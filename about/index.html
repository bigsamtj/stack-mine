Our story

Founded by an engineer, built for the AI era.

Technology should help a business grow faster, not slow it down.

The beginning. Stack18 began in January 2018 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, when Prateek Mathur, then in the final year of his engineering degree, set out to build technology that helps businesses grow faster instead of slowing them down. Jaipur has been home base from day one, and it still is.

The early years. The first work was practical and hands-on: websites, mobile apps, custom software and ERP systems for startups and established brands. That work shaped the standard Stack18 still holds itself to, with senior people accountable for every outcome, a fixed-price first step so scope and cost are clear before anyone commits, and working software at the end of every two-week sprint.

The AI shift. As AI matured, Stack18 rebuilt the way it delivers. The result is the Agentic SDLC, its AI-native delivery method. Ten specialised AI agents work across seven lifecycle stages, from discovery to operations, while five governance gates keep senior people in control of what gets approved. Work done for one need carries into the next, so each project leaves a client further ahead than the last.

Today. Over the past eight years that starting point has grown into a global enterprise transformation company. Stack18 now offers thirty service practices, six ready-made business systems for commerce, ERP, CRM, supply chain, finance and AI priced in rupees from ₹1.6L, Enterprise Transformation Pods on 12 to 36 month contracts, and Stack Ventures, which builds new companies in-house and spins them off with their own CEO. GST, e-invoicing, UPI, WhatsApp and Indian languages are designed in from day one, because the company was built in India, for Indian business first.

What comes next. The ambition is bigger than where we started: to help enterprises embrace technology, modernise operations and drive growth, in India and around the world, whether the need is a first product or a multi-year transformation.

Ask us anything
Who founded Stack18 and where is it based?

Stack18 was founded in January 2018 by Prateek Mathur, then in the final year of his engineering degree, and is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.

What does Stack18 do?

Stack18 is an AI-native technology company. It builds digital products, custom and enterprise software, ready-made business systems and AI agents, and runs enterprise transformation programmes for businesses in India and abroad.

What is the Agentic SDLC?

The Agentic SDLC is the method Stack18 delivers on. Ten specialised AI agents work across seven lifecycle stages, with five human governance gates, so AI handles the repetitive work and senior people approve the decisions that matter. Read the full explanation.

How is Stack18 different from a typical IT agency?

Three things: delivery runs on AI agents with human approval gates rather than headcount alone; every engagement starts with a written scope and a fixed-price first step; and six ready-made business systems mean many businesses start from working software rather than a blank page. See the agency vs in-house vs Transformation Pod guide.

Which industries does Stack18 serve?

Retail and D2C, manufacturing, distribution and trading, BFSI and fintech, healthcare, education, real estate, logistics, and SaaS and startups.

How much does Stack18 cost?

Ready-made solutions start at ₹1.6L one-time, excluding GST, with no per-user fees. Services are fixed price after a short discovery, and Enterprise Transformation Pods run on 12 to 36 month contracts. See pricing.

Does Stack18 work with clients outside India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur and works with businesses in India and abroad, with global connections in San Francisco, London and Dubai.

What is Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures is Stack18’s company-building arm. It builds new companies in-house to product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, then hires a dedicated CEO and spins each one off as a separate company.
