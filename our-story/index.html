In short

What is the story of Stack18?

Stack18 was founded in January 2018 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, by Prateek Mathur, while he was in the final year of his engineering degree. It began by building websites, mobile apps, custom software and ERP systems, rebuilt its delivery around AI with the Agentic SDLC, and has grown into an enterprise transformation company serving businesses in India and abroad.

Jaipur has been home base from day one, and it still is: headquarters, engineering, product development and business operations are all here.

Four chapters

How one idea became a company.

2018Chapter one
1

The beginning

In January 2018, Prateek Mathur, then in the final year of his engineering degree, started Stack18 in Jaipur with one goal: technology that speeds a business up instead of slowing it down. Jaipur has been home base from day one.

  • Founded in Jaipur
  • Engineer-led from the start
BuildingChapter two
2

The early years

The first work was practical and hands-on: websites, mobile apps, custom software and ERP systems for startups and established brands. It shaped the standard Stack18 still holds itself to: senior people accountable for every outcome, a fixed-price first step so scope and cost are clear before anyone commits, and working software at the end of every two-week sprint.

  • Websites and apps
  • Custom software and ERP
  • Fixed-price first step
AIChapter three
3

The AI shift

As AI matured, Stack18 rebuilt the way it delivers. The result is the Agentic SDLC: ten specialised AI agents working across seven lifecycle stages, with five governance gates that keep senior people in control of what gets approved. Work done for one need carries into the next, so each project leaves a client further ahead.

  • 7 lifecycle stages
  • 10 AI agents
  • 5 human gates
2026Chapter four · Today
4

Enterprise transformation

Stack18 now offers thirty service practices, six ready-made business systems for commerce, ERP, CRM, supply chain, finance and AI priced in rupees from ₹1.6L, Enterprise Transformation Pods on 12 to 36 month contracts, and Stack Ventures, which builds new companies in-house. GST, e-invoicing, UPI, WhatsApp and Indian languages are designed in from day one, because the company was built in India, for Indian business first.

  • 30 practices
  • 6 systems
  • Enterprise Pods
  • Stack Ventures

What has not changed

Three standards, from the first website to multi-year programmes.

The work has grown from single apps to enterprise transformation. These have stayed exactly the same.

01

Senior people, accountable

Whoever scopes the work stands behind it until it ships, and is trusted to make decisions close to the work.

02

A fixed-price first step

Scope, timeline and cost are clear before anyone commits to anything larger.

03

Working software, every sprint

Two-week sprints end in a demo someone can click. Progress is shown, not reported.

When and where was Stack18 founded?

Stack18 was founded in January 2018 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, which is still its headquarters.

Prateek Mathur founded Stack18 while in the final year of his engineering degree. He leads the company from Jaipur.

What did Stack18 build first?

Websites, mobile apps, custom software and ERP systems for startups and established brands.
