Quick answer

Should you build or buy an ERP in India?

Build a custom ERP only when your core process is genuinely unique and a source of competitive advantage. Buy a packaged or SaaS ERP when your processes are standard and you accept the vendor’s roadmap and per-user fees. Start from a ready-made system, such as Stack18 Business OS, when you need Indian compliance (GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills) and mostly standard processes with a few of your own, live in 3 to 12 weeks at a one-time rupee price.

Key takeaways

The short version.

  • Build only for a unique core processA custom ERP makes sense when the process itself is a source of advantage.
  • Buy when processes are standardPackaged or SaaS ERP is quick to start but fees grow with every user.

Decide in five questions

Which path fits your business?

Answer honestly. The pattern across your answers points to your best path.

1

Is the process your edge?

If your core workflow is what customers pay you for, lean towards a custom build.

2

How many users in three years?

Per-user fees compound with headcount. More users favour ownership or no-per-user pricing.

3

How soon must you go live?

Days points to packaged SaaS. Weeks points to ready-made. Months or more leaves room for custom.

4

How heavy is your compliance load?

GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills and TDS come built in with ready-made Indian systems. A custom build starts from scratch.

5

Who will maintain it?

With no in-house team, choose a vendor-maintained option or a ready-made system with the optional Care plan, from 15% of build value per year.

FAQ

Common questions.

Ask us anything
How much does it cost to build a custom ERP in India?

Typical Indian agency quotes for comparable scope are often ₹5–20 lakh or more, and projects often take 3–6 months or more. Stack18 fixes the price after a short discovery so you know before you commit.

How long does a ready-made ERP take to implement?

For Stack18 Business OS, the Launch edition typically goes live in 3 to 4 weeks, Grow in 6 to 8 weeks and Scale in 10 to 12 weeks, depending on modules and integrations.

Is a ready-made ERP GST-ready?

Yes. Business OS includes GST accounting, e-invoicing with IRN and QR, e-way bills and TDS, with GSTR-ready reports.

Can we migrate from Tally?

Yes. Business OS includes Tally Migration and Sync, which brings masters, opening balances and history across, with ongoing sync if your CA prefers to keep filing from Tally.

Do we pay per user?

No. Stack18 solutions have no licence or per-user fees. You pay for cloud hosting on your own account and any third-party usage such as WhatsApp or payment gateway charges.

Can a ready-made ERP be customised?

Fully. You get production-ready modules on day one, and Stack18 can change workflows, screens and integrations to fit how you work, using the Agentic SDLC platform.

Which ERP option suits an SME moving off Tally and Excel?

Usually a ready-made ERP configured to your workflows. Business OS is built for Indian SMEs, includes Tally migration and sync in the Launch edition, and is priced once in rupees.

What should an ERP quote include?

All five parts of the cost: licence or build, setup and configuration, data migration, training and go-live, and running costs such as hosting and support. See the ERP cost guide.
