Quick answer

Should we buy ready-made software or build custom?

Choose ready-made software when your needs match common business functions such as ERP, CRM, commerce, supply chain, finance or AI agents, because it goes live in weeks at a known price. Choose custom software when the process is your competitive advantage or no existing system fits. Many businesses combine them: start with a ready-made system, then customise it or add custom modules on the same foundation.

Key takeaways

The short version.

  • Ready-made for common functionsERP, CRM, commerce, supply chain, finance and AI agents go live in weeks at a known price.
  • Custom for what makes you differentBuild when the workflow is unique or the software is your product.
  • You can combine bothStart ready-made and customise any module later on the Agentic SDLC.
  • Cost and time differ by an order of magnitudeEditions from ₹1.6L in weeks, against custom builds that often run to months.
  • Ownership is clear either wayReady-made runs on your cloud; custom deliverables are yours once paid for.

Decide in five questions

Where should you start?

Most teams answer yes to two or three of these. The pattern tells you where to begin.

1

Does an existing module cover most of it?

If a ready-made module already does most of what you need, start there and customise the rest.

2

Is the workflow your edge?

If customers choose you because of how you do it, build that part custom.

3

How fast do you need results?

Weeks points to ready-made. Months or more leaves room for custom.

4

What is the budget range?

Ready-made editions start at ₹1.6L. Typical custom builds are often ₹5–20 lakh or more.

5

Will your needs change?

Both can change. Ready-made modules can be customised on the Agentic SDLC, and custom code is changed at full cost.

FAQ

Common questions.

Ask us anything
Can we start ready-made and customise later?

Yes. You get production-ready modules on day one, and Stack18 can change workflows, screens and integrations to fit how you work, using the Agentic SDLC platform.

How much does custom software cost compared with ready-made?

Ready-made editions start at ₹1.6L, one-time. Typical Indian agency quotes for comparable custom builds are often ₹5–20 lakh or more, and Stack18 fixes custom prices after a short discovery.

Who owns the software?

For custom work, subject to payment, you own the deliverables created specifically for you. Stack18 retains ownership of its own platform, tools, frameworks and templates.

Where does ready-made software run?

On your own cloud account, typically in an Indian region, or on your servers. Your data never sits in a shared multi-tenant system.

How long does each take?

Launch editions typically go live in 3 to 5 weeks, and Grow and Scale editions in 6 to 14 weeks. Custom projects typically run 4 to 16 weeks as fixed-scope projects after a 1 to 4 week discovery, depending on scope.

What if we are unsure?

Start with a free 30-minute consultation. We will tell you honestly which route fits, even if that means a different vendor.

Is ready-made software the same as SaaS?

Not at Stack18. Its ready-made solutions are deployed on your own cloud or servers and priced once in rupees, with no per-user fees, unlike subscription SaaS.

What is hypercare?

A support period straight after go-live while users settle in. Stack18 includes 30 days of hypercare with its ready-made solutions.
