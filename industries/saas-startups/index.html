FAQ

SaaS & Startups questions.

Ask us anything
What if we only have an idea?

Start with a two-week, fixed-price discovery sprint. You leave with a scoped MVP, a prototype and a plan, and decide on the build afterwards.

Can you work alongside our existing team?

Yes. We can run as a dedicated product pod or extend your team on specific parts of the roadmap.

Can you build AI features into our product?

Yes. We build AI assistants and agents with evaluation, guardrails and monitoring so they are safe to ship.

What is Stack Ventures?

Stack Ventures is how Stack18 builds its own companies: in-house to product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, then a dedicated CEO and a spin-off. Operators can apply to lead or co-found a venture.

Do you build software for SaaS & Startups businesses in Jaipur and across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and works with SaaS & Startups businesses across India. You get a free 30-minute call, a fixed-price first step and working software every week, so the engagement works the same whether you are in Jaipur or elsewhere.
