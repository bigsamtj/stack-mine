Now live

Helios Academy has moved from concept to operation.

VERITAS Cohort 1 is the Academy's first programme and the standard for every cohort that follows.

VERITAS Cohort 1 is in session, focused on Digital Forensics.

It is an eight-week programme running from 12 October to 5 December 2026, free of charge, with over 100 registrations. The cohort marks the transition of the Academy from concept to operation.

The Academy now trains across three specialisations: Digital Forensics, Ethical Hacking and Cloud Security. Further tracks, including SOC Analyst Training, Network Security and Governance, Risk and Compliance, are planned as the Academy grows.

Questions, answered

Helios Academy: common questions

Is Helios Academy free?

VERITAS Cohort 1 is free of charge. Future programmes and cohorts will state their own terms.

Can I still register for VERITAS Cohort 1?

No. The cohort is in session until 5 December 2026. Future registration will be announced separately.

Are Ethical Hacking and Cloud Security available now?

They are defined Academy specialisations, but cohort dates have not been announced. The site does not present them as currently accepting students.

Is the Academy portal live?

No. The Academy Portal is a future student environment. A dedicated page explains the planned integration and current status.

Does the Academy issue certifications?

The site does not claim accreditation or certification that has not been confirmed. Programme completion and assessment arrangements are stated per programme.
