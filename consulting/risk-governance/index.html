Overview

Risk Governance at HELIOS SYSTEMS.

Make cyber risk a board-level decision, not a technical footnote.

Cyber risk is often reported as technical activity: incidents, vulnerabilities and controls. Boards need something different: the likelihood and impact of material outcomes, the assumptions behind them and the decisions available.

HELIOS SYSTEMS helps institutions build risk governance that connects technical evidence to business consequences, with clear ownership, tolerance and reporting.

Discuss an advisory mandate →How we work →

Independent by design

Advisory recommendations are based on evidence and the institution's context. Where a conflict exists, it is disclosed before the mandate begins.

Approach

Structured advisory, from evidence to decision.

The sequence is adapted to the mandate; no engagement begins without clear authority.

  1. Define the risk framework

    Connect business services, assets, threats and tolerance.

  2. Assess and quantify

    Use evidence and judgement to prioritise material risk.

  3. Design governance

    Owners, reporting, escalation and decision rights.

  4. Operate and improve

    Review, challenge and adapt as the environment changes.

Questions

Risk Governance: common questions.

Do you guarantee compliance?

No. Compliance depends on the institution's implementation, evidence and the assessor. HELIOS SYSTEMS provides advisory and readiness support, not a guarantee of certification.

Which frameworks do you use?

The framework follows the obligation and the institution's context. ISO 27001, NIST and NDPA are referenced in the whitepaper as relevant examples; others may apply.
