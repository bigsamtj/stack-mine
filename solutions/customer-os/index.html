Built for Indian regulations

Compliance built into Customer OS.

Customer data and outbound messaging are among the most regulated things a business does in India. Customer OS treats consent as a feature, not an afterthought.

DPDP Act and Rules, 2025

Notified on 14 November 2025. Businesses need clear notices, specific consent, easy withdrawal, reasonable security and timely erasure, with core duties phasing in over 18 months.

How Customer OS handles itThe Consent & DPDP Centre records what each contact agreed to, honours withdrawals on every channel and handles access and erasure requests.

TRAI DLT for SMS

Commercial SMS must use DLT-registered sender headers and templates, with promotional messages kept apart from service and transactional ones.

How Customer OS handles itSMS campaigns use your registered headers and templates, and message categories are enforced at send time.

Do-not-disturb preferences

Promotional calls and messages must respect customers' DND preferences under TRAI's regulations.

How Customer OS handles itContacts carry DND status, and promotional campaigns skip anyone who has not opted in.

WhatsApp Business Platform

Marketing, utility and authentication messages need prior opt-in and approved templates, and Meta bills per delivered template message.

How Customer OS handles itOpt-ins are captured with source and time, templates are managed in one place and message spend is reported by category.

Call recording

Recording customer calls requires telling the caller, and recordings are personal data.

How Customer OS handles itCalls play a recording notice, and recordings follow retention and access rules you set.

Regulated sectors

Banks, NBFCs, insurers and brokers face extra rules on calling hours, disclosures and record-keeping.

How Customer OS handles itCalling windows, mandatory scripts and record retention can be configured per team.

This summary is for planning and is not legal or tax advice. Thresholds and dates change, so confirm current requirements with your CA or legal adviser. We update configurations when rules change under the Stack18 Care plan.

FAQ

Customer OS questions, answered.

Pricing, compliance, data and timelines. If your question is not here, a senior specialist will answer it on a free 30-minute call.

Talk to Stack18
Can my team keep using WhatsApp?

Yes, on one company number through the WhatsApp Business API. Every conversation is shared, assigned and saved to the customer record.

Which lead sources can you connect?

Meta and Google ads, IndiaMART, JustDial, 99acres, MagicBricks, website forms, missed calls and more. New sources are scoped with your quote.

Does the AI agent speak Indian languages?

Yes. It answers in English, Hindi and Hinglish out of the box, and other Indian languages can be added. It hands over to a person whenever it should.

Is there a per-seat fee?

No. You pay once per module. WhatsApp, SMS and telephony usage is billed by those providers at their rates.

Is it DPDP-ready?

The Consent & DPDP Centre records consent, honours preferences and handles data requests, and all data stays on your own cloud account.

Can we migrate from our current CRM?

Yes. We bring across contacts, deals and history from tools like Zoho, LeadSquared, HubSpot or spreadsheets.

How much does a CRM cost in India?

Customer OS editions cost ₹1.6L (Launch), ₹4.95L (Grow) and ₹8.7L (Scale) one-time, excluding GST, with unlimited users. WhatsApp, SMS, calling and email usage is paid directly to each provider.

How is Customer OS different from a per-user CRM?

You pay once for the modules you need and add as many users as you like, and sales, marketing, WhatsApp and support share one customer record on your own cloud.

Does Customer OS help with DPDP Act compliance?

Yes. The Consent & DPDP Centre records notices and consent, honours withdrawals across channels and handles access, correction and erasure requests. Your legal team still decides your policies.

Do we need the WhatsApp Business API?

For team inboxes, campaigns and automation, yes. We set up the WhatsApp Business Platform in your company's name, and Meta bills you directly per message.

Can it sync leads from Meta, Google, 99acres, Housing or IndiaMART?

Yes. Ad & Portal Lead Sync pulls leads from ad platforms and Indian portals into the CRM within minutes and assigns them automatically.

Can we keep using our existing ERP or billing tool?

Yes. Customer OS connects to Business OS, Tally, Zoho Books and other systems so quotes, invoices and payments show on the customer record.
