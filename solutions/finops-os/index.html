Built for Indian regulations

Compliance built into FinOps OS.

Indian finance teams absorbed several rule changes in 2024 to 2026. FinOps OS is set up for the current versions.

GST Invoice Management System (IMS)

Since October 2024, recipients can accept, reject or keep pending each supplier invoice on the GST portal before it reaches GSTR-2B.

How FinOps OS handles itPurchase invoices are matched with IMS data, and suggested accept, reject or pending actions are prepared for review before GSTR-3B.

E-invoice 30-day reporting

Businesses with turnover of ₹10 crore or more must report e-invoices, credit notes and debit notes to the IRP within 30 days (since 1 April 2025).

How FinOps OS handles itInvoicing & E-invoicing generates IRNs at the point of billing and flags any document close to the limit.

Income-tax Act, 2025

From 1 April 2026, TDS and TCS use new sections (392, 393 and 394), new return forms such as Form 138 and Form 140 and new certificates such as Form 130 and Form 131.

How FinOps OS handles itTDS & TCS computes deductions under the new sections and prepares data for the new returns and certificates.

MSME 45-day payments

Dues to registered micro and small enterprises must be paid within 45 days, or the expense can lose its income-tax deduction for that year.

How FinOps OS handles itThe MSME Payment Tracker ages every MSME bill and puts it in the next payment run before the limit.

Companies Act audit trail

Accounting software must keep an edit log of every change, which cannot be disabled (since 1 April 2023).

How FinOps OS handles itEvery posting, edit and approval is logged with user, time and before-and-after values.

Marketplace TCS and TDS

Marketplaces collect GST TCS at 0.5% and deduct income-tax TDS on seller payouts.

How FinOps OS handles itMarketplace Settlement Recon matches payouts with orders, fees, TCS and TDS, so credits are claimed in full.

This summary is for planning and is not legal or tax advice. Thresholds and dates change, so confirm current requirements with your CA or legal adviser. We update configurations when rules change under the Stack18 Care plan.

FAQ

FinOps OS questions, answered.

Pricing, compliance, data and timelines. If your question is not here, a senior specialist will answer it on a free 30-minute call.

Talk to Stack18
Will our auditors and CA be comfortable?

Yes. The ledger keeps a full edit log in line with the Companies Act audit trail rule, and if your CA prefers Tally or Zoho Books, the bridge keeps both in sync.

Which settlements can you reconcile?

Bank statements, UPI and payment gateways such as Razorpay, Cashfree and PayU, COD remittances and marketplace payouts from Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho.

Does it file GST and TDS returns?

It prepares GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and TDS return data with checks, ready for filing through the portal or your filing provider.

How does the MSME tracker help?

Under the income-tax rule on MSME dues, payments to micro and small suppliers beyond the allowed period can lose their tax deduction. The tracker flags those bills before the deadline.

Is there a per-user fee?

No. You pay a one-time build and setup fee per module, and the system runs on your own cloud.

How fast can we close the month?

Most of the time goes into reconciliation and chasing. With matching and the close checklist automated, teams typically close in days rather than weeks.

How much does finance automation software cost in India?

FinOps OS editions cost ₹2.4L (Launch), ₹6.2L (Grow) and ₹9.5L (Scale) one-time, excluding GST, with no per-user fees. Bank, gateway and API charges are paid to each provider.

Do we have to replace Tally or Zoho Books?

No. FinOps OS can sit on top of Tally or Zoho Books through the bridge module, or run its own general ledger if you prefer.

Does FinOps OS support the GST Invoice Management System?

Yes. Purchase invoices are matched with IMS data and accept, reject or pending suggestions are prepared for your review before returns are filed.

Is TDS updated for the Income-tax Act, 2025?

Yes. Deductions follow the new sections from 1 April 2026, and data is prepared for the new return and certificate forms.

Can it reconcile Amazon, Flipkart and payment gateway settlements?

Yes. Settlements are matched order by order with commissions, fees, TCS and TDS, and differences are listed for follow-up.

How does FinOps OS help with MSME payments?

The MSME Payment Tracker ages every bill from registered micro and small suppliers and schedules it before the 45-day limit.
