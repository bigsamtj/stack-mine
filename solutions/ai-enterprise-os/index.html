Built for Indian regulations

Compliance built into AI Enterprise OS.

AI touches personal data, decisions and customers. AI Enterprise OS is built so your risk and compliance teams can say yes.

DPDP Act and Rules, 2025

Personal data sent to AI systems is still covered by notice, consent, purpose limitation, security and erasure duties, phasing in over 18 months from 14 November 2025.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itGuardrails & PII Redaction masks personal data before it reaches a model, and every request is logged against its purpose.

RBI FREE-AI framework

RBI's August 2025 framework for regulated entities recommends a board-approved AI policy, governance across the AI lifecycle, audits, incident reporting and telling customers when they deal with AI.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itThe Governance Suite keeps an inventory of agents, approval records, evaluation results and incident logs, and customer-facing agents disclose that they are AI.

CERT-In directions, 2022

Cyber incidents must be reported to CERT-In within six hours, and ICT system logs kept for 180 days within India.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itAudit Trail & Observability retains agent and system logs in your Indian cloud region for the period you set.

SEBI and IRDAI expectations

Market intermediaries must report AI and ML systems they use to SEBI, and insurers face IRDAI rules on outsourcing and data.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itAgent inventory and model documentation are kept ready for regulatory reporting.

Human approval for consequential actions

Responsible AI practice, and most sector regulators, expect a human to approve decisions that affect customers, money or employees.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itPermissions & Approvals routes refunds, payments, offers and HR actions to a named approver before an agent acts.

Data residency and model choice

Many Indian enterprises require data to stay in India and want to avoid lock-in to one model provider.

How AI Enterprise OS handles itEverything runs in your own cloud account, and the AI Gateway lets you switch between commercial and open-weight models.

This summary is for planning and is not legal or tax advice. Thresholds and dates change, so confirm current requirements with your CA or legal adviser. We update configurations when rules change under the Stack18 Care plan.

FAQ

AI Enterprise OS questions, answered.

Pricing, compliance, data and timelines. If your question is not here, a senior specialist will answer it on a free 30-minute call.

Talk to Stack18
Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Models are accessed through enterprise APIs or hosted in your cloud, and your data is not used for training. Everything runs in your environment.

Which AI models do you use?

The platform is model-agnostic. We route each task to the right model for quality and cost, including Claude, GPT, Gemini or open-source models hosted by you.

What does AI usage cost to run?

Model usage is billed at the provider's rates on your account. The AI Gateway sets budgets per team and agent, so there are no surprises.

Can agents take actions, not just answer?

Yes. Agents can update your CRM, create tickets, draft invoices and send messages, but only within the permissions you set, and sensitive actions wait for human approval.

Do agents work in Indian languages?

Yes. Agents work in English, Hindi and Hinglish out of the box, and other Indian languages can be added, including on voice.

Where should we start?

With one agent on one high-volume job, usually customer support. The Launch edition puts it in production with governance in about a month.

How much do enterprise AI agents cost in India?

AI Enterprise OS editions cost ₹3.2L (Launch), ₹7.9L (Grow) and ₹12.5L (Scale) one-time, excluding GST. AI model usage, telephony and cloud hosting are paid directly to each provider, with budgets and alerts set in the AI Gateway.

Does AI Enterprise OS help with RBI's FREE-AI framework?

Yes. It keeps the agent inventory, approvals, evaluation results, incident logs and customer AI disclosures that the framework expects. Your board-approved AI policy remains your own.

Which AI models can we use?

Commercial models from major providers and open-weight models hosted in your own cloud. The AI Gateway lets you switch models per agent and caps spend.

How do you stop agents from making mistakes?

Each agent is tested on your own past cases before launch, guardrails block unsafe actions and consequential steps such as refunds or payments wait for human approval.

How is ongoing AI cost controlled?

The AI Gateway sets budgets per team and agent, caches repeat answers and routes simple tasks to cheaper models, with a monthly cost report.
