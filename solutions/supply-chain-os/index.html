Built for Indian regulations

Compliance built into Supply Chain OS.

Stock, movement and suppliers in India come with tax, traceability and payment rules. Supply Chain OS keeps the paperwork in step with the goods.

E-way bills for every movement

Goods moved above the value threshold need an e-way bill, including stock transfers between your own GSTINs and job work movements.

How Supply Chain OS handles itE-way bills are generated from dispatches and stock transfers, with vehicle updates and expiry alerts.

Job work and ITC-04

Inputs sent to job workers must come back within the time limits in the GST law, and movements are reported in ITC-04.

How Supply Chain OS handles itJob Work & Subcontracting tracks every challan, flags inputs nearing their return deadline and prepares ITC-04 data.

Batch, expiry and recall traceability

Food (FSSAI), drugs and cosmetics need batch-level traceability, and sellers must be able to recall affected lots quickly.

How Supply Chain OS handles itBatch, Expiry & Serial traces every lot from supplier receipt to customer dispatch, with FEFO picking and one-click recall lists.

Pharma barcodes (Schedule H2)

Since 1 August 2023, the top drug formulations listed in Schedule H2 must carry a barcode or QR code with specified product details.

How Supply Chain OS handles itLabels and scanning support GS1 barcodes and QR codes, so packs are verified at receipt and dispatch.

MSME 45-day supplier payments

Payments to registered micro and small suppliers are due within 45 days, and late dues can lose their income-tax deduction.

How Supply Chain OS handles itSuppliers carry their MSME status from onboarding, and purchase invoices age against the 45-day limit.

Legal Metrology on packaged goods

Packed goods must carry MRP, net quantity and manufacturer or packer details.

How Supply Chain OS handles itThese declarations are item master fields that print on labels and flow to sales channels.

This summary is for planning and is not legal or tax advice. Thresholds and dates change, so confirm current requirements with your CA or legal adviser. We update configurations when rules change under the Stack18 Care plan.

FAQ

Supply Chain OS questions, answered.

Pricing, compliance, data and timelines. If your question is not here, a senior specialist will answer it on a free 30-minute call.

Do we need to replace our ERP or Tally?

No. Supply Chain OS can run alongside Tally, SAP Business One or Business OS, and post purchases, stock and dispatches to your books.

Does it handle e-way bills and job work?

Yes. E-way bills and e-invoices are generated with dispatches and transfers, and job work movements keep ITC-04 ready records.

How accurate is the AI forecasting?

It learns from your own sales history, seasonality, festivals and promotions, and improves as data builds up. Planners see the forecast, the reasoning and can override it.

Can our suppliers and distributors use it?

Yes. The Supplier Portal and Distributor Management give them their own logins, so POs, dispatch notes, invoices and secondary sales flow in directly.

Is there a per-user fee?

No. One-time build and setup per module, deployed on your cloud.

Can we start with one warehouse or plant?

Yes. Most teams start with one site on the Launch edition and roll out to others once the process is proven.

How much does supply chain or inventory software cost in India?

Supply Chain OS editions cost ₹2.3L (Launch), ₹6.6L (Grow) and ₹12.7L (Scale) one-time, excluding GST, with no per-user fees. Hosting is paid at cost on your own cloud.

Does Supply Chain OS generate e-way bills for stock transfers?

Yes. E-way bills are created from dispatches and inter-warehouse transfers, including transfers between your own GSTINs.

Can it handle batch, expiry and serial numbers?

Yes. Every lot is traced from supplier receipt to customer dispatch, with FEFO picking, expiry alerts and recall lists.

Is there a warehouse management system for barcode picking?

Yes. Warehouse Management covers bins, put-away, pick lists, packing and cycle counts on mobile scanners.

Does it work with our existing accounting or ERP?

Yes. Supply Chain OS connects with Business OS, Tally, Zoho Books and other ERPs so purchase invoices and stock values stay in step.

Does it help us pay MSME suppliers on time?

Yes. Suppliers' MSME status is captured at onboarding and purchase invoices are aged against the 45-day limit, with alerts to your finance team.
