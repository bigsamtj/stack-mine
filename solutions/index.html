How pricing works

Transparent, modular and in rupees.

Pay per module, once

Each module has a one-time build and setup price in rupees. No licence, no per-user fee.

Save 10–20% with editions

Launch, Grow and Scale bundle the modules most businesses need, at 10%, 15% and 20% off.

Never charged twice

Bigger modules include the smaller ones they contain, and required modules are added for you.

Running costs stay yours

Hosting and third-party usage are billed by providers at cost. Care plan optional, from 15% a year.

Included in every edition and module price

  • Deployment on your own cloud account or servers
  • Configuration of workflows, roles, approvals and templates
  • Standard data import from Excel, CSV or your current tool
  • Team training, live and recorded
  • 30 days of hypercare after go-live
  • Documentation handed over to you

Paid separately, at cost

  • Cloud hosting On your own AWS, Azure or Google Cloud account, typically a few thousand rupees a month to start.
  • Third-party usage Payment gateway, WhatsApp, SMS, telephony and AI model charges, paid directly to each provider at their rates.
  • Custom features Anything beyond a module's scope is estimated separately before work starts.

Optional Stack18 Care plan from 15% of build value per year: updates, security patches, priority support and small enhancements.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Talk to Stack18
How is pricing calculated?

Each module has a one-time build and setup price in rupees, excluding GST. Editions bundle modules at 10–20% below their combined price. Bigger modules include the smaller ones they contain, so nothing is charged twice.

Are there monthly or per-user fees?

No licence or per-user fees. You pay for cloud hosting on your own account and any third-party usage such as WhatsApp, payment gateway or AI model charges. The Stack18 Care plan for updates and support is optional, from 15% of build value per year.

Can we combine solutions?

Yes. All six share the same foundation, so customers, products, stock and money flow between them without duplicate entry. Many businesses start with one and add another within a year.

How customisable are they?

Fully. You get production-ready modules on day one, and Stack18 can change workflows, screens and integrations to fit how you work, using the Agentic SDLC platform.

Where is our data hosted?

On your own cloud account, typically in an Indian region, or on your servers. Your data never sits in a shared multi-tenant system.

How long does it take to go live?

Launch editions typically go live in 3–5 weeks. Grow and Scale editions are delivered in phases over 6–14 weeks, depending on modules and integrations.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us how your business runs today. We will map the right solution and edition, and show it working with your own data.
