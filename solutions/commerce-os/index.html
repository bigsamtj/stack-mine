Why teams switch

What slows commerce teams down today.

The same problems come up in almost every first conversation. Here is what changes once they run on Commerce OS.

Stock never matches

Marketplaces, website and stores each keep their own count, so you oversell on one channel and sit on dead stock in another.

COD and RTO eat your margin

Fake orders, wrong addresses and failed deliveries quietly eat into margin, and nobody sees the full cost until month end.

Settlements are a black box

Gateway, COD remittance and marketplace payouts arrive net of fees and returns. Matching them to orders takes days in Excel.

Every new channel is a new project

Adding ONDC, WhatsApp or a retail store means another tool, another login and another spreadsheet to reconcile.

TodayWith Commerce OS
  • Stock updated by hand across Amazon, Flipkart and the website→One live inventory feeding every channel, so no overselling
  • COD orders shipped blind→COD verified on WhatsApp, risky pincodes flagged before dispatch
  • Courier panels juggled one by one→Courier aggregation with rate and serviceability rules
  • GST invoices typed after dispatch→E-invoice and e-way bill generated with the order
  • Settlements matched in Excel at month end→Every gateway, COD and marketplace payout auto-matched to orders
Built for Indian regulations

Compliance built into Commerce OS.

Selling online in India means GST, consumer and packaging rules on every listing and order. Commerce OS builds them into the workflow.

Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

Sellers must show their details, a grievance officer, return, refund and exchange policies, and country of origin on listings.

How Commerce OS handles itSeller, grievance and policy blocks are built into the storefront, and country of origin is a required product attribute.

Dark patterns guidelines, 2023

The CCPA guidelines prohibit practices such as false urgency, basket sneaking, drip pricing and confirm-shaming.

How Commerce OS handles itCheckout shows the full price, including delivery and COD fees, before payment, and nothing is added to the cart without the customer's action.

Legal Metrology labelling

Pre-packaged goods sold online must display MRP, net quantity, unit sale price, manufacturer or importer details and consumer care information.

How Commerce OS handles itThese declarations are structured fields in the product catalogue and appear on every product page.

Marketplace TCS and settlements

Marketplaces collect GST TCS at 0.5% of net taxable value (since 10 July 2024), alongside income-tax TDS on e-commerce payouts.

How Commerce OS handles itSettlement Reconciliation matches payouts, commissions, TCS and TDS against each order, so credits can be claimed.

GST e-invoicing and e-way bills

B2B orders from businesses above the e-invoice threshold need an IRN, and shipments above the e-way bill limit need an e-way bill.

How Commerce OS handles itIRNs and e-way bills are created from the order and shipment, not typed in separately.

DPDP Act and Rules, 2025

The DPDP Rules were notified on 14 November 2025, with core notice, consent and security duties phasing in over 18 months.

How Commerce OS handles itConsent capture, privacy notices, account deletion and marketing opt-outs are part of customer accounts.

This summary is for planning and is not legal or tax advice. Thresholds and dates change, so confirm current requirements with your CA or legal adviser. We update configurations when rules change under the Stack18 Care plan.

FAQ

Commerce OS questions, answered.

Pricing, compliance, data and timelines. If your question is not here, a senior specialist will answer it on a free 30-minute call.

Talk to Stack18
Can I start with just a website and add marketplaces later?

Yes. Start with the Launch edition and add modules whenever you need them. Everything shares one catalogue and order book, so nothing is rebuilt when you grow.

Do I pay a percentage of my sales?

No. Commerce OS is a one-time build and setup fee per module, with no GMV commission and no per-seat fees. Gateway, courier and WhatsApp charges are paid directly to those providers at their rates.

How do you reduce RTO?

COD orders are confirmed on WhatsApp, addresses are checked for quality, risky pincodes follow your rules (prepaid-only, partial COD or hold), and NDRs are followed up automatically.

Will it work with my existing Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Yes. We can migrate your catalogue, customers and order history, or keep your storefront and run orders, inventory and reconciliation on Commerce OS.

Is GST handled for every channel?

Yes. HSN codes and tax rates live on the product, and e-invoices, credit notes and e-way bills are generated with the order, including for stock transfers.

How much does a D2C ecommerce platform cost in India?

Commerce OS editions cost ₹2.8L (Launch), ₹7.3L (Grow) and ₹13.95L (Scale) one-time, excluding GST. Stack18 takes no commission on your orders. Hosting, payment gateway, courier and messaging charges are paid directly to each provider.

Does Commerce OS take a percentage of our sales?

No. You pay once for the modules you choose. Your payment gateway and couriers charge their own fees, and marketplaces keep their commissions on marketplace orders.

Can we sell on ONDC?

Yes. The ONDC Seller App module connects your catalogue and orders to the ONDC network through a seller-side network participant.

How does Commerce OS reduce RTO on COD orders?

COD & RTO Control confirms COD orders on WhatsApp, checks pincodes and addresses, scores risky orders and nudges customers towards prepaid, before the parcel ships.

Is the checkout compliant with the dark patterns guidelines?

The checkout shows the full price including delivery and COD charges before payment, uses no pre-ticked add-ons and avoids false urgency, in line with the CCPA guidelines.

Can we migrate from Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes. Products, customers and order history are imported, and URL redirects are set up so search rankings carry across.
