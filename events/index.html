What are Stack18 Events?

Stack18 Events are in-person and online gatherings run by Stack18, an AI-native technology company headquartered in Jaipur, India. They bring founders, technology leaders, engineers, business owners and students together around AI, software, startups and digital transformation.

Formats range from small founder meetups and hands-on workshops to technology summits, networking evenings, community gatherings and partner-led sessions. Every event is announced on this page with its date, venue or online format, audience and registration details.

FAQ

Stack18 events: common questions

Ask us anything
Are there any upcoming Stack18 events?

Not at the moment. No Stack18 events are scheduled right now. New events are announced on this page with their date, venue or online format and registration details, and subscribers to event updates hear about them first.

What kind of events does Stack18 run?

Six formats: founder meetups, technology summits, workshops and masterclasses, networking events, community gatherings and partner events. Topics include AI agents, the Agentic SDLC, enterprise AI transformation, startups and software engineering.

Where are Stack18 events held?

Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. Each event announcement states whether it is in person, and where, or online.

Who can attend Stack18 events?

Founders, entrepreneurs, technology leaders, engineers, product people, business owners and students. Each event lists the audience it is designed for.

Are Stack18 events free?

Registration details, including whether an event is free or paid and any eligibility, are published with each event when it is announced.

How do I hear about the next Stack18 event?

Leave your email in the event updates form. You will only receive event announcements.

Can I speak at or co-host a Stack18 event?

Yes. Organisations, communities, speakers and venues can propose a joint event, a talk or a workshop through the collaborate on an event form. The team reviews every idea and replies by email.

How do events relate to Stack Connect and Stack School?

Events are where people meet. The Stack Connect keeps those conversations going between events, and Stack School turns workshop topics into deeper, practical learning.
