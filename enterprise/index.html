Quick answer

What is enterprise AI transformation?

Enterprise AI transformation means modernising technology, deploying governed AI agents in real workflows and measuring the results. Stack18 delivers it from one focused first engagement to a dedicated Enterprise Transformation Pod on a 12 to 36 month annual contract, with human approval at every critical gate.

01 · The challenge

AI pilots are easy to start. Enterprise transformation is harder.

A pilot shows what a model can do. A transformation changes how work gets done, on the systems that already run the business.

  1. 01
    The production gap

    AI experiments stay disconnected from production workflows.

    A demo that works in a sandbox still has to meet real approvals, real data and real exceptions before anyone relies on it.

  2. 02
    The foundations gap

    Legacy applications and fragmented data limit AI adoption.

    Agents are only as useful as the systems and data they can reach and trust. Foundations set the ceiling for what AI can do.

  3. 03
    The accountability gap

    Multiple vendors create disconnected delivery and accountability.

    When strategy, build and run are bought separately, context is lost at each handoff and no one owns the outcome end to end.

  4. 04
    The value and control gap

    Organisations struggle to measure value, govern AI and scale adoption.

    Without baselines, controls and named owners, it is hard to prove what is working, keep AI safe, or expand it beyond the first team.

Closing these gaps takes five disciplines working together.

  • Strategy
  • Engineering
  • Integration
  • Governance
  • Operating-model change

Strategy picks the right problems. Engineering and integration make solutions work on real systems. Governance keeps them safe and accountable. Operating-model change makes them stick.

02 · The transformation

Move from disconnected systems to an AI-native operating model.

Six shifts that take an enterprise from a collection of tools to a coordinated, governed way of working.

  1. FromManual workflows
    ToIntelligent, governed executionAgents handle the repeatable steps. People approve what carries risk.
  2. FromDisconnected applications
    ToIntegrated enterprise systemsApplications and data connected through APIs, not re-keyed between screens.
  3. FromIsolated AI experiments
    ToProduction-ready AI capabilitiesEvaluated, monitored and owned, inside workflows people already use.
  4. FromSlow, fragmented delivery
    ToAI-native software engineeringThe Agentic SDLC carries work from discovery to release, behind human-controlled gates.
  5. FromStatic reporting
    ToBetter-informed business decisionsTrusted data and a transformation scorecard, reviewed with leadership.
  6. FromSeparate vendors
    ToOne coordinated transformation partnerStrategy, architecture, build and improvement planned and owned together.
What this is not

Stack18 is not proposing to replace every system or automate every human decision. We help you identify where AI, automation, modernisation and better integration create meaningful value, and where existing systems and human judgement should stay exactly as they are.

05 · AI at work

Put AI to work inside real business workflows.

Three workflows, step by step: what the agent does, which systems it touches, where people approve, and how success could be measured.

◇ Illustrative examples. Not customer results or live deployments.

System An invoice arrives by email, upload or supplier portal and is logged against the supplier.

What the agent does

Reads incoming invoices, extracts the data, matches it to purchase orders, flags exceptions and prepares them for approval.

Systems it works with

Email or document inbox · Purchase order and receipt records · ERP or accounting system · Approval workflow

Where people approve

Approvers review flagged exceptions and invoices above agreed limits before anything is posted.

Success could be measured by

Invoices processed without manual touch · Exception rate · Time from receipt to approval · Cost per invoice · Audit-trail completeness

Related: FinOps OS · AI Enterprise OS (Finance Ops Agent module) · AI Transformation services

System A lead arrives from the website, a campaign or an inbound message and is logged.

What the agent does

Researches the company, scores fit against agreed criteria, enriches the lead record and drafts a personalised follow-up.

Systems it works with

CRM · Website and form inputs · Email or messaging · Public company information

Where people approve

A representative approves or edits outbound messages and any change to lead status.

Success could be measured by

Lead response time · Leads qualified with complete data · Representative time spent on research · Conversion from qualified lead to opportunity · Edit rate on drafts

Related: Customer OS · AI Enterprise OS (Sales & Lead Agent module) · AI Transformation services

System A customer request arrives by email, chat or messaging and is logged.

What the agent does

Retrieves customer and policy context, classifies the request, prepares responses and actions within pre-approved rules, and escalates the rest.

Systems it works with

Helpdesk or CRM · Order and account systems · Knowledge base · Messaging channels

Where people approve

People approve actions outside pre-approved policy, such as refunds above a set limit, and handle every escalated case.

Success could be measured by

First-contact resolution · Time to resolution · Handover rate · Answer accuracy against test sets · Customer satisfaction · Cost per resolved request

09 · Governance and enterprise trust

AI adoption with control built in.

Security, approvals and traceability are designed into delivery from the start. Specific terms, environments and responsibilities are set out in your agreement.

Five governance gates between an idea and production

  1. IntentScope, goals and constraints confirmed
  2. RequirementsAcceptance criteria approved by you
  3. ArchitectureDesign and data decisions signed off
  4. ReleaseQuality and security evidence reviewed
  5. ProductionPromotion approved by the right owner

Gates are configurable per project, team and risk level, and every decision is recorded to the audit trail.

  • Identity and access controls

    SSO, least-privilege access and joiner-mover-leaver controls for every Enterprise Pod member, with role-based access to data and policies enforced per workflow.

  • Data privacy and approved environments

    Work can run inside your cloud, repositories and tools. Handling is aligned with India's DPDP Act and your own policies, with data residency in Indian regions where needed. Your data is not used to train models without your prior written permission.

  • Human approvals and escalation

    People approve requirements, architecture, releases and production changes. Agents hand over to people on sensitive steps.

  • Evaluation and monitoring

    Agents are evaluated against test sets before launch and monitored in operation, with accuracy and handover rates tracked.

  • Traceability and auditability

    Every requirement, decision, approval and release is recorded on the Agentic SDLC, and every AI action is logged.

  • Security reviews and risk management

    Security evidence is reviewed at the release gate, and risks and issues are reviewed with your programme board every month.

  • Documentation and operational continuity

    Documentation and knowledge are captured as work happens, so nothing depends on one person. Every contract includes a documented handover plan, and team members work under NDAs and IP assignment.

11 · Questions

Questions leadership teams ask.

Straight answers on scope, delivery, governance and commercials.

What does AI transformation involve?

It changes how work gets done, not just which tools you use. It typically means identifying where AI creates value, preparing data and systems, building agents and workflows into real processes, governing them, and measuring results. Stack18 covers the lifecycle from discovery to scale, so strategy and delivery stay connected.

How is Stack18 different from an AI consultancy?

Many consultancies focus on strategy and recommendations. Stack18 combines advice with delivery: the team that scopes the work builds it on the Agentic SDLC, uses pre-built solutions where they fit, and stays accountable through measurement. If you only need a prioritised plan, an AI Opportunity Sprint is a defined first step. For a side-by-side view of options, see our decision guide.

Can Stack18 work with our existing systems?

Yes. New AI capabilities are designed to work with your existing applications, data and infrastructure, and legacy systems are modernised where they limit adoption. Nothing needs replacing by default. Integration scope is defined during discovery.

How does Agentic SDLC support the programme?

The Agentic SDLC carries software work from discovery to operation using ten specialised AI agents, with people approving requirements, architecture, releases and production changes at governance gates. It also gives leadership one view of progress, approvals and the audit trail across the programme.

How are security and AI governance addressed?

Controls are designed in from the start: access aligned to your policies, human approvals, evaluation and monitoring, and traceability. How do we begin with a focused use case?

Start with an AI Opportunity Sprint (typically 2–3 weeks, fixed price) to prioritise use cases, or go straight to an Agent Pilot to Production (typically 4–6 weeks, fixed price) if the workflow is already clear. The AI Transformation service page also describes a free 60-minute AI opportunity workshop for qualified businesses.

How is an Enterprise Transformation Pod structured?

A dedicated multidisciplinary team led by a Pod Director and an Enterprise Architect, with product and programme managers, engineering squads, and specialists in AI, data, cloud, security and change drawn in as needed, working alongside ten AI agents on the Agentic SDLC. Foundation Pods have 6–8 specialists, Scale Pods 12–20 and Enterprise Pods 25+, on 12, 24 or 36 month contracts. It works alongside your IT teams, builds your internal capability as it goes, and is not staff augmentation.

How are engagements scoped and priced?

Sprints and pilots are fixed price. Pod partnerships are annual contracts based on pod size and committed capacity, billed monthly or quarterly in INR or USD, and scoped after a transformation assessment. Capacity can flex within an agreed band each quarter, the skills mix can be rebalanced as priorities change, and part of the fee can be linked to outcomes.

How is business value measured?

We agree baselines, targets, owners and reporting cadence before work starts, across business value, productivity, software delivery performance, AI workflow performance, adoption, and risk and control. Progress is reported monthly to your programme board and reviewed with leadership each quarter.
