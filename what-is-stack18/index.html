What is Stack18?

Stack18 is an AI-native technology company founded in January 2018 by Prateek Mathur and headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It builds digital products, custom and enterprise software, ready-made business systems and AI agents, and runs enterprise transformation programmes, all delivered on its own AI-native method, the Agentic SDLC. It works with businesses across India and abroad.

Ask us anything
What is Stack18?

Stack18 is an AI-native technology company founded in January 2018 by Prateek Mathur and headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It builds digital products, custom and enterprise software, ready-made business systems and AI agents, and runs enterprise transformation programmes.

Who founded Stack18, and when?

Prateek Mathur founded Stack18 in January 2018, while in his final year of engineering. He leads the company from Jaipur, Rajasthan.

Where is Stack18 based?

Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India, and works with teams in India and abroad, across time zones, with English as the working language. Its global connections are San Francisco, London and Dubai.

What is the Agentic SDLC?

Stack18’s AI-native software development lifecycle and management platform: seven stages from discover to operate, ten specialised AI agents and five governance gates. See the Agentic SDLC explainer.

How many services and solutions does Stack18 offer?

30 service practices in five service lines (Strategy & Consulting, Product Engineering, AI Transformation, Modernization, Data & Growth) and six ready-made business systems with 171 modules.

How much does Stack18 cost?

Ready-made solutions start at ₹1.6L one-time, excluding GST, with no per-user fees. Services are fixed price after a 1–4 week discovery, and Enterprise Transformation Pods run on 12 to 36 month annual contracts.

Is Stack Ventures an investment fund?

No. Stack Ventures does not invest in outside startups. It builds Stack18’s own companies in-house to product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, then hires a CEO and spins each one off.

Has Stack18 delivered real projects?

Yes. Examples include a website, mobile app and ERP for a ₹100+ crore D2C brand in three months, and a student wellness app launched on Android and iOS in three months. See case studies.

Does Stack18 sign NDAs?

Yes. NDAs are available on request before anything sensitive is shared. See security and AI governance.

How do I get started with Stack18?

Book a free 30-minute consultation. A senior specialist replies within one business day, and a short, fixed-price first step follows so you can judge the fit before committing further.
