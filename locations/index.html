A global perspective

One company. A global perspective.

Stack18 is one team with one way of working. What these four places give it is range: more ways to see a problem, and more people to solve it with.

  • Relationships that travel

    Staying close to founders, enterprise leaders and technology teams in different markets is how we learn early what problems look like, and how they differ from place to place.

  • Partnerships across borders

    The right international partners can bring local knowledge and delivery capacity to work that crosses regions. See how we partner.

  • Expertise that doesn’t change with the postcode

    One method, the Agentic SDLC, and one engineering standard travel with us. Wherever a customer is, they get the same senior accountability and the same working-software rhythm.

Working with Stack18 from anywhere

Same process, same standard, wherever you are.

Working with Stack18 does not depend on being in the same room as the delivery team. The process is built on written scopes, visible progress and one accountable person.

  1. A free 30-minute call

    Talk to a senior specialist about what you are trying to build or change, from any time zone.

  2. A written scope and fixed price

    Before you commit, you get a written scope, timeline and fixed price for the first step.

  3. Working software every sprint

    Two-week sprints end in a demo you can click, so progress is visible wherever you sit.

  4. One accountable lead

    Whoever scopes the work stands behind it until it ships, with the same standard in every market.
