Business in Pune

What Pune businesses usually need.

Pune is one of India’s main automotive and engineering manufacturing centres, with industrial belts such as Chakan and Pimpri-Chinchwad, alongside a large IT base around Hinjewadi and a strong education sector.

For Pune manufacturers that means production planning, quality and inspection, warehouse and dealer systems, and integration with ERPs such as SAP Business One.

FAQ

Software for Pune questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Do you have an office in Pune?

No. Stack18 is based in Jaipur and serves Pune clients remotely, with visits by arrangement. Weekly video demos and a named delivery lead make remote delivery as smooth as working in the same city, and visits are arranged when they help.

Can you build around our existing ERP?

Yes. We integrate and extend rather than replace where that is wiser.

Can you handle shop-floor data?

Yes, with tablets, barcode scanning or machine integration where available.

How do we start?

A two-week fixed-price Discovery Sprint. The first step is a free 30-minute call with a senior specialist, followed by a fixed-price Discovery Sprint that settles scope, plan and cost.

Do you provide support?

Yes, under an annual agreement. Response times and scope are written into the support agreement, so you know who to call and how fast they will answer.

How much does software for Pune cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price Discovery Sprint that produces scope, a plan and a quote. Custom work is quoted at a fixed price once the scope is clear. Changes are agreed in writing before they are built, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

How do you measure success for software for Pune?

In week one we agree a baseline for time to launch, manual hours saved and reporting time. We report them at every weekly demo and review them against the baseline 30 days after go-live, so results are measured rather than assumed.

What should we prepare before the first call?

Bring a short description of how software for Pune works today, examples of the documents or screens your team uses, and the three numbers that matter most to you. A senior specialist will use them to outline a first step and a fixed price.

Do you provide Software for Pune across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Software for Pune for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
