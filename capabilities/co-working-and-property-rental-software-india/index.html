Quick answer

What is property rental and co-working software?

Property rental software manages leases, rent invoicing, deposits, utilities and maintenance for landlords and property managers, while co-working software adds desk and room bookings and memberships. Stack18 builds both with GST invoicing, payment links and tenant portals.

FAQ

Rental and Co-working Software questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Can it handle multiple properties and owners?

Yes. Properties, owners and tenants are separated with owner statements and consolidated manager views.

Can tenants pay online?

Yes. We integrate payment gateways with automatic reconciliation to invoices.

Can it run a co-working space?

Yes. Bookings, memberships, credits and visitor handling are supported.

Is GST handled?

Invoices apply GST as configured with your accountant.

Is it ready-made or custom?

Scoped in a two-week Discovery Sprint, with billing modules reused where they fit. We compare both routes with costs in the Discovery Sprint, so you can choose with the numbers in front of you.

How much does rental and co-working software cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price Discovery Sprint that produces scope, a plan and a quote. Custom work is quoted at a fixed price once the scope is clear. Changes are agreed in writing before they are built, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

How do you measure success for rental and co-working software?

In week one we agree a baseline for rent collection days, missed renewals and booking conflicts. We report them at every weekly demo and review them against the baseline 30 days after go-live, so results are measured rather than assumed.

What should we prepare before the first call?

Bring a short description of how rental and co-working software works today, examples of the documents or screens your team uses, and the three numbers that matter most to you. A senior specialist will use them to outline a first step and a fixed price.

Do you provide Rental and Co-working Software across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Rental and Co-working Software for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
