What are Odoo customisation and migration services?

FAQ

Odoo Customisation questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Do you implement Odoo or only customise it?

We focus on customisation, integration and migration, and scope implementation needs honestly in discovery.

Community or Enterprise?

Either. We confirm which edition fits your needs and licensing with you.

Will customisation block upgrades?

We follow upgrade-safe practices and document changes so upgrades stay manageable.

Can you migrate from Tally?

Yes. We migrate masters, opening balances and history and validate with your accountant. Your accountant reviews the ledger mappings before anything posts, and every posting is logged and traceable.

Could we move off Odoo later?

Yes. We keep data models documented so migration stays possible.

How much does Odoo customisation cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price Discovery Sprint that produces scope, a plan and a quote. Custom work is quoted at a fixed price once the scope is clear. Changes are agreed in writing before they are built, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

How do you measure success for Odoo customisation?

In week one we agree a baseline for process cycle time, manual re-entry hours and migration validation errors. We report them at every weekly demo and review them against the baseline 30 days after go-live, so results are measured rather than assumed.

What should we prepare before the first call?

Bring a short description of how Odoo customisation works today, examples of the documents or screens your team uses, and the three numbers that matter most to you. A senior specialist will use them to outline a first step and a fixed price.

Do you provide Odoo Customisation across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Odoo Customisation for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
