FAQ

AI Customer Support Implementation questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Will the AI make promises we cannot keep?

No. Answers come from your policies and data, with sources shown, and refunds or exceptions go to a person for approval. We also set topic limits so the agent declines what it should not answer.

Which channels are supported?

WhatsApp and web chat, connected to a shared helpdesk so your team can see every conversation and step in at any point.

Does it understand Hindi?

Yes. Hindi, English and mixed-language messages are supported, and we test quality on your own past tickets before launch. We review a sample of real conversations each week during the pilot to improve phrasing and coverage.

What happens when the AI is unsure?

It hands over to a person with the full conversation and a summary, so the customer does not repeat themselves and your team starts with context.

How long does implementation take?

Launch editions go live in 3 to 5 weeks. A first agent usually covers your top intents, such as order status, returns policy and delivery issues.

What does it cost?

Customer OS starts at ₹1.6L and AI Enterprise OS at ₹3.2L, both one-time, excluding GST, with no per-seat fees. Custom integrations are quoted at a fixed price after discovery.

Do you provide AI Customer Support Implementation across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers AI Customer Support Implementation for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
