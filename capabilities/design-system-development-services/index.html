Quick answer

What is a design system?

A design system is a shared library of design tokens, reusable components, patterns and guidelines used by designers and developers to build consistent interfaces. Stack18 audits your current UI, defines tokens for colour, type and spacing, builds accessible components in Figma and in React or Flutter, documents usage and sets up governance so the system keeps evolving.

Ask us anything
How much does Design System Development cost in India?

Most Design System Development projects start with a Discovery Sprint (1–4 weeks, fixed price), which fixes the scope, team and price before you commit. Design System v1 takes 6–12 weeks at a fixed price per phase. Design System Care runs on a monthly retainer. Prices exclude GST. Cloud hosting and third-party usage, such as messaging, payment or AI model charges, are paid at cost.

Do small teams need a design system?

A lightweight one helps even small teams: tokens and 20 to 30 core components save time quickly. Large systems suit multiple products or squads.

Which frameworks do you support?

React (including Next.js), React Native and Flutter, with Figma as the design source.

Can you build it on top of Material or another library?

Yes. Many design systems customise Material, Ant or shadcn/ui rather than starting from scratch.

How do teams adopt it?

We migrate key screens first, document usage with examples and run sessions with designers and developers.

Do you provide Design System Development across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Design System Development for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
