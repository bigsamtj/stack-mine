Quick answer

What is textile and garment ERP software?

Textile and garment ERP is business software that tracks yarn, fabric and finished garments through purchase, processing, production and sale, including job work with outside processors and size-colour variants. Stack18 delivers it on Supply Chain OS or as custom software, with GST billing and order tracking.

What we deliver

Textile and Garment ERP, end to end.

The features and building blocks of a Textile and Garment ERP engagement. Each is delivered by the same team on the Agentic SDLC, with your approval at every critical step.

Fabric, yarn and garment stock

Track material in the unit it is bought and sold.

  • Roll, lot and piece tracking
  • Multiple units with conversion
  • Shade and quality grading

Job work and processing

Know where every lot is and what it cost.

  • Challans to dyers and processors
  • Return reconciliation with wastage
  • Job work GST handling

Size-colour and style management

Style, size and colour variants are managed as a matrix with barcode labels, so stock stays accurate without a cluttered item list.

  • Size-colour matrix per style
  • Barcode labels
  • Season and collection views

Orders, production and dispatch

From buyer order to cutting, stitching and packed carton, status is visible without calls to the floor.

  • Order and delivery planning
  • Cutting and stitching status
  • Packing lists and dispatch

Costing, billing and reports

Style-wise costing, GST invoices with e-way bills and party-wise outstanding show margin and cash by style and customer.

  • Style-wise costing
  • GST invoices and e-way bills
  • Party-wise outstanding

FAQ

Textile and Garment ERP questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Can the system handle both manufacturing and trading?

Yes. We configure it for manufacturing flows, trading flows or both, and agree the scope in discovery.

Do you support job work challans?

Yes. Material sent to processors is tracked by challan, with return reconciliation, wastage and charges, and the GST treatment configured with your accountant.

Can we keep Tally for final accounts?

Yes. We can post to Tally so your accountant keeps working where they are comfortable. Your accountant reviews the ledger mappings before anything posts, and every posting is logged and traceable.

How long does a rollout take?

A Supply Chain OS Launch goes live in 4 to 5 weeks. Custom textile flows are scoped in a two-week Discovery Sprint.

Do you provide barcode labels for garments?

Yes. Labels can be generated per size and colour and scanned at dispatch and stock-taking.

How much does textile and garment ERP cost?

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price Discovery Sprint that produces scope, a plan and a quote. Supply Chain OS Launch is available from ₹2.3L, one-time, no per-user fees, excluding GST. Changes are agreed in writing before they are built, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

How do you measure success for textile and garment ERP?

In week one we agree a baseline for stock mismatch, job work turnaround and style costing accuracy. We report them at every weekly demo and review them against the baseline 30 days after go-live, so results are measured rather than assumed.

What should we prepare before the first call?

Bring a short description of how textile and garment ERP works today, examples of the documents or screens your team uses, and the three numbers that matter most to you. A senior specialist will use them to outline a first step and a fixed price.

Do you provide Textile and Garment ERP across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Textile and Garment ERP for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
