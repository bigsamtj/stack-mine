AI Agent Development, end to end.

The features and building blocks of an AI Agent Development engagement. Each is delivered by the same team on the Agentic SDLC, with your approval at every critical step.

Support and sales agents

Agents that answer customers on WhatsApp and web, qualify leads, look up orders and policies, and hand the conversation to a person when it matters.

  • Lead qualification
  • Order and policy answers
  • Handover to people

Finance and operations agents

Agents that read invoices, match payments, prepare reconciliation exceptions and draft reports, with every posting held for approval inside your limits.

  • Invoice processing
  • Reconciliation exceptions
  • Report drafting

HR and recruiting agents

Agents that screen applications against your criteria, answer policy questions from your handbook and propose interview slots, leaving decisions to your team.

  • Candidate screening
  • Policy Q&A
  • Scheduling

Tools and integrations

Agents act only through the tools you permit, such as your ERP, CRM or helpdesk, using scoped credentials and wrappers around older systems.

  • API tools
  • Permissioned actions
  • Legacy wrappers

Evaluation

Every agent is tested on real past cases before launch and scored again after, so quality is measured and regressions are caught early.

  • Test sets from real cases
  • Quality scorecards
  • Regression checks

Governance

Approval gates, redaction of personal data and a complete audit trail mean you can see, limit and explain what each agent did.

  • Approval gates
  • PII redaction
  • Audit trail

Ask us anything
What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent completes multi-step tasks, such as checking an order, drafting a refund and routing it for approval, by using tools and your data inside permissions you set. Stack18 builds both and recommends the simpler one when it is enough.

How do you test an agent before it goes live?

We assemble real past cases from your tickets, invoices or leads, define what a good outcome looks like and score the agent against them. After launch we sample live work weekly and re-run the test set whenever prompts, models or data change.

Which departments benefit first?

Teams with high-volume, rules-bound work see value soonest: support, sales follow-up, finance operations and HR screening. We start with one workflow, measure the result against your baseline, and extend only when the numbers justify it.

Where does our data live, and is it used to train models?

Agents run in your cloud or an approved environment with your permissions. We design so your data is not used to train public models and put the model and data terms in writing before build begins.

Can an agent take actions, or only suggest?

It can take actions through permissioned tools, for example creating a ticket or posting a draft entry. Sensitive steps such as refunds, payments or hiring decisions always wait in an approval queue for a person.

How is an agent project priced?

AI Enterprise OS is available from ₹3.2L one-time, excluding GST, with no per-user fees. Custom agents are quoted at a fixed price after a Discovery Sprint, so scope and cost are agreed before development starts.

Do you provide AI Agent Development across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers AI Agent Development for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
