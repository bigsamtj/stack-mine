Quick answer

What is the difference between a prototype and a proof of concept?

A clickable prototype shows how the product will look and feel, so users and investors can react to it before any code exists. A proof of concept is a small piece of working code that proves a risky technical question, such as an integration, an AI model or a performance target, can be solved. Stack18 builds either, or both, in one to four weeks.

FAQ

Clickable Prototype & Proof of Concept questions.

Cost, timelines, integrations and ownership. If your question is not here, ask a senior specialist on a free 30-minute call.

Ask us anything
Is the prototype reusable in the real product?

Designs are reused directly. Prototype code is usually throwaway by design, so the real build follows proper architecture.

Which tools do you use for prototypes?

Figma for clickable prototypes and the production stack you plan to use for proofs of concept, so results are realistic.

Can a prototype help us raise funding?

Yes. A realistic prototype tested with users is far more convincing than slides, and we package it for investor demos.

How much does a prototype cost?

Most clickable prototypes and proofs of concept are fixed-price engagements of one to four weeks; the exact price depends on the number of flows or the technical question.

Do you provide Clickable Prototype & Proof of Concept across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers Clickable Prototype & Proof of Concept for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
