Quick answer

How do you migrate data to a new CRM?

A CRM migration involves exporting contacts, companies, deals, activities, notes and files from the old system, cleaning and deduplicating them, mapping fields, owners and pipeline stages to the new CRM, importing in trial runs and checking counts and samples. Stack18 migrates into HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho and other CRMs, preserving relationships and history so sales teams can continue without gaps.

Ask us anything
How much does CRM Data Migration & Cleanup cost in India?

Most CRM Data Migration & Cleanup projects start with a Discovery Sprint (1–4 weeks, fixed price), which fixes the scope, team and price before you commit. Customer OS Launch costs from ₹1.6L, one-time and typically takes 3–4 weeks. CRM Migration takes 2–6 weeks at a fixed price. Prices exclude GST. Cloud hosting and third-party usage, such as messaging, payment or AI model charges, are paid at cost.

Will we lose deal history?

No. Deals, activities, notes and attachments can be migrated with their links to contacts and companies.

Which CRMs do you migrate between?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive, Freshsales, LeadSquared, Microsoft Dynamics and spreadsheets.

How do you handle duplicates?

With matching on phone, email, company and name, merge rules agreed with you and review for uncertain cases.

Can you enrich missing data?

Yes, with approved enrichment sources, respecting consent and data protection rules.

Do you provide CRM Data Migration & Cleanup across India?

Yes. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and delivers CRM Data Migration & Cleanup for businesses across India, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad and Ahmedabad. Work runs remotely with weekly demos, and on-site workshops are arranged for key milestones.
