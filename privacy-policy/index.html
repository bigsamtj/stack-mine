Privacy policy

What this website collects, and what it does not.

This policy explains how the HELIOS SYSTEMS corporate website handles information. It is written for the site as it currently operates: no third-party analytics, no advertising trackers and no connected contact backend.

  • Last updated 19 November 2026
Contact HELIOS →Terms of service →

This policy will be reviewed when the official domain and contact channels are confirmed.

  • 01AnalyticsNo third-party analytics or advertising trackers are loaded.
  • 02FormsMessages are not transmitted until a delivery endpoint is configured.
  • 03Academy portalNot yet publicly available; no student account data is collected here.
  • 04CookiesNo tracking cookies are set by the site's own scripts.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the HELIOS SYSTEMS corporate website. HELIOS SYSTEMS is the organisation described in the official whitepaper v3.0. Its home base is Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria. Official company contact details are not yet confirmed and will be published when available.

This policy does not cover third-party websites linked from this site, or a future Academy portal once it becomes operational. Those services will have their own notices.

2. Information the website collects

The site is a static website. It does not include a login, student records, payment processing or a connected customer database. The only place where you may enter personal information is a form. The forms collect the fields you see on screen, such as your name, email address, organisation and message.

Current delivery status: no form delivery endpoint is configured. When you submit a form, the page performs local validation and displays a notice that nothing has been sent. The information remains in your browser and is not transmitted to HELIOS SYSTEMS until a backend is configured.

3. Analytics and tracking

This website does not load Google Analytics, advertising pixels, social media trackers or third-party chat widgets. No tracking cookies are set by the site's own scripts. The site's JavaScript is used for navigation, accessibility and form validation.

4. Cookies and local storage

The site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. If a future version introduces analytics or a portal, this policy and any required consent mechanism will be updated before those features are enabled.

5. How information would be used

If a delivery endpoint is configured in the future, information submitted through a form would be used to respond to the enquiry, route it to the relevant division and maintain appropriate records. It would not be sold. Access would be limited to people who need it for the stated purpose.

6. Legal bases and retention

Where personal data is processed, the legal basis and retention period will depend on the nature of the enquiry and the law applicable to it. HELIOS SYSTEMS will not retain information longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a legal or contractual obligation requires otherwise.

7. Sharing

HELIOS SYSTEMS does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or professional advisers only where necessary to operate the site or respond to an enquiry, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or where required by law.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and the website does not claim a certification it has not obtained. Do not submit credentials, evidence or sensitive personal data through the contact form.

9. Your choices

You may ask what information relating to you is held, request correction or deletion, or object to processing, subject to applicable law. Until official contact details are published, use the contact form and state that your enquiry is a privacy request. It will not be transmitted while no endpoint is configured.

10. Children

The corporate website is not directed at children. Academy programmes may have eligibility requirements that will be stated with each programme.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated as the organisation and its services evolve. The "last updated" date at the top of the page will change when it does.

Contact

Official privacy contact details are not yet confirmed. Use the contact page for privacy enquiries; the site will state clearly that the form is not yet connected.
