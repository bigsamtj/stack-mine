In short

What is Stack Connect?

Stack Connect is a professional network run by Stack18, the AI-native technology company headquartered in Jaipur, India. It connects founders, entrepreneurs, engineers, AI practitioners, technology leaders, business owners, product managers and students so they can learn from each other, collaborate and open doors for one another.

Members take part through six networks and formats: a founder network, a technology and AI network, a business leaders network, startup networking, open community discussions and member-led knowledge-sharing sessions, alongside Stack18 events and Stack School.

What makes our community different?

A network of people, not another group chat.

Plenty of groups collect members. We care about what happens between them: the introduction that leads to a project, the candid answer that saves a mistake, the conversation that becomes a company.

  • Meaningful professional connectionsIntroductions with a reason behind them, not a stack of business cards.
  • Founder-to-founder knowledge sharingWhat worked, what didn’t and what you’d do differently, from people who have been there.
  • Access to technology insightsA clearer view of AI, software and digital transformation from people applying them.
  • Collaboration and business opportunitiesPartnerships, projects and ventures that begin as conversations between members.
  • Networking with industry professionalsA mix of backgrounds, sectors and seniority, so the room is never one-note.
  • Learning from real-world experiencesCase-by-case stories rather than theory, shared by the people who lived them.
  • Events and workshops to take part inGatherings and practical sessions that turn online connection into real-world relationships.

How membership works

From a short form to real connections.

There is no fee to send a request and no pitch required. Here is what happens after you reach out.

  1. Tell us about you

    Fill in the short membership form: your role, what you are working on and the network you are most drawn to.

  2. We reply by email

    A person on the Stack18 team reads every request and replies with next steps for the network that fits.

  3. Join the conversation

    Take part in discussions and knowledge-sharing sessions, and meet members at Stack18 events.

  4. Give and grow

    Share what you have learned, make introductions and collaborate on projects, partnerships or ventures.

House rules

Six principles that keep the community useful.

Every community is shaped by what it tolerates. These are the norms we ask every member to hold to.

Give before you askAnswer a question, share a lesson or make an introduction before asking for one.
No hard sellingMembers are here to learn and build together. Pitches belong in a conversation someone asked for.
Share what really happenedReal numbers, real mistakes and real trade-offs are worth more than polished success stories.
Respect the roomWhat is shared in confidence stays in the room. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
Make newcomers welcomeStudents and first-time founders are part of the community, not an audience for it.
Follow throughIf you offer an introduction or a review, do it. Trust is what makes the network useful.

FAQ

Stack Connect: common questions

Ask us anything
What is Stack Connect?

A professional network run by Stack18 that brings together founders, entrepreneurs, engineers, AI practitioners, technology leaders, business owners, product managers and students to learn from each other and create opportunities.

Who can join Stack Connect?

Anyone who builds with technology, or wants to: you do not need a particular title or company. Curiosity, a willingness to share and an interest in what others are making matter more.

Is there a fee to join?

No fee is needed to send a membership request. If a specific programme or event has a cost, it is stated clearly before you sign up.

How do I join Stack Connect?

Fill in the membership enquiry form with your role and the network you are most interested in. The Stack18 team reads every request and replies by email about next steps.

What networks can I be part of?

The Founder Network, the Technology & AI Network, the Business Leaders Network and Startup Networking, plus open community discussions and member-led knowledge-sharing sessions.

Is the community only for people in Jaipur?

No. Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur and works with businesses in India and abroad. Discussions are open to members wherever they are; in-person events list their location.

Can students join?

Yes. Students and aspiring builders are welcome, and often gain the most from meeting people who are already building. Stack School is the place for structured learning.

Can I share my own expertise with the community?

Yes. Knowledge-sharing sessions are led by members who have done something worth explaining. Mention it in your request, or propose a talk on the Events page.
