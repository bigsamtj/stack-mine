Quick answer

What is Bland AI?

Bland is a platform for building AI phone agents that make and receive calls at scale. Its per-minute rate covers the language model, speech-to-text and text-to-speech, and plans unlock lower rates, higher daily call caps and more concurrency, with an Enterprise tier for dedicated infrastructure.

  • Best forTeams running high-volume phone campaigns
  • PricingFree Start ($0.14 a minute); Build $299 and Scale $499 a month plus $0.12 and $0.11 a minute. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationTransfers and telephony billed on top.
  • AlternativesVapi · Retell AI · Synthflow · compare all

Overview

Bland AI overview: who it is for and what it does.

Bland focuses on phone calls. Developers and enterprises design agents with conversational pathways, connect tools and knowledge, and run high volumes of inbound and outbound calls with features such as voice cloning, call transfer, SMS and analytics. It emphasises self-hosted models and infrastructure for enterprises that want control and reliability.

On 5 December 2025 Bland replaced its flat $0.09 a minute with plan-based pricing. Start is free with $0.14 a minute, 100 calls a day and 10 concurrent calls; Build costs $299 a month with $0.12 a minute, 2,000 calls a day and 50 concurrent; Scale costs $499 a month with $0.11 a minute, 5,000 calls a day and 100 concurrent; Enterprise is custom. Call transfers cost $0.03 to $0.05 a minute depending on plan, and telephony is billed separately.

Main problem it solves

Runs high volumes of phone calls with AI agents on predictable bundled per-minute rates.

Typical customer segment

Developers, mid-market and enterprises with heavy call volumes.

What makes it distinct

Bland AI questions and answers

What is Bland AI used for?

Bland is a platform for building AI phone agents that make and receive calls at scale. Its per-minute rate covers the language model, speech-to-text and text-to-speech, and plans unlock lower rates, higher daily call caps and more concurrency, with an Enterprise tier for dedicated infrastructure.

Is Bland AI free?

Yes. Start is free, with talk time at $0.14 a minute and caps on daily calls and concurrency.

How much does Bland AI cost?

Free Start ($0.14 a minute); Build $299 and Scale $499 a month plus $0.12 and $0.11 a minute.

Which models does Bland AI support?

Bundled model, STT and TTS in the per-minute rate.

What are the limitations of Bland AI?

Transfers and telephony billed on top. Developer-oriented setup. Voice cloning and concurrency gated to paid tiers.

Which platforms does Bland AI run on?

Bland AI is documented as available on: Web (cloud), Phone, SMS, API.

Is Bland AI an AI agent or an AI assistant?

We classify Bland AI as: Agentic tool. A human-directed tool that can plan, edit and run actions, with the user reviewing or approving changes. The classification is based on vendor documentation and is not an endorsement.

What are the main alternatives to Bland AI?

The main alternatives to Bland AI covered on Stack18 are Vapi, Retell AI, Synthflow and ElevenLabs Agents. See the Bland AI alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.
