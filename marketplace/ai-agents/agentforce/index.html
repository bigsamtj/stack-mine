Quick answer

What is Salesforce Agentforce?

Salesforce Agentforce is a platform for building, deploying and governing AI agents that work on Salesforce CRM and Data 360 data, for customer service, sales, field service and employee use. It is sold through consumption-based Flex Credits, per-user licences and add-ons, and bundled editions.

  • Best forCompanies already running Salesforce Service or Sales Cloud
  • PricingFlex Credits $500 per 100,000 ($0.10 per action); user licence $5, add-ons $125 and editions from $550 per user per month.
  • Main limitationPricing has changed several times and is complex to model.

Overview

Salesforce Agentforce overview: who it is for and what it does.

Agentforce lets Salesforce customers build agents with Agent Builder, ground them in CRM and Data 360 data, and deploy them to customer channels and inside Salesforce apps. Agents can answer customers, resolve cases, qualify leads and take actions through Salesforce flows and APIs, with a reasoning engine planning the steps.

Pricing has changed several times. Agentforce launched at $2 per conversation in 2024, added Flex Credits in May 2025 ($500 per 100,000 credits, 20 credits or $0.10 per standard action, 30 per voice action), then added per-user licensing. In 2026 Salesforce lists an Agentforce User Licence at $5 per user a month (drawing on Flex Credits), unmetered add-ons at $125 per user a month for Sales, Service and Field Service ($150 for Industries), and Agentforce 1 Editions from $550 per user a month. Salesforce also completed its acquisition of Intercom (Fin) in September 2026.

Main problem it solves

Brings autonomous agents to companies whose customer, case and sales data already live in Salesforce.

What makes it distinct

FAQ

Salesforce Agentforce questions and answers

What is Salesforce Agentforce used for?

Salesforce Agentforce is a platform for building, deploying and governing AI agents that work on Salesforce CRM and Data 360 data, for customer service, sales, field service and employee use. It is sold through consumption-based Flex Credits, per-user licences and add-ons, and bundled editions.

Is Salesforce Agentforce free?

No standalone free plan. Salesforce Foundations, at no cost for eligible Enterprise-edition orgs, includes a starter allowance of Flex Credits.

How much does Salesforce Agentforce cost?

Flex Credits $500 per 100,000 ($0.10 per action); user licence $5, add-ons $125 and editions from $550 per user per month.

Which models does Salesforce Agentforce support?

Multiple model providers through the Salesforce trust layer.

How much does Agentforce cost?

Salesforce lists Flex Credits at $500 per 100,000 credits ($0.10 per standard action), an Agentforce User Licence at $5 per user a month, add-ons at $125 per user a month and Agentforce 1 Editions from $550 per user a month.

Can I mix Flex Credits and Conversations?

No. Guides based on Salesforce's terms note that an org must choose either Flex Credits or Conversations.

What are the limitations of Salesforce Agentforce?

Pricing has changed several times and is complex to model. Requires Salesforce; Data 360 and implementation costs add up. The $5 user licence still draws on Flex Credits.

Which platforms does Salesforce Agentforce run on?

Salesforce Agentforce is documented as available on: Web (cloud), Salesforce apps, Chat and messaging, Voice, Slack.

Is Salesforce Agentforce an AI agent or an AI assistant?

We classify Salesforce Agentforce as: Autonomous agent. Completes multi-step tasks with tools and limited supervision, as documented by the vendor. The classification is based on vendor documentation and is not an endorsement.

What are the main alternatives to Salesforce Agentforce?

The main alternatives to Salesforce Agentforce covered on Stack18 are Fin AI Agent, Sierra, Decagon and Microsoft Copilot Studio. See the Salesforce Agentforce alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.
