Overview

NiCE Cognigy overview: who it is for and what it does.

Cognigy was founded in 2016 in Düsseldorf by Philipp Heltewig and grew into one of the leading enterprise conversational AI platforms, recognised as a leader by Gartner and Forrester. Its AI agents answer questions, book services and escalate to human agents across web chat, messaging apps and phone, integrate with Salesforce, SAP and ServiceNow, and support more than 100 languages. Customers named in coverage include Mercedes-Benz, Nestlé and Lufthansa Group, and Openreach used Cognigy agents across 15 million customer journeys.

NiCE announced a $955 million agreement to acquire Cognigy on 28 July 2025 and closed it on 8 September 2025, bringing Cognigy into its CXone Mpower platform and its base of more than 25,000 customers. Pricing is enterprise and custom-quoted.
