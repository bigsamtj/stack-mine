Quick answer

What is Stack AI?

Stack AI is a no-code platform for building AI agents and workflows that run inside enterprise back-office processes, combining a drag-and-drop builder, knowledge bases over private documents, interfaces and analytics, with private model deployment and compliance features. Asana acquired the company in May 2026.

Overview

Stack AI overview: who it is for and what it does.

Stack AI was founded in 2022 by MIT alumni Bernardo Aceituno and Antoni Rosinol and backed by Y Combinator (W23) and Sequoia. Teams build agents on a drag-and-drop canvas, connect knowledge bases such as SharePoint and uploaded documents, add templates and reusable Skills, and publish agents as chat interfaces, forms or APIs. It emphasises enterprise deployment: private models on AWS Bedrock or Azure, EU data residency, SSO, audit logs and SOC 2 Type II.

In May 2026 Asana bought Stack AI for a reported $75 million. Pricing moved to enterprise sales during 2026: the pricing page now lists only Free (500 runs a month, 2 projects, 1 seat) and custom Enterprise, after the self-serve Builder ($99 a month) and Team ($499) plans were withdrawn. LLM tokens are passed through at provider rates or via your own keys.
