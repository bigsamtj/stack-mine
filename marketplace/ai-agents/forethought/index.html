Quick answer

What is Forethought?

Forethought builds AI agents that resolve, triage and route customer support requests across chat, email and voice, generating workflows from historical tickets. Zendesk completed its acquisition of Forethought in March 2026 and sells the product as Forethought AI Agents by Zendesk, inside Zendesk and on a standalone basis for other platforms.

Overview

Forethought overview: who it is for and what it does.

Forethought, winner of TechCrunch Startup Battlefield in 2018 and led by co-founder and CEO Sami Ghoche, built AI agents for support teams that resolve tickets, triage and route requests, and learn workflows from historical ticket data. It raised about $117 million before its sale.

Zendesk announced its intent to acquire Forethought on 11 March 2026 and completed the deal on 26 March 2026, in what reporters called its largest acquisition in about two decades. Zendesk said existing customers would keep service and updates, new customers could buy Forethought standalone without Zendesk, and the agents would work within Zendesk and across other platforms, with expansion into voice. Forethought became available to buy through Zendesk on 4 June 2026.

Main problem it solves

Automates repetitive support work by learning workflows from past tickets instead of hand-building every flow.

Typical customer segment

Mid-market and enterprise support teams, on Zendesk or other help desks.

Target users

  • Customer support and CX leaders
  • Support operations teams
  • Contact centre managers

Primary use cases

  • Autonomous ticket resolution in chat and email
  • Ticket triage and routing
  • Generating workflows from historical tickets
  • Voice automation (planned under Zendesk)

What makes it distinct

  • Self-improving agents that learn from historical tickets.
  • Owned by Zendesk but sold standalone for other help desks.
  • Long track record in AI support (since 2018).
