Quick answer

What is UiPath Agentic Automation?

UiPath Agentic Automation is UiPath's platform for automating business processes with a mix of AI agents, software robots (RPA), APIs and people. Agent Builder creates and evaluates agents, and Maestro models, orchestrates and monitors processes that span agents, robots and human decisions.

  • PricingCommunity free; Basic from $25 a month; Standard and Enterprise quoted. Free plan available.

Overview

UiPath Agentic Automation overview: who it is for and what it does.

UiPath, the long-time RPA leader, rebuilt its platform around agentic automation in 2025. Studio builds automations, Robots execute them, Agent Builder provides a low-code canvas to create, debug and evaluate AI agents with templates, and Maestro orchestrates processes involving agents, robots and people with monitoring and process intelligence. In 2026 UiPath added a Conversational Agent with Gemini voice, Delegate (a desktop agent), Coding Agents and the option to run the agentic stack on premises, and it reported a $1.9 billion annualised renewal run-rate at 30 April 2026.

Pricing combines plans with licences and consumption units. As captured in September and October 2026, UiPath added a free-forever Community tier that includes Maestro, Basic still started at $25 a month while being repackaged, and Standard (with a 60-day trial) and Enterprise are quoted. In October 2026 Maestro was split into Maestro Automate and Maestro Orchestrate. Agentic workloads are priced on execution frequency and model token usage.

How much does UiPath Agentic Automation cost?

Community free; Basic from $25 a month; Standard and Enterprise quoted.

Which models does UiPath Agentic Automation support?

Multiple providers, including Gemini and Azure AI Foundry models; open-source models on premises.

What are the limitations of UiPath Agentic Automation?

Which platforms does UiPath Agentic Automation run on?
