Quick answer

What is ElevenLabs Agents?

ElevenLabs Agents (ElevenAgents, formerly Conversational AI) is a platform for building conversational AI agents that talk over phone and web, chat and email, using ElevenLabs' speech-to-text and text-to-speech models plus a choice of LLMs, tools, knowledge bases and telephony integrations.

  • Best forTeams prioritising voice quality
  • PricingFree tier; agents about $0.08 a minute (burst $0.16); plans up to $990 a month; Enterprise custom. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationLLM cost absorption could change.

Overview

ElevenLabs Agents overview: who it is for and what it does.

ElevenLabs, best known for realistic AI voices, extended into agents: teams configure an agent's prompt, voice, LLM, knowledge base and tools, then deploy it to a website widget, mobile apps, phone numbers through Twilio or SIP, and since 2026 chat and email. Its voice library spans 5,000+ voices in 70+ languages. The product was renamed from Conversational AI to Agents in September 2025.

On 7 May 2026 ElevenLabs cut API and Agents prices by up to 55% and introduced pay-as-you-go. As read on its pages in October 2026, agent minutes cost about $0.08, with burst capacity at $0.16 a minute and text messages at $0.003; subscription plans from Free to Business ($990 a month) include minutes, and telephony is billed by the carrier. ElevenLabs says it currently absorbs LLM costs, which independent reviewers note are not contractual.

Main problem it solves

Gives agents the most natural-sounding voices without stitching separate speech providers together.

Typical customer segment

Startups to enterprises building voice-first products.

Target users

  • Phone support and receptionist lines

What makes it distinct

  • ElevenLabs voice quality and 5,000+ voices.

  • Pay-as-you-go since May 2026.

What is ElevenLabs Agents used for?

ElevenLabs Agents (ElevenAgents, formerly Conversational AI) is a platform for building conversational AI agents that talk over phone and web, chat and email, using ElevenLabs' speech-to-text and text-to-speech models plus a choice of LLMs, tools, knowledge bases and telephony integrations.

Yes. A free plan includes a small allowance for testing.

How much does ElevenLabs Agents cost?

Free tier; agents about $0.08 a minute (burst $0.16); plans up to $990 a month; Enterprise custom.

Which models does ElevenLabs Agents support?

Choice of LLMs from major providers; ElevenLabs speech models.

What are the limitations of ElevenLabs Agents?

LLM cost absorption could change. Concurrency limits on lower plans. Telephony billed separately.

Which platforms does ElevenLabs Agents run on?

Is ElevenLabs Agents an AI agent or an AI assistant?
