Quick answer

What is Google Antigravity?

Google Antigravity is an agent-first development platform from Google. Launched in November 2025 as an IDE, it became a desktop app, CLI and SDK with version 2.0 in 2026, letting developers run several agents in parallel and review their Artifacts. Usage is free with limits for individuals and tied to Google AI Pro and Ultra plans for more quota.

  • Best forDevelopers already paying for Google AI Pro or Ultra
  • PricingFree individual quota; higher limits with Google AI Pro ($20 a month) or Ultra; Gemini Enterprise from $30 a seat. Free plan available.

Overview

Google Antigravity overview: who it is for and what it does.

Antigravity launched on 18 November 2025 alongside Gemini 3 as an agent-optimised IDE built on a heavily modified VS Code fork. Its Manager view shows which agent owns which subtask, and agents produce Artifacts such as task lists, plans, screenshots and browser recordings so developers can audit their work.

Antigravity 2.0, released at Google I/O 2026, turned the product into a platform: a rebuilt desktop app for macOS and Windows, a CLI and an SDK. Google also moved individual users of Gemini Code Assist and Gemini CLI on free and Google AI Pro or Ultra plans to Antigravity when it stopped serving those tiers on 18 June 2026.

Antigravity has no standalone subscription. Individuals get a free quota that refreshes weekly; Google AI Pro ($20 a month) and Ultra plans raise limits, refreshing every five hours up to a weekly ceiling, with optional AI credit overages. Developers protested quota tightening in March 2026, so limits are worth checking before relying on it.

Main problem it solves

Lets developers delegate work to several agents in parallel and audit what each did, inside a Google-built development environment.

Typical customer segment

Individuals on free or Google AI plans; organisations through Gemini Enterprise on Google Cloud.

Target users

  • Developers in the Google and Gemini ecosystem
  • Developers who want to run several agents in parallel
  • Teams on Google Cloud who want governed agent access

Primary use cases

  • Parallel agent work with the Manager view
  • Browser-testing with a browser subagent
  • Building and deploying to Firebase
  • Agent automation through the CLI and SDK

What makes it distinct

  • Agent-first design with a Manager view and auditable Artifacts.
  • Desktop app, CLI and SDK in version 2.0.
  • Quota bundled with Google AI plans rather than a separate subscription.

What is Google Antigravity used for?
