Quick answer

What is Gumloop?

Gumloop is an AI automation platform where teams build workflows on a node-based canvas and create AI agents that use tools and integrations, including its Gummie assistant that helps build flows. It offers 35+ models or bring-your-own keys and is billed in credits.

Overview

Gumloop overview: who it is for and what it does.

Gumloop is used by operations, marketing and sales teams to automate AI-heavy work such as research, data enrichment, content generation and document processing. Flows are built from nodes for models, scrapers, integrations and logic, and agents can run tools and chat with users. Plans include unlimited agents, seats and teams, 35+ models and bring-your-own API keys.

Pricing changed in 2026: the earlier free and Solo plans were replaced by Pro, starting at $37 a month with 20,000 credits, and custom Enterprise. There is a 14-day trial of Pro that needs a card, an 8% orchestration fee on top of credits, optional overage at $0.005 per credit (capped at one million credits a period by default), and credit rollover only on Enterprise. Pro allows five concurrent workflow runs and 25 concurrent agent chats.

Main problem it solves

Lets non-developers chain AI models, scrapers and apps into repeatable automations.
