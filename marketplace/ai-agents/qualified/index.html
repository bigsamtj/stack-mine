Quick answer

What is Qualified (Piper)?

Qualified is an agentic marketing platform built around Piper, an AI SDR agent that engages inbound website visitors in real time by text, voice and video, qualifies them, answers product questions, books meetings and follows up by email. It is Salesforce-native and sold in quoted Premier, Enterprise and Ultimate packages.

Overview

Qualified (Piper) overview: who it is for and what it does.

Qualified started in conversational marketing, as a Salesforce-native rival to Drift, and repositioned around Piper, an AI SDR agent that greets target accounts on the website, answers questions, qualifies them against the ICP, books meetings with reps and sends personalised follow-up emails. All plans include AI SDR Agent Studio, Agent Spotlight, account segmentation with waterfall enrichment and Salesforce integration.

Salesforce agreed to acquire Qualified in December 2025 and closed the deal on 1 April 2026 for consideration of about $1.2 billion, positioning Piper as its inbound pipeline agent alongside outbound agents. Pricing is quoted for Premier, Enterprise and Ultimate packages; independent trackers report contracts starting around $40,000 and list prices around $68,000 a year.

Main problem it solves

Converts high-intent website traffic into meetings around the clock without waiting for a human rep.

Typical customer segment

Mid-market and enterprise B2B companies on Salesforce.

Target users

  • Sales development teams
  • Revenue and growth leaders
  • Founders doing outbound

Primary use cases

  • Inbound website visitor engagement
  • Lead qualification and routing
  • Meeting booking from the website
  • Personalised follow-up email

What makes it distinct

  • Salesforce-native and now owned by Salesforce.
  • Text, voice and video conversations on the website.
  • Highly rated on G2.
