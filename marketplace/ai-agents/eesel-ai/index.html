Quick answer

What is eesel AI?

eesel AI is an AI layer for existing help desks: an AI agent that answers and triages tickets, a copilot that drafts replies, and internal chat for Slack and Microsoft Teams. It connects to Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gorgias, Intercom, Salesforce and 100+ sources and is priced in credits per task.

Overview

eesel AI overview: who it is for and what it does.

eesel sells AI for teams that want to keep their help desk. Its AI agent answers or triages tickets inside Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gorgias, Help Scout or Intercom; its copilot drafts replies for human agents; and its internal chat agent answers employee questions in Slack or Microsoft Teams. It learns from past tickets, help centres and connected documents.

eesel has changed its price list several times: per-interaction plans until March 2026, pay-as-you-go at about $0.40 per task from April to September 2026, then credit ladders. As of October 2026 paid plans run from $299 to $1,749 a month for 500 to 5,000 credits, with a free plan of 100 credits and add-ons for priority support and SSO. eesel publishes many comparison articles about competitors.

Main problem it solves

Adds AI answers, triage and drafting to an existing help desk without migrating.

Typical customer segment

Small to mid-size support teams on established help desks.

Target users

  • Customer support and CX leaders
  • Support operations teams
  • Contact centre managers

Primary use cases

  • AI agent inside Zendesk or Freshdesk
  • Ticket triage and tagging
  • Reply drafting for human agents
  • Internal IT and HR answers in Slack

What makes it distinct

  • Layer on top of the help desk rather than a replacement.
  • Free plan and published credit plans.
  • 100+ integrations, including Slack and Teams.
