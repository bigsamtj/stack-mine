Quick answer

What is Slack?

Slack is a team messaging and collaboration platform, owned by Salesforce, that organises conversations into channels and offers huddles, search, integrations and AI features, sold per user in Free, Pro, Business+ and Enterprise+ plans.

Overview

Slack overview: who it is for and what it does.

Slack organises team communication into channels, direct messages and huddles, with search, file sharing and integrations that connect it to other tools. Independent 2026 guides describe four plans: Free, Pro, Business+ and Enterprise+, priced per user per month with discounts for annual billing.

The plans differ most in history, integrations, AI and administration. The Free plan is reported to include 90 days of message history, up to 10 app integrations and one-to-one calls, while paid plans lift those limits. Guides consistently report that Slack's separate AI add-on was discontinued in 2025, with basic AI such as conversation summaries and huddle notes on Pro, advanced AI such as AI search, channel recaps and file summaries on Business+, and enterprise search on Enterprise+.

Slack is part of Salesforce, and Enterprise+ replaced the older Enterprise Grid. Costs rise with every active user and with third-party apps, so buyers should compare the per-seat price, the AI tier they need and any security requirements such as SSO, SCIM and compliance exports.

Main problem it solves

Replaces scattered email threads with searchable, channel-based conversations, calls and app integrations so teams can coordinate in real time.

Typical customer segment

Small teams on Free, growing businesses on Pro, mid-size organisations on Business+, and large enterprises on Enterprise+.

Target users

  • Teams that want real-time channel-based communication
  • Organisations that connect many tools into one workspace
  • Larger organisations that need compliance exports, SCIM and enterprise search

Primary use cases

  • Team and project channels
  • Huddles and quick calls
  • Searchable conversation history
  • Connecting tools with app integrations
  • AI summaries, recaps and search

What makes it distinct

  • Channel-first messaging with a large integration ecosystem.
  • A free plan, and AI bundled into paid tiers rather than sold separately.
  • Part of the Salesforce family, with Enterprise+ replacing Enterprise Grid.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Slack pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • A free plan lets small teams start without paying, with Slack Connect for external messaging. [5]
  • AI is bundled into paid tiers instead of sold as a separate add-on, with advanced AI on Business+. [3]
  • Unlimited integrations on Pro and Business+ let teams connect many tools. [2]
  • Business+ adds compliance exports and a 99.99 percent uptime SLA for organisations with governance needs. [4]

Limitations

  • Per-user pricing grows quickly with team size, and external guests can add to cost. [6]
  • The Free plan limits message history to 90 days and integrations to 10. [5]
  • Advanced AI needs Business+, so teams on Pro get only basic AI features. [1]
  • Many connected apps charge separately, which raises total cost. [2]

Best for

  • Teams that want real-time channel-based communication
  • Organisations that connect many tools into one workspace
  • Teams that want AI summaries and search bundled into the plan

May be less suited to

  • Teams that need long-term documentation rather than chat history
  • Very cost-sensitive teams that need unlimited history and many integrations for free

FAQ

Slack questions and answers

What is Slack used for?

Slack is used for team messaging in channels and direct messages, huddles and calls, file sharing, searchable history and connecting other tools through integrations.

Does Slack have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan is reported to include 90 days of message history, up to 10 app integrations and one-to-one calls, with paid plans lifting those limits.

How much does Slack cost?

Independent 2026 guides report Pro at $7.25 and Business+ at $15 per user per month on annual billing ($8.75 and $18 monthly), with Enterprise+ on a quote. Confirm on slack.com.

Is Slack AI included?

Guides report that Slack's separate AI add-on was discontinued in 2025. Basic AI such as conversation summaries is on Pro, advanced AI such as AI search and recaps is on Business+, and enterprise search is on Enterprise+.

Is Slack a replacement for a wiki?

Slack is built for conversation, while a wiki or knowledge base such as Confluence or Notion is built for lasting documentation. Many teams use both. See those profiles.

What are the main alternatives to Slack?

The main alternatives to Slack covered on Stack18 are Notion and Confluence. Notion may suit teams that want a flexible wiki with a light project layer. Confluence may suit teams that already use Jira and want connected documentation. See the Slack alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Slack support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Unlimited app integrations on paid plans, Slack Connect, Salesforce family of products. API details: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Slack?

Per-user pricing grows quickly with team size, and external guests can add to cost. The Free plan limits message history to 90 days and integrations to 10. Advanced AI needs Business+, so teams on Pro get only basic AI features.

Which platforms does Slack run on?

Slack is documented as available on: Web, Desktop apps, Mobile apps.
