Quick answer

What is Nuclino?

Nuclino is a lightweight team wiki and knowledge base with real-time collaboration, linked items, visual canvases and a graph view. It has a free plan limited to 50 items, Starter at $6 and Business at $10 per user per month billed annually ($8 and $12.50 monthly), and a custom Enterprise plan. Sidekick AI for Q&A, content generation and image creation is part of the Business plan.

Overview

Nuclino overview: who it is for and what it does.

Nuclino positions itself as a clean, fast internal wiki for teams that find tools such as Confluence or Notion heavier than they need. Reviewers describe a simple, intuitive interface with a low learning curve, a visual canvas workspace unique among wiki tools, and real-time collaboration. Capterra shows 42 reviews on its summary page, and independent pricing comparisons call it the most affordable paid plan in the wiki and knowledge base category at $6 per user per month.

The free plan supports unlimited members with up to 50 items, 3 canvases and 2 GB of total storage, and includes limited Sidekick AI. Starter ($6 annual, $8 monthly) removes the item cap with unlimited items and canvases, 10 GB storage per user, 30-day version history, groups, private and default workspaces, access and editing restrictions, admin tools, publishing and priority support. Business ($10 annual, $12.50 monthly) adds Sidekick AI for Q&A, content generation and image creation, audit log, team insights, advanced security controls, SAML single sign-on, unlimited version history, 20 GB storage per user and a custom publishing domain. Enterprise is available by quote.

Buyers should weigh three limits. The free plan's 50-item cap forces most real teams to Starter quickly, AI is only on the $10 Business plan, and per-user pricing grows linearly, with 50 users on Starter costing about $300 a month. Sources conflict on enterprise controls: GetApp, Toolradar and CostBench list SAML SSO and audit logs on Business, while the comparison site Docsie, which sells a competing product, says Nuclino has no SSO, API access or SOC 2 certification at any price. Confirm security requirements with Nuclino before purchase.

Strengths

  • At $6 per user per month annually, Starter is the most affordable paid plan in the wiki category. [5]

FAQ

Nuclino questions and answers

What is Nuclino?

Nuclino is a lightweight team wiki and knowledge base with real-time collaboration, linked items, visual canvases and Sidekick AI, priced per user.

How much does Nuclino cost?

Nuclino Starter costs $6 and Business costs $10 per user per month billed annually, or $8 and $12.50 billed monthly. There is a free plan limited to 50 items and an Enterprise plan by quote.

Is Nuclino free?

Yes. The free plan supports unlimited members, up to 50 items, 3 canvases and 2 GB of total storage, with limited Sidekick AI.

Does Nuclino have AI?

Yes. Sidekick AI provides Q&A, content generation and image creation, and is available on the Business plan, with limited use on the free plan.

Does Nuclino support SSO?

Several listings, including GetApp, Toolradar and CostBench, show SAML single sign-on on the Business plan, while one comparison site says it is unavailable, so confirm with Nuclino.

What is the difference between Nuclino Starter and Business?

Business adds Sidekick AI, audit log, team insights, advanced security controls, unlimited version history, 20 GB storage per user and a custom publishing domain.

Is Nuclino cheaper than Notion?

A comparison on Docsie lists Nuclino Starter at $6 per user against Notion Plus at $10 and Nuclino Business at $10 against Notion Business at $20, though the sites offer different features.

What are alternatives to Nuclino?

Common alternatives include Notion, Slite, Confluence, Coda and Google Workspace.

What integrations does Nuclino support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Real-time collaboration, Public publishing, SAML SSO (Business). API details: One comparison site reports no API access; other sources do not confirm API availability.

What are the limitations of Nuclino?

The free plan is capped at 50 items and 3 canvases, so real teams need Starter quickly. Sidekick AI is only on the $10 Business plan and limited on Free. Per-user pricing becomes expensive at scale, such as about $300 a month for 50 users on Starter.

Which platforms does Nuclino run on?

Nuclino is documented as available on: Web.

What are the main alternatives to Nuclino?

The main alternatives to Nuclino covered on Stack18 are Notion, Slite, Confluence, Coda (Superhuman Docs) and Google Workspace. Notion may suit teams that want a flexible wiki with a light project layer. Slite may suit teams that want a maintained internal knowledge base with AI answers.
