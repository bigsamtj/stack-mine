Quick answer

What is Lark?

Lark is an all-in-one collaboration and productivity suite from ByteDance, known in China as Feishu. It combines Messenger, Email, Calendar, Docs, Wiki, Base (a database and workflow tool), Approval, Meetings, OKR and a developer platform. Plans are Starter (free, up to 20 users), Basic ($6 per user per month billed annually), Pro ($12) and custom Enterprise, with monthly billing about 20 percent higher.

  • Best forSmall and mid-size teams that want an all-in-one suite at a low per-user price
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingStarter is free for up to 20 users. Basic is $6 and Pro is $12 per user per month billed annually ($7.20 and $14.40 monthly). Enterprise is custom, with unlimited users. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationStarter is limited to 20 users, 18 months of message history and 1-on-1 video meetings.
  • AlternativesGoogle Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion, Zoho Cliq · compare all

Overview

Lark overview: who it is for and what it does.

Lark launched internationally in 2019 on top of ByteDance's internal Feishu tooling, positioning itself as a single work operating system instead of a stack of separate chat, document and project tools. Every plan includes Messenger, Email, Calendar, Approval, Base, Docs, Wiki, Meetings, OKR, AnyCross and Lark Developer, so teams get chat, email, collaborative documents, a spreadsheet-database called Base, approvals and video meetings without stitching tools together. Reviewers highlight unlimited real-time translation in messaging, even on the free plan, which suits multilingual teams.

Lark's plans scale by users, storage, meetings and automation. Starter is free for up to 20 users with 100 GB of storage, 18 months of message history, 1-on-1 video meetings and 1,000 automations a month. Basic ($6 per user per month annually) supports up to 500 users with unlimited message history and 1-on-1 meetings. Pro ($12 annually) keeps the 500-user limit but adds 15 TB of storage, 500-participant video meetings, 50,000 automations a month, larger Base tables and advanced Base permissions. Enterprise supports unlimited users with 15 TB plus 30 GB per user, advanced security and compliance, more SSO applications and custom pricing. Monthly billing is about 20 percent higher, for example $7.20 for Basic and $14.40 for Pro, and yearly billing saves 16.6 percent.

Lark competes with Slack, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace on price: its Pro plan at $12 is comparable to Google Workspace Business Standard. AI features such as meeting summaries, task extraction and a Base AI trial have expanded in 2026. Buyers should check the limits that matter: the free plan is capped at 20 users, video meetings are 1-on-1 until Pro, sources conflict on older limits such as 50 free users, and Enterprise pricing is not published, with third-party estimates varying. Organisations should also review data residency and vendor-ownership policies against their own requirements.

Replaces separate chat, email, document, wiki, approval and meeting tools with one suite, reducing app switching and per-tool licence costs.

Key features

Lark features explained.

Communication and meetings

Messenger with real-time translation
Chat includes unlimited real-time translation on every plan, with 18 months of message history on Starter and unlimited history on paid plans.
Email and calendar
Email and calendar are included in every plan, integrated with Messenger and Meetings.
Video meetings
Starter and Basic allow 1-on-1 video meetings, while Pro and Enterprise support 500-participant meetings.
AI meeting summaries
Lark has expanded AI meeting summaries and automatic task extraction in 2026, according to product summaries.

Documents and knowledge

Docs and Wiki
Collaborative documents and wiki spaces are included in every plan for team knowledge and notes.
Storage
Storage is 100 GB on Starter, 5 TB on Basic reported by independent sources, 15 TB on Pro and 15 TB plus 30 GB per user on Enterprise.
OKR
OKR tracking is included in every plan for goal setting.
Lark Developer and AnyCross
Lark Developer lets teams build apps and AnyCross connects Lark with other tools, included in every plan.

Workflow, Base and administration

Base and automations
Base is a database and project tool, with 1,000 automation runs a month on Starter and Basic, 50,000 on Pro and 500,000 on Enterprise, and table limits of 2,000, 20,000 and 50,000 rows.
Approval workflows
Approval workflows route requests such as leave and expenses, and are included in every plan.
Base AI and advanced permissions
Pro adds an expanded trial of Base AI and advanced Base permissions.
Security and compliance
Enterprise adds advanced security and compliance controls, SSO for up to 500 applications and custom identity providers.

FAQ

Lark questions and answers

What is Lark?

Lark is an all-in-one collaboration suite from ByteDance, known in China as Feishu. It combines chat, email, calendar, Docs, Wiki, Base, approvals, OKR and video meetings in one app.

How much does Lark cost?

Lark Starter is free for up to 20 users. Basic is $6 and Pro is $12 per user per month billed annually, or $7.20 and $14.40 billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Is Lark free?

Yes. Lark's Starter plan is free for up to 20 users with 100 GB of storage, 18 months of message history, 1-on-1 video meetings, unlimited real-time translation and 1,000 automations a month.

What is the difference between Lark Basic and Pro?

Both support up to 500 users, but Pro adds 15 TB of storage, 500-participant video meetings, 50,000 automations a month, larger Base tables and advanced Base permissions.

Does Lark have AI features?

Yes. Lark includes an expanded trial of Base AI on paid plans and has added AI meeting summaries and task extraction in 2026.

Is Lark the same as Feishu?

Lark is the international version of the platform that ByteDance operates in China as Feishu. It launched internationally in 2019.

Which devices does Lark support?

Lark runs on web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android.

What are alternatives to Lark?

Common alternatives include Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion and Zoho Cliq.

What integrations does Lark support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Exchange and Outlook, Google Calendar and Gmail, AnyCross integration platform, Lark Developer APIs, Web, desktop and mobile apps. API details: Lark Developer provides APIs and tools for building apps and bots, included in every plan.

What are the limitations of Lark?

Starter is limited to 20 users, 18 months of message history and 1-on-1 video meetings. Starter and Basic restrict video meetings to 1-on-1 sessions, so larger meetings require Pro. Enterprise pricing is not published and advanced security and compliance are limited to Enterprise.

Which platforms does Lark run on?

Lark is documented as available on: Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android.
