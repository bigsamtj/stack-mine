Quick answer

What is Zoho Survey?

Zoho Survey is an online survey tool from Zoho for customer, employee and market research surveys, with question logic, piping, themes, offline collection and reports, sold in Free, Basic, Plus, Pro and Enterprise plans.

  • Best forZoho users
  • Team size fitSolo and freelancers, Small teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingFree; Plus $25 a month billed annually.
  • Main limitationFree plan response caps.
  • AlternativesSurveyMonkey, Zoho Forms, Google Forms, Typeform · compare all

Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team · 2 sources

Overview

Zoho Survey overview: who it is for and what it does.

Zoho Survey suits teams already using Zoho CRM, Zoho Desk or Zoho Campaigns that want surveys linked to their data.

A cost guide lists Free (15 questions and 150 responses per survey), Basic at about $7.42 a month billed annually, Plus at $35 monthly or $25 annually, Pro at $49 or $35 and Enterprise at $109 or $75. The free plan includes multiple question types and logic.

Response and user limits on paid plans are listed on Zoho’s pricing page.

Main problem it solves

Collects and analyses survey feedback inside the Zoho ecosystem.

FAQ

Zoho Survey questions and answers

What is Zoho Survey used for?

Zoho Survey is used for customer satisfaction surveys, employee surveys, market research and event feedback.

Does Zoho Survey have a free plan?

Yes. Yes (150 responses per survey). Free trial: Yes (vendor).

How much does Zoho Survey cost?

Free; Plus $25 a month billed annually. From a third-party cost guide; regional pricing differs.

What is the difference between Zoho Survey Basic and Plus?

Basic is listed at About $7.42 a month. Plus is listed at $35 monthly; $25 annual. Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Zoho Survey?

The main alternatives to Zoho Survey covered on Stack18 are SurveyMonkey, Zoho Forms, Google Forms and Typeform. SurveyMonkey may suit teams running regular surveys with built-in analysis. Zoho Forms may suit Zoho users wanting affordable business forms. Google Forms may suit anyone needing free, simple forms connected to Google Sheets. See the Zoho Survey alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Zoho Survey support?

Documented integrations include Zoho CRM, Zoho Desk, Zoho Campaigns and Google Sheets (vendor). API: Zoho API.

What are the limitations of Zoho Survey?

Free plan response caps. Fewer design options than Typeform. Basic billed annually only.

Which platforms does Zoho Survey run on?

Zoho Survey is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.

Is Zoho Survey available in India?

Yes. Zoho Survey is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
