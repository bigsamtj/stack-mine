Quick answer

What is Rippling?

Rippling is a modular workforce platform that combines HR, payroll, benefits, IT device and app management, and spend management on one employee record, with pricing built per module per employee.

  • Best forGrowing companies that want HR, payroll and IT on one platform

Overview

Rippling overview: who it is for and what it does.

Rippling builds HR, payroll, benefits, device and app management, and spend management on one employee record, so that hiring someone can trigger payroll, accounts and laptops together.

Pricing is modular. A November 2026 guide gives a starting rate of $8 per employee per month that multiplies across modules, with a platform minimum and module prices that come from a quote. Global payroll is sold as a further module.

One-time implementation is quoted separately; figures cited range from about $2,000 for light setups to $75,000 for enterprise. Multi-year terms lock in the modules chosen at signing, and auto-renewal can revert negotiated rates to list price.

Main problem it solves

Connects HR, payroll, IT and spend so employee changes flow through every system automatically.

Typical customer segment

Growing and mid-size companies, including those with international employees.

Target users

  • Companies that want HR, payroll and IT in one system
  • IT teams that manage devices and app access by role
  • Companies paying employees in several countries

Primary use cases

  • Payroll and benefits
  • HR records and onboarding
  • Device and app management
  • Spend and expense management
  • Global payroll (module)

What makes it distinct

  • One employee record shared by HR, IT and finance modules.
  • Priced per employee per module.
  • Global payroll as an add-on module.

FAQ

Rippling questions and answers

What is Rippling used for?

Rippling is a modular workforce platform that combines HR, payroll, benefits, IT device and app management, and spend management on one employee record, with pricing built per module per employee. Typical uses include payroll and benefits, hR records and onboarding, device and app management, spend and expense management.

How much does Rippling cost?

A November 2026 guide reports a starting rate of $8 per employee per month that multiplies across modules, with module prices and a platform minimum set by quote.

How much does Rippling implementation cost?

Figures cited range from about $2,000 for light setups to $12,000 for mid-complexity, $30,000 for complex and $75,000 for enterprise.

Does Rippling do global payroll?

Yes, global payroll is sold as an additional module.

How does Rippling compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of BambooHR vs Rippling, Gusto vs Rippling and Rippling vs Deel, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Rippling?

The main alternatives to Rippling covered on Stack18 are Gusto, Deel, ADP Workforce Now and BambooHR. Gusto may suit US small businesses that want full-service payroll with simple HR. Deel may suit companies hiring contractors or employees in other countries. ADP Workforce Now may suit mid-size US employers that want payroll, benefits and HR from one provider. See the Rippling alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Rippling?

Strengths: HR, payroll, IT and spend share one employee record. A low reported entry rate of $8 per employee per month. Limitations: Module prices and the platform minimum are not published. The per-employee rate multiplies across every module you add.

Who is Rippling best for?

Rippling may suit growing companies that want HR, payroll and IT on one platform; IT-heavy teams that provision devices and apps by role; companies with employees in several countries. It may be less suited to very small teams, where minimums raise the cost per head; buyers who want fully published pricing.

What integrations does Rippling support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include HR, payroll, benefits, IT and spend modules, Global payroll module. API: API tier pricing is not published.
