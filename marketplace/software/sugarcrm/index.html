Quick answer

What is SugarCRM?

SugarCRM, founded in 2004 and privately held by Accel-KKR since 2018, is a CRM platform sold as Sugar Sell (sales), Sugar Serve (customer service), Sugar Market (marketing automation) and an on-premises Sugar Enterprise edition. Sugar Sell is reported at $59, $85 and $135 per user per month billed annually with a 15-user minimum, Sugar Serve at $80 per user and Sugar Market at $1,000 a month for 10,000 contacts. There is no free plan.

Overview

SugarCRM overview: who it is for and what it does.

SugarCRM targets mid-size and enterprise sales and service teams that want a configurable CRM with strong AI, and positions its products around predictive and generative AI under the Sugar Intelligence and SugarAI names. It was named a leader in sales force automation by Nucleus Research for 2026, according to its pricing page. The portfolio spans Sugar Sell for pipeline, forecasting and selling guidance, Sugar Serve for case management, omnichannel engagement, agent workspaces, SLA management and dashboards, and Sugar Market, which came from the 2019 acquisition of Salesfusion, for campaigns, landing pages, lead nurturing and buyer-journey tracking.

Pricing is sales-led and varies by source, so treat any figure as indicative. Sugar's pricing page lists Standard at $59 and a higher tier at $85 per user per month billed annually, and 2026 analyses (UsagePricing, CostBench's June negotiation guide and Lark) report Sugar Sell Standard at $59, Advanced at $85 and Premier at $135, each with a 15-user minimum and annual billing. Sugar Serve is quoted from $80 per user per month, on-premises Sugar Enterprise from $85 per user and Enterprise+ from about $120 or $160 depending on source, and Sugar Market from $1,000 a month for 10,000 contacts, with extra contacts from $150 a month. An older CostBench snapshot lists Essentials at $19 for 3 to 9 users and a $19 to $160 ladder, which conflicts with the 2026 sources.

Buyers should plan for the commercial terms as much as the sticker price. SugarCRM requires a minimum one-year term, there is no free plan or self-serve checkout, and a team of 15 on Sell Advanced pays about $15,300 a year before implementation, with CostBench estimating a 50-seat Advanced rollout with implementation at roughly $82,800 in year one. Negotiation guides suggest savings of 15 to 30 percent on contracts. The product is a poor fit for teams under 15 users, who usually do better with Pipedrive, HubSpot or Zoho CRM, and a strong fit for organisations that need on-premises deployment, deep customisation and AI-assisted selling.

Key features

SugarCRM features explained.

Sugar Sell (sales)

Sales automation and pipeline
Sugar Sell covers opportunity management, forecasting and sales workflows across Standard, Advanced and Premier tiers.
Email and calendar integration
Advanced builds on Standard with smarter workflows and email and calendar integration.
Sugar Intelligence and Smart Guides
Predictive and generative AI features such as Smart Guides and sales-i are included from Advanced up, according to UsagePricing.
Sandbox environments
Higher tiers include sandbox instances for testing changes, with two on the Enterprise tier according to Tech.co.

Sugar Serve and Sugar Market

Case management and SLAs
Sugar Serve provides case management, SLA management, dashboards and support workflows for service teams.
Omnichannel service workspace
A service-agent workspace and omnichannel engagement help agents handle conversations across channels.
Marketing automation
Sugar Market offers landing pages, campaign management, lead nurturing and buyer journey tracking, priced from $1,000 a month for 10,000 contacts.
CRM-connected marketing
Sugar Market connects marketing programmes to CRM data, with unlimited users included.

Deployment and commercial terms

On-premises Enterprise edition
Sugar Enterprise offers on-premises deployment from about $85 per user per month, with Enterprise+ at a higher price.
Annual contracts
Sugar Sell is billed annually with a 15-user minimum, and SugarCRM has a minimum one-year subscription term.
Nonprofit pricing
Independent reviews describe a separate nonprofit pricing programme.
Sales-led purchasing
There is no self-serve checkout, and Sugar Market, Serve, Enterprise and the sales-i add-on are quoted by sales.

FAQ

SugarCRM questions and answers

What is SugarCRM?

SugarCRM is a CRM platform with Sugar Sell for sales, Sugar Serve for customer service, Sugar Market for marketing automation and an on-premises Sugar Enterprise edition, with predictive and generative AI.

How much does SugarCRM cost?

2026 sources report Sugar Sell at $59 (Standard), $85 (Advanced) and $135 (Premier) per user per month billed annually with a 15-user minimum, Sugar Serve at about $80 per user, and Sugar Market from $1,000 a month. Confirm with SugarCRM, because sources conflict.

Does SugarCRM have a free plan?

No. SugarCRM offers no free plan, and CostBench reports pricing starting at $59 per user per month as of July 2026.

What is the SugarCRM minimum number of users?

Sugar Sell has a 15-user minimum on all tiers and annual billing, according to UsagePricing and Toolradar. SugarCRM also requires a minimum one-year term.

What is Sugar Market?

Sugar Market is SugarCRM's marketing automation product, from the 2019 Salesfusion acquisition. It is priced from $1,000 a month for 10,000 contacts with unlimited users.

Can SugarCRM run on-premises?

Yes. Sugar Enterprise offers on-premises deployment, reported from about $85 per user per month, with Enterprise+ priced higher.

Does SugarCRM have AI features?

Yes. SugarCRM offers predictive and generative AI under Sugar Intelligence, now branded SugarAI, with Smart Guides and sales-i included from Advanced up.

What are alternatives to SugarCRM?

Common alternatives include Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Insightly.

What integrations does SugarCRM support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Email and calendar integration (Advanced), Sugar Market marketing automation, Sugar Serve service workspace, Sales-i add-on, On-premises deployment. API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of SugarCRM?

Sugar Sell has a 15-user minimum and annual billing, which raises the entry cost for small teams. There is no free plan or self-serve checkout, and most editions are quoted by sales. Sugar Market costs an additional $1,000 per month for 10,000 contacts.

Which platforms does SugarCRM run on?

SugarCRM is documented as available on: Cloud, On-premises (Sugar Enterprise).

When was the SugarCRM profile last verified?

7 sources are listed under Sources. Prices and plans can change, so confirm with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to SugarCRM?

The main alternatives to SugarCRM covered on Stack18 are Salesforce Sales Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Insightly. Salesforce Sales Cloud may suit organisations that expect to customise their sales process extensively. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales may suit organisations already standardised on Microsoft 365 and Outlook. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually.
