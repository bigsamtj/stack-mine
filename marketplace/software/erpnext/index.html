Overview

ERPNext overview: who it is for and what it does.

ERPNext is an open-source ERP from Frappe Technologies, licensed under GPL v3 with no per-user licence fees, covering accounting, inventory, manufacturing, HR and more.

A guide from an implementation firm (updated September 2026) lists Frappe Cloud shared hosting from $5 a month (Entry) to $50 (X-Large) and dedicated servers from $40, all with unlimited users, plus a 14-day trial with $10 credit.

Self-hosting is estimated at $50 to $135 a month in infrastructure. Implementation ranges from DIY to $15,000 to $100,000+ with partners; the guide estimates $20,000 to $60,000 all-in for a 25-user company in year one.

Main problem it solves

Gives SMBs a complete ERP without licence fees.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size businesses worldwide.

FAQ

ERPNext questions and answers

What is ERPNext used for?

ERPNext is a free, open-source ERP built on the Frappe framework, covering accounting, inventory, manufacturing, HR, CRM and projects, self-hosted or on Frappe Cloud. Typical uses include accounting, inventory, manufacturing, hR and payroll.

Is ERPNext free?

Yes, it is open source under GPL v3 with no per-user licence fees.

How much does Frappe Cloud cost?

Shared hosting from $5 to $50 a month and dedicated servers from $40, with unlimited users.

How much does ERPNext implementation cost?

From DIY to $15,000 to $100,000+ with partners; about $20,000 to $60,000 for 25 users in year one (estimate).

How does ERPNext compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of ERPNext vs Dynamics 365 Business Central, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to ERPNext?

The main alternatives to ERPNext covered on Stack18 are Dynamics 365 Business Central, SAP Business One and Acumatica. Dynamics 365 Business Central may suit SMBs on Microsoft 365 moving off entry-level accounting. SAP Business One may suit SMBs and subsidiaries wanting an SAP ERP. Acumatica may suit growing mid-market companies that want unlimited users. See the ERPNext alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of ERPNext?

Strengths: No licence fees and unlimited users. Managed hosting from $5 a month. Limitations: Services drive most of the cost. Self-hosting needs DevOps skills.

Who is ERPNext best for?

ERPNext may suit budget-conscious SMBs wanting a full open-source ERP; companies with in-house developers; companies using regional implementation partners. It may be less suited to large enterprises needing tier-one support; teams without technical help.

What integrations does ERPNext support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Frappe framework apps, REST API. API: REST API via the Frappe framework.
