Quick answer

What is Google Workspace?

Google Workspace is Google's subscription suite of business email, video meetings, document editing, shared storage and admin controls, sold per user per month. Business plans are Starter ($7), Standard ($14) and Plus ($22) on a one-year commitment, or $8.40, $16.80 and $26.40 on flexible monthly billing, with custom-priced Enterprise plans. Gemini AI has been included in all plans since January 2025.

Overview

Google Workspace overview: who it is for and what it does.

Google Workspace is used by organisations of every size as the foundation for email, calendar, documents, meetings and file storage in the cloud. Each user gets a custom-domain business email, collaborative Docs editing, Meet video conferencing and pooled cloud storage, managed from an admin console. Business Starter, Standard and Plus are limited to 300 users each, while Enterprise has no user cap and no published price.

Plans differ mainly in storage, meeting size and security. Starter ($7) gives 30 GB of pooled storage per user, Meet with up to 100 participants, 24/7 support and Gemini in Gmail and the Gemini chat app. Standard ($14) raises storage to 2 TB, Meet to 150 participants, adds electronic signatures and Gemini across Docs and Meet. Plus ($22) adds 5 TB of storage, Meet for 500 participants and eDiscovery and retention through Google Vault. Google raised Starter from $6, Standard from $12 and Plus from $18 when it bundled Gemini, which replaced separate add-ons that had cost $20 to $30 per user per month. Flexible monthly billing costs about 20 percent more than a one-year commitment, and nonprofits can receive discounts of 70 percent or more.

For buyers, the key questions are storage per user, meeting size, eDiscovery and the Gemini feature set, since Business Starter gets only limited Gemini features while Standard, Plus and Enterprise get full Gemini across the apps. Vault is a $5 per user per month add-on for Business editions and bundled into Enterprise Plus. Gemini Enterprise, the standalone AI agent and enterprise search platform, is priced separately. Teams already standardised on Microsoft Office or that need deep enterprise governance may prefer Microsoft 365, while teams that want browser-first collaboration usually choose Workspace.

Key features

Google Workspace features explained.

Email, documents and meetings

Business email with custom domain
Every plan includes custom business email, with Gemini in Gmail from Business Starter.
Docs, Sheets and real-time collaboration
Browser-based documents are co-edited in real time, with Gemini in Docs on Standard and above.
Meet video conferencing
Meet supports 100 participants on Starter, 150 on Standard, 500 on Plus and 1,000 or more on Enterprise.
Electronic signatures
Standard and above include electronic signature capabilities.

Storage, search and AI

Pooled cloud storage
Storage is 30 GB per user on Starter, 2 TB on Standard and 5 TB on Plus, with Enterprise 5 TB or more.
Gemini AI included
Starter gets limited Gemini access in Gmail and the chat app, while Standard, Plus and Enterprise get full Gemini across Docs, Meet and other apps.
Gemini Enterprise
A separate standalone AI agent and enterprise search platform is priced separately from Workspace, with a Business edition from $21 per seat per month.
AppSheet AI
Workspace Enterprise bundles AppSheet AI for citizen developers to build apps, according to a licensing review.

Administration, security and compliance

Admin console and support
All plans include 24/7 phone and email support, with enhanced support available on Standard and above.
eDiscovery and retention
Business Plus adds eDiscovery and retention, using Google Vault, which is a $5 per user add-on on lower Business editions.
Security controls
Plus and Enterprise add advanced security and management controls, according to independent plan comparisons.
Nonprofit and education discounts
Nonprofits can receive discounts such as $3.50 per user for Business Standard and 70 percent or more off Enterprise.

FAQ

Google Workspace questions and answers

What is Google Workspace?

Google Workspace is Google's subscription suite for business email, documents, video meetings, shared storage and administration, sold per user per month. It includes the Gemini AI assistant in all Business plans.

How much does Google Workspace cost?

Business Starter is $7, Standard $14 and Plus $22 per user per month on a one-year commitment. Flexible monthly billing is $8.40, $16.80 and $26.40. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Is Gemini included in Google Workspace?

Yes. Gemini has been included in all Business plans since January 2025. Starter has limited Gemini in Gmail and the chat app, while Standard, Plus and Enterprise include Gemini across Docs, Meet and other apps.

Does Google Workspace have a free plan?

There is no free business plan. Google Workspace is sold per user, and nonprofits can receive discounts such as $3.50 per user for Business Standard.

What is the difference between Business Starter, Standard and Plus?

Starter has 30 GB storage and Meet for 100 people; Standard has 2 TB, Meet for 150 and e-signatures; Plus has 5 TB, Meet for 500 and eDiscovery and retention. All three allow up to 300 users.

How many users can Google Workspace Business support?

Business Starter, Standard and Plus each support up to 300 users. Larger organisations move to Enterprise, which has no user limit and custom pricing.

Is Google Vault included in Google Workspace?

Vault is included with Business Plus and Enterprise Plus, and available as a $5 per user per month add-on for lower Business and Education editions.

What are alternatives to Google Workspace?

Common alternatives include Microsoft Teams with Microsoft 365, Slack, Notion, Zoom Workplace and Lark.

What integrations does Google Workspace support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Gmail, Docs, Meet and Drive, Gemini AI across apps, Google Vault, AppSheet (Enterprise). API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Google Workspace?

Business plans are capped at 300 users, so larger organisations must negotiate Enterprise. Business Starter has only limited Gemini features, and full Gemini starts on Standard. Flexible monthly billing costs about 20 percent more than a one-year commitment.

Which platforms does Google Workspace run on?

Google Workspace is documented as available on: Web, Mobile and desktop apps (not itemised in sources).
