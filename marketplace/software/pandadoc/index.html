Overview

PandaDoc overview: who it is for and what it does.

FAQ

PandaDoc questions and answers

What is PandaDoc used for?

PandaDoc is a document automation platform for creating, sending and e-signing proposals, quotes and contracts, with a free e-signature plan and Starter, Business and Enterprise tiers. Typical uses include proposals and quotes, contracts, e-signatures, approval workflows.

How much does PandaDoc cost?

There is a free plan; Starter is $19 a month and Business $49 per seat a month.

Is PandaDoc free?

Yes, with unlimited e-signatures and 60 documents a year.

Does PandaDoc integrate with CRMs?

CRM integrations are included on the Business plan.

How does PandaDoc compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of DocuSign vs PandaDoc and PandaDoc vs SignNow, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to PandaDoc?

The main alternatives to PandaDoc covered on Stack18 are DocuSign, SignNow, SignWell and Zoho Sign. DocuSign may suit organisations that want the most widely recognised e-signature brand and integrations. SignNow may suit cost-conscious teams wanting affordable e-signatures. SignWell may suit freelancers and small businesses wanting unlimited signing cheaply. See the PandaDoc alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of PandaDoc?

Strengths: Free plan with unlimited e-signatures. Proposal and quote building. Limitations: Free plan limited to 60 documents a year. CRM and branding require Business.

Who is PandaDoc best for?

PandaDoc may suit sales teams creating proposals, quotes and contracts; agencies sending proposals; teams needing CRM-linked documents. It may be less suited to pure high-volume signature sending; developers wanting envelope APIs.

What integrations does PandaDoc support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
