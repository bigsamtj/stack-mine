Quick answer

What is HappyFox?

HappyFox is a help desk and ticketing platform for support teams, with automation, SLA management and a knowledge base. It sells two ways: agent-based plans of Basic ($24), Team ($49), Pro ($99) and custom Enterprise Pro per agent per month, or unlimited-agent plans from $1,999 a month based on ticket volume. HappyFox AI is a separate per-agent add-on at $14, $29 or $39 per month.

Overview

HappyFox overview: who it is for and what it does.

HappyFox is an established help desk used by small and mid-size support teams that want configurable workflows without Zendesk-level complexity. Hiver's review describes core capabilities as email ticketing, automation, SLA management and a knowledge base, and G2-sourced commentary notes that some users find the customer-facing experience less smooth than a Google Workspace help desk. Capterra now highlights unlimited-agent pricing as a new option, and some third-party sites count HappyFox's AI-powered ticket routing and automation among strengths.

Pricing is more complex than most help desks. A September 2026 analysis by Macha, which read HappyFox's own pricing pages in a browser on 22 September 2026, lists agent-based annual pricing of Basic at $24 (capped at 5 agents), Team at $49, Pro at $99 ($119 on monthly billing) and custom Enterprise Pro. Unlimited-agent plans, which charge by ticket volume instead of seats, are Growth at $1,999 a month for 20,000 tickets a year, Scale at $3,999 and Scale Plus at $5,999, falling to $1,599, $3,199 and $4,799 on two-year terms. HappyFox AI is billed separately at $14 (Standard), $29 (Premium) and $39 (Enterprise) per agent per month with different interaction allowances for Agent Copilot, AI Knowledge and AI Resolve, which makes the real entry price with AI about $38 per agent.

Older and regional prices circulate widely, so buyers should be careful. The previous Mighty, Fantastic, Enterprise and Enterprise Plus ladder lists $26, $39, $52 and $64 for US customers and unlimited plans from $499 or $1,199 a month, and other sources quote Basic at $21 or $29. CostBench also reports workflow add-ons from $199 to $1,999 a month and strict ticket caps with overage fees on unlimited plans. The crossover point at which unlimited-agent pricing beats per-agent pricing depends on team size and ticket volume, and annual billing is required for unlimited-agent plans.

Gives support teams ticketing, SLAs, automation and a knowledge base with a choice of per-agent pricing or flat unlimited-agent pricing so costs stay predictable as teams grow.

FAQ

HappyFox questions and answers

What is HappyFox?

HappyFox is a help desk and ticketing platform with email ticketing, SLA management, automation and a knowledge base, available with per-agent or unlimited-agent pricing.

How much does HappyFox cost?

Per-agent plans are Basic at $24, Team at $49 and Pro at $99 per agent per month on annual billing, with Enterprise Pro custom. Unlimited-agent plans start at $1,999 a month, and HappyFox AI costs an extra $14 to $39 per agent.

What are HappyFox unlimited-agent plans?

Unlimited-agent plans charge a flat monthly fee based on annual ticket volume instead of seats: Growth at $1,999, Scale at $3,999 and Scale Plus at $5,999 a month, with lower prices on two-year terms.

Does HappyFox have a free plan?

No free plan was found. Basic is the cheapest plan at $24 per agent per month and is capped at 5 agents.

How much does HappyFox AI cost?

HappyFox AI is billed separately at $14 (Standard), $29 (Premium) and $39 (Enterprise) per agent per month, with different allowances for Agent Copilot, AI Knowledge and AI Resolve.

When is the unlimited-agent plan cheaper than per-agent pricing?

It depends on team size and ticket volume. Macha analyses the crossover point, and it generally favours teams with many agents and moderate ticket counts.

Why do HappyFox prices differ between websites?

HappyFox has changed its ladder over time and prices vary by region and billing term. Older plan names such as Mighty and Fantastic and lower prices appear in older articles.

What are alternatives to HappyFox?

Common alternatives include Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, Help Scout and Front.

What integrations does HappyFox support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Email ticketing, Knowledge base, Workflow automation, HappyFox AI add-on. API details: API and integration details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of HappyFox?

The Basic plan is capped at 5 agents with email-only support during Pacific business hours. HappyFox AI costs $14 to $39 per agent per month on top of the help desk, so the real entry price with AI is about $38. Unlimited-agent plans have strict ticket volume caps, overage fees and annual billing, and workflow add-ons can cost $199 to $1,999 a month.

Which platforms does HappyFox run on?
