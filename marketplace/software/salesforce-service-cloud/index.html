What is Salesforce Service Cloud?

Salesforce Service Cloud is Salesforce's customer service and contact center platform, offering case management, omnichannel routing, knowledge, service console and AI through Einstein and Agentforce. Reported 2026 list prices per user per month, billed annually, are Starter Suite $25, Pro Suite $100, Enterprise $175 (about $165 before an August 2025 increase), Unlimited $330 to $350 and Agentforce 1 Service $550. Agentforce for Service is a $125 per user add-on.

Overview

Salesforce Service Cloud overview: who it is for and what it does.

Service Cloud is the customer service half of Salesforce's CRM, built on the same data model as Sales Cloud so agents see accounts, contacts and opportunity history on every case. It is chosen by mid-size and large enterprises that need deep customisation, complex routing, field service and contact centre integration, and it typically sits above lighter tools such as Zendesk or Freshdesk in cost and implementation effort. Starter Suite is described as an entry-level CRM bundle for small teams, while Enterprise at about $175 per user is the realistic starting point for a mid-market service team, according to several analysts.

Editions climb steeply. Starter Suite is $25 per user per month, Pro Suite $100 (limited to five automated flows per organisation), Enterprise $165 before August 2025 and about $175 after Salesforce's announced 6 percent increase, Unlimited $330 after the increase (some sources list $350) and Agentforce 1 Service $550, which bundles unmetered Agentforce, 1 million Flex Credits a year and Data Cloud access. Incident management is available from Enterprise. AI is priced separately: Einstein add-ons are listed at about $50 per user per month, Digital Engagement at $75, and Agentforce for Service at $125 per user on top of Enterprise, with Flex Credits at roughly $0.10 per standard action. Unlimited includes 25 Einstein bot conversations per user per month.

The sticker price is only the start. One analysis shows a Pro Suite user with Digital Engagement and basic Einstein reaching about $225 per user per month, and enterprise contracts commonly carry a 7 percent annual uplift, three to five year terms and implementation partner costs. Negotiated Enterprise pricing for 500 users is reported in a range of about $105 to $140 per user per month versus the $175 list. Several of the sources cited are vendors of competing AI support tools, so confirm figures with Salesforce. For teams under about 50 agents that want fast setup, lighter help desks usually deliver better value.

Main problem it solves

Gives large service organisations a configurable case management and contact centre platform on the same data model as their sales CRM, with AI agents that can resolve cases.

Key features

Salesforce Service Cloud features explained.

Service operations

Case management and service console
Service Cloud provides case management and an agent console on the Salesforce data model, with capability increasing by edition.
Automation limits by edition
Pro Suite limits automated flows to five per organisation, which many mid-sized companies outgrow within a year.
Incident management
Incident management ships in Enterprise and is not available in Starter or Pro Suites.
Field Service add-on
Field Service is an add-on that requires at least one Service Cloud licence.

AI and engagement

Einstein AI
Einstein provides case classification, summarisation and reply suggestions, with a classic add-on listed around $50 per user per month.
Agentforce for Service
Agentforce for Service is an autonomous AI agent layer priced at $125 per user per month on top of Enterprise or higher.
Agentforce 1 Service
Agentforce 1 Service at $550 per user bundles unmetered Agentforce usage, 1 million Flex Credits a year and Data Cloud access.
Digital Engagement and Voice
Digital Engagement is listed at $75 per user per month and Service Cloud Voice is priced separately.

Commercial terms

Annual contracts
Salesforce uses per-user pricing with annual billing, typical enterprise agreements run three to five years and carry a default 7 percent annual uplift.
August 2025 price increase
Salesforce raised Enterprise and Unlimited list prices by about 6 percent in August 2025, leaving Starter and Pro unchanged.
Flex Credits
AI actions consume Flex Credits at about $0.10 per standard action beyond bundled entitlements.
Negotiated discounts
Negotiated Enterprise pricing for 500 users runs about $105 to $140 per user per month in one analysis.

FAQ

Salesforce Service Cloud questions and answers

What is Salesforce Service Cloud?

Salesforce Service Cloud is Salesforce's customer service and contact centre platform, offering case management, omnichannel routing, knowledge, a service console and AI through Einstein and Agentforce.

How much does Salesforce Service Cloud cost?

Reported 2026 list prices are $25 (Starter Suite), $100 (Pro Suite), about $175 (Enterprise), $330 to $350 (Unlimited) and $550 (Agentforce 1 Service) per user per month, billed annually. AI add-ons cost extra.

How much does Agentforce for Service cost?

Agentforce for Service is priced at $125 per user per month on top of Enterprise or higher, with Flex Credits at about $0.10 per standard action. Agentforce 1 Service at $550 per user bundles unmetered usage.

Did Salesforce raise Service Cloud prices?

Yes. Salesforce announced a roughly 6 percent increase on Enterprise and Unlimited editions in August 2025, moving Enterprise from about $165 to $175 per user, while Starter and Pro were unaffected.

Does Salesforce Service Cloud have a free trial?

Directory listings report a 30-day free trial, and Salesforce offers a separate Free Suite for up to 2 users, but Service Cloud itself has no permanent free plan.

What is the difference between Service Cloud Pro Suite and Enterprise?

Enterprise adds incident management, advanced automation and more customisation, while Pro Suite is limited to five automated flows per organisation.

What are hidden costs of Service Cloud?

Hidden costs include AI add-ons, Digital Engagement and Voice, Flex Credits, implementation partners and a default 7 percent annual uplift on enterprise contracts.

What are alternatives to Salesforce Service Cloud?

Common alternatives include Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot Service Hub, Zoho Desk and Kustomer.

What integrations does Salesforce Service Cloud support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Salesforce Sales Cloud data model, Field Service add-on, Service Cloud Voice, Einstein and Agentforce, Data Cloud (Agentforce 1). API details: Salesforce offers extensive APIs and a large AppExchange ecosystem; details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Salesforce Service Cloud?

Per-user prices climb steeply, and a realistic Pro Suite configuration with add-ons reaches about $225 per user. Pro Suite limits automated flows to five per organisation. Agentforce for Service adds $125 per user and Flex Credits add per-action cost.

Which platforms does Salesforce Service Cloud run on?

Salesforce Service Cloud is documented as available on: Web, Cloud.

What are the main alternatives to Salesforce Service Cloud?

The main alternatives to Salesforce Service Cloud covered on Stack18 are Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot Service Hub, Zoho Desk and Kustomer. Zendesk may suit support teams that need ticketing plus omnichannel messaging. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price. HubSpot Service Hub may suit support teams already using HubSpot CRM. See the Salesforce Service Cloud alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.
