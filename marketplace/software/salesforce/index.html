Overview

Salesforce Sales Cloud overview: who it is for and what it does.

Sales Cloud is Salesforce's CRM for managing leads, accounts, contacts, opportunities and campaigns. Salesforce sells it as a set of editions, listed on its pricing page as Starter Suite, Pro Suite, Enterprise and Unlimited, with a further top edition that adds generative AI.

The edition table on Salesforce's own pricing page shows how allowances change as you move up: items such as campaigns per opportunity, flows per organisation, record types per object, sandboxes and the number of custom applications differ between editions. This makes the edition choice a customisation and automation decision as well as a price decision.

Salesforce directs buyers to contact a sales representative for detailed pricing. Third-party licensing guides describe a per-user, per-month model, usually billed annually, with extra add-ons for areas such as AI agents, so total cost depends on the edition and add-ons chosen.

Scope: This profile covers Sales Cloud. Salesforce sells many other products, including Service Cloud and Marketing Cloud, which are priced separately.

Main problem it solves

Gives sales organisations a configurable system of record for leads, accounts and opportunities, with automation, reporting and a customisation platform that can be extended as processes become more complex.

Typical customer segment

Small teams through large enterprises, with edition choice driving how much customisation is available.

Target users

  • Sales teams on Starter Suite or Pro Suite
  • Mid-size and large organisations that need automation and customisation on Enterprise or Unlimited
  • Organisations adopting Salesforce's generative AI and agent features

Primary use cases

  • Lead and opportunity management
  • Campaign tracking against opportunities
  • Workflow automation with flows
  • Custom applications and record types
  • Sandbox-based development and testing

What makes it distinct

  • A staged edition ladder from Starter Suite to Unlimited, with a top edition adding generative AI.
  • Documented per-edition allowances for flows, record types, custom applications and sandboxes, indicating a customisation-oriented platform.
  • A dedicated agent offering, Agentforce, which third-party licensing guides describe as an add-on above Enterprise and Unlimited.
Key features

Salesforce Sales Cloud features explained.

Sales management

Opportunity and campaign tracking

Editions for different team sizes
Starter Suite, Pro Suite, Enterprise and Unlimited editions let teams choose a level of capability, with a higher edition adding generative AI.

Automation and customisation

Flows
The edition table lists flows per organisation as an allowance that varies by edition, with lower editions showing a fixed limit.
Record types and custom applications
Record types per object and the number of custom applications are listed as edition-dependent, including unlimited custom applications on higher editions.
Sandboxes
Developer Pro and partial sandboxes are listed per edition for building and testing changes.

AI

Generative AI in the top edition
Salesforce's pricing page describes its highest Sales Cloud edition as everything in Unlimited plus generative AI.
Agentforce add-ons
Third-party licensing guides describe Agentforce as an add-on above Enterprise and Unlimited, with additional usage-based pricing. Check Salesforce for current terms.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Salesforce Sales Cloud pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • Salesforce's edition table documents customisation allowances such as record types, custom applications and sandboxes, which suits organisations that expect to model complex processes. [1]
  • A wide edition range lets small teams start on Starter Suite while larger organisations choose Enterprise or Unlimited. [1]
  • Generative AI is offered in the top edition, with agent functionality sold as an add-on, giving an upgrade path for AI adoption. [1]

Limitations

  • Salesforce's pricing page points buyers to contact sales for detailed pricing, so costs are harder to estimate than on vendors that publish full price lists. [1]
  • Allowances for flows, record types and custom applications vary by edition, so lower editions can constrain automation and customisation. [1]
  • Add-ons for AI and other capabilities are priced on top of the edition, which can raise total cost. [2]

Best for

  • Organisations that expect to customise their sales process extensively
  • Teams that want to scale from a small edition to an enterprise edition on one vendor
  • Companies planning to adopt Salesforce's generative AI and agent features

May be less suited to

  • Teams that want fully published, self-serve pricing before talking to a vendor
  • Very small teams that only need a free pipeline tool

FAQ

Salesforce Sales Cloud questions and answers

What is Salesforce Sales Cloud used for?

Sales Cloud is used to manage leads, accounts, contacts, opportunities and campaigns, and to automate sales processes. It is one of several Salesforce clouds, alongside products such as Service Cloud and Marketing Cloud.

What editions does Sales Cloud have?

Salesforce lists Starter Suite, Pro Suite, Enterprise and Unlimited editions, and describes a top edition that adds generative AI. Third-party guides name that top edition Agentforce 1; earlier sources call it Einstein 1.

Does Salesforce Sales Cloud have a free plan?

No permanent free plan was identified in the sources reviewed. Check Salesforce's site for current trial options.

How much does Sales Cloud cost?

Salesforce prices per user per month by edition and directs buyers to a sales representative for detailed pricing. Third-party figures conflict, so no price is recorded here. Confirm with Salesforce.

What are the main alternatives to Salesforce Sales Cloud?

The main alternatives to Salesforce Sales Cloud covered on Stack18 are HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive and Freshsales. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM. See the Salesforce Sales Cloud alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Salesforce Sales Cloud support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Salesforce AppExchange app marketplace, Salesforce Platform customisation. API details: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Salesforce Sales Cloud?

Salesforce's pricing page points buyers to contact sales for detailed pricing, so costs are harder to estimate than on vendors that publish full price lists. Allowances for flows, record types and custom applications vary by edition, so lower editions can constrain automation and customisation. Add-ons for AI and other capabilities are priced on top of the edition, which can raise total cost.

Which platforms does Salesforce Sales Cloud run on?

Salesforce Sales Cloud is documented as available on: Web.
