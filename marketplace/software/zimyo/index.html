Overview

Zimyo overview: who it is for and what it does.

Zimyo, based in Gurugram, targets Indian startups and growing companies that want HR, payroll and performance in one affordable tool. It also sells benefits and earned-wage features through its wider platform.

A software directory lists HRMS Basic at INR 50, Standard at INR 100 and Enterprise at INR 200 per employee a month billed yearly (INR 60, 120 and 250 billed monthly), with a minimum of 20 employees. Companies under 20 employees can request Basic at INR 2,000 a month, and a separate blue-collar plan is quoted.

A free trial is available. Confirm which modules (payroll, performance, ATS) are included in each plan, because directory listings describe plan contents only partly.

Main problem it solves

Gives Indian SMBs affordable HR, attendance and payroll with statutory compliance in one system.

Typical customer segment

Startups and small to mid-size Indian companies, from under 20 to several hundred employees.

Target users

  • Founders and HR generalists in startups
  • Payroll administrators
  • Managers running reviews and approvals

Primary use cases

  • Payroll with Indian statutory compliance
  • Attendance and leave
  • Performance reviews and 360 feedback
  • Employee self-service
  • Training and compensation planning

What makes it distinct

  • Low per-employee pricing with a small-company flat option.
  • Blue-collar plan for staff without app logins.
  • HR, payroll and performance in one SMB product.

FAQ

Zimyo questions and answers

What is Zimyo used for?

Zimyo is used for payroll with Indian statutory compliance, attendance and leave, performance reviews and 360 feedback and employee self-service.

Does Zimyo have a free plan?

No. Zimyo does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Yes.

How much does Zimyo cost?

INR 50 per employee a month (Basic, yearly) with a 20-employee minimum. Prices come from a third-party directory and are per employee per month.

What is the difference between Zimyo HRMS Basic and HRMS Standard?

HRMS Basic is listed at INR 50 per employee a month billed yearly; INR 60 monthly (20-employee minimum; startups under 20 can request INR 2,000 a month). HRMS Standard is listed at INR 100 billed yearly; INR 120 monthly (More modules than Basic). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Zimyo?

The main alternatives to Zimyo covered on Stack18 are Keka, greytHR, Zoho People and Darwinbox. Keka may suit Indian companies that want modern HR with compliant payroll. greytHR may suit Indian SMEs that want affordable payroll and leave management. Zoho People may suit small businesses that want low-cost HR, attendance and performance tools. See the Zimyo alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Zimyo support?

Documented integrations include Biometric devices (vendor marketing), Accounting exports and Single sign-on (higher plans, reported). API: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Zimyo?

A 20-employee minimum on standard plans. Plan contents are only partly described in public listings. No free plan.

Which platforms does Zimyo run on?

Zimyo is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.

Is Zimyo available in India?

Yes. Zimyo is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
