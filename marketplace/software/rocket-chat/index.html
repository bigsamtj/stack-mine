Quick answer

What is Rocket.Chat?

Rocket.Chat is an open-source team messaging and secure communications platform, marketed as Secure CommsOS, that organisations deploy on-premises, in a private or dedicated cloud, or fully air-gapped. A free self-managed Starter edition is offered for technical evaluation, small businesses and non-profits, while commercial, government and defense plans are sold by quote and add compliance, federation, voice, video and sovereign AI options.

  • Best forGovernment, defense and regulated organisations that need sovereign or air-gapped chat
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingFree self-managed Starter edition; paid commercial plans are quote-based. Earlier pages and third-party sites reported Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationCommercial plans are quote-based, so buyers cannot see per-user prices on the current plans page.

Overview

Rocket.Chat overview: who it is for and what it does.

Rocket.Chat targets organisations where data sovereignty and compliance matter as much as chat: public sector bodies, defense and intelligence agencies, critical infrastructure, healthcare, financial services and commercial enterprises. Its website lists deployments at Audi, the City of Cologne, the US Army and Air Force, BAE Systems and General Dynamics Mission Systems. Teams get channels, direct and group messaging, threaded conversations, search, file sharing, message translation and mobile apps for iOS and Android, with an open API, embeddable chat and an Apps Engine for custom bots and workflows.

The platform's differentiators are deployment control and interoperability. Rocket.Chat can run on your own infrastructure, in a Rocket.Chat-managed dedicated cloud, or in air-gapped and classified environments, with high availability and disaster recovery for cloud workspaces. It supports native Matrix federation, XMPP federation and a Microsoft Teams bridge so that separate organisations can share channels without sharing infrastructure. Security features include optional end-to-end encryption, SSO via SAML, OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect, LDAP and Active Directory, MFA, role-based access control, audit trails and retention policies, and Rocket.Chat says it holds ISO 27001 certification and a SOC 2 Type 2 report.

Pricing has moved toward sales-led packaging. The current plans page offers Commercial, Government and Defense plans through sales, with voice calls, native video meetings, mission-critical support, professional services and sovereign AI listed as add-ons, while a free self-managed Starter edition is offered for evaluation, small businesses and non-profits. Earlier Rocket.Chat pricing and third-party sites described a Starter plan limited to about 25 to 50 users and a Pro plan at $8 per user per month billed annually, so buyers should confirm current terms. Self-managed deployments also bring infrastructure and operations work, and reviewers note setup complexity and the need to register self-hosted instances with Rocket.Chat's push gateway for mobile notifications unless a custom gateway is configured.

Main problem it solves

Gives organisations a team chat and secure communications platform they can host themselves or in a controlled cloud, with federation and compliance features that mainstream SaaS chat tools do not offer.

Typical customer segment

Government, defense, public sector, regulated enterprises, critical infrastructure and technical teams that need self-hosted or air-gapped collaboration.

Primary use cases

Key features

Rocket.Chat features explained.

Messaging and collaboration

Channels, threads and direct messages
Direct and group messaging, threaded conversations, message editing, formatting, delivery and read receipts and searchable chat history keep conversations organised.
Voice, video and screen sharing
Messaging, voice, video meetings and screen sharing are offered, with native voice and video as add-ons, Jitsi, Google Meet and Zoom integrations and Pexip for secure self-hosted video.
Automatic translation and localisation
Messages can be translated automatically across languages and the interface is localised into 64 languages.
Mobile and desktop apps
Mobile and desktop apps are available, including secure iOS and Android apps with biometrics, data-at-rest encryption and ID-only push notifications.

Security, identity and compliance

End-to-end encryption and DLP
Optional end-to-end encryption protects messages and files, alongside data loss prevention, message reporting and custom retention policies.
Identity and access control
SSO with SAML, OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect, LDAP and Active Directory, MFA, role-based access control, custom roles and group mapping are supported.
Audit and governance
Audit trails for messages, files and sessions, authentication logs, administrative event logging and compliance exports support investigations and regulators.
Regulatory support
Rocket.Chat lists HIPAA-supportive operation with a BAA, FINRA and MiFID II readiness, NIS2 and DORA support, GDPR and CCPA controls, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2.

Deployment, federation and extensibility

Flexible deployment
Deploy on-premises, in private cloud, in a dedicated Rocket.Chat-managed cloud, in isolated networks or fully air-gapped, with high availability and microservices scaling on Enterprise.
Federation and bridges
Native Matrix federation, XMPP federation, invitation-based federated channels and a Microsoft Teams bridge connect separate organisations.
Apps Engine, API and embedding
A TypeScript Apps Engine, open API, SDKs and iframe embedding allow custom apps, bots, workflows and white-labelled deployments.
Sovereign AI (add-on)
An add-on offers bring-your-own LLM, thread and channel summarisation, hybrid intelligent search and Q&A grounded in your own data, with MCP support listed as planned for 2026.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Rocket.Chat pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • Flexible deployment covers on-premises, private cloud, dedicated cloud and air-gapped environments. [1]
  • Native Matrix and XMPP federation and a Teams bridge allow collaboration between separate organisations. [1]
  • Compliance coverage includes ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2, HIPAA support with a BAA, FINRA, MiFID II, NIS2 and DORA. [1]
  • The open-source core offers an open API, Apps Engine and white-labelling for customised deployments. [1]
  • A free self-managed Starter edition lets teams evaluate before buying, with a reduced-price non-profit programme. [1]

Limitations

  • Commercial plans are quote-based, so buyers cannot see per-user prices on the current plans page. [1]
  • The Starter plan is self-managed only and capped in scale, with community support, and has changed limits over time. [2]
  • Self-hosting adds infrastructure and administration cost, and reviewers describe setup complexity. [5]
  • Mobile push notifications on self-hosted instances require registering with the vendor gateway unless a custom gateway is used. [5]
  • Voice, native video, sovereign AI and mission-critical support are paid add-ons, and some features are only planned. [1]

Best for

  • Government, defense and regulated organisations that need sovereign or air-gapped chat
  • Technical teams that want a self-hosted, open-source Slack alternative
  • Organisations that need federation with partners without sharing infrastructure

May be less suited to

FAQ

Rocket.Chat questions and answers

What is Rocket.Chat?

Rocket.Chat is an open-source team messaging and secure communications platform that can be self-hosted, run in a dedicated cloud or deployed in air-gapped environments, with federation, voice, video and compliance features.

Is Rocket.Chat free?

Rocket.Chat offers a free self-managed Starter edition aimed at technical evaluation, small businesses and non-profits. Earlier pages described user and contact limits, and paid commercial plans are sold by quote.

How much does Rocket.Chat cost?

Rocket.Chat's current plans page does not publish per-user prices for its commercial, government and defense plans, which are quoted by sales. Earlier pages and third-party listings reported Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, but that figure should be confirmed with Rocket.Chat.

Can Rocket.Chat run air-gapped?

Yes. Rocket.Chat supports on-premises, sovereign cloud and fully air-gapped deployments, including classified networks, and lists DoD ATO up to Impact Level 6 for defense use.

Does Rocket.Chat support federation?

Yes. Rocket.Chat supports native Matrix federation, XMPP federation and a Microsoft Teams bridge, so teams on different servers can share channels without shared infrastructure.

Is Rocket.Chat compliant with HIPAA and GDPR?

Rocket.Chat lists HIPAA-supportive operation with a BAA, GDPR and CCPA configuration, FINRA and MiFID II readiness, NIS2 and DORA support, ISO 27001 certification and a SOC 2 Type 2 audit.

Does Rocket.Chat have AI features?

Rocket.Chat offers a sovereign AI add-on with bring-your-own LLM, summarisation, hybrid search and Q&A grounded in your data, running within your own infrastructure. MCP support is listed as planned for 2026.

What are alternatives to Rocket.Chat?

Common alternatives include Mattermost, Zulip, Slack, Microsoft Teams and Zoho Cliq. Mattermost and Zulip are the closest self-hostable options.

What integrations does Rocket.Chat support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Matrix and XMPP federation, Microsoft Teams bridge, Jitsi, Google Meet and Zoom, Pexip secure video, LDAP, Active Directory and SAML SSO, Apps Marketplace, Open API and embeddable chat. API details: Rocket.Chat offers an open API, SDKs, iframe embedding and a TypeScript Apps Engine for custom apps, bots and workflows.

What are the limitations of Rocket.Chat?

Commercial plans are quote-based, so buyers cannot see per-user prices on the current plans page. The Starter plan is self-managed only and capped in scale, with community support, and has changed limits over time. Self-hosting adds infrastructure and administration cost, and reviewers describe setup complexity.

Which platforms does Rocket.Chat run on?

Rocket.Chat is documented as available on: Web, iOS app, Android app, Desktop apps, Self-hosted, Dedicated cloud, Air-gapped.

When was the Rocket.Chat profile last verified?

What are the main alternatives to Rocket.Chat?

The main alternatives to Rocket.Chat covered on Stack18 are Mattermost, Zulip, Slack, Microsoft Teams and Zoho Cliq. Mattermost may suit Regulated or security-conscious organisations that need to control where data lives. Zulip may suit distributed teams that want asynchronous chat organised by topic. Slack may suit teams that want real-time channel-based communication. See the Rocket.Chat alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.
