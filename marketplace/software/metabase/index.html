Overview

Metabase overview: who it is for and what it does.

Metabase is a popular open-source BI tool that lets non-technical users ask questions of a database and build dashboards, with SQL for analysts.

Metabase's pricing reference lists Open Source free and self-hosted; Starter at $100 a month with 5 users plus $6 per extra user; Pro at $575 a month with 10 users plus $12 per extra; and Enterprise from $20,000 a year. Annual billing saves about 10%.

Pro and Enterprise add SAML, JWT and SCIM, row- and column-level permissions, modular and full-app embedding and white-labelling. Starter and Pro have 14-day trials.

Main problem it solves

Gives teams self-service BI without an expensive licence.

Typical customer segment

Startups through enterprises embedding analytics.

FAQ

Metabase questions and answers

What is Metabase used for?

Metabase is an open-source business intelligence tool for querying data and building dashboards, free to self-host, with paid Starter, Pro and Enterprise plans. Typical uses include dashboards, sQL and no-code queries, embedded analytics, alerts.

Is Metabase free?

Yes, the open-source edition is free to self-host with unlimited users.

How much does Metabase Pro cost?

$575 a month including 10 users, plus $12 per extra user.

Does Metabase support SSO?

Google Auth and LDAP on all plans; SAML, JWT, OIDC and SCIM on Pro and Enterprise.

How does Metabase compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Metabase vs Looker Studio, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Metabase?

The main alternatives to Metabase covered on Stack18 are Looker Studio, Microsoft Power BI, Zoho Analytics and Sisense. Looker Studio may suit marketers and small teams reporting on Google data. Microsoft Power BI may suit organisations on Microsoft 365 that want low-cost per-user BI. Zoho Analytics may suit small and mid-size businesses that want affordable BI. See the Metabase alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Metabase?

Strengths: The open-source edition is free with unlimited users. Starter includes 5 users for $100 a month. Limitations: SAML SSO and row-level permissions need Pro at $575 a month. Embedded viewers count as billed users on Pro.

Who is Metabase best for?

Metabase may suit startups that want easy, open-source BI; SaaS teams embedding dashboards; analysts comfortable with SQL. It may be less suited to enterprises needing pixel-perfect reporting; teams without anyone to self-host.

What integrations does Metabase support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Databases and warehouses, Embedding SDK (Pro). API: API keys on all plans.
