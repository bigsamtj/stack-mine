Quick answer

What is Todoist?

Todoist, made by Doist, is a task management and to-do list app that organises work in projects, labels, priorities and filters. It has a free Beginner plan, Pro at $7 per month or $60 per year, and Business at $10 per user per month or $8 per user per month billed yearly. Paid plans add reminders, full activity history, backups and unlimited AI features, with a 7-day Pro trial and 14-day Business trial.

Overview

Todoist overview: who it is for and what it does.

Todoist is one of the most widely used task managers, designed to help people capture, organise and complete tasks across personal and team projects. Its building blocks are projects, sub-projects, sections, labels, priorities, due dates and custom filter views, with shared projects and comments for collaboration. Software Advice reviewers praise its ease of use (4.62) and note the subscription cost and some performance issues as drawbacks. Doist, the company behind it, is described as independent and bootstrapped, with no venture funding.

Todoist has three plans. Beginner is free with up to 5 personal projects, 5 team projects, 3 filter views, 7 days of activity history and 5 MB file uploads. Pro ($7 monthly or $60 yearly, equal to $5 per month) raises limits to 300 personal projects and 150 filters, adds full activity history, daily automatic backups, custom, location-based and recurring reminders, project insights, 25 MB uploads and unlimited AI features. Business ($10 per user monthly or $8 per user billed yearly) adds a shared team workspace with team projects, folders, restricted projects, team filters, team roles, up to 1,000 members and 100 MB uploads.

AI is built into the paid plans: Ramble turns voice, text, images or documents into structured tasks, with 10 sessions a month on the free plan and unlimited use on Pro and Business, while Todoist Assist handles busywork automation and Email Assist turns forwarded emails into tasks. Todoist raised prices on 10 December 2025, with Pro monthly rising about 40 percent and yearly about 25 percent, so older guides quoting lower prices are out of date. Because Business is billed per seat with no flat-rate option, a 10-person team on annual billing pays about $960 a year, and teams that need Gantt charts, dependencies, workload views or portfolio reporting will want a fuller project management suite.

Scope: Todoist is a task manager with shared projects and a team workspace rather than a full project portfolio suite, so it suits personal productivity and lightweight team task tracking.

Main problem it solves

Helps individuals and small teams capture every task in one place, prioritise it and get reminders, with AI to turn voice and email into organised tasks.

Key features

Todoist features explained.

Tasks and organisation

Projects, sections and labels
Tasks are organised in projects with sub-projects, sections, labels and priorities, with 5 projects on Beginner and 300 on Pro and Business.
Custom filter views
Filter views save custom task queries: 3 on Beginner and up to 150 on Pro and Business per member.
Reminders
Automatic reminders for tasks with date and time are on every plan, while custom, time-based, location-based and recurring reminders need Pro or Business.
Layouts and task duration
A list layout is on every plan, and calendar layout and task duration are Pro features according to reviewers.

Collaboration and teams

Shared projects and comments
Shared projects, comments and file uploads are on all plans, with 5 MB, 25 MB and 100 MB upload limits on Beginner, Pro and Business.
Team workspace
Business adds a shared team workspace with team projects, folders, restricted projects, team filters and a browse-and-join directory.
Team roles and scale
Business provides admin, member and guest roles and supports up to 1,000 team members, with up to 500 team projects.
Project insights
Project Insights are included on Pro and Business and not on Beginner.

AI, history and safety

Ramble and Task Capture Assist
Ramble captures tasks from voice, text, images and documents, with up to 10 sessions a month on Beginner and unlimited use on paid plans, subject to fair use.
Todoist Assist and Email Assist
Todoist Assist automates busywork and Email Assist converts forwarded emails into tasks, both on Pro and Business.
Activity history and backups
Beginner keeps 7 days of activity history, while Pro and Business keep full history with daily automatic backups.
Centralised billing
Business includes centralised billing and can run unlimited paid teams, each billed separately.

FAQ

Todoist questions and answers

What is Todoist?

Todoist is a task management and to-do list app from Doist. It organises tasks in projects with labels, priorities, filters and reminders, and offers shared projects, a team workspace and AI features.

How much does Todoist cost?

Todoist Pro costs $7 per month or $60 per year ($5 per month billed yearly). Business costs $10 per user per month or $8 per user per month billed yearly ($96 per user per year). Beginner is free.

Is Todoist free?

Yes. The Beginner plan is free with up to 5 personal projects, 5 team projects, 3 filter views, 7 days of activity history and 10 Ramble sessions a month.

What is the difference between Todoist Pro and Business?

Pro is a single-user plan with higher limits and AI. Business adds a shared team workspace with team projects, folders, restricted projects, team filters, roles, up to 1,000 members and centralised billing.

Does Todoist have AI features?

Yes. Ramble turns voice, text, images and documents into tasks, Todoist Assist automates busywork and Email Assist converts forwarded emails into tasks. Pro and Business include unlimited use under fair use limits.

Does Todoist have a free trial?

Yes. Todoist offers a 7-day free trial of Pro and a 14-day free trial of Business.

Did Todoist raise prices?

Yes. Todoist raised prices on 10 December 2025, with Pro monthly rising about 40 percent and yearly about 25 percent, according to a June 2026 pricing review.

What are alternatives to Todoist?

Common alternatives include Asana, Trello, ClickUp, monday.com and Basecamp. Trello is closest for simple boards, while Asana and ClickUp offer fuller project management.

What integrations does Todoist support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Email and calendar integration, Email Assist (forward emails to create tasks), Ramble voice, text, image and document capture. API details: API and third-party integration details were not reviewed.

What are the limitations of Todoist?

Core features like custom reminders and calendar layout require paid plans. Business is billed per seat with no flat-rate option, so costs rise linearly with headcount. Monthly billing costs about 40 percent more than yearly, and Pro prices rose in December 2025.

Which platforms does Todoist run on?

Todoist is documented as available on: Web, iOS app, Android app.

What are the main alternatives to Todoist?

The main alternatives to Todoist covered on Stack18 are Asana, Trello, ClickUp, monday.com and Basecamp. Asana may suit teams that want a free start and a clear upgrade path. Trello may suit small teams that want simple Kanban boards. ClickUp may suit teams that want a generous free plan to evaluate. See the Todoist alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.
