Quick answer

What is Jira Service Management?

Jira Service Management (JSM) is Atlassian's service desk and IT service management platform. It provides customer request portals, ticket queues, SLAs, automation, knowledge base, and, on higher plans, incident, problem and change management, asset management and AI via Rovo. Pricing is per agent per month with volume-based tiers: a free plan for up to 3 agents, Standard from about $20, Premium from about $51.42 and custom Enterprise pricing.

  • Best forIT service desks that already use Jira or Confluence
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingFree for up to 3 agents. Reported list prices per agent per month: Standard from about $20 and Premium from about $51.42 (at 75 agents); higher for small teams ($25 and $57.30 at 15 agents). Enterprise is custom. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationChange management and advanced ITSM features require Premium, which costs about 2.5 times Standard.

Overview

Jira Service Management overview: who it is for and what it does.

Jira Service Management grew out of Jira Service Desk and is built on the Jira work platform, which makes it a natural fit for organisations that already use Jira Software, Confluence or other Atlassian tools. It serves IT service desks first, with templates for ITSM, HR, legal and facilities, and it connects tickets to development work so incidents can become bugs and changes can link to releases. Customers submit requests through a portal, email or chat, and agents work queues with SLAs, automation and a knowledge base.

Atlassian prices JSM per agent, not per customer, and the price per agent falls as the agent count grows. Independent analysis of Atlassian's calculator reports Standard at $20 and Premium at $51.42 per agent per month at a 75-agent default, but $25 and $57.30 for the first 15 agents on monthly billing. The Free plan supports up to 3 agents with 2 GB of storage and limited features. Standard adds 250 GB of storage and a core service desk, while Premium adds advanced incident, problem and change management, asset management, unlimited storage, a 99.9 percent uptime SLA, 24/7 support for critical issues and Atlassian Rovo AI with a virtual service agent handling 1,000 conversations per month. Enterprise is custom, with a reported starting price of $168,500 a year for 201 to 300 agents.

Real costs depend on add-ons and limits. Reviewers note that change management arrives only on Premium, that Assets objects beyond the plan allowance cost $0.02 per object per month, that single sign-on requires Atlassian Guard Standard at about $4.20 per user per month, and that the Standard-to-Premium step is roughly 2.5 times the price. Teams that want a simple customer support inbox may find Zendesk or Freshdesk easier; teams that want ITSM tied to development workflows will find JSM a strong choice.

Main problem it solves

Gives IT, HR and business teams a structured service desk with request portals, SLAs and automation, tightly connected to Jira work so that incidents and changes flow to the teams who fix them.

Key features

Jira Service Management features explained.

Service desk and requests

Request portal and queues
Customers submit requests through a portal, email or chat, and agents work queues with templates for IT, HR and business teams.
SLAs
Service level agreements track response and resolution times; the Free plan lacks SLAs and Standard and above include them.
Knowledge base
A knowledge base lets customers find answers before raising requests.
Automation
Automation rules handle routing and routine updates, with Standard listed at 5,000 rule runs per month.

ITSM and operations

Incident and problem management
Advanced incident and problem management, along with AIOps capabilities, are Premium capabilities according to independent analyses.
Change management
Change management with approvals and risk assessment is gated to Premium, which is the main reason teams upgrade.
Asset management
Assets track hardware, software and configuration items, with 50,000 objects included on Premium and overage at $0.02 per object.
Jira integration
Tickets link to Jira Software issues so service teams and developers collaborate on the same work.

AI, security and administration

Rovo AI and virtual service agent
Premium includes Atlassian Rovo for AI search, chat and agents and a virtual service agent for 1,000 conversations a month.
Single sign-on and access
SSO requires Atlassian Guard Standard, priced at about $4.20 per user per month, and is not bundled in Free, Standard or Premium.
Storage and uptime
Storage is 2 GB on Free, 250 GB on Standard and unlimited on Premium, with a 99.9 percent uptime SLA and 24/7 critical support on Premium.
Enterprise scale
Enterprise supports multiple business units and subsidiaries with custom pricing and dedicated support.

FAQ

Jira Service Management questions and answers

What is Jira Service Management?

Jira Service Management is Atlassian's service desk and ITSM platform. It offers request portals, queues, SLAs, automation, knowledge base, incident, problem and change management, asset management and AI, built on the Jira platform.

How much does Jira Service Management cost?

Atlassian lists a free plan for up to 3 agents. Independent analyses report Standard from about $20 and Premium from about $51.42 per agent per month at a 75-agent tier, with higher per-agent prices for smaller teams, such as $25 and $57.30 for the first 15 agents on monthly billing.

Is Jira Service Management free?

Yes, up to 3 agents with 2 GB of storage and limited features. It does not include SLAs or advanced automation, so it suits small pilots.

What is the difference between JSM Standard and Premium?

Premium adds advanced incident, problem and change management, asset management, unlimited storage, Rovo AI, a virtual service agent with 1,000 conversations per month and a 99.9 percent uptime SLA.

Does Jira Service Management include AI?

Premium includes Atlassian Rovo for AI search, chat and agents, and a virtual service agent. Standard receives a basic Rovo experience.

Is single sign-on included in Jira Service Management?

No. SSO requires Atlassian Guard Standard, reported at about $4.20 per user per month, in addition to the JSM plan.

Is Jira Service Management only for IT?

No. It includes templates for IT, HR, legal and facilities requests and can be used as a general service desk, though ITSM is its strongest area.

What are alternatives to Jira Service Management?

Common alternatives include Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, Help Scout and Front. Freshservice and ServiceNow compete in ITSM.

What integrations does Jira Service Management support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Jira Software, Confluence, Atlassian Rovo, Atlassian Marketplace apps. API details: JSM exposes REST APIs through the Atlassian platform; details were not reviewed.

What are the limitations of Jira Service Management?

Change management and advanced ITSM features require Premium, which costs about 2.5 times Standard. The per-agent price depends on volume tiers, so the $20 figure often does not apply to small teams. Single sign-on is not bundled and needs Atlassian Guard Standard at about $4.20 per user per month.

Which platforms does Jira Service Management run on?

Jira Service Management is documented as available on: Web, Cloud.
