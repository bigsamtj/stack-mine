Quick answer

What is ClickUp?

ClickUp is an all-in-one work and project management platform that combines tasks, docs, goals, dashboards and automation, sold per member in Free Forever, Unlimited, Business and Enterprise plans.

  • Best forTeams that want a generous free plan to evaluate
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingFree Forever plan; Unlimited reported at $7 per member per month billed annually. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationMany features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited.
  • AlternativesAsana, monday.com, Trello, Jira · compare all

Overview

ClickUp overview: who it is for and what it does.

ClickUp positions itself as a single workspace for tasks, docs, dashboards, goals and automation. Independent pricing guides describe a Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks and members, and paid Unlimited and Business plans priced per member, with a custom Enterprise plan.

The tiers draw clear lines. Guides report that Goals, time tracking and workload management are locked behind the Business plan rather than Unlimited, that Business adds Google SSO, and that Enterprise adds advanced permissions, unlimited custom roles and an enterprise API. Some features are metered by number of uses on lower plans rather than being truly unlimited.

ClickUp Brain, the AI product, is billed as a separate per-member add-on and is not included in Free, Unlimited or Business. Guides stress that Brain is billed on every paid member, not just those who use it, and that guests are free but members are billable seats.

Main problem it solves

Gives teams one workspace for tasks, docs, goals and dashboards so they can replace several separate tools.

Typical customer segment

Individuals and small teams on Free Forever, growing teams on Unlimited and Business, and larger organisations on Enterprise.

Target users

  • Individuals and small teams that want a generous free plan
  • Teams that want tasks, docs and goals in one tool
  • Larger organisations that need advanced permissions and an enterprise API

Primary use cases

  • Task and project management
  • Docs and wikis next to tasks
  • Goal tracking and time tracking
  • Dashboards and reporting
  • Workflow automation

What makes it distinct

  • Unlimited tasks and members on the free plan.
  • Many features bundled into one workspace.
  • AI sold as a separate per-member add-on rather than bundled into plans.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

ClickUp pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • The free plan includes unlimited tasks and members, which is unusually generous. [5]
  • Entry paid pricing of $7 per member is reported among the lowest for a full work management suite. [1]
  • Many functions such as docs, goals and dashboards sit in one workspace. [4]

Limitations

  • Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited. [2]
  • AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost. [3]
  • Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited. [4]
  • API limits of 100 calls a minute on most plans can bottleneck automation-heavy workflows. [2]

Best for

  • Teams that want a generous free plan to evaluate
  • Teams that want tasks, docs and goals in one tool
  • Budget-conscious teams that can live without AI or will pay for the add-on

May be less suited to

  • Teams that want AI included without a per-member add-on
  • Teams that need predictable, fully published Enterprise pricing

FAQ

ClickUp questions and answers

What is ClickUp used for?

ClickUp is used to manage tasks and projects, write docs, track goals and time, build dashboards and automate workflows in one workspace.

Does ClickUp have a free plan?

Yes. Free Forever includes unlimited tasks and members, with limits on storage and on the number of uses of some features.

How much does ClickUp cost?

Independent guides report Unlimited at $7 and Business at $12 per member per month billed annually, with Enterprise on a custom quote. The Brain AI add-on is extra, at $5 to $9 per member in different sources. Confirm on clickup.com.

Is ClickUp AI included in paid plans?

No. Guides report ClickUp Brain is a separate add-on that is not included in Free, Unlimited or Business, and is billed per paid member.

What are the main alternatives to ClickUp?

The main alternatives to ClickUp covered on Stack18 are Asana, monday.com, Trello and Jira. Asana may suit teams that want a free start and a clear upgrade path. monday.com may suit teams that want a flexible visual board-based tool. Trello may suit small teams that want simple Kanban boards. See the ClickUp alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does ClickUp support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Integrations on paid plans (unlimited on Unlimited), Enterprise API, MCP access (reported). API details: Guides report a rate limit of 100 calls a minute on Free, Unlimited and Business, 1,000 on Business Plus and 10,000 on Enterprise.

What are the limitations of ClickUp?

Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited. AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost. Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited.

Which platforms does ClickUp run on?

ClickUp is documented as available on: Web, Mobile apps.
