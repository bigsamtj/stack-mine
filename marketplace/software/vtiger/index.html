Quick answer

What is Vtiger CRM?

Vtiger CRM is an all-in-one CRM suite that combines sales, marketing automation, a help desk, projects and inventory. It has a free One Pilot plan for up to 10 users and 3,000 records, and paid plans per user per month billed annually: One Growth ($12), One Professional ($30), One Enterprise ($42) and One AI ($50), with Single App seats at $20, $30 and $38. Monthly billing is $15, $42, $58 and $66.

Overview

Vtiger CRM overview: who it is for and what it does.

Vtiger grew out of an open-source CRM heritage and now sells a cloud suite called Vtiger One that covers the customer lifecycle in a single database. Its pricing page describes One Pilot as a forever-free plan for companies starting their CRM journey, with up to 10 users and 3,000 records, and each paid tier layering on more users, records and capabilities. Prospeo's 2026 comparisons describe it as a focused CRM for teams under 15 that want simplicity, responsive support and Calculus AI starting at $12 per user per month, and credit it with serving more than 300,000 customers.

The plans scale on three axes: users, records and AI. One Growth ($12 annual, $15 monthly) caps at 15 users and 100,000 records and adds predictive and generative AI, natural language querying, a chatbot, multiple pipelines, lead auto-assignment and email limits. One Professional ($30 annual, $42 monthly) and One Enterprise ($42 annual, $58 monthly) have unlimited users and records, with Professional adding profile and lead scoring, sales forecasting and quotas, and Enterprise adding multi-currency, internal ticketing, work orders and advanced project and inventory features. One AI ($50 annual, $66 monthly) adds enterprise-grade predictive and generative AI, voice assistants integration, an AI prompt builder, AI query analytics and AI bot management. Storage rises from 3 GB on Pilot to 10, 25, 50 and 100 GB.

Single App User seats are Vtiger's distinctive cost lever: a user with full access to one app (Sales, Marketing, Help Desk, Projects or Inventory) and view-only access to the rest costs $20, $30 or $38 per month instead of $30, $42 or $50. Buyers should note the monthly-to-annual gap of about 40 percent on Professional, the 15-user cap on Growth, and that Vtiger's integration ecosystem is smaller than Zoho's 1,100-plus integrations. Older articles quote Growth at $20 per user or the free plan at 15 users, which do not match Vtiger's current pages.

  • The free One Pilot plan supports 10 users and 3,000 records, with no credit card required for trials. [1]
  • Calculus AI features are included from the $12 Growth plan, far below the price of comparable AI tiers elsewhere. [3]

FAQ

Vtiger CRM questions and answers

What is Vtiger CRM?

Vtiger CRM is an all-in-one CRM suite combining sales, marketing automation, a help desk, projects and inventory, with Calculus AI features, offered in a free One Pilot plan and paid One plans.

How much does Vtiger cost?

Vtiger's One Growth costs $12, One Professional $30, One Enterprise $42 and One AI $50 per user per month billed annually. Monthly billing is $15, $42, $58 and $66. One Pilot is free.

Is Vtiger CRM free?

Yes. One Pilot is free forever for up to 10 users and 3,000 records, with core lead and contact management, One View, idle alerts and a mobile app.

What are Vtiger Single App User seats?

Single App Users have full access to one app (Sales, Marketing, Help Desk, Projects or Inventory) and view-only access to the others, at $20 on Professional, $30 on Enterprise and $38 on One AI.

Does Vtiger have AI features?

Yes. Calculus AI (predictive and generative AI, natural language querying and a chatbot) starts on One Growth at $12, and One AI adds an AI prompt builder, voice assistants, query analytics and bot management.

What is the difference between Vtiger Growth and Professional?

Growth caps at 15 users and 100,000 records. Professional removes those caps and adds lead and profile scoring, sales forecasting and quotas.

Does Vtiger offer a free trial?

Yes. Vtiger offers a free trial with no credit card required in addition to the free One Pilot plan.

What are alternatives to Vtiger?

Common alternatives include Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Freshsales, Bitrix24 and SugarCRM.

What integrations does Vtiger CRM support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Email and phone integration, Zoom and Google Meet, Voice assistants integration (One AI), Mobile app, Third-party integrations (smaller ecosystem than Zoho). API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Vtiger CRM?

Growth is capped at 15 users and 100,000 records, so growth forces a jump to $30 per user. Monthly billing costs about 40 percent more than annual billing on Professional. The integration ecosystem is smaller than Zoho CRM's.

Which platforms does Vtiger CRM run on?

Vtiger CRM is documented as available on: Web, Mobile app.

What are the main alternatives to Vtiger CRM?

The main alternatives to Vtiger CRM covered on Stack18 are Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Freshsales, Bitrix24 and SugarCRM. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Freshsales may suit startups and small teams that want an affordable CRM with a dialer.
