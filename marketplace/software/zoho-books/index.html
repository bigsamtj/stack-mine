Overview

Zoho Books overview: who it is for and what it does.

Zoho Books is the accounting app in Zoho's business suite, with invoicing, expenses, bank reconciliation, sales tax and reporting, and integrations with Zoho CRM, Inventory and other Zoho apps.

A November 2026 pricing tracker lists a Free plan (1 user plus an accountant, 1,000 invoices a year) and paid plans at $15, $40, $60, $120 and $240 a month billed annually ($20, $50, $70, $150 and $275 monthly) for Standard, Professional, Premium, Elite and Ultimate.

Higher plans add purchase orders, multi-currency and inventory (Professional), budgets and fixed assets (Premium), warehouse management and Shopify integration (Elite), and advanced analytics (Ultimate).

Main problem it solves

Gives small businesses full accounting, sales tax and inventory at a low price, connected to other Zoho apps.

Typical customer segment

Sole traders on Free through growing businesses with up to 15 users on Ultimate.

Target users

  • Sole proprietors who want free accounting
  • Small businesses already using Zoho CRM or other Zoho apps
  • Growing product businesses that need inventory and warehouses

Primary use cases

  • Invoicing and expenses
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Sales tax and 1099 tracking
  • Inventory and purchase orders (Professional)
  • Budgets and fixed assets (Premium)

What makes it distinct

  • A free plan with 1,000 invoices a year.
  • Six plans scaling to warehouse management and analytics.
  • Native connections to the wider Zoho suite.

FAQ

Zoho Books questions and answers

What is Zoho Books used for?

Zoho Books is cloud accounting software from Zoho, with a free plan and Standard, Professional, Premium, Elite and Ultimate paid plans that add users, inventory and analytics. Typical uses include invoicing and expenses, bank reconciliation, sales tax and 1099 tracking, inventory and purchase orders (Professional).

How much does Zoho Books cost?

A November 2026 tracker lists Free, then $15, $40, $60, $120 and $240 a month billed annually for Standard, Professional, Premium, Elite and Ultimate.

Is Zoho Books free?

Yes, there is a free plan for one user plus an accountant and up to 1,000 invoices a year.

Does Zoho Books have inventory?

Inventory tracking starts on the Professional plan; warehouse management is on Elite.

How does Zoho Books compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of TallyPrime vs Zoho Books and Xero vs Zoho Books, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Zoho Books?

The main alternatives to Zoho Books covered on Stack18 are QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks and Wave. QuickBooks Online may suit US small businesses that want the most widely used cloud accounting tool. Xero may suit small businesses and accountants that want clean, collaborative cloud books. FreshBooks may suit freelancers and service businesses that bill clients by invoice. See the Zoho Books alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Zoho Books?

Strengths: A free plan covers one user and 1,000 invoices a year. Paid plans start at about $15 a month billed annually. Limitations: The free plan is limited to one user plus an accountant. Warehouse management and Shopify integration need Elite at $120 a month or more.

Who is Zoho Books best for?

Zoho Books may suit small businesses that want low-cost accounting inside the Zoho ecosystem; sole proprietors who want a free plan; product businesses that need inventory without a separate tool. It may be less suited to teams that rely on an accountant ecosystem built around QuickBooks; large multi-entity groups.

What integrations does Zoho Books support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Zoho CRM and other Zoho apps, Shopify (Elite and above), API access (from Standard). API: API access is listed from the Standard plan.
