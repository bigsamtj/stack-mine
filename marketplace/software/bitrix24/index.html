Quick answer

What is Bitrix24?

Bitrix24 is an all-in-one business platform that combines CRM, tasks and projects, team chat and video calls, telephony, a website builder and workflow automation. Instead of per-user pricing, paid plans are priced per organisation per month, billed annually: Basic $49 (5 users), Standard $99 (50 users), Professional $199 (100 users) and Enterprise from $399 (250 users), with a free plan for 1 to 2 users.

Overview

Bitrix24 overview: who it is for and what it does.

Bitrix24 is a long-running work platform used by small and mid-size businesses that want CRM, collaboration and project tools under one roof. The free plan includes basic CRM with deals and contacts, tasks with Kanban and Gantt views, shared calendars, chat and video calls and 5 GB of storage. Paid plans add sales pipelines, recurring deals, email integration, telephony, a web form designer and a website builder on Basic, with higher tiers adding marketing automation, advanced analytics, online documents, administration tools, AI-powered sales, call tracking, email marketing and workflow automation.

Pricing is flat per organisation with a user cap rather than per user. Bitrix24's own price page shows Basic at $49 per month billed annually ($69 billed monthly) for 5 users and 24 GB of storage, Standard at $99 annually ($144 monthly) for 50 users and 100 GB, and Professional for 100 users and 1,024 GB of storage. Independent price trackers report Professional at $199 and Enterprise at $399 per month billed annually for 250 users, with monthly billing about 20 percent higher. For a team of 50, Standard works out to about $2 per user per month, which is far below most per-seat CRMs.

The catch is breadth versus depth. Reviewers describe a large feature set that can feel complex, a dated interface and a learning curve, and the free tier has been described both as unlimited users and as limited to 1 to 2 users depending on the date, so check the current limit. Storage, telephony minutes, e-commerce and some automation limits vary by plan, and the self-hosted on-premise edition is licensed separately. Bitrix24 suits teams that want one inexpensive system for sales, tasks and communication; teams that need a polished, CRM-only experience may prefer a focused product.

Scope: Bitrix24 spans CRM, tasks and projects, and team communication; it is listed under CRM because sales pipelines and contact management are its core paid offering, with tasks, projects and communication tools included alongside.

Main problem it solves

Replaces separate CRM, task management, chat and telephony subscriptions with a single platform billed per organisation, keeping costs flat as headcount grows within each plan limit.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size businesses, distributed teams and cost-conscious organisations that want CRM, tasks and collaboration in one suite.

Target users

  • Small businesses that want CRM, tasks and chat in one tool
  • Teams of 10 to 100 that benefit from flat organisation pricing
  • Sales teams that need telephony, email and pipelines together
  • Companies that want to automate workflows without separate tools

Primary use cases

  • CRM with sales pipelines and recurring deals
  • Task and project management with Kanban and Gantt
  • Team chat, video calls and shared calendars
  • Telephony and call tracking
  • Web forms and website building
  • Workflow and business process automation

What makes it distinct

  • Flat per-organisation pricing with user caps instead of per-user fees
  • CRM, projects, chat, telephony and website builder in one platform
  • A free plan with CRM, tasks, chat and video calls
  • Cloud and self-hosted deployment options

FAQ

Bitrix24 questions and answers

What is Bitrix24?

Bitrix24 is an all-in-one business platform combining CRM, tasks and projects, team chat and video calls, telephony, a website builder and workflow automation. It runs in the cloud or on-premise.

How much does Bitrix24 cost?

Bitrix24's price page lists Basic at $49 per month billed annually for 5 users and Standard at $99 for 50 users. Independent trackers report Professional at $199 for 100 users and Enterprise at $399 for 250 users. Monthly billing is about 20 percent higher.

Is Bitrix24 priced per user?

No. Bitrix24 charges a flat monthly price per organisation with a user cap on each plan, so cost per user falls as you fill the plan.

Does Bitrix24 have a free plan?

Yes. Bitrix24's free plan includes basic CRM, tasks with Kanban and Gantt, shared calendars, chat and video calls and 5 GB of storage. The current user limit is 1 to 2 users, though older articles describe unlimited users.

Is Bitrix24 a CRM or a project management tool?

Both. CRM with pipelines and contacts is its core, and it also offers tasks with Kanban and Gantt views, projects, chat and video, which is why it is also listed under project management and collaboration.

Does Bitrix24 offer an on-premise version?

Yes. Third-party summaries report a self-hosted on-premise edition with separate licensing alongside the cloud service.

Does Bitrix24 have telephony?

Yes. Telephony is included from the Basic plan, and call tracking is added on Professional.

What are alternatives to Bitrix24?

Common alternatives include Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales and monday.com.

What integrations does Bitrix24 support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Email integration, Telephony and call tracking, WhatsApp and messaging (listed on paid plans), Online store and website builder, Web forms, REST API (reported). API details: Bitrix24 is described by third parties as offering REST API access and integrations; details were not reviewed.

What are the limitations of Bitrix24?

User caps of 5, 50 and 100 force an upgrade when headcount passes each limit. The free plan is limited in users and CRM features, and storage is only 5 GB. Sources describe the free plan as unlimited users in older articles and as 1 to 2 users now, so limits should be confirmed.

Which platforms does Bitrix24 run on?

Bitrix24 is documented as available on: Web, Cloud, On-premise (separate licence).

What are the main alternatives to Bitrix24?

The main alternatives to Bitrix24 covered on Stack18 are Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales and monday.com. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM.
