Overview

Keka overview: who it is for and what it does.

Keka is one of the better-known Indian HR and payroll platforms, covering employee records, onboarding, leave and attendance, payroll with statutory compliance, expenses, and performance management.

A reseller page lists three plans priced by headcount: Foundation (core HR, attendance and payroll with PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax and gratuity), Strength (adds e-signatures, asset tracking, analytics, geo-fencing and SSO) and Growth (adds OKRs, 360-degree reviews and workflow automation).

A September 2026 listing reports Foundation at $108 a month covering 50 employees plus about $1.20 per additional employee, Strength at $156 and Growth at $192 a month. Hire (ATS) and PSA are separate products. Invoices are in INR with 18% GST.

Main problem it solves

Gives Indian companies compliant payroll and modern HR on one platform.

Typical customer segment

Indian startups and mid-size companies.

Target users

  • Indian startups running payroll with PF and ESI
  • HR teams that want attendance, geo-fencing and selfie clock-in
  • Companies running OKRs and 360-degree reviews

Primary use cases

  • Payroll with statutory compliance
  • Leave and attendance
  • Onboarding and exits
  • Expenses and loans
  • Performance management (Growth)

What makes it distinct

  • Statutory Indian payroll on the entry plan.
  • Geo-fencing and selfie clock-in from Strength.
  • Separate Hire (ATS) and PSA products.

FAQ

Keka questions and answers

What is Keka used for?

Keka is an HR and payroll platform built for Indian businesses, covering core HR, leave and attendance, payroll with statutory compliance (PF, ESI, TDS, PT) and performance, sold in Foundation, Strength and Growth plans. Typical uses include payroll with statutory compliance, leave and attendance, onboarding and exits, expenses and loans.

How much does Keka cost?

Keka quotes in INR by headcount. One September 2026 listing reports Foundation at about $108 a month for 50 employees, Strength at $156 and Growth at $192.

Does Keka handle PF, ESI and TDS?

Yes. The Foundation plan covers PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax and gratuity, with rule changes applied automatically.

Does Keka have a free trial?

Yes, a free trial is available, but there is no permanent free plan.

How does Keka compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Keka vs greytHR, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Keka?

The main alternatives to Keka covered on Stack18 are greytHR, Zoho People, BambooHR and Deel. greytHR may suit Indian SMEs that want affordable payroll and leave management. Zoho People may suit small businesses that want low-cost HR, attendance and performance tools. BambooHR may suit small and mid-size businesses that want an easy HRIS. See the Keka alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Keka?

Strengths: Statutory payroll with PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax and gratuity is on the entry plan. Strength adds geo-fencing, selfie clock-in and SSO. Limitations: Prices are quoted by headcount and not published in INR. Performance management needs the Growth plan.

Who is Keka best for?

Keka may suit Indian companies that want modern HR with compliant payroll; startups with field staff that need geo-fenced attendance; companies running OKRs and 360-degree reviews. It may be less suited to companies outside India; very small teams that want a free plan.

What integrations does Keka support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Keka Hire (ATS), Keka PSA (projects and timesheets). API: Not reviewed for this profile.
