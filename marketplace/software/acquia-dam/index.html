FAQ

Acquia DAM questions and answers

What is Acquia DAM used for?

Acquia DAM (formerly Widen Collective) is a digital asset management and product information management platform from Acquia, with portals and broad integrations. Typical uses include dAM, pIM, portals, integrations.

How much does Acquia DAM cost?

Acquia DAM is quote-only, per an August 2026 guide.

Is Acquia DAM the same as Widen?

Yes, Acquia DAM was formerly Widen.

Does Acquia DAM include PIM?

Yes.

How does Acquia DAM compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of MediaValet vs Acquia DAM, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Acquia DAM?

The main alternatives to Acquia DAM covered on Stack18 are Bynder, Aprimo and MediaValet. Bynder may suit enterprise marketing teams managing large asset libraries. Aprimo may suit large marketing organisations needing governance and workflow. MediaValet may suit Microsoft-centric organisations needing an enterprise DAM. See the Acquia DAM alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Acquia DAM?

Strengths: About 300 integrations. DAM plus PIM. Limitations: No public pricing. Sold within wider DXP.

Who is Acquia DAM best for?

Acquia DAM may suit brands needing DAM and PIM with many integrations; brands with product catalogues; drupal and Acquia customers. It may be less suited to small teams; self-serve buyers.

What integrations does Acquia DAM support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Drupal, Shopify, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
