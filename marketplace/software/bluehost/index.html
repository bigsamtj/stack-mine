What is Bluehost?

Bluehost is a web hosting provider owned by Newfold, offering shared, WordPress and VPS hosting with guided WordPress setup.

Overview

Bluehost overview: who it is for and what it does.

Bluehost guides beginners through WordPress setup and is officially recommended by WordPress.org.

One 2026 review lists Basic at $3.99 a month on a 36-month term renewing at $9.99, owner Newfold, and a move to Oracle Cloud that improved global speed; another lists $2.95 renewing at $10.99 and measured 320 ms TTFB.

They note upsell-heavy support, renewals that roughly double and weekly backups on entry.

FAQ

Bluehost questions and answers

What is Bluehost used for?

Bluehost is a web hosting provider owned by Newfold, offering shared, WordPress and VPS hosting with guided WordPress setup. Typical uses include wordPress sites, blogs, small business sites, wooCommerce starters.

How much does Bluehost cost?

Basic starts around $2.95 to $3.99 a month on a long term and renews around $9.99 to $10.99.

Who owns Bluehost?

Newfold.

Is Bluehost recommended by WordPress?

Yes, it is WordPress.org-recommended.

How does Bluehost compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Hostinger vs Bluehost, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Bluehost?

The main alternatives to Bluehost covered on Stack18 are Hostinger, SiteGround and DreamHost. Hostinger may suit beginners and small sites wanting the lowest entry price. SiteGround may suit small businesses wanting great support and daily backups. DreamHost may suit site owners wanting month-to-month billing and many sites. See the Bluehost alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Bluehost?

Strengths: WordPress.org-recommended. Guided setup. Limitations: Upsell-heavy support. Renewal about doubles.

Who is Bluehost best for?

Bluehost may suit beginners starting a WordPress site; small business websites; bloggers. It may be less suited to performance-critical sites; agencies.

What integrations does Bluehost support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include WordPress, WooCommerce, Cloudflare, Microsoft 365. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
