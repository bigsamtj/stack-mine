Overview

STAAH overview: who it is for and what it does.

STAAH, founded in New Zealand, is one of the most used channel managers among independent hotels in India and Asia-Pacific. It is a distribution platform, not a full property management system, and connects to many PMS products.

A competitor’s 2026 guide reports STAAH Instant from about $70 a month and STAAH MAX as a fuller distribution suite, with typical totals of $100 to $250 a month when a booking engine is added, depending on property size. Booking engine, metasearch, ReviewMinder and business intelligence are separate add-ons.

Pricing is a fixed monthly fee with no booking commission; contract terms are agreed in sales.

Main problem it solves

Keeps hotel rates and availability in sync across OTAs to prevent overbookings.

Typical customer segment

Independent hotels, resorts and small chains.

Target users

  • Revenue managers
  • Hotel owners
  • Reservations teams

Primary use cases

  • OTA rate and inventory sync
  • Direct booking engine
  • Metasearch
  • Review management
  • Distribution reports

What makes it distinct

  • Strong OTA coverage in India and APAC.
  • No booking commission.
  • Connects to many PMS products.

FAQ

STAAH questions and answers

What is STAAH used for?

STAAH is used for OTA rate and inventory sync, direct booking engine, metasearch and review management.

Does STAAH have a free plan?

No. STAAH does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Demo.

How much does STAAH cost?

Reported from about $70 a month. From a competitor’s 2026 guide; STAAH quotes by property.

What is the difference between STAAH STAAH Instant and With booking engine?

STAAH Instant is listed at From about $70 a month (reported). With booking engine is listed at About $100–250 a month (reported) (By property size). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to STAAH?

The main alternatives to STAAH covered on Stack18 are eZee Absolute (Yanolja Cloud PMS), Hotelogix, Cloudbeds and Little Hotelier. eZee Absolute (Yanolja Cloud PMS) may suit independent hotels. Hotelogix may suit budget-conscious independent hotels. Cloudbeds may suit independent hotels wanting one vendor for PMS and distribution. See the STAAH alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does STAAH support?

Documented integrations include Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and MakeMyTrip (vendor), PMS systems (vendor) and GDS (MAX). API: Partner API (vendor).

What are the limitations of STAAH?

Not a full PMS. Add-ons priced separately. Quote-based pricing.

Which platforms does STAAH run on?

STAAH is documented as available on: Web, Mobile app (vendor).

Is STAAH available in India?

Yes. STAAH is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
