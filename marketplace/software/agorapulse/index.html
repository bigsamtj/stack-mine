Overview

Agorapulse overview: who it is for and what it does.

Agorapulse is known for its social inbox, which brings comments, messages and reviews into one queue, plus publishing, listening and social ROI reporting.

A competitor's guide (17 September 2026) lists Standard at $79, Professional at $119 and Advanced at $149 per user a month billed annually ($99, $149 and $199 monthly), each including 10 social profiles, with Custom by quote. Extra profiles cost about $10 to $15 a month.

Agorapulse ended its free plan on 6 July 2026, according to its help centre. A 30-day trial needs no card, and eligible nonprofits get 20% off.

Agorapulse questions and answers

What is Agorapulse used for?

Agorapulse is a social media management platform for publishing, a unified social inbox, monitoring and ROI reporting, priced per user in Standard, Professional, Advanced and Custom plans. Typical uses include social inbox, publishing, reporting and ROI, listening (add-on).

How much does Agorapulse cost?

Standard is $79, Professional $119 and Advanced $149 per user a month billed annually.

Does Agorapulse still have a free plan?

No. Its help centre says the free plan ended on 6 July 2026.

How many profiles does Agorapulse include?

10 social profiles on each paid plan; extras cost about $10 to $15 a month.

How does Agorapulse compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Agorapulse vs Sprout Social, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Agorapulse?

The main alternatives to Agorapulse covered on Stack18 are Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Sendible and SocialPilot. Sprout Social may suit brands that want strong analytics, inbox and listening for growing social teams. Hootsuite may suit teams and enterprises that want scheduling, inbox, listening and approvals in one platform. Sendible may suit agencies managing many client brands. See the Agorapulse alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Agorapulse?

Strengths: Strong unified social inbox. 30-day free trial. Limitations: Free plan ended on 6 July 2026. Seats are charged per user.

Who is Agorapulse best for?

Agorapulse may suit agencies and teams that live in the social inbox and need reporting; community managers; nonprofits. It may be less suited to solo creators; teams needing many low-cost seats.
