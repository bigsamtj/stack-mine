Overview

Acumatica overview: who it is for and what it does.

Acumatica is a cloud ERP known for licensing by resources consumed rather than per user, which suits companies with many occasional users.

A July 2026 guide says pricing combines the licensed edition and modules, a resource tier tied to transaction volume and the deployment model, with unlimited user accounts on every plan. Acumatica publishes no price list; resellers prepare annual quotes.

Editions include General Business, Distribution, Manufacturing (highest cost), Construction and Retail-Commerce. Costs range from a few thousand dollars a year for small deployments to six figures for large multi-entity operations, plus reseller implementation.

FAQ

Acumatica questions and answers

What is Acumatica used for?

Acumatica is a cloud ERP for mid-market companies with industry editions (General Business, Distribution, Manufacturing, Construction, Retail-Commerce), priced by resources and modules with unlimited users. Typical uses include financials, distribution, manufacturing, construction job costing.

How is Acumatica priced?

By edition, modules and a resource tier tied to transaction volume, with unlimited users.

Does Acumatica charge per user?

No, user accounts are unlimited on every plan.

What Acumatica editions exist?

General Business, Distribution, Manufacturing, Construction and Retail-Commerce.

How does Acumatica compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Acumatica vs Dynamics 365 Business Central and Sage X3 vs Acumatica, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Acumatica?

The main alternatives to Acumatica covered on Stack18 are Dynamics 365 Business Central, SAP Business One, Epicor Kinetic and Sage X3. Dynamics 365 Business Central may suit SMBs on Microsoft 365 moving off entry-level accounting. SAP Business One may suit SMBs and subsidiaries wanting an SAP ERP. Epicor Kinetic may suit discrete and mixed-mode manufacturers. See the Acumatica alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Acumatica?

Strengths: Unlimited users on every plan. Industry-specific editions. Limitations: No published prices. Implementation can exceed first-year software cost.

Who is Acumatica best for?

Acumatica may suit growing mid-market companies that want unlimited users; distributors and construction firms; companies with many occasional users. It may be less suited to very small businesses; large global enterprises.

What integrations does Acumatica support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Ecommerce connectors, CRM, Open APIs. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
