Quick answer

What is monday.com?

monday.com Work Management is a board-based work and project management platform from monday.com Ltd., sold per seat in Free, Basic, Standard, Pro and Enterprise plans.

  • Best forTeams that want a flexible visual board-based tool

Overview

monday.com overview: who it is for and what it does.

monday.com organises work on customisable boards that can be viewed as tables, timelines, calendars and more. Independent pricing guides describe a free plan for up to two seats, paid Basic, Standard and Pro plans priced per seat, and a quote-based Enterprise plan.

Each paid tier adds named capabilities. Standard is reported to add timeline and calendar views, guest access and 250 automation actions a month; Pro adds time tracking, formula columns and 25,000 automations a month and includes monday's AI features; Enterprise adds governance, security and scale on a custom quote with a minimum 12-month term.

Buyers should note the paid-plan minimum of three seats, which affects very small teams, and that monday CRM, monday dev and monday service are priced separately from Work Management. Published prices differ slightly between sources, so confirm current figures with monday.com.

Scope: This profile covers monday.com Work Management. monday CRM, monday dev and monday service are separate products with their own pricing.

Main problem it solves

Gives teams a flexible visual workspace to plan projects, track tasks, automate routine steps and report on progress without custom development.

Typical customer segment

Very small teams on the free plan, small and mid-size teams on Basic to Pro, and large organisations on Enterprise.

Target users

  • Small teams that want a visual board-based tool
  • Teams that need timelines, calendars and automations
  • Organisations that want governance and scale on Enterprise

Primary use cases

  • Project and task tracking on boards
  • Timeline and calendar planning
  • Workflow automation
  • Time tracking and formula-based reporting
  • Guest collaboration with external partners

What makes it distinct

  • A flexible, board-first interface with many views.
  • A free plan plus clear Basic, Standard and Pro steps.
  • Separate products for CRM, dev and service teams on the same platform family.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

monday.com pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • A free plan and clear Basic, Standard and Pro steps make it easy to start small. [2]
  • Timeline, calendar and guest access arrive at the mid-priced Standard plan. [1]
  • Automation allowances scale from 250 to 25,000 actions a month by plan. [1]

Limitations

  • Paid plans require a minimum of three seats, which penalises very small teams. [3]
  • Enterprise pricing is quote-based and reported to carry a minimum 12-month term. [3]
  • monday CRM, dev and service are priced separately, so multi-product use costs more. [1]
  • The free plan is limited to two seats and three boards in at least one source. [5]

Best for

  • Teams that want a flexible visual board-based tool
  • Small and mid-size teams that need timelines and automation at mid-range prices
  • Organisations that might later add monday CRM, dev or service

May be less suited to

FAQ

monday.com questions and answers

What is monday.com used for?

monday.com Work Management is used to plan and track projects and tasks on customisable boards, automate routine work and report on progress.

Does monday.com have a free plan?

Yes. Independent guides report a free plan limited to two seats. Paid plans need at least three seats.

How much does monday.com cost?

Guides report $9 (Basic), $12 (Standard) and $19 (Pro) per seat per month billed annually, with Enterprise on a custom quote. Confirm on monday.com.

What is the difference between monday.com Standard and Pro?

Standard adds timeline and calendar views, guest access and 250 automations a month. Pro adds time tracking, formula columns, 25,000 automations and AI features.

What are the main alternatives to monday.com?

The main alternatives to monday.com covered on Stack18 are Asana, ClickUp, Trello and Jira. Asana may suit teams that want a free start and a clear upgrade path. ClickUp may suit teams that want a generous free plan to evaluate. Trello may suit small teams that want simple Kanban boards. See the monday.com alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does monday.com support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Basic integrations (Standard), Advanced integrations on higher plans (reported). API details: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of monday.com?

Paid plans require a minimum of three seats, which penalises very small teams. Enterprise pricing is quote-based and reported to carry a minimum 12-month term. monday CRM, dev and service are priced separately, so multi-product use costs more.

Which platforms does monday.com run on?

monday.com is documented as available on: Web, Mobile apps.
