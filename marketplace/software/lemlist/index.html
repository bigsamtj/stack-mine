Overview

lemlist overview: who it is for and what it does.

lemlist is known for personalised cold email (images, landing pages) and multichannel sequences that add LinkedIn and call steps, with built-in lead finding.

A January 2026 guide from a competing tool lists Free (lead finding only, no sending), Email Pro at $69 and Multichannel Expert at $99 per user a month, and Enterprise by quote with unlimited guest seats.

Email Pro includes 3 sending mailboxes per user and Multichannel Expert 5. Paid plans have a 14-day free trial without a card.

Main problem it solves

Helps small teams book meetings through personalised outreach.

FAQ

lemlist questions and answers

What is lemlist used for?

lemlist is a cold outreach platform for personalised email and multichannel sequences (LinkedIn, calls), with a lead database, priced per user. Typical uses include cold email, linkedIn outreach, lead finding, multichannel sequences.

How much does lemlist cost?

Email Pro is $69 and Multichannel Expert $99 per user a month; Enterprise is custom.

Does lemlist have a free plan?

Yes, for lead finding only; it cannot send emails.

Does lemlist automate LinkedIn?

Multichannel Expert adds LinkedIn and call steps.

How does lemlist compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of lemlist vs Instantly, Reply.io vs lemlist and Woodpecker vs lemlist, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to lemlist?

The main alternatives to lemlist covered on Stack18 are Instantly, Smartlead, Reply.io and Woodpecker. Instantly may suit high-volume cold emailers and agencies using many mailboxes. Smartlead may suit agencies running cold email for many clients. Reply.io may suit teams wanting multichannel sequences plus an AI SDR option. See the lemlist alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of lemlist?

Strengths: Multichannel sequences at $99 per user. 14-day trial without a card. Limitations: Free plan cannot send emails. Per-user pricing grows with team size.

Who is lemlist best for?

lemlist may suit small teams running personalised multichannel cold outreach; agencies; founders booking meetings. It may be less suited to enterprise forecasting and deal management; very high-volume senders on many mailboxes.

What integrations does lemlist support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, LinkedIn. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
