Quick answer

What is Instamojo?

Instamojo is an Indian online payments and commerce platform that lets small businesses and creators accept money through payment links, Smart Pages and a hosted online store, charging a per-transaction fee with no setup fee.

Overview

Instamojo overview: who it is for and what it does.

Instamojo became popular with freelancers, tutors and home businesses because a payment link can be created in minutes with minimal paperwork. It later added an online store builder, Smart Pages and marketing tools.

A 2026 comparison reports fees of 2% + INR 3 + GST on physical goods and services, 5% + INR 3 + GST on digital goods and 3% + INR 3 + GST on international cards, with standard T+3 settlement and instant payouts for an extra 1% + GST. Store and payment-link plans are sold separately; a price tracker lists Payment Link Smart from about INR 417 a month billed annually.

The same source says Instamojo’s RBI payment aggregator licence application was rejected in September 2023 and that international acceptance is case by case. Larger merchants usually compare it with full payment gateways.

FAQ

Instamojo questions and answers

What is Instamojo used for?

Instamojo is used for payment links, selling digital downloads, small online store and smart Pages for offers.

Does Instamojo have a free plan?

No. Instamojo does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Not applicable.

How much does Instamojo cost?

2% + INR 3 + GST per transaction on physical goods; no setup fee. Fee figures come from a 2026 comparison by a competing payments company; store plan prices were mostly unpublished when checked.

What is the difference between Instamojo Payments (physical goods/services) and Digital goods?

Payments (physical goods/services) is listed at 2% + INR 3 + GST per transaction (No setup fee; T+3 settlement). Digital goods is listed at 5% + INR 3 + GST. Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Instamojo?

The main alternatives to Instamojo covered on Stack18 are Razorpay, Cashfree Payments, PayU and CCAvenue. Razorpay may suit Indian businesses collecting online payments. Cashfree Payments may suit Indian businesses wanting the lowest headline rate. PayU may suit Indian merchants with EMI-heavy, high-ticket baskets. See the Instamojo alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Instamojo support?

Documented integrations include WhatsApp sharing, Shipping partners (reported) and WooCommerce and other plug-ins (reported). API: Payment API for developers.

What are the limitations of Instamojo?

Higher fee (5% + INR 3) on digital goods. International payments are case by case and settle in INR. RBI payment aggregator licence application was rejected in 2023, per source.

Which platforms does Instamojo run on?

Instamojo is documented as available on: Web, Android, iOS.

Is Instamojo available in India?

Yes. Instamojo is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
