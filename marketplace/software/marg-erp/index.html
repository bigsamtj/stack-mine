What is Marg ERP 9+?

Marg ERP 9+ is an inventory, GST billing and accounting software from Marg Compusoft built for Indian distributors and retailers, especially in pharma, FMCG and retail, with batch, expiry and scheme handling and perpetual licences in Basic, Silver and Gold editions.

Overview

Marg ERP 9+ overview: who it is for and what it does.

Marg is a staple in Indian pharmaceutical distribution and chemist shops, where batch numbers, expiry dates, schemes and rate differences matter. It also has vertical editions for FMCG distribution, supermarkets, garments, jewellery and other trades.

An ERP pricing site lists one-time licences of about INR 8,100 for Basic, INR 12,600 for Silver and INR 25,200 for Gold (unlimited users), excluding 18% GST, with a Nano edition near INR 5,550 from some partners. Annual maintenance of about INR 2,000 to 5,000 and implementation of INR 3,000 to 50,000 are reported, and dealers set their own prices.

Marg is sold and supported largely through dealers, so prices and service quality vary by partner. Reviewers point to a dated interface and vertical modules priced separately.

Main problem it solves

Handles billing, stock with batches and expiry, schemes and GST accounts for Indian distributors and retailers in one package.

Typical customer segment

Indian pharma and FMCG distributors, chemists, supermarkets and retailers.

Target users

  • Distributors managing batches, expiry and schemes
  • Chemists and retail counters billing fast
  • Accountants handling GST returns

Primary use cases

  • Batch and expiry tracking
  • GST billing, e-invoice and e-way bill
  • Scheme and discount management
  • Salesman order booking on mobile
  • Accounting and outstanding tracking

What makes it distinct

  • Deep pharma and FMCG distribution features.
  • Perpetual licences with low annual maintenance.
  • Wide dealer network across India.

FAQ

Marg ERP 9+ questions and answers

What is Marg ERP 9+ used for?

Marg ERP 9+ is used for batch and expiry tracking, GST billing, e-invoice and e-way bill, scheme and discount management and salesman order booking on mobile.

Does Marg ERP 9+ have a free plan?

No. Marg ERP 9+ does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Demo.

How much does Marg ERP 9+ cost?

Basic reported at about INR 8,100 one-time (excl. GST). Prices are third-party figures that vary by dealer.

What is the difference between Marg ERP 9+ Nano and Basic?

Nano is listed at About INR 5,550 (Partner-quoted entry edition). Basic is listed at About INR 8,100 (Excl. 18% GST). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Marg ERP 9+?

The main alternatives to Marg ERP 9+ covered on Stack18 are TallyPrime, Zoho Inventory, BUSY and Vyapar. TallyPrime may suit Indian businesses that need GST-ready accounting and inventory. Zoho Inventory may suit small and mid-size sellers, especially Zoho customers. BUSY may suit Indian traders and distributors who want GST books and stock in one package. See the Marg ERP 9+ alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Marg ERP 9+ support?

Documented integrations include Mobile ordering apps, Tally data import (reported) and GST portal. API: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Marg ERP 9+?

Prices and service vary by dealer. Dated interface, per reviewers. Vertical modules priced separately.

Which platforms does Marg ERP 9+ run on?

Marg ERP 9+ is documented as available on: Windows desktop, Android (companion apps).

Is Marg ERP 9+ available in India?

Yes. Marg ERP 9+ is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
