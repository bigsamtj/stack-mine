Overview

Splashtop overview: who it is for and what it does.

Splashtop offers remote access for individuals and teams plus separate remote support products for IT and MSPs.

An October 2026 guide (prices as of June 2026) lists Solo at $6 a month, Pro at $8.25 per user and Performance at $13 per user, billed annually, describing frame rate per dollar as excellent.

It notes there is no free business tier and the product lineup is confusing. A competitor's comparison lists Remote Support SOS at $22 per user a month.

Main problem it solves

Gives fast remote access without high per-seat costs.

Typical customer segment

Professionals, SMBs and IT teams.

Target users

Primary use cases

What makes it distinct

FAQ

Splashtop questions and answers

What is Splashtop used for?

Splashtop is a remote access and remote support product family for professionals, IT teams and MSPs, known for performance per dollar. Typical uses include remote access, on-demand support (SOS), high-performance access.

How much does Splashtop cost?

Solo is $6 a month; Pro $8.25 and Performance $13 per user a month billed annually.

Is Splashtop free for business?

No free business tier, per an October 2026 guide.

Does Splashtop offer remote support?

Yes, via separate products such as Remote Support SOS.

How does Splashtop compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Splashtop vs RemotePC and TeamViewer vs Splashtop, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Splashtop?

The main alternatives to Splashtop covered on Stack18 are TeamViewer, AnyDesk, RemotePC and Zoho Assist. TeamViewer may suit IT teams and MSPs needing broad platform coverage. AnyDesk may suit freelancers and small teams wanting lightweight, fast access. RemotePC may suit small businesses wanting cheap, simple remote access. See the Splashtop alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Splashtop?

Strengths: Excellent performance per dollar. Low per-user prices. Limitations: No free business tier. Confusing product lineup.

Who is Splashtop best for?

Splashtop may suit professionals and small teams wanting fast access at low cost; creative teams; IT teams on a budget. It may be less suited to users wanting a free business plan; self-hosting.

What integrations does Splashtop support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include ServiceNow, Freshservice, Zendesk, Microsoft Intune. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
