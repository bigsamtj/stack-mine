Quick answer

What is Kommo?

Kommo, formerly amoCRM, is a conversational sales CRM that brings WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and other chats into one inbox tied to visual pipelines, with Salesbot automation and AI agents, sold per user in Base, Advanced, Pro and Enterprise plans.

Overview

Kommo overview: who it is for and what it does.

Kommo is designed for businesses that sell through chat. Every conversation from WhatsApp, Instagram and other messengers lands in a unified inbox, creates or updates a lead in a visual pipeline and can be handled by people, Salesbot flows or an AI agent.

Kommo’s own pricing guide lists Base at $25, Advanced at $35 and Pro at $45 per user a month, with Enterprise on quote and a six-month minimum term (discounts on 9, 12 and 24 months). A 14-day trial includes Pro features. From 1 September 2026 Base and Advanced use regional pricing.

Kommo’s plan overview says new Base customers no longer get the AI agent from 1 June 2026; Advanced adds broadcasting, Salesbot, workflow automation, webhooks and the AI agent, and Pro adds lead scoring, ROI analytics, ad tools and a booking page. AI usage runs on plan credits with paid top-ups.

Main problem it solves

Turns chat conversations from WhatsApp, Instagram and other messengers into tracked sales pipelines with automation.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size businesses that sell through messaging, especially in Latin America, the Middle East, India and other WhatsApp-heavy markets.

Target users

  • Sales reps who close deals in WhatsApp and Instagram
  • Managers tracking pipeline and response times
  • Marketers running broadcasts and chatbots

Primary use cases

  • WhatsApp and Instagram sales
  • Lead capture from chats and ads
  • Automated follow-ups with Salesbot
  • Broadcast campaigns
  • AI replies and booking

What makes it distinct

  • Messenger inbox is the centre of the CRM, not an add-on.
  • Salesbot no-code chat automation.
  • AI agent and AI credits built into plans.

FAQ

Kommo questions and answers

What is Kommo used for?

Kommo is used for WhatsApp and Instagram sales, lead capture from chats and ads, automated follow-ups with Salesbot and broadcast campaigns.

Does Kommo have a free plan?

No. Kommo does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Yes (14 days).

How much does Kommo cost?

Base $25 per user a month (six-month minimum term). Prices are from Kommo’s own pricing guide; regional pricing for Base and Advanced started on 1 September 2026, so your price may differ.

What is the difference between Kommo Base and Advanced?

Base is listed at $25 per user a month (Inbox, pipelines, AI copilot (no AI agent for new customers)). Advanced is listed at $35 per user a month (Salesbot, broadcasts, workflows, webhooks, AI agent). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Kommo?

The main alternatives to Kommo covered on Stack18 are Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, HubSpot and Bitrix24. Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. See the Kommo alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Kommo support?

Documented integrations include WhatsApp Business API, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, Telegram and other messengers and 100+ marketplace integrations. API: REST API and webhooks (webhooks from Advanced).

What are the limitations of Kommo?

Six-month minimum term on paid plans. New Base customers do not get the AI agent. AI usage is limited by plan credits.

Which platforms does Kommo run on?

Kommo is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.
