Quick answer

What is Freshservice?

Freshservice is Freshworks' ITSM and service desk platform for IT, HR and facilities teams. It offers incident, problem, change and release management, a service catalog, asset management and automation, with Freddy AI available as an add-on or on Enterprise. Plans are Starter ($19), Growth ($49) and Pro ($99) per agent per month on annual billing, or $29, $59 and $119 monthly, plus custom Enterprise pricing and a 14-day free trial.

Overview

Freshservice overview: who it is for and what it does.

Freshservice is positioned as a modern, easier alternative to ServiceNow and a more ITIL-oriented option than customer support tools such as Freshdesk. Independent guides describe it as the ITSM tool teams buy to escape ServiceNow quotes and Jira complexity. Starter is for small teams moving from shared inboxes to a real service desk; Growth adds a service catalog, approvals, problem management, automation and asset management with 100 managed assets; Pro delivers full ITSM with change, release and project management, a CMDB, workload management and intelligent routing; and Enterprise adds the Freddy AI suite and enterprise governance.

Pricing is per agent per month and annual billing saves roughly 20 to 35 percent. The published ladder is Starter $19, Growth $49 and Pro $99 on annual billing and $29, $59 and $119 monthly. Enterprise is custom, and third-party data places it around $109 to $119 per agent per month on annual terms. HR, finance or facilities staff can use Business Agent licences at $39 per agent per month on Growth and above. A 15-agent IT team on Growth at the annual rate pays about $735 a month before add-ons.

The real cost depends on AI and automation meters. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent per month add-on on annual billing ($35 monthly), available only on Pro and Enterprise, which raises a Pro seat to about $128. Freddy AI Agent is included with Enterprise at 1,200 sessions per license per year, with overage by quote. Orchestration transactions are capped at 1,000, 2,000, 5,000 and 20,000 a month on Starter, Growth, Pro and Enterprise, with overage fees. Several of these sources come from eesel, a competing AI helpdesk vendor, so buyers should confirm add-on prices and limits with Freshworks.

Key features

Freshservice features explained.

Service desk and ITSM processes

Ticketing, SLAs and CSAT
Incident ticketing, SLA management and CSAT surveys are included from the entry plan, with a service catalog available as plans grow.
Service catalog and approvals
Growth adds a service catalog, approvals and problem management for structured service delivery.
Change and release management
Pro adds change management, release management, project management and workload management for full ITSM maturity.
Intelligent routing
Intelligent routing is listed on Pro and above to direct tickets to the right agent.

Assets and automation

Asset management and CMDB
Growth includes asset management with 100 managed assets, and Pro adds a CMDB.
Workflow automation
Workflow automation is available on Growth and above, with orchestration transactions metered by plan.
Orchestration transactions
Monthly orchestration allowances are 1,000 on Starter, 2,000 on Growth, 5,000 on Pro and 20,000 on Enterprise, with overage fees.
Business Agent licences
Business Agent licences for non-IT teams cost $39 per agent per month and are available on Growth, Pro and Enterprise.

Freddy AI

Freddy AI Copilot (add-on)
Copilot assists agents with ticket summaries and drafting for $29 per agent per month annually, on Pro and Enterprise only.
Freddy AI Agent (Enterprise)
Freddy AI Agent handles conversations autonomously and is included on Enterprise with 1,200 sessions per licence per year.
Freddy AI Insights
Predictive analytics through Freddy AI Insights is listed as part of the Enterprise AI suite.
Sandbox and audit logs
Sandbox and audit logs are listed on higher plans, though sources differ on whether they start at Pro or Enterprise.

FAQ

Freshservice questions and answers

What is Freshservice?

Freshservice is Freshworks' ITSM and service desk platform. It provides incident, problem, change and release management, a service catalog, asset management, automation and Freddy AI for IT, HR and facilities teams.

How much does Freshservice cost?

Freshservice costs $19 (Starter), $49 (Growth) and $99 (Pro) per agent per month billed annually, or $29, $59 and $119 billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Does Freshservice have a free plan or trial?

There is no free plan, but Freshservice offers a 14-day free trial.

Is Freddy AI included in Freshservice?

Freddy AI Agent is included with Enterprise, with 1,200 sessions per licence per year. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent per month add-on on Pro and Enterprise.

What is the difference between Freshservice Growth and Pro?

Pro adds change management, release management, project management, a CMDB, workload management and intelligent routing, and raises orchestration transactions from 2,000 to 5,000 a month.

Can HR and finance teams use Freshservice?

Yes. Business Agent licences are available at $39 per agent per month on Growth, Pro and Enterprise for non-IT departments.

Is Freshservice an alternative to ServiceNow?

Independent guides describe Freshservice as the ITSM tool teams buy to escape ServiceNow quotes and Jira complexity, with a simpler mid-market price ladder.

What are alternatives to Freshservice?

Common alternatives include Jira Service Management, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk and Help Scout.

What integrations does Freshservice support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Orchestration and workflow automation, Freddy AI, Business Agent licences for non-IT teams. API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Freshservice?

There is no free plan, and AI features such as Freddy Copilot are paid add-ons even at the Pro tier. Freddy Copilot adds $29 per agent per month, which raises a Pro seat to about $128. Orchestration transactions are capped by plan, with overage fees for heavy automation.

Which platforms does Freshservice run on?

Freshservice is documented as available on: Web.
