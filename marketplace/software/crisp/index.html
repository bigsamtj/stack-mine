Quick answer

What is Crisp?

Crisp is a cloud customer messaging and support platform that combines a live chat widget, shared inbox, chatbot and knowledge base. It is priced as flat monthly plans per workspace, each with a fixed number of agent seats, rather than per agent.

  • Best forStartups and small teams that want live chat and a shared inbox at a flat price
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingFree plan with 2 seats; Mini $45, Essentials $95 and Plus $295 per workspace per month, reported in dollars or euros depending on the guide. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationEach plan caps the number of agent seats, so growing teams must upgrade or buy extra seats.
  • AlternativesIntercom, Help Scout, Freshdesk, Zendesk, LiveAgent · compare all

Overview

Crisp overview: who it is for and what it does.

Crisp is an all-in-one customer communication platform aimed at startups and small support teams. Its chat widget and shared inbox bring website chat and email conversations into one place, and higher plans add an omnichannel inbox, a workflow builder, a knowledge base, an AI chatbot, analytics and, on the top plan, ticketing and white labelling.

The defining feature is the pricing model. Crisp's own pricing page says it charges a flat rate per workspace, not per agent, so adding colleagues within a plan's seat count does not raise the bill. Each plan includes a fixed number of seats: reviews report 2 on Free, 4 on Mini, 10 on Essentials and 20 or more on Plus, with extra agents available at a per-seat price reported at $10 per month.

Independent guides report the paid plans at $45 (Mini), $95 (Essentials) and $295 (Plus) per workspace per month, shown in euros by some and dollars by others. AI features are gated: reviews say Free and Mini have none, Essentials has a limited allowance and Plus has more. Every workspace is billed separately, so several brands mean several subscriptions, and Crisp offers a 14-day trial of any plan.

Main problem it solves

Gives small teams live chat, a shared inbox and basic self-service tools at a predictable flat monthly price instead of paying for each support agent.

Typical customer segment

Startups and small teams, with the higher plans aimed at growing support teams of up to around 20 agents.

Target users

  • Founders and small teams adding website chat for the first time
  • Support teams of two to ten agents that want predictable costs
  • Teams that want a chatbot and knowledge base without a per-agent bill

Primary use cases

  • Website live chat
  • Shared email and chat inbox
  • AI chatbot and knowledge base self-service
  • Proactive chat triggers and campaigns
  • Customer profiles and conversation analytics

What makes it distinct

  • Flat per-workspace pricing instead of per-agent pricing
  • A free plan with unlimited conversations and 2 seats, according to reviews
  • Plans that scale seats, profiles and AI credits together

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Crisp pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • Flat per-workspace pricing keeps costs predictable as long as the team stays within the plan's seat count. [1]
  • A free plan with unlimited conversations and two seats lets very small teams start without paying. [4]
  • Essentials is reported to bundle an omnichannel inbox, workflow builder, knowledge base and AI chatbot in one plan. [4]
  • Any plan can be tried free for 14 days, according to Crisp's pricing page. [1]

Limitations

  • Each plan caps the number of agent seats, so growing teams must upgrade or buy extra seats. [2]
  • AI features are gated to Essentials and Plus, with usage limits, so AI-heavy teams may outgrow the cheaper plans. [2]
  • Every workspace is billed separately, so companies with several brands pay for several subscriptions. [2]
  • Price figures are not visible in the pricing page's text, so amounts rely on independent guides and should be confirmed. [5]

Best for

  • Startups and small teams that want live chat and a shared inbox at a flat price
  • Support teams of two to ten agents that want predictable costs
  • Teams that want a chatbot and knowledge base without per-agent pricing

May be less suited to

  • Large support teams that need many seats on a low plan
  • Companies running many brands that would need a workspace and plan for each

FAQ

Crisp questions and answers

What is Crisp used for?

Crisp is used to add live chat to a website, manage customer conversations in a shared inbox, and add a chatbot, knowledge base and automation as teams grow.

How much does Crisp cost?

Independent guides report Mini at $45, Essentials at $95 and Plus at $295 per workspace per month, with a free plan for two seats. Some guides show the same amounts in euros. Confirm current prices with Crisp.

Does Crisp charge per agent?

No. Crisp's pricing page says it charges a flat rate per workspace, not per agent, but each plan includes a fixed number of seats and extra agents are reported at about $10 per month.

Does Crisp have a free plan?

Yes. Reviews report a free plan with two seats, unlimited conversations and 100 customer profiles. Crisp also offers a 14-day free trial of any plan.

Which Crisp plan includes AI?

Independent guides report no AI on Free and Mini, a limited AI allowance on Essentials and more on Plus, with AI credits metered by plan.

Can I use Crisp for more than one brand?

Each workspace is billed separately, so a company with several brands would need a workspace and plan for each, according to independent reviews.

What integrations does Crisp support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include WordPress chat widget, Mobile access, More than 100 integrations on Plus (reported). API details: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Crisp?

Each plan caps the number of agent seats, so growing teams must upgrade or buy extra seats. AI features are gated to Essentials and Plus, with usage limits, so AI-heavy teams may outgrow the cheaper plans. Every workspace is billed separately, so companies with several brands pay for several subscriptions.

Which platforms does Crisp run on?

Crisp is documented as available on: Web, Mobile.

What are the main alternatives to Crisp?

The main alternatives to Crisp covered on Stack18 are Intercom, Help Scout, Freshdesk, Zendesk and LiveAgent. Intercom may suit teams that want chat-first support with an AI agent. Help Scout may suit small email-first support teams. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price.
