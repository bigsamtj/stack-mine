What is AWeber?

AWeber is an email marketing platform for small businesses and creators that provides newsletters, autoresponders, landing pages and automations, with a free plan for up to 500 subscribers and paid Lite and Plus plans priced by subscriber count.

Overview

AWeber overview: who it is for and what it does.

AWeber has served small businesses since 1998 and is known for autoresponders and approachable support. It covers newsletters, drag-and-drop templates, sign-up forms, landing pages, simple automations and a basic commerce layer for selling products and subscriptions.

A 2026 pricing review lists Free for up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month, Lite at $15 a month ($12.50 billed annually) and Plus at $30 a month ($20 billed annually) at 500 subscribers. Lite limits landing pages and automations to three each and adds a 1 percent sales fee; Plus is unlimited, removes AWeber branding and lowers the fee to 0.6 percent.

At 10,000 subscribers the review reports Lite at $100 and Plus at $135 a month on monthly billing. A 14-day trial requires a card. Reviewers mention limited automation depth and dated templates compared with newer tools.

Main problem it solves

Helps small businesses and creators grow an email list and send newsletters and autoresponders without technical help.

Typical customer segment

Solo creators, coaches, bloggers and small businesses with lists of a few hundred to tens of thousands.

Target users

  • Creators and bloggers sending newsletters
  • Small business owners running autoresponders
  • Course sellers using landing pages and sales tools

Primary use cases

  • Newsletters
  • Welcome and autoresponder sequences
  • Landing pages and sign-up forms
  • Selling digital products
  • RSS-to-email

What makes it distinct

  • Long-standing reputation for deliverability and support.
  • Free plan for up to 500 subscribers.
  • Built-in landing pages and commerce with low transaction fees on Plus.

FAQ

AWeber questions and answers

What is AWeber used for?

AWeber is used for newsletters, welcome and autoresponder sequences, landing pages and sign-up forms and selling digital products.

Does AWeber have a free plan?

Yes. Yes (500 subscribers). Free trial: Yes (14 days, card required).

How much does AWeber cost?

Free for 500 subscribers; Lite from $12.50 a month billed annually. Prices shown are for 500 subscribers and rise with list size (Lite $100 and Plus $135 a month at 10,000 subscribers, monthly billing).

What is the difference between AWeber Lite and Plus?

Lite is listed at $15 a month; $12.50 billed annually (3 landing pages, 3 automations, 3 users, 1% sales fee). Plus is listed at $30 a month; $20 billed annually (Unlimited lists, pages, automations and users; no branding; 0.6% fee). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to AWeber?

The main alternatives to AWeber covered on Stack18 are Mailchimp, MailerLite, Kit (ConvertKit) and GetResponse. Mailchimp may suit small businesses that want a well-known all-round email tool. MailerLite may suit small businesses that want low-cost email with landing pages. Kit (ConvertKit) may suit creators and solo businesses building a newsletter. See the AWeber alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does AWeber support?

Documented integrations include Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress, PayPal and Stripe, Zapier and Canva (reported). API: Public REST API.

What are the limitations of AWeber?

Lite caps automations and landing pages at three each. Automation depth trails newer tools, per reviewers. Upgrades are automatic as lists grow; downgrades are manual.

Which platforms does AWeber run on?

AWeber is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.
