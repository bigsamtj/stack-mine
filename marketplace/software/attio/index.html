Quick answer

What is Attio?

Attio is a cloud CRM that lets teams model their own data with custom objects, sync email and calendar, enrich records automatically and run AI and workflow automations. It is priced per user per month on Free, Plus, Pro and Enterprise plans, with AI and workflow usage drawn from monthly credits.

  • Best forStartups and small teams that want a flexible, custom CRM data model
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingFree for up to 3 seats; Plus $35 and Pro $79 per user per month billed annually ($44 and $99 billed monthly); Enterprise is custom. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationPlus is capped at 10 seats, so larger teams move to Pro for every seat.

Overview

Attio overview: who it is for and what it does.

Attio describes itself as an AI-native CRM for startups and builders. Its core idea is a flexible data model: People and Companies sit alongside standard objects such as Deals, Users and Workspaces, and teams can add their own objects and link records through relationship attributes in what Attio calls a graph model. Records can be shown as tables or kanban boards and filtered across large datasets.

Email and calendar sync builds records and interaction history automatically, and enrichment fills in company and person attributes. On top of that data sit reports, workflows, email sequences, call recording with AI transcripts, and Ask Attio, an AI assistant. AI features and workflows draw on credits: each seat gets a personal monthly allowance and each workspace gets a shared pool, and paid plans can buy more credit packs.

Pricing is per seat per month. The Free plan covers up to three seats, Plus up to ten, and Pro and Enterprise have no seat limit. New workspaces start with a 14-day trial of Pro. Attio also publishes a developer platform with a REST API, an app SDK and a hosted MCP server, and documents imports from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Excel and CSV.

Main problem it solves

Lets teams shape a CRM around how they actually sell and operate, with synced email and calendar data, automatic enrichment and AI-assisted automation, instead of working inside a fixed schema.

Typical customer segment

Startups and growing teams, from very small teams on the Free plan to larger organisations that need unlimited objects, SSO and SCIM on Enterprise.

Target users

  • Startup founders and early sales teams
  • RevOps and operations owners who want a custom data model
  • Teams that track partners, investors or product usage as well as customers

Primary use cases

  • Sales pipeline and deal tracking
  • Custom objects for partners, investors or product usage
  • Relationship tracking from email and calendar activity
  • Pipeline reporting and funnel analysis
  • AI-assisted research, summaries and workflows

What makes it distinct

  • Custom objects and relationship attributes on a graph-style data model
  • AI and workflow usage metered in credits on top of per-seat pricing
  • A published developer platform with a REST API, app SDK and hosted MCP server
Key features

Attio features explained.

Data model and enrichment

Custom objects and attributes
Attio's data model page describes custom objects, custom attributes and associations between objects. The pricing page caps objects at 3 on Free, 5 on Plus, 12 on Pro and unlimited on Enterprise, with custom relationship and formula attributes listed from Pro.
Automatic enrichment
Company and person records are enriched automatically. Free covers attributes such as name, description, logo, location and social links; Plus adds employee count and ARR; Pro adds funding raised.
Interaction history from email and calendar sync
Synced email and calendar populate first and last interaction and connection strength. Free and Plus sync one mailbox per member, Pro two, and Enterprise three or more.
Views and record capacity
Tables and kanban views are described on the data model page. Record limits are 50,000 on Free, 250,000 on Plus and 1,000,000 on Pro, with a custom limit on Enterprise.

Automation and AI

Workflows
Workflows and AI features share workspace credits: 250 per month on Free, 1,500 on Plus and 10,000 on Pro, with extra packs available on paid plans.
Email sequences
Email sequences to automate outreach are listed from the Pro plan.
Ask Attio
An AI assistant that uses seat credits: 100 per seat per month on Free, 500 on Plus, 1,000 on Pro and 2,500 on Enterprise.
Call recording with AI transcripts
Recording calls in Attio with AI transcripts and insights is listed from the Pro plan.
Agents and web research
Attio's navigation lists Custom Agents and a Web Agent for research. Their capabilities were not tested for this profile.

Reporting, admin and developer platform

Reports and funnels
Report limits rise by plan. Attio's pricing page shows up to 3 on Free, 15 on Plus and 100 on Pro, while its help centre says 20 on Plus, so confirm the Plus limit with Attio.
Permissions and teams
Access permissions are basic on Plus and advanced on Pro. Pro supports up to 10 teams and Enterprise unlimited teams.
Security and admin
SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning are Enterprise features. Pro and Enterprise can pay by invoice.
Developer platform
Attio documents a REST API with OAuth, an OpenAPI specification, an app SDK with server functions and a hosted MCP server. Its help centre says apps and API access are available on all plans.

FAQ

Attio questions and answers

What is Attio used for?

Attio is used to manage contacts, companies, deals and other custom records in one CRM. Teams use it for sales pipelines, relationship tracking from email and calendar activity, reporting, and AI-assisted research and automation.

How much does Attio cost?

On Attio's pricing page reviewed on 20 Oct 2026, Free is $0 for up to three seats, Plus is $35 per user per month billed annually ($44 monthly), and Pro is $79 ($99 monthly). Enterprise is a custom quote billed annually. Prices can vary by region.

Does Attio have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to three seats, three objects and 50,000 records, with enrichment and email and calendar sync. New workspaces also start with a 14-day trial of the Pro plan.

How do Attio credits work?

Attio uses seat credits, which each user spends on Ask Attio, and workspace credits, which the whole workspace spends on AI features and workflows. Every plan includes an allowance and paid plans can buy more workspace credits.

Can I import data from another CRM into Attio?

Yes. Attio documents imports from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive and Zoho, as well as Excel and CSV files.

Does Attio have an API?

Yes. Attio documents a REST API with OAuth, an OpenAPI specification, an app SDK and a hosted MCP server. Attio's help centre says API access is available on all plans.

What integrations does Attio support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Zapier, Mailchimp, Segment, Outreach, Mixmax, Typeform, ChatGPT and Claude apps. API details: A REST API with OAuth, an OpenAPI specification, webhooks, an app SDK with server functions and a hosted MCP server, per Attio's developer overview. API access is available on all plans.

What are the limitations of Attio?

Plus is capped at 10 seats, so larger teams move to Pro for every seat. AI features and workflows draw on credits, so heavy use may need paid credit packs on top of seat prices. Sequences, call recording and advanced permissions need Pro, and SSO and SCIM need Enterprise.

Which platforms does Attio run on?

Attio is documented as available on: Web, macOS, iOS, Android, Chrome extension for Gmail.

What are the main alternatives to Attio?

The main alternatives to Attio covered on Stack18 are HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and Salesforce Sales Cloud. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry.
