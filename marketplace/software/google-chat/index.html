Quick answer

What is Google Chat?

Google Chat is Google’s team messaging app for direct messages and group spaces with threaded conversations, file sharing through Drive, Meet calls and Gemini AI summaries, included with Google Workspace business plans and free with personal Google accounts.

Overview

Google Chat overview: who it is for and what it does.

Google Chat replaced Hangouts as Google’s messaging tool and lives inside Gmail, a standalone web app and mobile apps. Spaces hold team conversations, files and shared tasks, and huddles or Meet calls start from a chat.

Chat is not sold on its own. G2’s 2026 comparison lists Google Workspace at $7 (Business Starter), $14 (Standard) and $22 (Plus) per user a month, with Enterprise quoted, and notes that Gemini in Chat is not available on Starter.

Its integrations are mostly native to Workspace (Drive, Calendar, Meet). Slack, by comparison, has more than 2,600 integrations, so teams that rely on many third-party bots often prefer Slack.

Main problem it solves

Gives Google Workspace teams chat, spaces and quick calls without another tool or licence.

Typical customer segment

Organisations using Google Workspace, from small teams to enterprises.

Target users

  • Managers catching up via Gemini summaries

Primary use cases

  • Quick Meet calls and huddles
  • Shared tasks in spaces

What makes it distinct

  • Included with Google Workspace at no extra cost.

Google Workspace Business Starter is listed at $7 per user a month (Chat included; no Gemini in Chat). Business Standard is listed at $14 (Gemini features). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

The main alternatives to Google Chat covered on Stack18 are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoho Cliq and Mattermost. Slack may suit teams that want real-time channel-based communication.
