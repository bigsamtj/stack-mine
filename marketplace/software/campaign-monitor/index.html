Overview

Campaign Monitor overview: who it is for and what it does.

Campaign Monitor is an email marketing platform focused on well-designed, branded emails, with a drag-and-drop builder, custom coded templates and an AI Writer. It is part of Marigold.

A June 2026 guide reports Lite at $13, Essentials at $31 and Premier at $171 a month for 500 contacts, while an October 2026 listing shows $12, $29 and $159. Lite caps sends at about 5x your contacts; Essentials and Premier have unlimited sends.

One guide states there is no permanent free plan, only a test sandbox for a few subscribers, while another lists a limited free tier. The website builder is a $10 a month add-on below Premier, and yearly billing saves about 10%.

Main problem it solves

Helps brands send polished, on-brand email campaigns with simple automation.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size brands and agencies, with an Enterprise plan for multi-brand organisations.

Target users

  • Brands that care about email design quality
  • Agencies managing campaigns for clients
  • Multi-brand organisations on Enterprise

Primary use cases

  • Designed email campaigns
  • Automated and transactional emails
  • Segmentation and send-time optimisation (Premier)

What makes it distinct

  • A design-led builder with custom coded templates.
  • Unlimited sends from Essentials.
  • Multi-brand management and deliverability monitoring on Enterprise.

FAQ

Campaign Monitor questions and answers

What is Campaign Monitor used for?

Campaign Monitor is an email marketing platform, part of Marigold, known for its email design tools and sold in Lite, Essentials, Premier and Enterprise plans priced by contact count. Typical uses include designed email campaigns, automated and transactional emails, segmentation and send-time optimisation (Premier).

How much does Campaign Monitor cost?

For 500 contacts, guides report Lite at $12 to $13, Essentials at $29 to $31 and Premier at $159 to $171 a month. Enterprise is custom.

Does Campaign Monitor have a free plan?

Sources conflict. One 2026 guide says there is no permanent free plan, only a test sandbox for a few subscribers, while another lists a limited free tier.

Who owns Campaign Monitor?

Campaign Monitor is part of Marigold.

What are the main alternatives to Campaign Monitor?

The main alternatives to Campaign Monitor covered on Stack18 are Mailchimp, Constant Contact, MailerLite and Brevo. Mailchimp may suit small businesses that want a well-known all-round email tool. Constant Contact may suit small businesses and nonprofits that want guided, all-in-one email. MailerLite may suit small businesses that want low-cost email with landing pages. See the Campaign Monitor alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Campaign Monitor?

Strengths: Design tools include custom coded templates and an AI Writer from the entry plan. Essentials and Premier include unlimited email sends. Limitations: No permanent free plan is documented consistently; one guide lists only a test sandbox. Lite caps sends at about 5x your contact count.

Who is Campaign Monitor best for?

Campaign Monitor may suit brands and agencies that prioritise polished email design; teams that want unlimited sends at a mid-range price; multi-brand organisations on Enterprise. It may be less suited to ecommerce stores that need deep product-based automation; budget users who want a free plan.
