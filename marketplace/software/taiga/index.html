Quick answer

What is Taiga?

Taiga is an open-source agile project management platform for developers, designers and project teams, built around Scrum and Kanban. It is developed by Taiga Agile LLC, which took over from Kaleidos Open Source, and is available as a hosted cloud service or self-hosted. The free plan covers up to 15 users and the Premium plan is reported at $7 per user per month ($5 billed annually), with custom Enterprise options.

  • Best forSmall agile teams that want an open-source Scrum and Kanban tool
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingFree for up to 15 users. Premium is $7 per user per month, or $5 billed annually, with unlimited users and projects. Enterprise is custom. Self-hosting the open-source edition is free. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationThe free plan limits private projects and storage to about 300 MB.
  • AlternativesJira, Linear, OpenProject, Plane, Trello · compare all

Overview

Taiga overview: who it is for and what it does.

Taiga began in 2014 when a team at Kaleidos Open Source, a company focused on free software, built its own agile project management tool, and it has remained open source with a sponsorship and hosted-service business model. Project-management.com describes a Scrum tool and template for managing backlogs and sprints on a dashboard, with Kanban to divide development into stages. Capterra lists backlog management, task prioritisation, Kanban boards, Scrum support and workflow management, and 88 reviews show 88 percent positive sentiment.

Hosted pricing is per user. Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice and Vantaige agree on a free Basic plan for up to 15 users with a limited number of private projects and about 300 MB of private storage, and a Premium plan at $7 per user per month, or $5 billed annually, with unlimited users and projects, up to 10 GB of storage per private project, an estimation tool, individual project backups and premium support. Enterprise plans for private cloud or on-premise deployments are quoted. Vantaige also reports an AI assistant that automates task breakdown. Self-hosting is free under the open-source licence but requires Python and Django expertise.

Older and alternative pricing sources add confusion. Fitgap and Toolradar describe a different earlier structure of Enthusiast (€5 a month), Basic (€20) and Premium (€60 a month, with 3 GB of storage), priced per project tier rather than per user, and Subger lists a Premium price of about €4.60. Sources also disagree on whether a free trial exists, with Software Advice reporting a 30-day trial and Fitgap saying none. Taiga suits small agile teams that want an open, focused tool with a self-hosting path, while teams needing large marketplaces, advanced reporting and enterprise governance may find the ecosystem smaller than mainstream trackers.

Main problem it solves

Gives agile teams a clean Scrum and Kanban workspace with an open-source licence so they can self-host or use a low-cost hosted plan.

FAQ

Taiga questions and answers

What is Taiga?

Taiga is an open-source agile project management platform for Scrum and Kanban teams. It is available as a hosted service or self-hosted and is developed by Taiga Agile LLC.

Is Taiga free?

Yes. The hosted Basic plan is free for up to 15 users with limited private projects and storage, and the self-hosted open-source edition is free to run on your own servers.

How much does Taiga cost?

Taiga Premium is reported at $7 per user per month, or $5 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited users and projects. Enterprise for private cloud or on-premise is custom priced.

Can I self-host Taiga?

Yes. Taiga is open source and can be deployed on your own infrastructure, though setup requires Python and Django expertise.

Does Taiga support Scrum and Kanban?

Yes. Taiga provides Scrum backlogs and sprints and Kanban boards, and listings describe support for Scrumban workflows.

Does Taiga have AI features?

Vantaige reports an AI assistant that automates task breakdown. Scope and availability by plan were not verified.

Is Taiga better than Jira?

Taiga is simpler and open source with a self-hosting option, while Jira offers a far larger ecosystem. Taiga suits small agile teams that find enterprise tools too heavy.

What are alternatives to Taiga?

Common alternatives include Jira, Linear, OpenProject, Plane and Trello.

What integrations does Taiga support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Share links with permission control, Mobile and desktop apps (reported), Open-source codebase for custom integrations. API details: API and integration details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Taiga?

The free plan limits private projects and storage to about 300 MB. Self-hosting requires Python and Django skills and administration work. The ecosystem is smaller than mainstream trackers, with fewer integrations and extensions.

Which platforms does Taiga run on?

Taiga is documented as available on: Web, Self-hosted, Mobile and desktop apps (reported by ToolQuestor).
