What is Zoho Projects?

Zoho Projects is a cloud project management tool from Zoho Corporation with task lists, Gantt charts, Kanban and calendar views, templates, time tracking and reporting. It is sold per user per month in Premium, Enterprise and Ultimate plans, with a small free plan for a few users.

Overview

Zoho Projects overview: who it is for and what it does.

Zoho Projects is a project management product from Zoho Corporation, and independent reviewers consistently describe it as an affordable option for small and mid-sized teams. Reviews and price guides list Gantt charts, Kanban and calendar views, task lists with subtasks, project templates, time tracking, blueprints for repeatable processes, issue tracking and reporting, with a roster of third-party integrations.

The plan ladder is Free, Premium, Enterprise and Ultimate. Reviewers report that Premium adds unlimited projects, templates, time tracking and blueprints, while Enterprise adds read-only users, custom fields and statuses, custom roles and profiles, single sign-on, two-factor authentication, user hierarchy and teams, and Zia Search. Storage and user limits rise by plan, and sources report a 50-user cap on Premium.

This profile is built from independent sources because Zoho's own pricing and help pages could not be reviewed. Reported prices agree across several guides (Premium about $4 and Enterprise about $9 per user per month billed annually), but the size of the free plan and the length of the trial vary between sources, so confirm the current figures with Zoho before buying.

Main problem it solves

Gives small and mid-sized teams planning, task tracking, time tracking and reporting for projects at a low per-user price, inside the wider Zoho ecosystem.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-sized teams, with higher plans aimed at larger organisations that need custom roles, single sign-on and hierarchy.

Target users

  • Small teams that need Gantt charts and time tracking at a low price
  • Teams already using other Zoho products
  • Project managers who want templates and repeatable process blueprints

Primary use cases

  • Project planning with Gantt charts
  • Task and issue tracking
  • Time tracking and timesheets
  • Reusable project templates and blueprints
  • Project reporting for clients and managers

What makes it distinct

  • A low entry price reported at about $4 per user per month billed annually
  • A free plan for a few users
  • Blueprints and templates for repeatable processes

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Zoho Projects pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • Reviewers report a very low entry price of about $4 per user per month on annual billing. [3]
  • A free plan for a few users lets small teams try Gantt charts and basic project management before paying. [4]
  • Premium is reported to include unlimited projects, project templates, time tracking and blueprints. [4]
  • Support is offered through email, live chat and toll-free phone, according to TechRepublic. [3]

Limitations

  • This profile relies on independent sources, so plan details should be confirmed with Zoho. [5]

Best for

FAQ

Zoho Projects questions and answers

What is Zoho Projects used for?

Zoho Projects is used to plan and track projects with task lists, Gantt charts, Kanban and calendar views, time tracking, templates and reports, mainly by small and mid-sized teams.

How much does Zoho Projects cost?

Independent guides report Premium at about $4 per user per month billed annually ($5 monthly) and Enterprise at about $9 ($10 monthly), with an Ultimate plan around $14 annually. Zoho's own pricing page was not reviewed, so confirm current prices with Zoho.

Does Zoho Projects have a free plan?

Yes. Sources report a small free plan, but they disagree on its limits: some say up to 3 users and 2 projects with 5 GB, and an older guide says up to 5 users and 3 projects.

Does Zoho Projects have a free trial?

Sources report a free trial of paid plans, but they give different lengths of 10, 14 and 15 days, so confirm the current length with Zoho.

What is the difference between Zoho Projects Premium and Enterprise?

Reviewers report that Enterprise adds custom fields and statuses, custom roles and profiles, read-only users, user hierarchy and teams, single sign-on, two-factor authentication and Zia Search, and removes the 50-user cap.

Does Zoho Projects support Gantt charts and time tracking?

Yes. Reviewers report Gantt charts, including on the free plan, and time tracking and blueprints on the Premium plan.

What integrations does Zoho Projects support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Third-party integrations (reported by reviewers; specific apps not verified). API details: Not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Zoho Projects?

The free plan is small, and sources disagree on its limits (3 or 5 users, 2 or 3 projects). Premium is reported to cap at 50 users, so larger teams need Enterprise. Custom roles, single sign-on, custom fields and user hierarchy are reported to need Enterprise or above.

Which platforms does Zoho Projects run on?

Zoho Projects is documented as available on: Web.

When was the Zoho Projects profile last verified?

11 sources are listed under Sources. Prices and plans can change, so confirm with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Zoho Projects?

The main alternatives to Zoho Projects covered on Stack18 are Asana, ClickUp, monday.com, Smartsheet and Teamwork.com. Asana may suit teams that want a free start and a clear upgrade path. ClickUp may suit teams that want a generous free plan to evaluate. monday.com may suit teams that want a flexible visual board-based tool.
