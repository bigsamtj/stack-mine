Overview

Smartsheet overview: who it is for and what it does.

Smartsheet is a work management platform that starts from a familiar spreadsheet grid and adds project management on top. Smartsheet's own FAQ says teams can monitor progress through Gantt, grid, board, calendar and timeline views, share updates in reports and dashboards, and use forms and automation. It positions itself as better than Excel for managing work because of automation, real-time collaboration, forms, dashboards and shared visibility across projects.

Pricing is per member. Independent guides report Pro at $9 per member per month billed annually for one to ten members, with 250 automations per month, and Business at $19 billed annually for three or more members, with free guests. Enterprise and Advanced Work Management are custom. Viewers are free, and the older free plan is described as legacy and closed to new sign-ups, with a 30-day trial offered instead.

Smartsheet lists AI features including formula generation from plain-language prompts, AI-generated charts and metrics, text and summary generation, intelligent form fill and an AI help experience. Its integrations include Jira, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud and Tableau. The company was taken private by funds managed by Blackstone and Vista Equity Partners in a deal valued at about $8.4 billion, which closed in January 2025.

Scope: This profile covers Smartsheet's core work management plans (Pro, Business, Enterprise and Advanced Work Management). Add-ons such as Bridge and other premium modules are priced separately.

Main problem it solves

Lets teams manage projects, programmes and operational work in a spreadsheet-style interface they already understand, while adding Gantt views, dashboards, forms, automation and enterprise controls.

Typical customer segment

Mid-size to large organisations and PMOs, with Pro available to individuals and small teams of up to 10 members.

Target users

  • Project managers and PMOs who think in rows, columns and Gantt timelines
  • Operations, marketing and IT teams moving work out of Excel
  • Mid-size and large organisations that need governance and integrations
  • Teams that need free viewers and guests so stakeholders can see progress

Primary use cases

  • Project and portfolio tracking with Gantt and dashboards
  • Replacing Excel for shared work tracking
  • Request intake with forms and automated approvals
  • Cross-system workflows with Jira, Salesforce and Microsoft Teams
  • Executive reporting with dashboards and reports
  • Resource and capacity planning on higher plans
  • AI-assisted formulas, charts and summaries
  • Enterprise programme management

What makes it distinct

  • A spreadsheet-first interface with Gantt, board, calendar and timeline views
  • Free viewers on plans and free guests on Business and Enterprise
  • Enterprise-oriented integrations with Jira, Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Tableau
  • Documented AI features for formulas, charts, summaries and forms

Smartsheet pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • A spreadsheet-style grid with Gantt, board, calendar and timeline views lowers the learning curve for teams coming from Excel. [1]
  • Documented AI features cover plain-language formulas, charts, summaries and intelligent form fill. [1]
  • Free viewers, and free guests on Business and Enterprise, let stakeholders follow progress without a paid seat. [4]
  • Integrations with Jira, Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Tableau suit enterprise tool stacks. [1]
  • Pro and Business have published per-member prices of $9 and $19 billed annually. [3]

Limitations

  • The free plan is legacy and closed to new sign-ups, so new teams start with a trial rather than a free tier. [4]

  • Reviewers note a steeper learning curve for enterprise features, and advanced automation such as Bridge is a separate module. [7]

FAQ

Smartsheet questions and answers

What is Smartsheet?

Smartsheet is a cloud work management platform that combines a spreadsheet-style grid with project management. Teams can use Gantt, grid, board, calendar and timeline views, forms, reports, dashboards, automation and AI features. It is sold per member in Pro, Business, Enterprise and Advanced Work Management plans.

How much does Smartsheet cost per user per month?

Independent guides report Pro at $9 per member per month billed annually ($12 monthly) and Business at $19 billed annually ($24 monthly). Enterprise and Advanced Work Management are custom-quoted. Viewers are free, and guests are free on Business and Enterprise.

Does Smartsheet have a free plan?

Not for new sign-ups. Guides describe the old free plan as legacy and closed to new customers, with a 30-day trial offered instead. Existing free users were grandfathered. Teams that need a permanent free tier often look at other project tools.

What is the difference between Smartsheet Pro and Business?

Pro is for one to ten members and includes 250 automations per month and free viewers. Business is for three or more members and adds free guests and more advanced tracking, collaboration and governance features. Enterprise and Advanced Work Management add enterprise controls at custom prices.

Is Smartsheet better than Excel?

Smartsheet says it is better than Excel for managing work because it adds automation, real-time collaboration, forms, dashboards and shared visibility across projects. Excel remains stronger for complex calculations and analysis, so many teams use both.

Does Smartsheet have AI features?

Yes. Smartsheet lists plain-language formula generation, AI-generated charts and metrics, text and summary generation, intelligent form fill, AI-guided setup for new trial users and an AI help experience. Which AI features come with which plan was not confirmed, so check the current plan table.

What does Smartsheet integrate with?

Smartsheet lists integrations with Jira, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud and Tableau, and independent sources add Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Advanced cross-system automation is handled by a separate module called Bridge.

Who owns Smartsheet?

Smartsheet is owned by funds managed by Blackstone and Vista Equity Partners. They completed the acquisition in a transaction valued at about $8.4 billion, which Smartsheet announced on 21 January 2025, taking the company private.

How much would Smartsheet cost for 20 users?

At reported annual prices, 20 members on Business cost $380 per month ($4,560 a year). Pro is limited to 10 members, so 10 members on Pro cost $90 per month ($1,080 a year). Viewers and guests are free, and Bridge and other add-ons are extra.

What are the best alternatives to Smartsheet?

Asana, monday.com, ClickUp and Jira are the main alternatives in this directory. Asana and monday.com are easier for general teams, ClickUp offers a free tier, and Jira suits software teams. See the linked profiles for side-by-side summaries.

What integrations does Smartsheet support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Jira, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud, Tableau. API details: API details were not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Smartsheet?

The free plan is legacy and closed to new sign-ups, so new teams start with a trial rather than a free tier. Pro is limited to one to ten members and Business requires at least three, which constrains plan choice for very small or growing teams. Enterprise and Advanced Work Management are custom-quoted, so enterprise costs are not published.

Which platforms does Smartsheet run on?
