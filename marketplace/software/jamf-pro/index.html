Overview

Jamf Pro overview: who it is for and what it does.

Jamf Pro manages Macs and Apple mobile devices deeply, with same-day support for new Apple OS releases.

Two August 2026 guides list Jamf for Mac at $12.50 per device a month and Jamf for Mobile at $5.75 (annual, 25-device minimum), Jamf Now from $4 per device and free for 3 devices at organisations under 25 employees, with Pro bundles quote-only and Vendr observing $8 to $20+ per device.

They report Francisco Partners took Jamf private on 30 January 2026 for about $2.2 billion.

FAQ

Jamf Pro questions and answers

What is Jamf Pro used for?

Jamf Pro is an Apple enterprise management platform for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, with deep configuration, app deployment and security, alongside Jamf Now for small businesses. Typical uses include mac management, iOS management, app deployment, zero-touch setup.

How much does Jamf cost?

Jamf for Mac is $12.50 per device a month and Jamf for Mobile $5.75; Jamf Now starts at $4; Pro bundles are quoted.

Who owns Jamf?

Francisco Partners, which took it private on 30 January 2026.

Is there a free Jamf plan?

Jamf Now is free for up to 3 devices at organisations under 25 employees.

How does Jamf Pro compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Jamf Pro vs Iru (formerly Kandji) and Jamf Pro vs Mosyle, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Jamf Pro?

The main alternatives to Jamf Pro covered on Stack18 are Iru (formerly Kandji), Mosyle, Addigy and Microsoft Intune. Iru (formerly Kandji) may suit Apple-first teams wanting MDM plus security in one console. Mosyle may suit Apple fleets wanting the lowest published price. Addigy may suit MSPs managing Macs for many clients. See the Jamf Pro alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Jamf Pro?

Strengths: Deepest Apple management. Same-day support for new OS releases. Limitations: Pro bundles quote-only. Apple only.

Who is Jamf Pro best for?

Jamf Pro may suit organisations wanting the deepest Apple management; education and enterprise Mac fleets; security-focused Apple shops. It may be less suited to Windows-heavy fleets; tiny budgets at scale.

What integrations does Jamf Pro support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Apple Business, ServiceNow. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
