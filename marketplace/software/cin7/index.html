Overview

Cin7 overview: who it is for and what it does.

Cin7 Core manages stock, purchasing, light manufacturing and sales across channels; Cin7 Omni adds retail and EDI capabilities.

An August 2026 guide lists Cin7 Core Standard at $349 a month (5 users, 6,000 sales orders a year), Pro $599 (24,000 orders) and Advanced $1,199 (120,000 orders), with billing cycle not stated, and Omni by quote.

It notes Core was formerly DEAR Systems, Cin7 Orderhive was discontinued on 31 March 2024, and that annual order caps and no full MRP are limitations.

FAQ

Cin7 questions and answers

What is Cin7 used for?

Cin7 offers Cin7 Core (formerly DEAR Systems) and Cin7 Omni, inventory and order management platforms for product brands selling wholesale, retail and online. Typical uses include inventory, b2B and B2C orders, purchasing, light manufacturing.

How much does Cin7 cost?

Cin7 Core Standard is $349, Pro $599 and Advanced $1,199 a month; Omni is quoted.

Is Cin7 Core the same as DEAR?

Yes, Cin7 Core was formerly DEAR Systems.

What happened to Cin7 Orderhive?

It was discontinued on 31 March 2024.

How does Cin7 compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Cin7 vs Unleashed, Linnworks vs Cin7 and Zoho Inventory vs Cin7, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Cin7?

The main alternatives to Cin7 covered on Stack18 are Unleashed, Zoho Inventory, Linnworks and Fishbowl. Unleashed may suit distributors and importers needing landed costs and batch control. Zoho Inventory may suit small and mid-size sellers, especially Zoho customers. Linnworks may suit high-volume multichannel retailers. See the Cin7 alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Cin7?

Strengths: Multichannel and B2B in one. Published Core prices. Limitations: Annual order caps can run out in peak quarters. No full MRP.

Who is Cin7 best for?

Cin7 may suit growing wholesalers and multichannel brands; brands selling B2B and B2C; retailers needing EDI (Omni). It may be less suited to very small sellers; complex manufacturers.

What integrations does Cin7 support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Shopify, Amazon, Xero, QuickBooks. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
