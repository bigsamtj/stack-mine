Quick answer

What is Fathom AI Notetaker?

Fathom is an AI notetaker that records, transcribes and summarises Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, with a free plan, Premium for individuals, Team for shared workspaces and Business with CRM field sync and coaching.

Overview

Fathom AI Notetaker overview: who it is for and what it does.

Fathom became popular for its generous free plan: unlimited recording, transcription and storage. Paid plans add unlimited advanced AI summaries, team features and CRM sync.

A competitor’s 2026 guide lists Premium at $20 monthly or $16 yearly, Team at $19 or $15 per user with a two-seat minimum, Business at $34 or $25 with Salesforce and HubSpot field sync, coaching metrics and AI scorecards, and Enterprise with SSO, SCIM and a HIPAA BAA. It reports that free advanced AI summaries are capped at five calls a month.

Fathom is not to be confused with Fathom Analytics, a separate web analytics product.

Main problem it solves

Lets people focus on calls while AI captures notes and follow-ups.
