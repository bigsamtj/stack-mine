What is Snowflake?

Snowflake is a cloud data warehouse and data platform that separates storage and compute, with virtual warehouses billed per credit and features for data sharing, AI and apps.

Overview

Snowflake overview: who it is for and what it does.

Snowflake runs SQL analytics on separated storage and compute, with data sharing, governance and AI features.

A vendor comparison (August 2026) lists credits at $2, $3 and $4 on Standard, Enterprise and Business Critical, an XS warehouse at $2 an hour on Standard and storage at about $23 to $40 per TB-month, with an April 2026 "AI Credits" change; CostBench lists a 30-day trial with $400 in credits.

The comparison cites a Vendr median contract near $100,000 a year and says a 60-second minimum per resume suits spiky workloads poorly.

FAQ

Snowflake questions and answers

What is Snowflake used for?

Snowflake is a cloud data warehouse and data platform that separates storage and compute, with virtual warehouses billed per credit and features for data sharing, AI and apps. Typical uses include batch analytics, data sharing, eLT, aI features.

How much does Snowflake cost?

Credits list at $2, $3 and $4 by edition; an XS warehouse is about $2 an hour on Standard, plus storage.

Does Snowflake have a free trial?

Yes, 30 days with $400 in credits, per CostBench.

What is the Snowflake minimum billing?

60 seconds each time a warehouse resumes.

How does Snowflake compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Snowflake vs Amazon Redshift, Snowflake vs Databricks and Snowflake vs Google BigQuery, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Snowflake?

The main alternatives to Snowflake covered on Stack18 are Databricks, Google BigQuery, Amazon Redshift and Microsoft Fabric. Databricks may suit teams combining BI, data engineering and ML on one copy of data. Google BigQuery may suit Google Cloud teams wanting serverless analytics. Amazon Redshift may suit AWS-centric teams needing a warehouse close to S3. See the Snowflake alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Snowflake?

Strengths: Strong for large enterprise batch ETL. 30-day trial with $400 credits. Limitations: 60-second minimum per resume. Poor fit for spiky dashboards.

Who is Snowflake best for?

Snowflake may suit enterprises running large batch analytics and data sharing; regulated industries (Business Critical); data marketplaces. It may be less suited to tiny datasets; spiky per-user apps.

What integrations does Snowflake support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include dbt, Fivetran, Tableau, Power BI. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
