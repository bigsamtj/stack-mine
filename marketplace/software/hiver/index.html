Quick answer

What is Hiver?

Hiver is a customer service platform from Hiver Inc. that turns shared addresses such as support@ into a team queue, either inside Gmail as a Chrome extension (Hiver in Gmail) or as the standalone Hiver Omni workspace for email, chat, Slack, voice, WhatsApp and social channels. Plans are Growth ($25), Pro ($55) and Elite ($85) per user per month on annual billing, with a 7-day free trial and AI agents and Copilot included.

  • Best forTeams on Gmail or Google Workspace that want shared inbox and ticketing without leaving email
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingNo free plan on the live pricing page. Growth $25, Pro $55, Elite $85 per user per month billed annually; $35, $65 and $95 billed monthly. 7-day free trial of Elite, no card needed.
  • Main limitationThere is no free plan on the live pricing page, and seats are sold in blocks starting at two.

Overview

Hiver overview: who it is for and what it does.

Hiver began as GrexIt in 2011 and built its reputation on letting teams run support, operations and finance mailboxes from inside Gmail without switching tools. Today the company describes itself as an agentic omnichannel customer service platform used by more than 10,000 teams, with customers such as Flexport, Upwork, Bynder and Egnyte. Hiver in Gmail suits teams whose support is mostly email and who live in Gmail or Google Workspace. Hiver Omni is the standalone platform that works with Gmail, Outlook and other email providers and adds live chat, Slack, voice, WhatsApp and social channels in one workspace.

Hiver's pricing page lists three paid plans that include AI. Growth covers shared inboxes, ticketing, a help center, rule-based automations, pre-built analytics and AI agents and Copilot. Pro adds a customer portal, SLAs, CSAT surveys, advanced analytics, premium integrations such as Asana, Shopify and CRMs, and API access. Elite adds AI QA, AI insights, skill-based routing, custom objects, personal-to-shared inbox rules, SSO, HIPAA-compliant handling and custom roles. Hiver says it is GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, CCPA and HIPAA compliant.

Buyers should note the packaging rules. All plans start at two seats and grow to five and then in blocks of five, Growth is capped at 10 users and 2 shared inboxes, and plan limits on automations, custom fields and analytics retention rise by tier. The price is the same for Hiver in Gmail and Hiver Omni. Because Hiver's pricing has changed over time, with older guides listing Lite, Pro and Elite at $19, $49 and $79 and some snapshots of Hiver's site showing a free tier, always check the live pricing page and confirm whether a free plan exists before relying on older articles.

Main problem it solves

Gives support, success, operations and finance teams a shared, trackable inbox with ownership, SLAs and AI assistance, without leaving Gmail or adopting a heavyweight ticketing suite.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size teams on Gmail or Google Workspace that handle customer email and want shared inbox and ticketing capabilities, plus teams that need omnichannel support through Hiver Omni.

Target users

  • Customer support teams that work from a support@ mailbox
  • Customer success, finance, HR and operations teams managing shared mailboxes
  • Teams that already use Gmail or Google Workspace and want to avoid a separate help desk
  • Support managers who need SLAs, CSAT and analytics on email conversations

Primary use cases

  • Shared inbox and ticketing for support@ and info@
  • Email routing, assignment and collision detection
  • AI-drafted replies and AI agents for common requests
  • SLA and CSAT tracking
  • Help center and customer portal
  • Omnichannel support across chat, Slack, voice and WhatsApp

What makes it distinct

  • Works inside Gmail as a Chrome extension, with a standalone Omni option
  • AI agents, Copilot and AI knowledge tools included in every paid plan
  • Same per-user price for Hiver in Gmail and Hiver Omni
  • Compliance coverage that includes SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, CCPA and HIPAA
Key features

Hiver features explained.

Shared inbox, ticketing and collaboration

Shared inbox and ticketing
Team inboxes such as support@ track every request with clear ownership, tags, custom views, templates and full history.
Collaboration tools
Private notes with @mentions, collaborators outside the inbox, shared drafts, linked conversations, collision detection and Slack discussions keep teams aligned.
Routing and assignment
Routing and assignments are on all plans, with round-robin, load balancing, SLA and CSAT-based automations, and skill-based routing on Elite.
Mobile apps
Hiver mobile apps for Android and iOS manage email and chat conversations on the go.

AI and knowledge

AI agents
AI agents triage and resolve requests, look up orders and update records across connected systems, pausing for human approval on actions marked sensitive.
AI Copilot
Copilot drafts replies, summarises long threads, polishes tone, answers questions from internal docs and suggests next steps.
Help center and knowledge builder
A public or internal help center, AI-powered search and an AI knowledge builder that recommends content updates are included.
AI QA and insights (Elite)
Elite adds AI QA scoring of agent replies, AI topics that organise conversations by theme, and customer intelligence on sentiment and account health, which Hiver lists as coming soon.

Analytics, integrations and security

SLAs, CSAT and analytics
Pro adds SLAs, CSAT surveys, a customer portal, custom reports and dashboards, while analytics retention is 12, 18 and 24 months on Growth, Pro and Elite.
Integrations and API
Integrations include Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, Shopify, WooCommerce, QuickBooks, Jira, Asana, Trello, ClickUp, monday.com, NetSuite, Zapier and Okta, with API access from Pro.
Channels in Hiver Omni
Hiver Omni brings email, live chat, Slack, voice, WhatsApp and social channels into one workspace.
Security and compliance
Hiver states compliance with GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, CCPA and HIPAA, with SSO and custom roles on Elite.

FAQ

Hiver questions and answers

What is Hiver used for?

Hiver is used to manage shared inboxes and customer conversations as a team, with ownership, tags, SLAs, analytics and AI assistance. It runs inside Gmail as a Chrome extension (Hiver in Gmail) or as the standalone Hiver Omni workspace.

How much does Hiver cost?

Hiver's pricing page lists Growth at $25, Pro at $55 and Elite at $85 per user per month billed annually, or $35, $65 and $95 billed monthly. Seats start at 2 and then increase in blocks.

Does Hiver have a free plan or free trial?

Hiver offers a 7-day free trial of the Elite plan with no credit card needed. The current pricing page lists only paid Growth, Pro and Elite plans, although older snapshots of Hiver pricing showed a free tier, so confirm before signing up.

What is the difference between Hiver in Gmail and Hiver Omni?

Hiver in Gmail lives inside your Gmail account as a Chrome extension and suits email-heavy teams. Hiver Omni is a standalone platform that works with Gmail, Outlook and other providers and adds live chat, Slack, voice, WhatsApp and social channels.

Does Hiver have AI features?

Yes. All paid plans include AI agents and AI Copilot, and Elite adds AI QA and AI insights. Copilot drafts replies, summarises threads and answers questions from your knowledge sources.

Is Hiver secure and compliant?

Hiver states that it is GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, CCPA and HIPAA compliant, with SSO and custom roles available on the Elite plan.

Can I use Hiver without Gmail?

Yes, through Hiver Omni, which works independently of any email provider and with Gmail, Outlook and other email accounts. Hiver in Gmail requires a Gmail or Google Workspace account.

What are alternatives to Hiver?

Common alternatives include Front, Help Scout, Freshdesk, Zendesk and Intercom. Front and Help Scout are closest for shared-inbox support.

What integrations does Hiver support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Slack, Salesforce and HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive and Copper, Shopify and WooCommerce, Jira, Asana, Trello, ClickUp and monday.com, QuickBooks and NetSuite, Zapier and Okta, Notion, Confluence and Google Drive for AI knowledge. API details: API access is included from the Pro plan, and Hiver publishes developer documentation.

What are the limitations of Hiver?

There is no free plan on the live pricing page, and seats are sold in blocks starting at two. Growth is limited to 2 shared inboxes and 10 users, with key features such as SLAs, CSAT and API access gated to Pro or Elite. Hiver in Gmail depends on Gmail or Google Workspace and a Chrome extension.

Which platforms does Hiver run on?

Hiver is documented as available on: Gmail Chrome extension, Hiver Omni web workspace, iOS app, Android app.

The main alternatives to Hiver covered on Stack18 are Front, Help Scout, Freshdesk, Zendesk and Intercom. Front may suit teams that run customer communication from shared inboxes. Help Scout may suit small email-first support teams. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price.
