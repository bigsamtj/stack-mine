Quick answer

What is Chatwoot?

Chatwoot is an open-source customer support platform from Chatwoot Inc. that brings live chat, email, social and messaging conversations into a shared inbox. It runs as a managed cloud service with a free Hacker plan and paid Startups ($19), Business ($39) and Enterprise ($99) plans per agent per month, or as a free self-hosted Community Edition. Captain AI adds an assistant, copilot and reply suggestions using monthly credits.

  • Best forStartups and engineering-led teams that want an open-source support platform
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingFree Hacker plan (2 agents). Cloud paid plans per agent per month: Startups $19, Business $39, Enterprise $99. Self-hosted: Community Edition free, Premium Support $19, Enterprise $99. 15-day free trial. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationThe free Hacker plan is limited to live chat, 2 agents, 500 conversations a month and 30-day retention.
  • AlternativesIntercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Crisp, LiveChat · compare all

Overview

Chatwoot overview: who it is for and what it does.

Chatwoot positions itself as the modern, open-source, self-hosted alternative to Intercom and Zendesk, and says more than 15,000 organisations use it to run support. Conversations from website live chat and other channels land in one inbox where agents collaborate with private notes, labels, teams and automation. It is backed by Y Combinator and is also a Meta Business Partner, and its mobile apps let agents reply from phones. The open-source model is a real differentiator: teams can inspect, deploy and extend the code, and keep customer data on their own infrastructure.

Chatwoot offers two commercial routes. The cloud service runs on AWS in the United States and is priced per agent per month: Hacker is free for up to two agents with live chat only, 500 conversations a month and 30 days of data retention, while Startups, Business and Enterprise add all channels, a help center and Captain AI credits (300, 500 and 800 a month) with 1, 2 and 3 years of data retention. Business adds voice, teams and automation rules, and Enterprise adds SSO/SAML and audit logs. The self-hosted route is the free Community Edition, Premium Support at $19 per agent per month and Enterprise Edition at $99.

Free does not mean zero cost. Self-hosting needs a Linux server with PostgreSQL and Redis, with at least 4 GB of RAM and 2 CPU cores recommended for production, plus upgrades, backups and security work. Captain AI is not part of the Community Edition, runs on credits that cost $20 per 1,000 beyond the monthly allowance, and 'unlimited' conversations on paid cloud plans are subject to a fair use policy. Teams with strong engineering skills and data-control requirements will find it compelling, while teams that want a fully managed help desk with deep ticketing and reporting may prefer larger vendors.

Main problem it solves

Gives support teams a single inbox for live chat and messaging channels with AI assistance, without being locked into a closed SaaS, and lets technical teams self-host to control their data.

Typical customer segment

Startups, SMBs and engineering-led companies that want an open-source or self-hosted support platform, plus teams migrating from Intercom or Zendesk to reduce cost.

Key features

Chatwoot features explained.

Inbox, channels and collaboration

Omnichannel shared inbox
Paid cloud plans include all channels in one inbox, while the free Hacker plan includes live chat only.
Teams and automation rules
Teams and automation rules are included from the Business plan, allowing conversations to be grouped, routed and acted on automatically.
Help center
A knowledge-base help center is included from the Startups plan and not on Hacker.
Voice channel
Voice is included on Business and Enterprise cloud plans, and is listed as coming soon for self-hosted editions.

Captain AI

Assistant and copilot
Captain includes a chatbot that answers customer questions around the clock and a copilot that suggests what agents should say.
Reply suggestions and summaries
One-click draft replies and conversation summaries speed up handling of long threads.
Triage, scenarios and memories
Captain can set priority, labels and team on new conversations, follow written scenarios and keep notes about each customer.
Content gap detection
Content gap analysis finds questions that the help center does not yet answer.

Deployment, security and administration

Self-hosted Community Edition
The free Community Edition runs on your own Linux server with PostgreSQL and Redis, with Docker, Kubernetes and cloud guides.
SSO/SAML and audit logs
SSO/SAML and audit logs are included on Enterprise cloud and SSO/SAML plus SLA policies on Enterprise Edition self-hosted.
Data retention by plan
Cloud plans keep data for 30 days on Hacker, 1 year on Startups, 2 years on Business and 3 years on Enterprise.
API and data export
The API lets teams export conversations, contacts and other data, and there is a Chatwoot CLI and API documentation for developers.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

Chatwoot pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • A free cloud plan for two agents and a free open-source Community Edition lower the cost of getting started. [1]
  • Self-hosting keeps customer data on your own infrastructure, with deployment guides for Docker, Kubernetes and major clouds. [2]
  • Captain AI is included in per-agent pricing with monthly credits rather than charged per resolution. [1]
  • Paid cloud plans include all channels and a help center with long data retention. [1]
  • Cloud plans are refundable based on unused time, and open-source projects can get the Startups plan free. [1]

Limitations

  • The free Hacker plan is limited to live chat, 2 agents, 500 conversations a month and 30-day retention. [1]
  • The Community Edition excludes Captain AI, voice, custom branding, roles and permissions, SSO and SLA policies. [2]
  • Self-hosting requires your own server, PostgreSQL, Redis, upgrades and security work, so total cost exceeds the licence fee. [3]
  • Captain AI runs on credits, with extra credits at $20 per 1,000, which can make high-volume deflection cost unpredictable. [3]
  • Cloud data is hosted in the United States, which may not meet every residency requirement. [1]

FAQ

Chatwoot questions and answers

What is Chatwoot?

Chatwoot is an open-source customer support platform that combines live chat, a shared inbox, a help center and Captain AI. You can use it as a managed cloud service or self-host it on your own servers.

Is Chatwoot free?

Yes, in two ways. The cloud Hacker plan is free for up to two agents with live chat only and 500 conversations a month, and the self-hosted Community Edition is open source and free forever, though you pay for your own infrastructure.

How much does Chatwoot cost?

On Chatwoot Cloud, Startups costs $19, Business $39 and Enterprise $99 per agent per month. Self-hosted Premium Support is $19 and Enterprise Edition is $99 per agent per month, while the Community Edition is free.

Does Chatwoot have AI features?

Yes. Captain AI includes an assistant, copilot, reply suggestions, summaries, triage and content gap detection. Paid cloud plans include 300, 500 or 800 monthly credits, and extra credits cost $20 per 1,000. Captain is not included in the free Community Edition.

Can I self-host Chatwoot?

Yes. You need a Linux server with PostgreSQL and Redis, S3-compatible storage for uploads, and at least 4 GB of RAM and 2 CPU cores for production. Chatwoot provides guides for Docker, Kubernetes and most cloud providers.

Does Chatwoot offer a free trial?

Yes. Chatwoot offers a 15-day free trial of its cloud service in addition to the free Hacker plan.

Where is Chatwoot Cloud hosted?

Chatwoot Cloud runs on AWS with servers in the United States. Customers with specific data residency or compliance needs, such as GDPR or SOC 2 questions, are asked to contact Chatwoot.

What are alternatives to Chatwoot?

Common alternatives include Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Crisp and LiveChat. Chatwoot stands out as an open-source option that can be self-hosted.

What integrations does Chatwoot support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Website live chat, Email and messaging channels (all channels on paid plans), Meta Business Partner platform, Help center, API and Chatwoot CLI, Mobile apps. API details: Chatwoot publishes API documentation for exporting conversations, contacts and other data, and provides a CLI for agents and automation.

What are the limitations of Chatwoot?

The free Hacker plan is limited to live chat, 2 agents, 500 conversations a month and 30-day retention. The Community Edition excludes Captain AI, voice, custom branding, roles and permissions, SSO and SLA policies. Self-hosting requires your own server, PostgreSQL, Redis, upgrades and security work, so total cost exceeds the licence fee.

Which platforms does Chatwoot run on?

Chatwoot is documented as available on: Web, Mobile apps, Self-hosted (Docker, Kubernetes, major clouds), Cloud (AWS, United States).

What are the main alternatives to Chatwoot?

The main alternatives to Chatwoot covered on Stack18 are Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Crisp and LiveChat. Intercom may suit teams that want chat-first support with an AI agent. Zendesk may suit support teams that need ticketing plus omnichannel messaging. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price.
