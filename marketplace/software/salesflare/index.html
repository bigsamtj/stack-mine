Quick answer

What is Salesflare?

Salesflare is an automated CRM for small B2B sales teams, built by Salesflare BV in Antwerp, Belgium and launched in 2014. It captures contact and company data automatically from email, calendar and social profiles. Plans are Growth ($29), Pro ($49) and Enterprise ($99, five-user minimum) per user per month on annual billing, or $39, $64 and $124 monthly, with a 30-day free trial and no free plan.

Overview

Salesflare overview: who it is for and what it does.

Salesflare's core idea is that sales reps should not type data into a CRM. It reads email, calendar and signature data to create and update contacts, companies and interaction history automatically, then adds email, link and website tracking, a Gmail and Outlook sidebar with LinkedIn information, a drag-and-drop sales pipeline and mobile apps with a business card scanner. Hackceleration's 2026 review calls the zero-touch data entry the standout feature for small B2B teams and notes most users are working inside it the same afternoon.

Pricing is per user per month. Growth ($29 annual, $39 monthly) covers automatic CRM data capture, tracking, sidebars, personalised email campaigns and 5 lead credits a month. Pro ($49 annual, $64 monthly) adds multi-step email workflows, user permission management, custom dashboards and reports and 100 lead credits. Enterprise ($99 annual, $124 monthly) adds guided setup and training, data migration by Salesflare, a dedicated account manager and 250 lead credits, with a five-user minimum. Extra lead credits are sold in team-wide packages from 250 credits at $49 a month to 1,000 credits at $139 a month, and independent sources say contact records are not capped.

Salesflare is strongest for small B2B teams that live in Gmail or Outlook, and weaker for teams that need heavy reporting, built-in calling or large-scale lead databases. Reviewers note that some features sit behind higher tiers, that reporting and dashboard customisation are limited for enterprise needs, that built-in contact finding could be stronger, that there is no universal search field and that email aliases cost extra per seat. There is no free plan, only a 30-day trial. Sources disagree on whether lead credits are pooled per team or granted per seat, so confirm that with Salesflare before sizing a plan.

  • Automated data entry from email and calendar is described as saving real time and as a daily-routine change. [3]

FAQ

Salesflare questions and answers

What is Salesflare?

Salesflare is an automated CRM for small B2B sales teams. It captures contacts, companies and interactions automatically from email and calendar and adds tracking, a pipeline, campaigns and a Gmail and Outlook sidebar.

How much does Salesflare cost?

Salesflare costs $29 (Growth), $49 (Pro) and $99 (Enterprise) per user per month on annual billing, or $39, $64 and $124 on monthly billing. Enterprise requires at least five users.

Does Salesflare have a free plan or trial?

There is no free plan. Salesflare offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required.

What are lead credits in Salesflare?

Lead credits fund contact and company lookups with the lead finder. Growth includes 5 a month, Pro 100 and Enterprise 250, and extra packs of 250, 500 or 1,000 credits cost $49, $79 and $139 a month.

What is the difference between Salesflare Growth and Pro?

Pro adds multi-step email workflows, user permission management, custom dashboards and reports and 100 lead credits a month, compared with 5 on Growth.

Does Salesflare work with Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. Salesflare provides Gmail and Outlook sidebars with CRM data and LinkedIn information, and captures data from email and calendar.

Where is Salesflare based?

Salesflare is operated by Salesflare BV in Antwerp, Belgium, and launched in 2014.

What are alternatives to Salesflare?

Common alternatives include Pipedrive, Close, Copper, HubSpot and Nutshell.

What integrations does Salesflare support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Gmail and Outlook, Google and Microsoft calendars, LinkedIn, Mobile apps with business card scanner. API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Salesflare?

There is no free plan, only a 30-day trial. Reporting and dashboard customisation are limited for enterprise needs. Lead credits are small on Growth, with 5 a month, and extra credit packs add cost.

Which platforms does Salesflare run on?
