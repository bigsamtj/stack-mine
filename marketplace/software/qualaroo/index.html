Overview

Qualaroo overview: who it is for and what it does.

Qualaroo shows targeted nudge surveys based on page, behaviour or user attributes.

CostBench (checked October 2026) lists Free, Essentials at $19.99 a month billed annually ($39.99 monthly), Business at $49.99 annually ($99.99 monthly) and quote-based Enterprise.

It reports a 15-day money-back guarantee, contracts that do not auto-renew, and a Business pageview cap of 100,000 a month.

FAQ

Qualaroo questions and answers

What is Qualaroo used for?

Qualaroo runs short "nudge" surveys on websites and in products, targeted by behaviour, to capture visitor and user feedback. Typical uses include website surveys, in-app surveys, exit-intent feedback.

How much does Qualaroo cost?

Essentials is $19.99 and Business $49.99 a month billed annually, per CostBench (October 2026).

Is Qualaroo free?

Yes, there is a free plan.

Does Qualaroo auto-renew?

CostBench reports contracts do not auto-renew.

How does Qualaroo compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Survicate vs Qualaroo, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Qualaroo?

The main alternatives to Qualaroo covered on Stack18 are Survicate, UserVoice and Retently. Survicate may suit product and marketing teams running in-app and website surveys. UserVoice may suit B2B SaaS companies centralising feature requests at scale. Retently may suit SMBs running relationship and transactional NPS. See the Qualaroo alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Qualaroo?

Strengths: Free plan. Low annual pricing. Limitations: Branding removal costs extra. Pageview caps.

Who is Qualaroo best for?

Qualaroo may suit teams collecting on-site and in-app visitor feedback; UX research; conversion teams. It may be less suited to review collection; enterprise CXM.

What integrations does Qualaroo support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Analytics, Slack. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
