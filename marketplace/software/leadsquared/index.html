Quick answer

What is LeadSquared?

LeadSquared is a sales execution CRM and marketing automation platform from India built for high-volume lead capture, distribution and follow-up, used widely in education, healthcare, BFSI and field sales, with Sales CRM priced per user and Marketing Automation priced per account.

Overview

LeadSquared overview: who it is for and what it does.

LeadSquared focuses on businesses that receive thousands of leads and need to route them quickly to inside-sales or field teams. Lead capture from ads and forms, scoring, auto-distribution, tasks and a mobile CRM with offline mode are its core.

A 2026 pricing review reports Sales Pro at INR 2,500 (about $50) and Sales Super at INR 5,000 (about $100) per user a month billed annually, with Ultimate on quote. Marketing Automation Pro is reported at INR 90,000 a year and Super at INR 1,49,000 a year, each with two users. Many tiers show “Let’s talk” on the official page, and add-ons such as chatbots and landing pages are priced separately.

Reviewers rate lead management highly. The most repeated complaint is slow, laggy performance, followed by inconsistent support and limited customisation in marketing automation.

Main problem it solves

Helps high-volume B2C sales teams capture, score, route and follow up leads fast, in the office and in the field.

Typical customer segment

Mid-size and large B2C sales organisations in India and emerging markets: edtech, universities, hospitals, lenders and insurers.

Target users

  • Inside-sales and call-centre teams
  • Field sales agents using the mobile CRM
  • Marketing teams running nurture campaigns

Primary use cases

  • Lead capture from ads, forms and calls
  • Lead scoring and auto-distribution
  • Field force management with offline mobile
  • Admissions and patient enquiry management
  • Email and SMS nurture campaigns

What makes it distinct

  • Built for lead volume and speed-to-lead rather than complex B2B deals.
  • Industry editions for education, healthcare and BFSI.
  • Mobile CRM with offline mode and check-ins for field teams.

FAQ

LeadSquared questions and answers

What is LeadSquared used for?

LeadSquared is used for lead capture from ads, forms and calls, lead scoring and auto-distribution, field force management with offline mobile and admissions and patient enquiry management.

Does LeadSquared have a free plan?

No. LeadSquared does not offer a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free trial: Demo.

How much does LeadSquared cost?

Sales Pro reported at INR 2,500 (about $50) per user a month billed annually. Figures come from a 2026 third-party pricing review; LeadSquared shows “Let’s talk” on several tiers.

What is the difference between LeadSquared Sales Pro and Sales Super?

Sales Pro is listed at INR 2,500 (about $50) per user a month (Reported; core lead management). Sales Super is listed at INR 5,000 (about $100) per user a month (Reported; advanced automation and reporting). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to LeadSquared?

The main alternatives to LeadSquared covered on Stack18 are Zoho CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud, Freshsales and HubSpot. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. Salesforce Sales Cloud may suit organisations that expect to customise their sales process extensively. Freshsales may suit startups and small teams that want an affordable CRM with a dialer. See the LeadSquared alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does LeadSquared support?

Documented integrations include Facebook and Google lead ads, Telephony and IVR providers, WhatsApp and SMS gateways and Payment and portal connectors. API: REST APIs available for lead and activity data.

What are the limitations of LeadSquared?

Slow or laggy performance is the most repeated complaint. Several tiers are quote-only and add-ons are unpriced. Marketing automation customisation is limited, per reviewers.

Which platforms does LeadSquared run on?

LeadSquared is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.

Is LeadSquared available in India?

Yes. LeadSquared is built for or widely used in India, and pricing is available in INR where noted. Confirm GST and local terms with the vendor.
