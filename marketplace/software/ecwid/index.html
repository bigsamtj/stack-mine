What is Ecwid?

Ecwid is an ecommerce platform that embeds an online store into existing websites and social channels, sold in Starter, Venture, Business and Unlimited plans.

  • Best forSmall sellers adding a store to an existing site
  • Team size fitSmall teams

Overview

Ecwid overview: who it is for and what it does.

Ecwid lets merchants add a store to an existing website, blog or social profile instead of rebuilding their site, and syncs products across channels.

A July 2026 review lists Starter at $5 a month (10 products), Venture at $35 (100 products), Business at $65 (2,500 products) and Unlimited at $149, with 16% off annual billing on all but Starter.

The review says there is no free trial and that Venture cannot list products on Amazon or eBay. Prices exclude sales tax and VAT.

Main problem it solves

Adds ecommerce to an existing site without migrating platforms.

Typical customer segment

Small sellers and businesses with existing websites.

Target users

  • Bloggers and creators adding a store
  • Small businesses with an existing site
  • Sellers on social channels

Primary use cases

  • Embedded online store
  • Social selling
  • Marketplace listings (higher plans)
  • POS (Unlimited)

What makes it distinct

  • Embeds into existing sites.
  • Plans limited by product count.
  • Full POS on Unlimited.

FAQ

Ecwid questions and answers

What is Ecwid used for?

Ecwid is an ecommerce platform that embeds an online store into existing websites and social channels, sold in Starter, Venture, Business and Unlimited plans. Typical uses include embedded online store, social selling, marketplace listings (higher plans), pOS (Unlimited).

How much does Ecwid cost?

A July 2026 review lists Starter $5, Venture $35, Business $65 and Unlimited $149 a month.

Can I add Ecwid to my existing website?

Yes, Ecwid is designed to embed a store into existing sites and social channels.

Does Ecwid have a free trial?

The review says there is no free trial.

What are the main alternatives to Ecwid?

The main alternatives to Ecwid covered on Stack18 are Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and Zoho Commerce. Shopify may suit merchants who want a hosted store with a large app ecosystem. Wix may suit small businesses that want a design-led website with a basic store. Squarespace may suit creatives and small brands that want polished templates with selling. See the Ecwid alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Ecwid?

Strengths: Adds a store to an existing website without migrating. Starter costs $5 a month. Limitations: Starter allows only 10 products and no SEO features. Venture cannot list on Amazon or eBay.

Who is Ecwid best for?

Ecwid may suit small sellers adding a store to an existing site; creators selling a small catalogue; businesses selling on social channels. It may be less suited to large catalogues on lower plans; merchants needing deep customisation.

What integrations does Ecwid support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Website and social channel embeds, POS (Unlimited). API: Not reviewed for this profile.
