Quick answer

What is Rank Math?

Rank Math is an SEO plugin for WordPress that adds on-page SEO analysis, XML sitemaps, schema markup, redirects, 404 monitoring, Google Search Console data and AI writing tools, with a free version and Pro, Business and Agency plans.

  • Best forWordPress sites wanting advanced free features
  • Team size fitSolo and freelancers, Small teams, Mid-size teams
  • PricingFree; Pro $59 a year.
  • Main limitationSetup takes longer than Yoast.

Overview

Rank Math overview: who it is for and what it does.

Rank Math grew quickly by including features in its free version that rivals charge for, such as a redirect manager, 404 monitor, up to five focus keywords per post and more than 18 schema types.

A 2026 comparison lists Pro at $59 a year for unlimited personal sites, Business at $199 a year for 100 client sites and Agency at $499 a year for 500 client sites. Content AI, llms.txt generation and an AI search tracker are offered, and it reports more than 3 million active installs.

It integrates well with page builders such as Elementor and Divi and can import settings from Yoast SEO.

Main problem it solves

Helps WordPress site owners optimise pages, schema and technical SEO without separate plugins.

Typical customer segment

FAQ

Rank Math questions and answers

What is Rank Math used for?

Rank Math is used for on-page SEO analysis, schema markup, redirects and 404 monitoring and XML sitemaps.

Does Rank Math have a free plan?

Yes. Yes. Free trial: Not applicable.

How much does Rank Math cost?

Free; Pro $59 a year. Prices from a 2026 third-party comparison; Rank Math frequently runs discounts.

What is the difference between Rank Math Pro and Business?

Pro is listed at $59 a year (Unlimited personal sites). Business is listed at $199 a year (100 client sites). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Rank Math?

The main alternatives to Rank Math covered on Stack18 are Yoast SEO, Semrush, Surfer and Ahrefs. Yoast SEO may suit writers and bloggers new to SEO. Semrush may suit marketing teams that want one broad SEO and competitive research suite. Surfer may suit content teams that want on-page optimisation guidance while writing. See the Rank Math alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What integrations does Rank Math support?

Documented integrations include Google Search Console and Analytics, WooCommerce, Elementor and Divi and Yoast import. API: WordPress hooks and filters.

What are the limitations of Rank Math?

Setup takes longer than Yoast. Smaller install base than Yoast. AI features use credits.
