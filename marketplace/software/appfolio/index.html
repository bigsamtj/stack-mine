What is AppFolio?

AppFolio Property Manager is cloud property management software with accounting, leasing, maintenance, tenant and owner portals and automation for residential, community association and commercial portfolios.

  • Best forProperty managers with roughly 200+ units
  • Team size fitMid-size teams, Large enterprises

Overview

AppFolio overview: who it is for and what it does.

AppFolio combines accounting, leasing, maintenance, tenant and owner portals and automation in one mobile-ready platform.

A 2026 guide (prices as of October 2026) lists Core at about $1.49 per unit a month with a $298 minimum, Plus about $3.20 ($960 minimum) and Max about $5 ($7,500 minimum), partly behind a quote request.

It calls AppFolio strong on automation and adoption but a poor fit below about 200 units because of the minimums.

FAQ

AppFolio questions and answers

What is AppFolio used for?

AppFolio Property Manager is cloud property management software with accounting, leasing, maintenance, tenant and owner portals and automation for residential, community association and commercial portfolios. Typical uses include accounting, leasing, maintenance, owner portals.

How much does AppFolio cost?

Core is about $1.49 per unit a month with a $298 minimum, per a 2026 guide; Plus and Max cost more.

Is AppFolio good for small landlords?

The guide says minimums make it a poor fit below about 200 units.

Does AppFolio have a free trial?

None is listed.

How does AppFolio compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of AppFolio vs Buildium, AppFolio vs Yardi Breeze and Rent Manager vs AppFolio, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to AppFolio?

The main alternatives to AppFolio covered on Stack18 are Buildium, Yardi Breeze, Rent Manager and DoorLoop. Buildium may suit residential and HOA managers needing solid accounting. Yardi Breeze may suit managers mixing residential and commercial properties. Rent Manager may suit managers needing configurable workflows and reports. See the AppFolio alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of AppFolio?

Strengths: Strong automation. Full mobile functionality. Limitations: High monthly minimums. Poor fit under ~200 units.

Who is AppFolio best for?

AppFolio may suit property managers with roughly 200+ units; multifamily operators; community associations. It may be less suited to small landlords; DIY investors.

What integrations does AppFolio support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include QuickBooks, Zillow, Apartments.com, Zapier. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
