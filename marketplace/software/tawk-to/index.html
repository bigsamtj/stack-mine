Quick answer

What is tawk.to?

tawk.to is a live chat and customer messaging platform whose core product is free with unlimited agents, chats and websites. It includes live chat, ticketing, a hosted knowledge base, a contacts CRM and mobile apps. It earns revenue from optional add-ons: branding removal (reported at $29 per month billed annually), AI Assist from about $29 per month for 1,000 AI messages, voice and video, and hired chat agents at about $1 per hour.

Overview

tawk.to overview: who it is for and what it does.

tawk.to, founded in 2012 and described as privately held and bootstrapped, offers one of the most generous free tiers in customer support software. Independent 2026 reviews agree that the core product has no limit on agents, conversations, chat history or websites, and includes a ticketing system that turns emails into a shared queue, a knowledge base, a contacts CRM, visitor monitoring with geolocation and apps for iOS, Android, Windows and Mac. Integrations cited by reviewers include WordPress, Shopify, Magento and Joomla.

The company monetises through four optional paid add-ons billed per property: Remove Branding, AI Assist, voice and video, and hired agents. Reported 2026 prices are $29 per month billed annually ($39 monthly) to remove the tawk.to badge, AI Assist from $29 per month for 1,000 AI messages scaling to about $99 for 5,000 and $399 for 20,000, voice agents from about $49 plus call usage, and human chat agents at roughly $1 per hour. AI Assist includes a free tier for limited volume and answers from your knowledge base, with human handoff.

Because the add-ons stack, the all-in cost can exceed the headline: a team that wants AI and a branded widget could pay about $58 to $68 per month, which is why comparison sites note that the real cost is higher than $0. Older articles quote $19 per month for branding removal, so check the date of any source. The platform suits small businesses, startups and agencies that need free chat with no agent limits; teams that need deeper ticketing, SLAs, reporting or white-glove support may outgrow it.

FAQ

tawk.to questions and answers

What is tawk.to?

tawk.to is a live chat and customer support platform whose core product is free. It includes live chat, ticketing, a knowledge base, a contacts CRM and mobile apps, with optional paid add-ons.

Is tawk.to really free?

Yes. The core product is free with unlimited agents, chats, chat history and websites, and no credit card is required. Costs come from optional add-ons such as branding removal, AI Assist and hired agents.

How much does tawk.to cost to remove branding?

2026 sources report Remove Branding at $29 per month billed annually ($39 billed monthly) per property. Older articles quote $19 per month, so check the current price in your dashboard.

How much does tawk.to AI Assist cost?

Reported pricing starts at about $29 per month for 1,000 AI messages, with higher tiers around $99 for 5,000 and $399 for 20,000. AI Assist also has a free tier for limited volume.

Does tawk.to have unlimited agents?

Yes. tawk.to does not limit the number of agents, conversations or websites on the free plan.

What are tawk.to hired agents?

tawk.to offers a service where outsourced human chat agents and virtual assistants answer chats on your site, reported at about $1 per hour.

Does tawk.to have a mobile app?

Yes. Independent summaries list tawk.to apps for iOS, Android, Windows and Mac.

What are alternatives to tawk.to?

Common alternatives include Tidio, Crisp, LiveChat, Chatwoot and Freshdesk. Tidio and Crisp also offer free plans with limits.

What integrations does tawk.to support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include WordPress, Shopify, Magento, Joomla, Knowledge base integration for AI Assist, iOS, Android, Windows and Mac apps. API details: Integration and API details were not reviewed; reviewers note that advanced integrations or API access are limited on the free plan.

What are the limitations of tawk.to?

The free plan shows tawk.to branding, and removing it is a paid add-on of about $29 per month billed annually. AI Assist is a separate paid add-on priced by message volume, so AI costs scale with usage. Add-ons are billed per property, which multiplies costs across several websites.

Which platforms does tawk.to run on?

tawk.to is documented as available on: Web, iOS app, Android app, Windows app, Mac app.
