Overview

TallyPrime overview: who it is for and what it does.

TallyPrime is the current version of Tally, the accounting software used by a very large share of Indian small and mid-size businesses and their chartered accountants. It covers accounting, inventory, GST returns, e-invoicing and e-way bills, payroll and banking.

Tally's own July 2026 cost guide lists a perpetual Silver licence (single user) at ₹22,500 and Gold (multi-user on a local network) at ₹67,500, both plus 18% GST and including a year of Tally Software Services (TSS). Rentals run ₹750 a month for Silver and ₹2,250 for Gold.

TSS renewal costs ₹4,500 a year for Silver and ₹13,500 for Gold, and covers upgrades and connected services. Cloud access, WhatsApp, TallyDrive storage and the Docs by Ira AI document reader are priced separately.

Main problem it solves

Keeps Indian business books, inventory and GST compliance in one system that most accountants already use.

Typical customer segment

Indian small and mid-size businesses, traders, manufacturers and their CA firms.

Target users

  • Indian SMEs that file GST returns, e-invoices and e-way bills
  • Traders and manufacturers that track inventory
  • Chartered accountants and auditors (Auditor edition)

Primary use cases

  • Accounting and books of accounts
  • GST returns, e-invoicing and e-way bills
  • Inventory management
  • Banking and payments
  • Payroll

What makes it distinct

  • Perpetual licences as well as rentals.
  • Deep Indian statutory compliance.
  • Annual TSS for upgrades and connected services.

FAQ

TallyPrime questions and answers

What is TallyPrime used for?

TallyPrime is business management and accounting software from Tally Solutions, used widely in India for accounting, inventory, GST and statutory compliance, sold as perpetual Silver (single user) and Gold (multi-user) licences or as a rental. Typical uses include accounting and books of accounts, gST returns, e-invoicing and e-way bills, inventory management, banking and payments.

What is the price of TallyPrime in 2026?

Tally's July 2026 guide lists Silver at ₹22,500 and Gold at ₹67,500 plus 18% GST as perpetual licences, or rentals from ₹750 (Silver) and ₹2,250 (Gold) a month.

What is Tally TSS and how much is renewal?

Tally Software Services gives upgrades and connected GST, banking and WhatsApp services. Renewal is ₹4,500 a year for Silver and ₹13,500 for Gold, plus GST.

What is the difference between TallyPrime Silver and Gold?

Silver is for a single user on one computer; Gold supports multiple users on a local network.

How does TallyPrime compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of TallyPrime vs Zoho Books, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to TallyPrime?

The main alternatives to TallyPrime covered on Stack18 are Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, Odoo Accounting and Xero. Zoho Books may suit small businesses that want low-cost accounting inside the Zoho ecosystem. QuickBooks Online may suit US small businesses that want the most widely used cloud accounting tool. Odoo Accounting may suit businesses that want accounting inside a modular, open-source ERP. See the TallyPrime alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of TallyPrime?

Strengths: A perpetual licence avoids ongoing subscription fees, with optional TSS renewal. E-invoicing and e-way bill features are on both Silver and Gold. Limitations: Upgrades and connected services require an active TSS renewal. Multi-user access needs the Gold licence at ₹67,500 plus GST.

Who is TallyPrime best for?

TallyPrime may suit Indian businesses that need GST-ready accounting and inventory; firms whose CA already works in Tally; businesses that prefer a one-time licence. It may be less suited to businesses outside India; teams that want browser-first cloud software without add-ons.

What integrations does TallyPrime support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include GST, banking and WhatsApp connected services (TSS), Cloud Access add-on, TallyDrive storage, Bharat Connect and Amazon data import. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
