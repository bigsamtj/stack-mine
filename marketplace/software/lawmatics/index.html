Overview

Lawmatics overview: who it is for and what it does.

Lawmatics automates intake forms, follow-ups, marketing emails, scheduling and e-signatures for prospective clients.

A September 2026 guide says Lawmatics publishes no rates; Essential has 500 contacts, a mandatory 3-user minimum and 10 automations, Premium has 10,000 contacts and unlimited automations, and Enterprise is custom.

It lists paid add-ons for Time and Billing, credit-based QualifyAI lead qualification, MMS and extra contacts.

FAQ

Lawmatics questions and answers

What is Lawmatics used for?

Lawmatics is legal client relationship management software for intake, marketing automation, e-signatures, scheduling and conflict checks. Typical uses include intake, marketing automation, e-signatures, conflict checks.

How much does Lawmatics cost?

Lawmatics publishes no rates; all tiers are quoted, per a September 2026 guide.

Does Lawmatics have a user minimum?

Essential requires at least 3 users.

Does Lawmatics include billing?

Time and Billing is a separate add-on.

How does Lawmatics compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Lawmatics vs Clio, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Lawmatics?

The main alternatives to Lawmatics covered on Stack18 are Clio, MyCase and Filevine. Clio may suit law firms wanting the broadest integration ecosystem. MyCase may suit small and mid-size firms wanting public pricing and payments. Filevine may suit litigation and PI firms needing deep customisation. See the Lawmatics alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Lawmatics?

Strengths: Strong intake automation. Unlimited automations on Premium. Limitations: No published prices. 3-user minimum on Essential.

Who is Lawmatics best for?

Lawmatics may suit firms focused on intake and marketing automation; PI and immigration practices with high lead volume; multi-location firms. It may be less suited to solo attorneys; firms wanting billing first.

What integrations does Lawmatics support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Zapier. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
