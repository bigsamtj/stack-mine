Overview

Constant Contact overview: who it is for and what it does.

Constant Contact is a long-established email marketing service for small businesses and nonprofits. Alongside email it offers event management, social posting and ads tools, and live call and chat support on every plan.

Guides report Lite at $12, Standard at $35 and Premium at $80 a month for up to 500 contacts, rising to $430, $460 and $575 at 50,000 contacts. Monthly sends are capped at 10x contacts on Lite, 12x on Standard and 24x on Premium.

There is no free plan. A 14-day trial (reduced from 30 days) and a 30-day money-back guarantee are reported, with 15% off for prepaying a year and larger discounts for nonprofits.

Main problem it solves

Gives small businesses and nonprofits email, events and social tools with hands-on support.

Typical customer segment

Small businesses, nonprofits and associations, from single users on Lite to teams on Premium.

Target users

  • Small businesses that want guided email marketing
  • Nonprofits and associations that run events
  • Marketing teams that want ads and SEO tools on Premium

Primary use cases

  • Newsletters and promotions
  • Event registration and promotion
  • Basic automations
  • Social posting and ads
  • SMS marketing (add-on credits)

What makes it distinct

  • Built-in event management and selling.
  • Live call and chat support on every plan.
  • Automated emails do not count toward monthly send limits.

FAQ

Constant Contact questions and answers

What is Constant Contact used for?

Constant Contact is an email marketing platform for small businesses and nonprofits, sold in Lite, Standard and Premium plans priced by contact count. Typical uses include newsletters and promotions, event registration and promotion, basic automations, social posting and ads.

How much does Constant Contact cost?

Guides report Lite at $12, Standard at $35 and Premium at $80 a month for up to 500 contacts, rising with list size.

Does Constant Contact have a free plan?

No. A 14-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee are reported.

Is Constant Contact good for nonprofits?

It offers built-in event tools and nonprofit discounts of up to about 30% when prepaying, according to one guide.

How does Constant Contact compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Mailchimp vs Constant Contact, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Constant Contact?

The main alternatives to Constant Contact covered on Stack18 are Mailchimp, Campaign Monitor, MailerLite and Brevo. Mailchimp may suit small businesses that want a well-known all-round email tool. Campaign Monitor may suit brands and agencies that prioritise polished email design. MailerLite may suit small businesses that want low-cost email with landing pages. See the Constant Contact alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Constant Contact?

Strengths: Event management, selling and payments are built in on every plan. Live call and chat support is reported on all plans. Limitations: There is no free plan, and the trial was cut from 30 to 14 days. Lite is single-user with one automation template and one custom segment.

Who is Constant Contact best for?

Constant Contact may suit small businesses and nonprofits that want guided, all-in-one email; organisations that run events; teams that value phone and chat support. It may be less suited to businesses that want a free plan; teams that need advanced automation without paying for Premium.
