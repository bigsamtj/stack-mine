Quick answer

What is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI meeting assistant that provides live transcription, AI summaries, action items and searchable notes for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, sold in Basic, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans by transcription minutes.

Overview

Otter.ai overview: who it is for and what it does.

Otter is one of the original AI transcription apps and is widely used by students, journalists and business teams for live captions and meeting notes.

A 2026 pricing page lists Basic free (300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation), Pro at $16.99 monthly or $8.33 billed annually (1,200 minutes, 90 minutes per conversation, exports and integrations), Business at $30 monthly or $19.99 annually (four-hour meetings, auto-join for Zoom, Meet and Teams, CRM integrations, three concurrent meetings) and Enterprise with SSO, SCIM, API, an MCP server and HIPAA add-on.

Annual billing saves up to 51 percent, according to the same source.

What is Otter.ai used for?

What is the difference between Otter.ai Pro and Business?

Pro is listed at $16.99 monthly; $8.33 annual (1,200 minutes). Business is listed at $30 monthly; $19.99 annual (4-hour meetings). Confirm current plan contents with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to Otter.ai?

What are the limitations of Otter.ai?

Which platforms does Otter.ai run on?
