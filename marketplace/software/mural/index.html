Quick answer

What is Mural?

Mural is a digital whiteboard and visual collaboration platform that teams use for workshops, brainstorming, retrospectives and facilitated sessions. It has a free plan with the first 3 murals, Team+ at $9.99 per member per month billed annually ($12 monthly), Business at $17.99 (annual only) and a custom Enterprise plan. Mural AI is included from Team+, and Business adds SAML 2.0 SSO and unlimited external collaborators.

  • Best forFacilitators and teams that run structured remote workshops
  • Team size fitSmall teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
  • PricingFree plan (first 3 murals). Team+ $9.99 per member per month billed annually ($12 or $12.99 monthly); Business $17.99 per member, annual billing only; Enterprise custom. Free plan available.
  • Main limitationThe free plan is limited to the first 3 murals.
  • AlternativesMiro, Notion, Confluence, Zoom Workplace, Slack · compare all

Overview

Mural overview: who it is for and what it does.

Mural is built around structured, facilitated collaboration rather than free-form whiteboarding. Its pricing page and independent reviews describe a guided visual collaboration platform for structured teamwork: an infinite and resizable canvas, a full template library, member-built custom templates, and facilitation features for running remote workshops. Business and Enterprise plans include the LUMA System, a structured framework for innovation and problem-solving. Toolradar describes it as best for collaborative teams and enterprises needing robust visual collaboration, and aggregators list ratings around 4.5 out of 5.

Pricing is per member per month. The free plan includes all visual collaboration and facilitation features, the full template library, unlimited members, unlimited view-only visitors, open rooms and the first 3 murals. Team+ at $9.99 per member per month billed annually ($12 or $12.99 monthly, depending on source) adds unlimited editable murals, unlimited visitors with editing rights, private rooms, Mural AI and basic integrations such as Zoom and Webex. Business at $17.99 per member billed annually only adds SAML 2.0 SSO and unlimited external collaboration, and Enterprise adds SCIM, audit logs and custom terms. USAGE Pricing reports that Mural removed Business's earlier 10-member minimum and bundled Mural AI into Team+ and above.

Compared with Miro, a CASRAI analysis finds Mural Business at $17.99 undercuts Miro Business at $20 per member, and that Mural Team+ includes unlimited editing visitors and AI that Miro unlocks only on Business. The trade-offs: the free plan caps editable murals at 3, SSO and unlimited external guests need Business, and CostBench warns that implementation, training and support can add 30 to 60 percent to the base licence for larger deals. Sources differ on the Team+ monthly price ($12 versus $12.99), so confirm at checkout.

FAQ

Mural questions and answers

What is Mural?

Mural is a digital whiteboard and visual collaboration platform for workshops, brainstorming, retrospectives and facilitated sessions, with an infinite canvas, templates and Mural AI.

How much does Mural cost?

Team+ costs $9.99 per member per month billed annually (about $12 to $12.99 monthly), and Business costs $17.99 per member billed annually. Enterprise is custom, and there is a free plan.

Is Mural free?

Yes. The free plan includes all visual collaboration and facilitation features, the full template library, unlimited members and view-only visitors, and the first 3 murals.

What is the difference between Mural Team+ and Business?

Business adds SAML 2.0 SSO, unlimited external collaborators and the LUMA System to what Team+ includes, at $17.99 per member annually.

Does Mural have AI features?

Yes. Mural AI, which helps summarise and cluster session content, is included in Team+ and higher plans.

Is Mural cheaper than Miro?

A CASRAI analysis finds Mural Business at $17.99 per member undercuts Miro Business at $20, and Team+ includes unlimited editing visitors and AI that Miro offers only on Business.

Does Mural support single sign-on?

Yes. SAML 2.0 SSO is available on Business and Enterprise, and Enterprise adds SCIM and audit logs.

What are alternatives to Mural?

Common alternatives include Miro, Notion, Confluence, Zoom Workplace and Slack. Miro is the closest whiteboard alternative.

What integrations does Mural support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Zoom, Webex, Advanced integrations (Business), SAML 2.0 SSO, SCIM (Enterprise). API details: API and marketplace details were not reviewed in the sources used.

What are the limitations of Mural?

The free plan is limited to the first 3 murals. SSO and unlimited external collaborators require the Business plan at $17.99 per member. Business is billed annually only, with no published monthly rate.

Which platforms does Mural run on?

Mural is documented as available on: Web.
