Overview

Gorgias overview: who it is for and what it does.

Gorgias is a helpdesk designed for online stores. It brings email, live chat, SMS, social messages, WhatsApp and voice into one inbox and shows order context beside each ticket, so agents can answer questions and take actions such as refunds, order edits and subscription changes without leaving the helpdesk. It integrates most deeply with Shopify and also connects to BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce.

Unlike most helpdesks, Gorgias does not charge per agent. Independent guides report that every plan includes unlimited users and that price is set by a bucket of billable tickets per month: Starter at $10 for 50 tickets, Basic at $50 billed annually for 300, Pro at $300 for 2,000 and Advanced at $750 for 5,000, with overage of about $0.36 to $0.40 per ticket. Enterprise is custom.

Gorgias also sells an AI Agent that resolves common questions such as order status, returns and cancellations. Guides report it is priced per resolution, at about $0.90 on annual billing and $1.00 on monthly billing, and that a resolved conversation can also count as a billable ticket. That makes it important to model both meters before choosing a plan.

Scope: This profile covers the Gorgias helpdesk and its AI Agent add-on. Gorgias prices by billable ticket volume, not by seat, so costs depend on how many conversations your store handles.

Main problem it solves

Lets online stores answer customer questions and handle refunds, cancellations and order changes from one inbox that already shows Shopify order data, and automate repetitive tickets with macros, rules and an AI Agent.

Typical customer segment

Small to mid-sized e-commerce brands, mainly on Shopify, with ticket volumes from dozens to several thousand a month.

Target users

  • Shopify and other e-commerce merchants with a growing support queue
  • D2C brands that handle email, chat, SMS and social messages together
  • Support teams that want to avoid per-seat pricing
  • Stores that want to automate order-status, returns and cancellation requests

Primary use cases

  • Unified inbox for email, chat, SMS, social, WhatsApp and voice
  • Refunds, order edits and subscription changes from inside a ticket
  • Macros with order variables such as name, order number and tracking link
  • Automating order-status and returns questions with an AI Agent
  • Routing and tagging tickets with rules
  • Connecting reviews, loyalty, returns and shipping apps
  • Support revenue and CSAT reporting
  • Handling seasonal ticket spikes without adding seats

What makes it distinct

  • Pricing is based on billable tickets with unlimited users, rather than per agent
  • Deep, bi-directional Shopify integration with in-ticket order actions
  • A per-resolution AI Agent that takes actions rather than only linking to articles
  • A directory of 150+ integrations across reviews, loyalty, returns, shipping and SMS tools

FAQ

Gorgias questions and answers

What is Gorgias?

Gorgias is a helpdesk built for e-commerce brands. It unifies email, chat, SMS, social, WhatsApp and voice conversations in one inbox, shows Shopify order data beside each ticket, and lets agents refund, edit orders and change subscriptions from the ticket. It also offers macros, rules and a per-resolution AI Agent.

How much does Gorgias cost per month?

Independent guides report Starter at $10 (50 tickets), Basic at $50 billed annually or $60 monthly (300 tickets), Pro at $300 or $360 (2,000 tickets) and Advanced at $750 or $900 (5,000 tickets). Overage is about $0.36 to $0.40 per ticket, and Enterprise is custom.

Does Gorgias charge per agent?

No. Gorgias prices by billable ticket volume, and guides report that all plans include unlimited users. That makes it attractive for larger support teams, but costs rise with ticket volume and with AI Agent usage rather than with headcount.

How much does the Gorgias AI Agent cost?

Guides report the AI Agent costs about $0.90 per resolution on annual billing and $1.00 on monthly billing, in addition to the helpdesk plan. Several guides note that a resolved conversation can also count as a billable ticket, so model both meters before enabling it.

Does Gorgias have a free plan?

No free plan is documented. The cheapest option is Starter at $10 per month on monthly billing for 50 tickets, and one guide reports that Gorgias offers a free trial. Starter has no automation or AI Agent.

How much would 2,500 tickets a month cost on Gorgias?

On Pro billed annually, 2,000 tickets are included for $300, and the remaining 500 tickets at about $0.36 each add roughly $180, for about $480 per month before any AI Agent charges. On monthly billing the Pro base is $360.

Does Gorgias work with Shopify, BigCommerce and WooCommerce?

Yes. Gorgias integrates most deeply with Shopify and also connects to BigCommerce, Magento and WooCommerce. Reviewers say agents can see order history and take actions from the ticket, and that the most advanced AI capabilities are engineered for Shopify.

Which channels does Gorgias support?

Reviewers list email, live chat, SMS, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp and voice in one inbox. A review notes that SMS and voice channels may include usage-based fees, so check channel costs as well as plan prices.

Is Gorgias better than Zendesk for Shopify stores?

Gorgias is built for e-commerce and bills by ticket volume with unlimited users, while Zendesk is a general help desk with per-agent pricing and broader integrations. Stores with many order-related tickets often favour Gorgias, while teams that need wide enterprise integrations may prefer Zendesk.

What are the best alternatives to Gorgias?

Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout and Intercom are the main alternatives in this directory. Per-agent tools can be cheaper at low volume with small teams, while Gorgias can be cheaper for large teams with moderate ticket volume. See the linked profiles for details.

What integrations does Gorgias support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Attentive, Recharge, Loop Returns, Yotpo, LoyaltyLion, AfterShip. API details: Third-party connector vendors document a Gorgias API. Gorgias developer documentation was not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Gorgias?

The AI Agent is billed per resolution, and a resolved conversation can also count as a billable ticket, so costs can compound. Ticket overage of $0.36 to $0.40 each can make costs rise quickly when volume spikes. There is no free plan, and the Starter plan has no automation or AI Agent.
