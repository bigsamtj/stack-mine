Overview

Squarespace overview: who it is for and what it does.

Squarespace is known for polished templates and is used by creatives, service businesses and small brands for websites, stores and scheduling.

Squarespace replaced its Personal, Business and Commerce plans with Basic ($16 a month billed annually), Core ($23), Plus ($39) and Advanced ($99). The rollout completed in February 2026, and US prices reportedly rose 18% to 26% on three plans in July 2026.

Transaction fees depend on plan: Basic charges 2% on physical products and 7% on digital; Core 0% and 5%; Plus 0% and 1%; Advanced 0%. Card processing is extra.

Main problem it solves

Gives small brands a polished website with built-in selling and scheduling.

FAQ

Squarespace questions and answers

What is Squarespace used for?

Squarespace is a hosted website builder with ecommerce, scheduling and payments, sold in Basic, Core, Plus and Advanced plans after a 2025 to 2026 restructure. Typical uses include website building, online store, digital products, scheduling (Plus).

What are Squarespace's new plans?

Basic, Core, Plus and Advanced replaced Personal, Business, Commerce Basic and Commerce Advanced.

How much does Squarespace cost?

Basic $16, Core $23, Plus $39 and Advanced $99 a month billed annually, before a reported July 2026 US increase.

Does Squarespace charge transaction fees?

Basic charges 2% on physical and 7% on digital sales; higher plans charge less or nothing.

How does Squarespace compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Wix vs Squarespace, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Squarespace?

The main alternatives to Squarespace covered on Stack18 are Wix, Shopify, Ecwid and BigCommerce. Wix may suit small businesses that want a design-led website with a basic store. Shopify may suit merchants who want a hosted store with a large app ecosystem. Ecwid may suit small sellers adding a store to an existing site. See the Squarespace alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Squarespace?

Strengths: Transaction fees fall to 0% on higher plans. Advanced adds abandoned cart, subscriptions and API access. Limitations: Basic charges 2% on physical and 7% on digital sales. US prices reportedly rose 18% to 26% in July 2026.

Who is Squarespace best for?

Squarespace may suit creatives and small brands that want polished templates with selling; Service businesses on Plus; brands selling subscriptions on Advanced. It may be less suited to large catalogues; merchants needing many integrations.

What integrations does Squarespace support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Squarespace Payments, Stripe and PayPal. API: Developer API access on Advanced.
