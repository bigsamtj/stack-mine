Overview

Wix overview: who it is for and what it does.

Wix is a drag-and-drop website builder used by small businesses for brochure sites, bookings and online stores.

A November 2026 guide lists Light at $17 a month, Core at $29 and Business at $39 billed yearly, $84 a year cheaper than monthly billing. Light cannot sell online; Core accepts payments, and Business adds fuller store tools. Business Elite and Enterprise sit above.

A free plan lets you build with Wix ads and a Wix address but not sell. Card fees are 2.9% online and 2.6% in person.

Main problem it solves

Lets small businesses build an attractive website and start selling without code.

Typical customer segment

Small businesses and creators.

Target users

  • Creators selling a small catalogue

FAQ

Wix questions and answers

What is Wix used for?

Wix is a hosted website builder with ecommerce, bookings and payments, sold in Light, Core, Business, Business Elite and Enterprise plans; online selling starts on Core. Typical uses include website building, online store, bookings, payments.

How much does a Wix online store cost?

Selling starts on Core at $29 a month billed yearly; Business is $39.

Can I sell on the Wix free plan?

No. The free plan and Light cannot sell online.

What are Wix payment fees?

2.9% for standard online cards and 2.6% in person, per the guide reviewed.

How does Wix compare with its competitors?

Stack18 publishes head-to-head comparisons of Shopify vs Wix and Wix vs Squarespace, each with a feature table, pricing and best-fit scenarios.

What are the main alternatives to Wix?

The main alternatives to Wix covered on Stack18 are Squarespace, Shopify, Ecwid and Zoho Commerce. Squarespace may suit creatives and small brands that want polished templates with selling. Shopify may suit merchants who want a hosted store with a large app ecosystem. Ecwid may suit small sellers adding a store to an existing site. See the Wix alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Wix?

Strengths: A free plan lets you build before paying. Selling starts at $29 a month billed yearly. Limitations: The Light plan cannot sell online. The free plan shows Wix ads and cannot sell.

Who is Wix best for?

Wix may suit small businesses that want a design-led website with a basic store; Service businesses taking bookings; first-time sellers. It may be less suited to large catalogues; merchants needing deep ecommerce customisation.

What integrations does Wix support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Wix Payments, Wix App Market. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
