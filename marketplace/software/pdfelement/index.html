FAQ

PDFelement questions and answers

What is PDFelement used for?

Wondershare PDFelement is PDF editing software with word-processor-style editing, OCR, form building and an AI add-on. Typical uses include oCR, form building, editing, conversion.

How much does PDFelement cost?

$89.99 a year, $119.99 for two years or $139.99 perpetual, per a September 2026 guide.

Is PDFelement good at OCR?

Yes, the guide highlights its OCR and form tools.

Does PDFelement include AI?

AI is a $3.99 monthly add-on after a free allotment.

What are the main alternatives to PDFelement?

The main alternatives to PDFelement covered on Stack18 are Adobe Acrobat, UPDF and Foxit PDF Editor. Adobe Acrobat may suit high-stakes documents for clients, lawyers and regulators. UPDF may suit users split across operating systems wanting value. Foxit PDF Editor may suit Windows teams wanting a lighter Acrobat alternative. See the PDFelement alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of PDFelement?

Strengths: Strong OCR. Strong form tools. Limitations: First-purchase discounts hide renewal price. AI costs extra.

Who is PDFelement best for?

PDFelement may suit OCR accuracy and form building on scanned documents; small offices; perpetual-licence buyers. It may be less suited to Linux users; large enterprises.

What integrations does PDFelement support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Microsoft 365, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
