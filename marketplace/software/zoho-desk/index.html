Overview

Zoho Desk overview: who it is for and what it does.

Zoho Desk is the customer support product in the Zoho family of business applications. It organises incoming requests as tickets, assigns and escalates them with rules and service level agreements (SLAs), and gives customers a branded help centre. Reviewers consistently describe it as an affordable, automation-friendly help desk for small to mid-sized teams, especially those already using Zoho CRM or other Zoho apps.

Pricing is per agent per month across five editions: Free, Express, Standard, Professional and Enterprise. Independent guides report Free for up to three agents, Express at $7 for up to five agents, and then Standard at $14, Professional at $23 and Enterprise at $40 per agent per month billed annually, with no ticket or resolution meter. Paying month to month costs more, and light agents who only need limited access are priced as an add-on below Enterprise.

Zoho's AI assistant is called Zia. Reviewers describe an Answer Bot from the Standard plan, broader Zia AI features from Professional, and a fuller AI suite on Enterprise, and note that Zoho's Zia Agent Studio starts on the lower tiers with a monthly token allowance. Reviewers also flag that the product's flexibility brings some setup complexity, so teams should test Blueprint, SLAs and channel limits before committing.

Scope: This profile covers Zoho Desk as a standalone help desk. Zoho also sells it inside bundles such as Zoho One, and related tools such as Zoho SalesIQ (live chat) and Zoho CRM are priced separately.

Main problem it solves

Centralises customer questions from several channels into one ticketing workspace, enforces response targets with SLAs and Blueprint processes, and lets customers help themselves through a branded help centre, at a per-agent price below many enterprise help desks.

Typical customer segment

Small to mid-sized support teams on Free to Standard, growing multi-department teams on Professional, and larger organisations on Enterprise.

Target users

  • Small and mid-sized support teams that want a low per-agent price
  • Companies already using Zoho CRM, Zoho One or other Zoho applications
  • Support managers who need SLA tracking, escalation rules and reporting
  • Larger organisations that need multi-brand help centres and security controls on Enterprise

Primary use cases

  • Email and multichannel ticketing
  • SLA management and escalation
  • Blueprint-driven support processes
  • Self-service help centre and knowledge base
  • AI answers with Answer Bot and Zia
  • Linking tickets to customer and deal history in Zoho CRM
  • Telephony and call handling on higher plans
  • Multi-brand support on Enterprise

What makes it distinct

  • A genuine free edition for three agents plus a $7 Express plan, with unlimited-agent plans from $14
  • No ticket or resolution meter on any plan, according to a pricing review
  • Native, bi-directional sync with Zoho CRM and a wider Zoho ecosystem
  • Blueprint process automation and SLA tooling at mid-market prices
Key features

Zoho Desk features explained.

Ticketing and channels

Omnichannel ticketing
Reviewers describe tickets arriving from email, phone, social media and web forms, with live chat available through Zoho SalesIQ. Sources disagree on which plan includes live chat, so check the current plan table.
SLA management and escalation
Teams create triggers for SLA breaches and supervisor alerts, according to independent reviews.
Light agents
Read-only or limited agents are an add-on of about $6 per agent per month on paid plans, and Enterprise includes 50 at no extra charge.

Automation and processes

Blueprint
Reviewers call Blueprint, Zoho's visual process automation, a standout feature for mid-market teams.
Workflow rules and automation
Express adds basic workflows, and Standard adds automation rules, social channels and community features, per pricing guides.
Telephony
Reviewers report telephony from the Professional plan, with included inbound and outbound minutes and call charges beyond them.

Self-service and knowledge

Branded help centre
Reviewers describe a help centre with custom domains and categorised articles.
Community forums
Community features are listed from the Standard plan.
Multi-brand support
Enterprise adds multi-brand help centres for organisations that run several brands.

AI features

Answer Bot
Reviewers report an Answer Bot from the Standard plan that answers common questions.
Zia AI assistant
Reviewers describe Zia as detecting sentiment, categorising tickets and suggesting articles, with the fullest AI suite on Enterprise.
Zia Agent Studio
A guide reports that Zia Agent Studio, for building AI agents, starts on lower tiers with a monthly token allowance of about 30 million tokens.

FAQ

Zoho Desk questions and answers

What is Zoho Desk?

Zoho Desk is a cloud help desk from Zoho Corporation. It turns customer requests from email, phone, social media, chat and web forms into tickets, then manages them with SLAs, Blueprint automation, a help centre and the Zia AI assistant. It is sold per agent in five editions from Free to Enterprise.

How much does Zoho Desk cost per agent per month?

Independent guides report Free for up to three agents, then Express at $7, Standard at $14, Professional at $23 and Enterprise at $40 per agent per month billed annually. Express is limited to five agents. Monthly billing costs more, and regional and INR prices differ.

Is Zoho Desk free?

Zoho Desk has a free edition that guides report for up to three agents. It is a genuine free plan rather than a short trial, but it is limited. Teams that need more than three agents move to Express for up to five agents or to Standard for unlimited agents.

What is the difference between Zoho Desk Standard, Professional and Enterprise?

Standard ($14) is reported to add automation, Answer Bot, social and community features and Zoho CRM integration. Professional ($23) adds broader Zia AI and telephony. Enterprise ($40) adds the fullest AI suite, security controls, multi-brand support and 50 included light agents.

Does Zoho Desk integrate with Zoho CRM?

Yes. Reviewers describe native, bi-directional sync between Zoho Desk and Zoho CRM, so support agents see customer and deal history and sales teams see tickets. Zoho Desk also connects with Zoho SalesIQ, Zoho Analytics and Zoho Projects, and is included in some Zoho bundles.

Does Zoho Desk have AI?

Yes. Zoho's AI assistant is Zia. Reviewers report an Answer Bot from Standard, broader Zia features such as sentiment detection, ticket categorisation and article suggestions from Professional, and the fullest AI suite on Enterprise. Zia Agent Studio, for building AI agents, starts on lower tiers.

How much would Zoho Desk cost for 10 agents?

At reported annual prices, 10 agents cost $140 per month on Standard ($1,680 a year), $230 per month on Professional ($2,760 a year) and $400 per month on Enterprise ($4,800 a year). Express is capped at five agents. Light agents, telephony usage and monthly billing are extra.

Is Zoho Desk cheaper than Zendesk and Freshdesk?

Reviewers generally place Zoho Desk below Zendesk on per-agent price and close to Freshdesk's paid entry tiers. Freshdesk's free plan reportedly covers up to 10 agents versus three for Zoho Desk. Compare the specific features and channels you need rather than headline prices.

Is Zoho Desk good for small business?

It can be. The free edition and $7 to $14 per agent plans suit small teams, and the native Zoho CRM link helps companies already in the Zoho ecosystem. Reviewers caution that setup can be complex and that some features, such as Answer Bot and telephony, need higher plans.

What are the best alternatives to Zoho Desk?

Freshdesk is the closest budget alternative and has a larger free tier, Zendesk suits teams that need extensive integrations, Help Scout focuses on shared-inbox simplicity, and Intercom emphasises chat and AI. See the linked profiles for side-by-side summaries.

What integrations does Zoho Desk support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Zoho CRM (native, bi-directional), Zoho SalesIQ, Zoho Analytics, Zoho Projects, Zoho One suite, Zoho Marketplace extensions. API details: API details were not reviewed for this profile.

What are the limitations of Zoho Desk?

The free edition is capped at three agents and Express at five, so a sixth full agent needs Standard at $14. Light agents are an add-on below Enterprise, and sources differ on which plan first offers them. Paying monthly raises per-agent cost noticeably, by roughly 29% to 56% on the UK price list one guide checked.
