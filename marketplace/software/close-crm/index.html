Overview

Close CRM overview: who it is for and what it does.

Close is a sales CRM built around conversations. Instead of connecting separate phone, SMS and email tools, it puts calling, texting and two-way email into the CRM itself, so every touchpoint is logged against the lead. Close's own plans page describes Solo as a full CRM with calling, email and SMS built in, Essentials as adding pipelines and team-level visibility, Growth as adding workflows, and Scale as adding predictive dialing, lead visibility and advanced controls.

Pricing is published and per user. Close lists Solo at $9, Essentials at $35, Growth at $99 and Scale at $139 per user per month billed annually, with higher monthly-billing prices of $19, $49, $109 and $149. Every plan includes AI credits that power Chloe and other AI features, from 500 credits per user per month on Solo to 2,000 on Scale, with shared pools on the team plans.

Close positions Chloe as an AI sales teammate that calls leads, qualifies them, books meetings and updates the CRM. Independent reviewers describe Chloe as English-only and note that calling and some AI features can carry usage-based charges. Close also offers a REST API, webhooks and an MCP server, and a free trial is available, which Close says includes calling and AI credits.

Main problem it solves

Removes the need to stitch together a CRM, phone dialer, SMS tool and email sequencer by running calling, texting, email and follow-up workflows inside one lead record, with AI help for summaries, drafts and qualification.

Typical customer segment

Founders, small teams and mid-sized inside sales teams in sectors such as SaaS, financial services, real estate, insurance, recruiting and professional services.

Target users

  • Founders and solo sellers who want a real CRM instead of a spreadsheet
  • Inside sales and outbound teams that make many calls and send many emails
  • Small and mid-sized sales teams, typically up to about 100 reps
  • Sales managers who need call activity and pipeline visibility in one place

Primary use cases

  • Outbound calling with power and predictive dialers
  • Two-way email and SMS follow-up in the CRM
  • Multi-step sales workflows mixing email, SMS and call tasks
  • AI lead summaries, email drafts and meeting notes
  • AI voice qualification and meeting booking with Chloe
  • Pipeline management with multiple pipelines and Smart Views
  • Lead capture with web forms
  • Connecting sales data to ChatGPT, Claude and other tools via MCP

What makes it distinct

  • Calling, SMS and email are native to the CRM rather than added through integrations
  • Every plan includes access to the Chloe AI agent with a monthly AI credit allowance
  • Clear four-tier per-user pricing with a $9 per user entry point
  • Official REST API, webhooks and MCP server for teams that build their own sales stack

FAQ

Close CRM questions and answers

What is Close CRM?

Close is a sales CRM for founders and small to mid-sized inside sales teams. It builds calling, SMS and email into the lead record, adds workflows and pipelines, and includes the Chloe AI sales agent. It is sold per user in four tiers: Solo, Essentials, Growth and Scale.

How much does Close CRM cost per user per month?

Close lists Solo at $9, Essentials at $35, Growth at $99 and Scale at $139 per user per month billed annually. Monthly billing costs $19, $49, $109 and $149. Solo is limited to one user, and calling or some AI usage may carry separate charges.

Does Close CRM have a free plan?

No. Close does not offer a permanent free plan. It offers a free trial that includes calling and AI credits, and a Solo plan at $9 per user per month billed annually for individual users.

What is Chloe in Close CRM?

Chloe is Close's built-in AI sales agent. Close says she calls leads, qualifies them through conversations, books meetings and updates the CRM. Chloe is included on every plan with AI credits, and reviewers note she currently works in English only.

Does Close CRM include calling and SMS?

Yes. Close builds VoIP calling, SMS and email into the CRM from the Solo plan upward. The power dialer arrives on Growth and the predictive dialer on Scale. Independent reviewers warn that calling, SMS and some AI usage can add usage-based charges.

Which Close plan has workflows and automation?

Automated workflows start on the Growth plan at $99 per user per month billed annually, and Scale includes them too. Growth also adds the power dialer, bulk email and the ability to use Chloe inside workflows. Solo and Essentials do not include workflows.

How much would Close CRM cost for 10 users?

At published annual prices, 10 users cost $350 per month on Essentials ($4,200 a year), $990 per month on Growth ($11,880 a year) and $1,390 per month on Scale ($16,680 a year). Solo is limited to one user. Usage-based calling and AI charges are extra.

Does Close CRM have an API or work with ChatGPT and Claude?

Close lists a REST API, webhooks and an MCP server on its site, and a G2 listing says the MCP server works with ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and other tools. It also connects with Zapier, Zoom, HubSpot and other tools. Check Close's documentation for plan-specific limits.

Is Close CRM better than Pipedrive or HubSpot?

It depends on your sales motion. Close is built for high-volume calling, texting and email with native dialers, while Pipedrive focuses on visual pipeline management and HubSpot offers a free tier and a broader platform. See the linked profiles to compare plans and limits.

What integrations does Close CRM support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Gmail and Microsoft 365 email sync, Google Calendar, Zoom, HubSpot, Zapier, SavvyCal, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor via MCP. API details: Close lists a REST API, webhooks and an MCP server for building on its platform. Check its documentation for plan-specific limits.

What are the limitations of Close CRM?

There is no free plan, only a trial and a $9 annual Solo plan limited to one user. Workflows are only available on Growth and Scale, which are $99 and $139 per user per month billed annually. Calling, SMS and some AI tools can carry separate usage-based charges that affect budgeting.

Which platforms does Close CRM run on?

Close CRM is documented as available on: Web, iOS, Android.

What are the main alternatives to Close CRM?

The main alternatives to Close CRM covered on Stack18 are Pipedrive, HubSpot, Freshsales and Zoho CRM. Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Freshsales may suit startups and small teams that want an affordable CRM with a dialer.
