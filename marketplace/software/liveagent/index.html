Overview

LiveAgent overview: who it is for and what it does.

LiveAgent is a budget-friendly help desk from the Slovak software company Quality Unit. It unifies email, live chat, phone, contact forms and social channels such as Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, Viber and Telegram into one inbox, and its hybrid ticket stream follows a customer across channels inside a single ticket.

LiveAgent's own pricing page lists three plans billed per agent per month annually: Small at $15, Medium at $29 and Large at $49. It states that API access and integrations are included on every plan, and a 30-day free trial requires no credit card. Some third-party sources describe a limited free plan and a higher Enterprise tier near $69, so confirm the live plan list.

Independent analysis warns that the headline price is not always the final bill. One review of a functioning setup estimates $73 per agent on the Small plan once add-ons are included, and another notes some messaging channels cost extra. The product is often chosen for feature density per dollar, gamified agent performance and free data migration.

Main problem it solves

Brings tickets, live chat, phone and social messages into one low-cost inbox so small teams do not need several tools.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-sized support teams, BPO-style operations and cost-conscious businesses.

Target users

  • Small support teams that want live chat and phone in one tool
  • Businesses comparing low-cost alternatives to Zendesk
  • BPO and outsourced support teams that value time tracking and gamification

Primary use cases

  • Email ticketing
  • Live chat with proactive invitations
  • Call center with IVR
  • Social media support
  • Knowledge base and customer portal

What makes it distinct

  • Per-agent pricing that starts at $15 and includes API and integrations.
  • A hybrid ticket stream that follows a customer across channels.
  • Agent gamification with badges, levels and leaderboards.

FAQ

LiveAgent questions and answers

What is LiveAgent?

LiveAgent is a help desk and live chat platform that combines email ticketing, live chat, a call center and social channels in one inbox. It is made by Quality Unit.

How much does LiveAgent cost?

LiveAgent's pricing page lists Small at $15, Medium at $29 and Large at $49 per agent per month billed annually, with a 30-day free trial. Add-ons can raise the total.

Does LiveAgent have a free plan?

Sources disagree. Some describe a limited free plan, others say there is none. LiveAgent offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card.

Does LiveAgent have a call center?

Yes. A built-in call center with IVR is reported on higher plans, alongside live chat and social channels.

Is LiveAgent cheaper than Zendesk?

List prices are lower, with reviewers comparing LiveAgent's $15 to $49 per agent with Zendesk's higher tiers. Compare total cost including add-ons.

What are the alternatives to LiveAgent?

Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout and Zoho Desk are common alternatives.

What integrations does LiveAgent support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include Slack, Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, Viber, Telegram, REST API. API details: LiveAgent states that API and integrations are included in each subscription at no additional cost.

What are the limitations of LiveAgent?

One analysis says total cost per agent can be several times the list price once add-ons are included. Some messaging channels cost extra, which can surprise buyers. Reviewers say LiveAgent's chat is slightly less robust than dedicated live chat products.

Which platforms does LiveAgent run on?

LiveAgent is documented as available on: Web, Mobile apps.

When was the LiveAgent profile last verified?

5 sources are listed under Sources. Prices and plans can change, so confirm with the vendor.

What are the main alternatives to LiveAgent?

The main alternatives to LiveAgent covered on Stack18 are Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout and Zoho Desk. Zendesk may suit support teams that need ticketing plus omnichannel messaging. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price. Help Scout may suit small email-first support teams.
