What is Pipedream?

Pipedream is a developer-focused workflow automation platform where steps can be code (Node.js, Python) or prebuilt actions, with Pipedream Connect for embedding integrations.

Pipedream overview: who it is for and what it does.

FAQ

Pipedream questions and answers

What is Pipedream used for?

Pipedream is a developer-focused workflow automation platform where steps can be code (Node.js, Python) or prebuilt actions, with Pipedream Connect for embedding integrations. Typical uses include code workflows, aPI automation, embedded integrations, mCP for agents.

How much does Pipedream cost?

Free for 100 credits a month; Basic about $29, Advanced $49 and Connect $99 a month.

Is Pipedream being acquired?

A June 2026 article notes Workday agreed to acquire Pipedream in November 2025.

Can I self-host Pipedream?

No.

What are the main alternatives to Pipedream?

The main alternatives to Pipedream covered on Stack18 are n8n, Zapier and Activepieces. n8n may suit technical teams wanting per-execution pricing and self-hosting. Zapier may suit non-technical teams connecting many SaaS apps. Activepieces may suit teams wanting open-source automation with predictable pricing. See the Pipedream alternatives guide for a side-by-side table.

What are the pros and cons of Pipedream?

Strengths: Code-first with ~3,000 connectors. Mature hosted MCP server. Limitations: No self-hosting. Developer-oriented.

Who is Pipedream best for?

Pipedream may suit developers wanting code-first workflows and embedded integrations; AI agent builders; SaaS products embedding integrations. It may be less suited to non-technical users; self-hosting needs.

What integrations does Pipedream support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include GitHub, Slack, OpenAI, Google Sheets. API: Not reviewed for this profile.
