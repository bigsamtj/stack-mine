Quick answer

What is HelpDesk.com?

HelpDesk.com, formerly HelpDesk and now part of the Text platform alongside LiveChat and ChatBot, is a ticketing system and shared inbox for support teams. Paid plans are Essential ($19 per user per month billed yearly, $25 monthly) and Growth ($79 yearly, $99 monthly), plus quote-based Enterprise. Tickets are unlimited, there is a 14-day free trial, and AI is metered by resolutions with 10 included on Essential and 200 on Growth.

Overview

HelpDesk.com overview: who it is for and what it does.

HelpDesk.com is built by Text S.A., the company behind LiveChat and ChatBot, and works as the ticketing layer of the Text platform. It turns email and other conversations into tickets with assignment rules, canned responses, ticket rules, Messenger and SMS channels and an AI agent. Independent reviewers praise fast setup and ease of use, and Capterra shows a 4.6 average from about 180 reviews, though some reviewers note missing advanced features and no mobile app.

Pricing has just changed, so older guides are wrong. The live plan cards show Essential at $19 and Growth at $79 per user per month billed yearly ($25 and $99 monthly), with HelpDesk.com's own blog confirming the $19 and $79 figures. Older pages, including a still-published help center article, list Team at $29 and Business at $50 per agent. Tickets are unlimited on every plan with no setup fee, but several meters limit usage: Essential allows 3 ticketing rules, 3 workflow rules, 300 workflow executions a month, 60 days of conversation history and 10 AI resolutions a month, while Growth raises these to 10 rules, 1,000 executions, unlimited history and 200 AI resolutions.

The AI meter drives real costs. One resolution is when the AI directly solves a customer question, extra resolutions are auto-added in 50-packs at $49.50 (about $0.99 each), and reply suggestions and AI text edits are capped per user each month. An analysis by eesel, a competing AI helpdesk vendor, calculates that at four or more seats Essential plus top-up packs is cheaper than Growth on AI alone, so Growth is better justified by unlimited history, chat routing and reports. HelpDesk.com also has no connectors to other helpdesks, so adopting it means migrating the whole queue, and its HIPAA status is described inconsistently on its pricing page.

Main problem it solves

Turns a shared support inbox into a managed ticket queue with rules, canned responses, reports and AI help, so small teams can track and answer every request.

Typical customer segment

Small and mid-size support teams, existing LiveChat customers and teams that want a quick-to-launch ticketing system.

Target users

  • Support teams moving from a shared email inbox to tickets
  • LiveChat and ChatBot customers adding ticketing
  • Support managers who need rules, routing and reports
  • Small teams that want unlimited tickets without per-ticket fees

Primary use cases

What makes it distinct

  • Reply suggestions generated from past ticket conversations
  • 14-day trial with no card and no setup or platform fee
Key features

HelpDesk.com features explained.

Ticketing and workflow

Ticketing with unlimited tickets
Tickets are unlimited on every plan, and HelpDesk.com says it never charges per ticket or per visitor.
Ticketing rules and automated assignment
Essential includes 3 ticketing rules and automated assignment, and Growth raises this to 10 rules.
Workflow rules and executions
Essential allows 3 workflow rules and 300 executions a month, Growth 10 rules and 1,000 executions.
Canned responses and email templates
Canned responses are included, with shared canned responses and 10 email templates on Growth.

AI

AI agent resolutions
AI resolutions are metered, with 10 included a month on Essential and 200 on Growth, plus auto-added 50-packs at $49.50.
Reply suggestions and text edits
Reply suggestions and AI text edits are included per user: 5 each a month on Essential, and 50 and 100 on Growth.
Summaries and tag suggestions
Ticket and chat summaries and automated tag suggestions start on Growth, with multilingual AI in 48 languages.
Knowledge-based answers
Reply suggestions use past ticket conversations, and a similar-tickets feature searches recently solved tickets.

Reporting, administration and security

Reports and routing
Chat routing rules, shared canned responses and agent activity and performance reports start on Growth.
History and storage
Essential keeps 60 days of conversation and file history with 2 GB storage, while Growth offers unlimited history and 15 GB.
API and webhooks
Essential includes 10,000 API calls a month and Growth 20,000, with a 100,000-call top-up at $29.50 and webhooks for custom integrations.
Enterprise controls
Enterprise adds SSO (SAML 2.0), audit logs, white label, custom roles, multiple brands and scheduled report exports.

Strengths, limitations and best fit

HelpDesk.com pros and cons.

Strengths, limitations and best fit. Each point cites a source; editorial summaries are not customer reviews.

Strengths

  • Unlimited tickets on every plan with no per-ticket or setup fees keep pricing predictable. [3]
  • Reviewers consistently praise fast setup and ease of use, with a 4.6 average on Capterra. [3]
  • Reply suggestions use past ticket conversations, which is unusual at this price. [3]
  • It integrates tightly with LiveChat and ChatBot as part of the Text platform. [3]
  • The entry seat price of $19 per user per month matches Freshdesk Growth, with AI and Messenger included. [1]

Limitations

  • Essential limits conversation history to 60 days and caps AI resolutions at 10 a month. [3]
  • AI overage is auto-added in 50-resolution packs at $49.50, so a single extra resolution costs the full pack. [3]
  • The jump from Essential to Growth is $60 more per user per month. [3]
  • There are no connectors to other helpdesks, so adoption means migrating the queue. [3]
  • Reviewers cite a lack of advanced features and no mobile app. [4]

Best for

  • Small teams that want a quick-to-launch ticketing system with unlimited tickets
  • LiveChat customers moving to HelpDesk.com
  • Teams that value predictable seat pricing over per-ticket fees

May be less suited to

  • Teams that rely heavily on AI deflection and need a high resolution allowance on a small budget
  • Teams that need HIPAA assurances without written confirmation
  • Teams that need a mobile app and advanced reporting at the entry tier

FAQ

HelpDesk.com questions and answers

What is HelpDesk.com?

HelpDesk.com, previously called HelpDesk, is a ticketing system and shared inbox from Text S.A., the company behind LiveChat. It converts emails and chats into tickets with rules, templates, reports and AI.

How much does HelpDesk.com cost?

Essential costs $19 and Growth costs $79 per user per month billed yearly, or $25 and $99 billed monthly. Enterprise is custom. Older Team ($29) and Business ($50) prices are out of date.

Does HelpDesk.com have a free plan or trial?

There is no permanent free plan, but there is a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Does HelpDesk.com charge per ticket?

No. Tickets are unlimited on every plan. Usage meters apply to AI resolutions, workflow executions and API calls.

How does HelpDesk.com bill for AI?

AI resolutions are metered: 10 are included monthly on Essential and 200 on Growth. Extra resolutions are auto-added in 50-packs at $49.50, about $0.99 each, and billed at the next renewal.

Is HelpDesk.com the same as LiveChat?

No, but both are made by Text. HelpDesk.com is the ticketing product, while LiveChat is the live chat product, and they integrate closely.

Does HelpDesk.com integrate with Shopify or HubSpot?

Yes. Independent analysis lists Shopify, WooCommerce, HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, WordPress and Webflow, plus Slack notifications through workflows and webhooks.

What are alternatives to HelpDesk.com?

Common alternatives include Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout, Front and LiveChat.

What integrations does HelpDesk.com support?

Documented integrations and ecosystem items include LiveChat and ChatBot (Text platform), Shopify and WooCommerce, HubSpot and Salesforce, Klaviyo, WordPress and Webflow, Slack notifications via workflows, Webhooks and API. API details: An API is included with monthly call limits (10,000 on Essential, 20,000 on Growth), plus webhooks for custom connections.

What are the limitations of HelpDesk.com?

Essential limits conversation history to 60 days and caps AI resolutions at 10 a month. AI overage is auto-added in 50-resolution packs at $49.50, so a single extra resolution costs the full pack. The jump from Essential to Growth is $60 more per user per month.

Which platforms does HelpDesk.com run on?

HelpDesk.com is documented as available on: Web.

What are the main alternatives to HelpDesk.com?

The main alternatives to HelpDesk.com covered on Stack18 are Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout, Front and LiveChat. Zendesk may suit support teams that need ticketing plus omnichannel messaging. Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price. Help Scout may suit small email-first support teams.
