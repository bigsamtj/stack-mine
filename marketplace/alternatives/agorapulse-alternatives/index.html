Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Agorapulse?

The main alternatives to Agorapulse covered here are Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Sendible and SocialPilot. Sprout Social may suit brands that want strong analytics, inbox and listening for growing social teams, while Hootsuite may suit teams and enterprises that want scheduling, inbox, listening and approvals in one platform. None is best for everyone: the right social media management tool depends on team size, budget and which Agorapulse limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Agorapulse for documented reasons: free plan ended on 6 July 2026; seats are charged per user. Agorapulse remains a reasonable fit for agencies and teams that live in the social inbox and need reporting, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Agorapulse.

Moving off Agorapulse means reconnecting every social profile, re-creating scheduled posts and content libraries, rebuilding approval workflows, user roles and report templates, and exporting historical analytics you want to keep. Overlap both tools for one publishing cycle. Schedule one real week of posts and run one client or management report in each shortlisted tool.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Agorapulse data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Agorapulse.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
