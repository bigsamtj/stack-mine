Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to NordLayer?

The main alternatives to NordLayer covered here are Proton VPN for Business, Check Point Harmony SASE, Twingate and OpenVPN Access Server. Proton VPN for Business may suit privacy-focused teams needing business admin controls, while Check Point Harmony SASE may suit teams consolidating VPN and network security policy. None is best for everyone: the right ZTNA / VPN tool depends on team size, budget and which NordLayer limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond NordLayer for documented reasons: dedicated gateways cost extra; 5-user minimum. NordLayer remains a reasonable fit for growing teams wanting a business VPN with a path to zero trust, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from NordLayer.

Moving off NordLayer means re-creating access policies, groups and resources, deploying the new client or connectors, updating IP allowlists if egress IPs change, and reconnecting identity providers. Run old and new access side by side until every app is reachable. Replace VPN access for one team and three internal apps with each shortlisted tool, then compare setup effort, user experience and policy controls.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your NordLayer data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with NordLayer.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

NordLayer alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to NordLayer?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Proton VPN for Business, Check Point Harmony SASE, Twingate and OpenVPN Access Server. Proton VPN for Business may suit privacy-focused teams needing business admin controls. Check Point Harmony SASE may suit teams consolidating VPN and network security policy. Twingate may suit engineering-led teams replacing broad VPN access. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to NordLayer?

Yes. Twingate and OpenVPN Access Server list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which NordLayer alternative is best for small teams?

Proton VPN for Business, Check Point Harmony SASE, Twingate and OpenVPN Access Server are documented for small teams. Proton VPN for Business may suit privacy-focused teams needing business admin controls. Check Point Harmony SASE may suit teams consolidating VPN and network security policy. Twingate may suit engineering-led teams replacing broad VPN access.

Which NordLayer alternatives suit larger organisations?

Check Point Harmony SASE and Twingate are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from NordLayer?

The limitations documented on the NordLayer profile are: Dedicated gateways cost extra. 5-user minimum. Enterprise needs 200 users. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with NordLayer?

NordLayer may still suit growing teams wanting a business VPN with a path to zero trust; SaaS teams needing static IPs; remote companies.
