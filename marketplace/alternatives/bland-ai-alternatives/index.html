Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Bland AI?

The alternatives to Bland AI covered here are Vapi, Retell AI, Synthflow, ElevenLabs Agents and PolyAI. Each is reviewed on pricing structure, free plan, automation, AI and fit, and none is best for everyone: the right choice depends on team size, budget, workflow and how much control you need.

Teams look for Bland alternatives when they want provider choice, a no-code builder, or lower all-in costs at small volume.

V

Vapi

Vapi · Developer platform for building voice AI agents

Vapi is a developer platform for building, testing and deploying voice AI agents that answer and place phone calls or run in web and mobile apps. Developers choose their own speech-to-text, language model and text-to-speech providers, and Vapi orchestrates them with low latency, charging a per-minute hosting fee.

Pricing
$0.05 a minute hosting plus provider costs (often $0.10 to $0.30 all-in); Scale custom.
Free plan
No
Strengths
  • Maximum control over providers and latency.
  • Low platform fee and pay-as-you-go start.
Trade-offs versus Bland AI
  • Stacked provider costs; developer-oriented.

Retell AI

Retell AI · Voice AI agent platform with visual builder and API

Retell AI is a platform for building, testing and deploying AI voice agents for inbound and outbound phone calls, plus chat agents. Teams design agents in a visual conversation-flow builder or through the API, connect telephony, knowledge bases and tools, and pay per minute with no platform fee.

Synthflow AI · No-code voice AI agent platform (enterprise)

PolyAI · Enterprise voice AI agents (Agentic Dialog Platform)

PolyAI builds enterprise voice AI agents for customer service that answer phone calls naturally and resolve requests, and can also run on chat, SMS and other channels. Its Agentic Dialog Platform includes a no-code Agent Studio, an Agent Development Kit for developers and the Wren assistant, and ongoing use is priced per minute.

Switching

How to switch from Bland AI.

Moving away from Bland AI means porting numbers or reconfiguring SIP trunks, rebuilding call flows, re-testing voices and latency, and reviewing telephony costs. Run a pilot on one call type before moving all traffic.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with the current vendor.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Bland AI alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Bland AI?

No single alternative is best for everyone. Vapi, Retell AI, Synthflow, ElevenLabs Agents and PolyAI each suit different needs; test two or three on a real task before choosing.

Is there a free alternative to Bland AI?

Yes. ElevenLabs Agents document a free plan, free tier or free open-source use. Free tiers usually cap usage, and model costs may still apply.

What is the cheapest alternative to Bland AI?

Entry prices from the profiles: Vapi: $0.05 a minute hosting plus provider costs (often $0.10 to $0.30 all-in); Scale custom.; Retell AI: $0.07 to $0.31 a minute for voice; chat from $0.002 a message; $10 free credit.; Synthflow: Enterprise contracts from $30,000 a year (since June 2026).; ElevenLabs Agents: Free tier; agents about $0.08 a minute (burst $0.16); plans up to $990 a month; Enterprise custom.; PolyAI: Per-minute enterprise pricing; two free months for self-serve builders since May 2026. Compare total cost at your expected usage, not just the entry price.
