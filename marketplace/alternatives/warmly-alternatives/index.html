Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Warmly?

The alternatives to Warmly covered here are Qualified (Piper), Unify, Clay, 11x and Persana AI. Each is reviewed on pricing structure, free plan, automation, AI and fit, and none is best for everyone: the right choice depends on team size, budget, workflow and how much control you need.

Teams look for Warmly alternatives when they want month-to-month billing, a cheaper visitor ID tool, or a Salesforce-native inbound agent.

Qualified, part of Salesforce · Inbound AI SDR agent (Piper) for websites, Salesforce-native

Qualified is an agentic marketing platform built around Piper, an AI SDR agent that engages inbound website visitors in real time by text, voice and video, qualifies them, answers product questions, books meetings and follows up by email. It is Salesforce-native and sold in quoted Premier, Enterprise and Ultimate packages.

Switching

How to switch from Warmly.

Moving away from Warmly means exporting tables and workflows, reconnecting data providers and the CRM, and rebuilding signals, scoring and routing. Compare output on the same account list before switching.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with the current vendor.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Warmly alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Warmly?

No single alternative is best for everyone. Qualified (Piper), Unify, Clay, 11x and Persana AI each suit different needs; test two or three on a real task before choosing.

Is there a free alternative to Warmly?

Yes. Unify, Clay and Persana AI document a free plan, free tier or free open-source use. Free tiers usually cap usage, and model costs may still apply.

What is the cheapest alternative to Warmly?

Entry prices from the profiles: Qualified (Piper): Quoted annual packages; reported from about $40,000 a year.; Unify: Free for 3 seats; Base $20 and Pro $60 per seat a month; Business custom.; Clay: Free; Launch $185 and Growth $495 a month (less annually); Enterprise custom.; 11x: Alice Growth $3,750 a month billed annually; Pro and Enterprise custom.; Persana AI: Free; Starter $85, Growth $151, Pro $400 and Unlimited $600 a month; Enterprise custom. Compare total cost at your expected usage, not just the entry price.
