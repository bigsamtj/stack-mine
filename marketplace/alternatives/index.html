    Our approach

    How alternatives guides are built.

    At least three profiles

    A guide is published only when at least three alternatives have verified profiles, so shortlists are never padded.

    Reasons from the sources

    Why teams switch comes from pricing pages, documentation and independent sources, not from opinion.

    Trade-offs, not rankings

    Every alternative is shown with its limits. We do not rank products or declare a best option.

    Independent

    No paid placements, affiliate links, ratings or reviews. External links are labelled.

    What is inside

    What every guide includes.

    Short answer

    A plain-English list of the main alternatives and the one thing that separates them.

    Why teams switch

    The practical reasons people look elsewhere, from pricing to limits.

    Best fit, pricing, free plan and trade-offs side by side.

    Each alternative in detail

    Strengths, limits and a link to the full profile and comparison.

    Best-fit scenarios

    Which option suits which situation, without picking a winner.

    Switching guide

    What to export, rebuild and test before you move.
