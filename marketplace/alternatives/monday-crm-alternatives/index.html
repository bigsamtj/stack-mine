Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to monday CRM?

The main alternatives to monday CRM covered here are HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and Salesforce Sales Cloud. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually, while Pipedrive may suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM. None is best for everyone: the right CRM software depends on team size, budget and which monday CRM limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond monday CRM for documented reasons: three-seat minimum; basic lacks email integration. monday CRM remains a reasonable fit for sales teams already on monday.com, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with monday CRM in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Each monday CRM alternative described.

Pricing, strengths and trade-offs are taken from each product's profile, where every point is linked to a dated source.

H

HubSpot

HubSpot, Inc. · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

HubSpot is a customer platform from HubSpot, Inc. that combines a CRM (Smart CRM) with product modules called Hubs for sales, marketing, customer service, content, data and revenue operations.

Pricing
Free tools; Sales Hub from $9 per seat per month billed annually (Starter).
Free plan
Yes
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
Strengths
  • A free tier with no onboarding fee gives teams a low-risk place to start.
  • One platform covers CRM plus sales, marketing, service and other Hubs on shared customer data.
Trade-offs to weigh against monday CRM
  • Professional and Enterprise carry one-time onboarding fees of $1,500 and $3,500 on top of seat prices.
  • Custom objects are limited to Sales Hub Enterprise, which matters for teams with non-standard data models.

P

Pipedrive

Pipedrive · Sales CRM (SaaS)

Pipedrive is a sales CRM built around customisable deal pipelines, sold per user per month in four plans: Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate.

Pricing
No permanent free plan; plans reported from $14 per seat per month billed annually (Lite).
Free plan
No
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams
Strengths
  • Per-seat pricing based on users rather than contact volume makes costs predictable for stable teams.
  • The entry plan covers customisable pipelines and deal tracking at a reported low per-seat price.
Trade-offs to weigh against monday CRM
  • There is no permanent free plan, only a trial.
  • Automations are reported to need the Growth plan or above, so the cheapest plan lacks them.

Zoho CRM

Zoho Corporation · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

Zoho CRM is a customer relationship management product from Zoho Corporation, sold in five editions (Free, Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate) and licensed per user per month.

Pricing
Free for up to 3 users; paid editions reported from $14 per user per month billed annually.
Free plan
Yes
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
Strengths
  • A permanent free edition for up to three users lets very small teams start without a trial deadline.
  • Entry paid pricing is reported among the lowest for a full sales CRM, with unlock points clearly tied to editions.
Trade-offs to weigh against monday CRM
  • Per-user prices are only part of the cost: sources point to add-ons, storage, phone and configuration time.
  • Month-to-month billing is reported to cost noticeably more than annual billing.

Salesforce Sales Cloud

Salesforce, Inc. · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

Sales Cloud is the sales-focused CRM product from Salesforce, licensed per user in editions that add automation, customisation and AI capabilities as you move up.

Pricing
Per user per month by edition; Salesforce directs buyers to contact sales for detailed pricing.
Free plan

Switching

How to switch from monday CRM.

Moving off monday CRM usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your monday CRM data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with monday CRM.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

Documented reasons include: Three-seat minimum. Basic lacks email integration. Campaigns module still in beta.

HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and Salesforce Sales Cloud are documented as fitting small teams. Compare free plans, minimums and starting prices in the table above.

Is monday CRM still worth using?

How do I switch from monday CRM?
