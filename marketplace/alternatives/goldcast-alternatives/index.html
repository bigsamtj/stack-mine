Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Goldcast?

The main alternatives to Goldcast covered here are Airmeet, Cvent, Bizzabo and Accelevents. Airmeet may suit teams running interactive virtual events and webinars, while Cvent may suit enterprises running large multi-event programmes. Teams usually start looking beyond Goldcast for documented reasons: expensive per reviewers; key analytics need Pro or higher. Goldcast remains a reasonable fit for B2B marketing teams running polished webinars and virtual events, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Goldcast.

Moving off Goldcast means exporting attendee, registration and payment records, rebuilding registration forms, ticket types and event websites, reconfiguring badges and apps, and reconnecting CRM and payment integrations. Switch between event cycles, not mid-registration. Run one smaller event on each shortlisted platform, or a full test registration with real payment and badge printing, before committing to an annual contract.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Goldcast data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Goldcast.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
