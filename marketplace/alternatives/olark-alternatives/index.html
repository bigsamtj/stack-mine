Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Olark?

The main alternatives to Olark covered here are LiveChat, Tidio, Crisp, tawk.to and Freshchat. LiveChat may suit small and mid-size teams that want dependable website chat, while Tidio may suit small businesses and online stores that want live chat and a chatbot. None is best for everyone: the right help desk depends on team size, budget and which Olark limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Olark for documented reasons: most current sources show no free plan, though older reviews still mention a 20-chat free tier; PowerUps bill separately and stack on top of per-agent pricing. Olark remains a reasonable fit for small teams that want simple live chat with human agents, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Olark in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

LiveChat

Text, Inc. (LiveChat) · Live chat and customer service software (SaaS)

LiveChat is a customer service tool from Text, Inc. that lets support and sales teams chat with website visitors in real time, manage tickets and run reports. It is sold per agent on Starter ($19), Team ($49) and Business ($79) plans billed annually, plus a custom Enterprise plan, and offers a 14-day free trial with no card required. A separate AI customer service agent, ChatBot.com and KnowledgeBase products extend it.

  • There is no permanent free plan, so teams pay from the first agent after the trial.

Switching

How to switch from Olark.

Moving off Olark usually means exporting tickets, customers, macros and knowledge base articles, rebuilding views, SLAs and automation rules, and re-pointing support email, chat and social channels. Schedule the channel cut-over for a quiet period and keep the old system read-only for ticket history. Route one real channel, such as a secondary support inbox, through each shortlisted help desk for a week.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Olark data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Olark.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
