Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Jules?

The main alternatives to Jules covered here are Devin, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex and OpenHands. Devin may suit teams that want to delegate engineering tasks to a cloud agent, while GitHub Copilot may suit developers and teams standardised on GitHub. None is best for everyone: the right AI coding tool depends on team size, budget and which Jules limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Jules for documented reasons: task caps and concurrency limits apply on every plan; reports on the price of Google AI Ultra conflict. Jules remains a reasonable fit for delegating routine engineering tasks, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Jules in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Switching

How to switch from Jules.

Moving away from Jules means re-creating project instructions and rules files, re-adding MCP servers and other tool connections, and adjusting how your team reviews agent output. Run a pilot on a branch of a real repository before committing. Give each shortlisted tool the same real task on a branch of your own repository and review the diffs.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Jules data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Jules.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Jules alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Jules?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Devin, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex and OpenHands. Devin may suit teams that want to delegate engineering tasks to a cloud agent. GitHub Copilot may suit developers and teams standardised on GitHub. OpenAI Codex may suit ChatGPT subscribers who write code. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Jules?

Yes. Devin, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex and OpenHands list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Why do teams switch from Jules?

The limitations documented on the Jules profile are: Task caps and concurrency limits apply on every plan. Reports on the price of Google AI Ultra conflict. Uses Gemini models only. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Jules?

Jules may still suit delegating routine engineering tasks; GitHub-centred teams with Google AI plans; parallel background work.
