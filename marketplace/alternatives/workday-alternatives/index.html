Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Workday HCM?

The main alternatives to Workday HCM covered here are ADP Workforce Now, Rippling, BambooHR and Darwinbox. ADP Workforce Now may suit mid-size US employers that want payroll, benefits and HR from one provider, while Rippling may suit growing companies that want HR, payroll and IT on one platform. None is best for everyone: the right HR & payroll software depends on team size, budget and which Workday HCM limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Workday HCM for documented reasons: no published pricing or trial; multi-year contracts with annual uplifts. Workday HCM remains a reasonable fit for enterprises with 500+ employees, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Workday HCM.

Moving off Workday HCM usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Workday HCM data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Workday HCM.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
