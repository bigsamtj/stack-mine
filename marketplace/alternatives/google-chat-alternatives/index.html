Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Google Chat?

The main alternatives to Google Chat covered here are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoho Cliq and Mattermost. Slack may suit teams that want real-time channel-based communication, while Microsoft Teams may suit organisations already using Microsoft 365. None is best for everyone: the right collaboration software depends on team size, budget and which Google Chat limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Google Chat for documented reasons: not sold separately from Workspace; gemini in Chat not available on Starter. Google Chat remains a reasonable fit for Google Workspace organisations, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Google Chat in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Switching

How to switch from Google Chat.

Moving off Google Chat usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Google Chat data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Google Chat.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
