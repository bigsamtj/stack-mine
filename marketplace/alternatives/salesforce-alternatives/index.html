Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Salesforce Sales Cloud?

The alternatives to Salesforce Sales Cloud covered here are HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive and Freshsales. Each is reviewed on pricing structure, free plan, automation, AI and fit, and none is best for everyone: the right choice depends on company size, budget and how much customisation you need.

Teams look for Salesforce Sales Cloud alternatives for practical reasons: pricing that is confirmed through a sales conversation, edition limits that shape customisation and automation, add-ons that raise cost, or simply a smaller team that wants a lighter start. Salesforce is a strong fit for organisations that expect heavy customisation, so alternatives are not automatically better, only different.

The shortlist below covers HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive and Freshsales. We describe what independent and vendor sources support, mark gaps as unknown, and avoid declaring a winner. The right choice depends on your team size, budget, sales process and how much customisation or AI you need.

Why switch

Why teams look for Salesforce Sales Cloud alternatives.

The most common reasons teams evaluate other options, drawn from the sources reviewed.

01

Pricing visibility

Salesforce points buyers to sales for detailed pricing. Some alternatives publish more of their pricing, which makes early budgeting easier.

02

Free or lower-cost entry

HubSpot has a free tier, Zoho CRM has a free edition for three users, and Pipedrive offers a low-priced Lite plan.

03

Edition and add-on costs

Salesforce edition allowances and add-ons can raise total cost, which some teams want to avoid.

04

Right-sizing for a smaller team

Smaller teams may prefer a simpler, pipeline-first tool with fewer settings to administer.

Each Salesforce Sales Cloud alternative described.

HubSpot

HubSpot, Inc. · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

HubSpot is a customer platform from HubSpot, Inc. that combines a CRM (Smart CRM) with product modules called Hubs for sales, marketing, customer service, content, data and revenue operations.

Pricing
Free tools; Sales Hub from $9 per seat per month billed annually (Starter).
Free plan
Yes
Strengths
  • A free tier with no onboarding fee gives teams a low-risk place to start.
  • One platform covers CRM plus sales, marketing, service and other Hubs on shared customer data.
Trade-offs versus Salesforce Sales Cloud
  • Custom objects are limited to Enterprise, onboarding fees apply on Professional and Enterprise, and costs rise as seats and Hubs are added.

Zoho CRM

Zoho Corporation · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

Zoho CRM is a customer relationship management product from Zoho Corporation, sold in five editions (Free, Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate) and licensed per user per month.

Pricing
Free for up to 3 users; paid editions reported from $14 per user per month billed annually.
Free plan
Yes
Strengths

Trade-offs versus Salesforce Sales Cloud
  • Key features such as Blueprint and Zia AI sit on higher editions, and sources disagree on some edition details, so verify what you need.

Pipedrive

Pipedrive · Sales CRM (SaaS)

Pipedrive is a sales CRM built around customisable deal pipelines, sold per user per month in four plans: Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate.

Pricing
No permanent free plan; plans reported from $14 per seat per month billed annually (Lite).
Free plan
No
Strengths

Switching

How to switch from Salesforce Sales Cloud.

Moving off Salesforce means exporting accounts, contacts, opportunities and history, mapping custom fields and objects, and rebuilding automation such as flows. Because custom objects and automation limits depend on the plan in each alternative, confirm that the target plan supports your data model before you migrate, and run a pilot with a sample of real records.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with the current vendor.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Salesforce Sales Cloud alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Salesforce Sales Cloud?

No single alternative is best for everyone. HubSpot suits teams that want a free start and published pricing, Zoho CRM suits cost-conscious teams, and Pipedrive suits pipeline-focused small teams. Test them against your own process.

Is there a free alternative to Salesforce?

HubSpot offers free tools for up to two users, Zoho CRM and Freshsales each offer a free plan for up to three users, and Pipedrive has no permanent free plan.

Why do people leave Salesforce?

Common reasons include cost and add-ons, pricing that requires a sales conversation, and a preference for simpler tools. Others stay because they need Salesforce's customisation depth.

Can I migrate my Salesforce data?

Usually yes, but you must map custom fields, objects and automation. Check that the target plan supports your data model, and pilot first.
