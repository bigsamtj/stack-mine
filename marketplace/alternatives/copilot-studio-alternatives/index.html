Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Microsoft Copilot Studio?

The alternatives to Microsoft Copilot Studio covered here are Salesforce Agentforce, Dify, Stack AI, Relevance AI and NiCE Cognigy. Each is reviewed on pricing structure, free plan, automation, AI and fit, and none is best for everyone: the right choice depends on team size, budget, workflow and how much control you need.

Teams look for Copilot Studio alternatives when they are not on Microsoft 365, want predictable pricing, or need more developer control and model choice.

Each Microsoft Copilot Studio alternative described.

SA

Salesforce Agentforce

Salesforce · Enterprise agent platform on the Salesforce CRM (service, sales and employee agents)

Salesforce Agentforce is a platform for building, deploying and governing AI agents that work on Salesforce CRM and Data 360 data, for customer service, sales, field service and employee use. It is sold through consumption-based Flex Credits, per-user licences and add-ons, and bundled editions.

Pricing
Flex Credits $500 per 100,000 ($0.10 per action); user licence $5, add-ons $125 and editions from $550 per user per month.
Free plan
No
Strengths
  • Native access to Salesforce CRM data, flows and permissions.
  • Several buying models to match usage patterns.
Trade-offs versus Microsoft Copilot Studio
  • Requires Salesforce; complex pricing.

D

Dify

LangGenius · Open-source platform for agentic workflows, RAG and chatbots

Dify is an open-source platform from LangGenius for building and running LLM applications: agents, agentic workflows, chatbots and RAG pipelines, designed on a visual canvas and published as web apps, APIs, embeds or MCP-compatible tools. It is available as free self-hosted software and as Dify Cloud.

Pricing
Free self-hosted; Cloud Professional $59 and Team $159 a month; Enterprise custom.
Free plan
Yes
Strengths
  • Free self-hosting with the full visual builder.
  • Broad model and plugin support.
Trade-offs versus Microsoft Copilot Studio
  • Licence conditions; enterprise features gated.

SA

Stack AI

Stack AI, part of Asana · No-code enterprise AI agent builder ("AI Agent Factory")

Stack AI is a no-code platform for building AI agents and workflows that run inside enterprise back-office processes, combining a drag-and-drop builder, knowledge bases over private documents, interfaces and analytics, with private model deployment and compliance features. Asana acquired the company in May 2026.

Pricing
Free plan; Enterprise custom (Builder $99 and Team $499 plans withdrawn in 2026).
Free plan
Yes

  • No-code for non-engineers.
Trade-offs versus Microsoft Copilot Studio

Relevance AI

Relevance AI · AI workforce platform for building and managing teams of AI agents

Relevance AI is a no-code and low-code platform for building AI agents and multi-agent workforces that research, write, update systems and run calls and meetings, with tools, knowledge and integrations. It bills two meters: Actions for each tool run and Vendor Credits for AI model costs passed through at wholesale.

Docs list Pro from $19 and Team from $234 a month (annual); public page shows Enterprise only.

NiCE Cognigy (Cognigy.AI) is an enterprise conversational and agentic AI platform for building AI agents that understand, reason and act across web, messaging and phone channels in more than 100 languages, integrated with contact-centre and enterprise systems. Switching

How to switch from Microsoft Copilot Studio.

Moving away from Microsoft Copilot Studio means reviewing contract terms, reconnecting enterprise systems and identity, rebuilding agents or processes, and repeating security and compliance review. Pilot one process before migrating.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with the current vendor.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Microsoft Copilot Studio alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Microsoft Copilot Studio?

No single alternative is best for everyone. Salesforce Agentforce, Dify, Stack AI, Relevance AI and NiCE Cognigy each suit different needs; test two or three on a real task before choosing.

Is there a free alternative to Microsoft Copilot Studio?

Yes. Dify and Stack AI document a free plan, free tier or free open-source use. Free tiers usually cap usage, and model costs may still apply.

What is the cheapest alternative to Microsoft Copilot Studio?

Entry prices from the profiles: Salesforce Agentforce: Flex Credits $500 per 100,000 ($0.10 per action); user licence $5, add-ons $125 and editions from $550 per user per month.; Dify: Free self-hosted; Cloud Professional $59 and Team $159 a month; Enterprise custom.; Stack AI: Free plan; Enterprise custom (Builder $99 and Team $499 plans withdrawn in 2026).; Relevance AI: Docs list Pro from $19 and Team from $234 a month (annual); public page shows Enterprise only.; NiCE Cognigy: Custom enterprise pricing. Compare total cost at your expected usage, not just the entry price.

Is there an open-source alternative to Microsoft Copilot Studio?

Yes. Dify are documented as open source or source-available.
