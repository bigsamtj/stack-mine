Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Houzz Pro?

The main alternatives to Houzz Pro covered here are Buildertrend, Buildxact, Contractor Foreman and Fieldwire. Buildertrend may suit home builders and remodelers working with homeowners, while Buildxact may suit residential builders who estimate and quote a lot. Teams usually start looking beyond Houzz Pro for documented reasons: shallow project management; lighter document control. Houzz Pro remains a reasonable fit for remodelers and designers who want homeowner leads, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Houzz Pro.

Moving off Houzz Pro means exporting project records, drawings, RFIs, submittals, daily logs, budgets and change orders, rebuilding cost codes, templates and permissions, reconnecting accounting, and retraining office and field users. Switch between projects, not mid-job. Run one live project in each shortlisted tool, from estimate and schedule to daily logs and a change order, with at least one superintendent using the mobile app.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Houzz Pro data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Houzz Pro.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
