Switching

How to switch from PracticePanther.

Moving off PracticePanther means exporting matters, contacts, documents, time entries, invoices and trust ledgers, reconciling trust balances to the penny, rebuilding templates and workflows, and reconnecting email, calendars and payment processing. Migrate after a month-end trust reconciliation. Run a handful of real matters through each shortlisted tool, from intake and conflict check to time entry, invoice, payment and trust reconciliation.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your PracticePanther data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with PracticePanther.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
