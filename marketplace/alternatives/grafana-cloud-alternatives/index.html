Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Grafana Cloud?

The main alternatives to Grafana Cloud covered here are Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty and GitHub. Datadog may suit engineering teams wanting full-stack observability in one platform, while Sentry may suit developers who want error tracking and performance monitoring. None is best for everyone: the right developer tool depends on team size, budget and which Grafana Cloud limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Grafana Cloud for documented reasons: usage costs can grow quickly; scrape interval affects metric billing. Grafana Cloud remains a reasonable fit for teams using Prometheus and OpenTelemetry, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Grafana Cloud.

Moving off Grafana Cloud usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Grafana Cloud data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Grafana Cloud.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Grafana Cloud alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Grafana Cloud?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty and GitHub. Datadog may suit engineering teams wanting full-stack observability in one platform. Sentry may suit developers who want error tracking and performance monitoring. PagerDuty may suit engineering teams running on-call. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Grafana Cloud?

Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty and GitHub document a free plan or free tier. Free tiers usually have user, volume or feature limits, so check them against your needs.

Why do teams switch from Grafana Cloud?

Documented reasons include: Usage costs can grow quickly. Scrape interval affects metric billing. Enterprise minimum commit.

Which Grafana Cloud alternative is best for small businesses?

Sentry, PagerDuty and GitHub are documented as fitting small teams. Compare free plans, minimums and starting prices in the table above.

Which Grafana Cloud alternative suits large organisations?

Datadog, Sentry, PagerDuty and GitHub are documented as fitting large enterprises. Check security, administration and support terms with each vendor.

How much do Grafana Cloud alternatives cost?

Datadog: Infrastructure Pro $15 per host; APM $31 per host; logs $0.10 per GB ingested (annual). Sentry: Developer free; Team $26 and Business $80 a month billed annually. PagerDuty: Free for 5 users; Professional $21 per user a month billed annually. GitHub: Free; Team $4 and Enterprise $21 per user per month.

Is Grafana Cloud still worth using?

Grafana Cloud remains a reasonable fit for teams using Prometheus and OpenTelemetry. Generous permanent free tier. Compare it with the alternatives before renewing.

How do I switch from Grafana Cloud?

Export a sample of your Grafana Cloud data, test an import into the new tool, rebuild automations and integrations, pilot with one team and check your contract’s notice period before cancelling.
