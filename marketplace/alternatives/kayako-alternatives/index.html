Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Kayako?

The main alternatives to Kayako covered here are Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout and Zoho Desk. Zendesk may suit support teams that need ticketing plus omnichannel messaging, while Freshdesk may suit small and mid-size teams that want ticketing with automation at a low entry price. None is best for everyone: the right customer support software depends on team size, budget and which Kayako limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Kayako for documented reasons: pricing is described inconsistently and costs rise with AI volume; steep admin learning curve and reported lag at high volume. Kayako remains a reasonable fit for teams starting fresh with AI-first support, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Kayako in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Zendesk

Zendesk, Inc. · Customer service and ticketing platform (SaaS)

Zendesk is a customer service software platform from Zendesk, Inc. that offers ticketing and an omnichannel Customer Service Suite, priced per agent per month with AI agents billed per automated resolution.

Pricing
Support Team reported at $19 per agent per month; Suite Team $55 (annual billing).
Free plan
No
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
Strengths
  • A clear plan ladder from ticketing-only to a full omnichannel Suite.
  • AI agents are included in plans, with an allowance of automated resolutions per agent.
Trade-offs to weigh against Kayako
  • Per-agent pricing means costs rise with headcount, and monthly billing is reported about 30 percent higher.
  • AI resolutions beyond the allowance are billed per resolution, so costs vary with volume.

Switching

How to switch from Kayako.

Moving off Kayako usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Kayako data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Kayako.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
