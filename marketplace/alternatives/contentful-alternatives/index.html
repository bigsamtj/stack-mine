Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Contentful?

The main alternatives to Contentful covered here are Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi and HubSpot Content Hub. Sanity may suit teams prioritising structured content and a customisable editor, while Storyblok may suit marketing teams wanting visual editing on a headless stack. None is best for everyone: the right CMS depends on team size, budget and which Contentful limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Contentful for documented reasons: steep jump from free to $300; learning curve for editors. Contentful remains a reasonable fit for enterprise content operations delivering to many channels, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Contentful.

Moving off Contentful means exporting content and media, rebuilding templates or front ends, re-creating forms and integrations, and mapping 301 redirects for every URL so search rankings carry over. Keep the old site live until redirects and metadata are verified. Rebuild one high-traffic page and one blog post in each shortlisted CMS, then have a marketer publish an update without developer help.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Contentful data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Contentful.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

Contentful alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Contentful?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi and HubSpot Content Hub. Sanity may suit teams prioritising structured content and a customisable editor. Storyblok may suit marketing teams wanting visual editing on a headless stack. Is there a free alternative to Contentful?

Yes. Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi and HubSpot Content Hub list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which Contentful alternative is best for small teams?

Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi and HubSpot Content Hub are documented for small teams. Sanity may suit teams prioritising structured content and a customisable editor. Storyblok may suit marketing teams wanting visual editing on a headless stack. Strapi may suit developers wanting a self-hosted headless CMS without usage fees.

Which Contentful alternatives suit larger organisations?

Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi and HubSpot Content Hub are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Contentful?

The limitations documented on the Contentful profile are: Steep jump from free to $300. Learning curve for editors. Needs developers to build front ends. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Contentful?

Contentful may still suit enterprise content operations delivering to many channels; global brands with many sites; product teams with apps.
