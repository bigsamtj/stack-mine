Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Pipedrive?

The main alternatives to Pipedrive covered here are HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Freshsales. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually, while Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. None is best for everyone: the right CRM depends on team size, budget and which Pipedrive limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Pipedrive for documented reasons: there is no permanent free plan, only a trial; automations are reported to need the Growth plan or above, so the cheapest plan lacks them. Pipedrive remains a reasonable fit for small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Pipedrive in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Each Pipedrive alternative described.

H

HubSpot

HubSpot, Inc. · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

HubSpot is a customer platform from HubSpot, Inc. that combines a CRM (Smart CRM) with product modules called Hubs for sales, marketing, customer service, content, data and revenue operations.

Pricing
Free tools; Sales Hub from $9 per seat per month billed annually (Starter).
Free plan
Yes
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
Strengths
  • A free tier with no onboarding fee gives teams a low-risk place to start.
  • One platform covers CRM plus sales, marketing, service and other Hubs on shared customer data.
Trade-offs to weigh against Pipedrive
  • Professional and Enterprise carry one-time onboarding fees of $1,500 and $3,500 on top of seat prices.
  • Custom objects are limited to Sales Hub Enterprise, which matters for teams with non-standard data models.

Zoho CRM

Zoho Corporation · CRM and sales platform (SaaS)

Zoho CRM is a customer relationship management product from Zoho Corporation, sold in five editions (Free, Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate) and licensed per user per month.

Pricing
Free for up to 3 users; paid editions reported from $14 per user per month billed annually.
Free plan
Yes
Team size
Small teams, Mid-size teams, Large enterprises
Strengths
  • A permanent free edition for up to three users lets very small teams start without a trial deadline.
  • Entry paid pricing is reported among the lowest for a full sales CRM, with unlock points clearly tied to editions.
Trade-offs to weigh against Pipedrive
  • Per-user prices are only part of the cost: sources point to add-ons, storage, phone and configuration time.
  • Month-to-month billing is reported to cost noticeably more than annual billing.

Switching

How to switch from Pipedrive.

Moving off Pipedrive usually means exporting contacts, companies, deals and activity history, rebuilding pipelines, custom fields and automations, and reconnecting email, calendar and telephony. Map every field before the first import and run both systems side by side for at least one sales cycle. Import a real slice of your pipeline and run a week of actual deals through each shortlisted CRM.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Pipedrive data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Pipedrive.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Pipedrive alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Pipedrive?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Freshsales. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. Salesforce Sales Cloud may suit organisations that expect to customise their sales process extensively. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Pipedrive?

Yes. HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Freshsales list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which Pipedrive alternative is best for small teams?

HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Freshsales are documented for small teams. HubSpot may suit small teams that want to start on a free CRM and upgrade gradually. Zoho CRM may suit very small teams that want a free CRM with no expiry. Salesforce Sales Cloud may suit organisations that expect to customise their sales process extensively.

Which Pipedrive alternatives suit larger organisations?

HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce Sales Cloud and Freshsales are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Pipedrive?

The limitations documented on the Pipedrive profile are: There is no permanent free plan, only a trial. Automations are reported to need the Growth plan or above, so the cheapest plan lacks them. Add-ons such as Campaigns and LeadBooster are priced separately and can raise the total cost. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Pipedrive?

Pipedrive may still suit small sales teams that want a pipeline-first CRM; teams that prefer per-seat pricing over contact-based pricing; teams that will add outreach tools as optional add-ons.
