Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Proton Pass?

The main alternatives to Proton Pass covered here are Bitwarden, NordPass, 1Password and Dashlane. Bitwarden may suit budget-conscious teams and individuals who value open source, while NordPass may suit budget-conscious individuals and small teams. None is best for everyone: the right password manager depends on team size, budget and which Proton Pass limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Proton Pass for documented reasons: free plan lacks 2FA and sharing; latest public audit dates from 2023. Proton Pass remains a reasonable fit for privacy-minded individuals and teams wanting email aliases, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Proton Pass.

Moving off Proton Pass usually means exporting your vault (often as an encrypted file or CSV), importing it into the new manager, securely deleting any plaintext export, re-creating shared vaults and permissions, and reconfiguring SSO or SCIM. Keep both active until every user has migrated. Import a test vault into each shortlisted password manager and check autofill on your most-used sites and devices.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Proton Pass data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Proton Pass.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
