Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Yoast SEO?

The main alternatives to Yoast SEO covered here are Rank Math, Semrush, Surfer and Screaming Frog SEO Spider. Rank Math may suit wordPress sites wanting advanced free features, while Semrush may suit marketing teams that want one broad SEO and competitive research suite. None is best for everyone: the right SEO software depends on team size, budget and which Yoast SEO limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Yoast SEO for documented reasons: redirects require Premium; one focus keyword on the free version. Yoast SEO remains a reasonable fit for writers and bloggers new to SEO, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Yoast SEO.

Moving off Yoast SEO usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Yoast SEO data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Yoast SEO.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Yoast SEO alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Yoast SEO?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Rank Math, Semrush, Surfer and Screaming Frog SEO Spider. Rank Math may suit wordPress sites wanting advanced free features. Semrush may suit marketing teams that want one broad SEO and competitive research suite. Surfer may suit content teams that want on-page optimisation guidance while writing. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Yoast SEO?

Rank Math, Surfer and Screaming Frog SEO Spider document a free plan or free tier. Free tiers usually have user, volume or feature limits, so check them against your needs.

Why do teams switch from Yoast SEO?

Documented reasons include: Redirects require Premium. One focus keyword on the free version. Per-site pricing adds up for agencies.

Which Yoast SEO alternative is best for small businesses?

Rank Math, Semrush, Surfer and Screaming Frog SEO Spider are documented as fitting small teams. Compare free plans, minimums and starting prices in the table above.

Which Yoast SEO alternative suits large organisations?

Semrush and Screaming Frog SEO Spider are documented as fitting large enterprises. Check security, administration and support terms with each vendor.

How much do Yoast SEO alternatives cost?

Rank Math: Free; Pro $59 a year. Semrush: Pro reported at $139.95 per month ($117.33 billed annually); extra users from $45 a month. Surfer: Essential (now Standard) reported at $99 a month or $79 billed annually. Screaming Frog SEO Spider: Free up to 500 URLs; €245 per licence per year.

Is Yoast SEO still worth using?

Yoast SEO remains a reasonable fit for writers and bloggers new to SEO. Simplest setup among major SEO plugins. Compare it with the alternatives before renewing.

How do I switch from Yoast SEO?

Export a sample of your Yoast SEO data, test an import into the new tool, rebuild automations and integrations, pilot with one team and check your contract’s notice period before cancelling.
