Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Qualtrics?

The main alternatives to Qualtrics covered here are SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Formstack and Google Forms. SurveyMonkey may suit teams running regular surveys with built-in analysis, while Typeform may suit marketers who want engaging, branded forms and surveys. None is best for everyone: the right form builder depends on team size, budget and which Qualtrics limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Qualtrics for documented reasons: no public pricing; analytics add-ons cost extra. Qualtrics remains a reasonable fit for enterprises and universities running advanced research and XM programmes, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Qualtrics.

Moving off Qualtrics means exporting responses (usually CSV), rebuilding forms and logic, reconnecting integrations and webhooks, and updating embeds and links on your site. Keep old forms live until new ones are tested. Rebuild your most important form in each shortlisted tool and test it on mobile with real respondents.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Qualtrics data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Qualtrics.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Qualtrics alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Qualtrics?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Formstack and Google Forms. SurveyMonkey may suit teams running regular surveys with built-in analysis. Typeform may suit marketers who want engaging, branded forms and surveys. Formstack may suit organisations automating data collection, documents and signatures. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Qualtrics?

Yes. SurveyMonkey, Typeform and Google Forms list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which Qualtrics alternative is best for small teams?

SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Formstack and Google Forms are documented for small teams. SurveyMonkey may suit teams running regular surveys with built-in analysis. Typeform may suit marketers who want engaging, branded forms and surveys. Formstack may suit organisations automating data collection, documents and signatures.

Which Qualtrics alternatives suit larger organisations?

SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Formstack and Google Forms are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Qualtrics?

The limitations documented on the Qualtrics profile are: No public pricing. Analytics add-ons cost extra. Contracts typically tens of thousands of dollars. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Qualtrics?

Qualtrics may still suit enterprises and universities running advanced research and XM programmes; CX and EX leaders; market research teams.
