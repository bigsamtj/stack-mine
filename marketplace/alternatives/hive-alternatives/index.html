Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Hive?

The main alternatives to Hive covered here are Asana, ClickUp, monday.com, Wrike and Trello. Asana may suit teams that want a free start and a clear upgrade path, while ClickUp may suit teams that want a generous free plan to evaluate. None is best for everyone: the right project management tool depends on team size, budget and which Hive limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Hive for documented reasons: add-ons such as automations, proofing, timesheets and analytics can nearly triple the base per-user price; free plan limits are reported inconsistently, including a 10-member cap. Hive remains a reasonable fit for small teams that want a free project management tool with AI, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Hive in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Switching

How to switch from Hive.

Moving off Hive usually means exporting projects, tasks, comments and attachments, rebuilding templates, custom fields and automations, and reconnecting chat, calendar and file tools. Migrate active projects first and archive finished ones rather than moving everything. Run one live project with its real team in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Hive data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Hive.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

Which Hive alternative is best for small teams?

Which Hive alternatives suit larger organisations?

Why do teams switch from Hive?
