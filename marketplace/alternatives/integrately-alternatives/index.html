Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Integrately?

The main alternatives to Integrately covered here are Zapier, Pabbly Connect, Make and IFTTT. Zapier may suit non-technical teams connecting many SaaS apps, while Pabbly Connect may suit budget-conscious small businesses. None is best for everyone: the right workflow automation software depends on team size, budget and which Integrately limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Integrately for documented reasons: starter caps premium apps; automations stop at the task limit. Integrately remains a reasonable fit for small businesses new to automation, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Integrately.

Moving off Integrately usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Integrately data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Integrately.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Integrately alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Integrately?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Zapier, Pabbly Connect, Make and IFTTT. Zapier may suit non-technical teams connecting many SaaS apps. Pabbly Connect may suit budget-conscious small businesses. Make may suit teams building complex, branching automations on a budget. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Integrately?

Zapier, Pabbly Connect, Make and IFTTT document a free plan or free tier. Free tiers usually have user, volume or feature limits, so check them against your needs.

Why do teams switch from Integrately?

Documented reasons include: Starter caps premium apps. Automations stop at the task limit. Taxes extra.

Which Integrately alternative is best for small businesses?

Zapier, Pabbly Connect, Make and IFTTT are documented as fitting small teams. Compare free plans, minimums and starting prices in the table above.

Which Integrately alternative suits large organisations?

Zapier are documented as fitting large enterprises. Check security, administration and support terms with each vendor.

How much do Integrately alternatives cost?

Zapier: Free (100 tasks, two-step Zaps); Professional $19.99 annual ($29.99 monthly; 750 tasks); Team $69 annual ($103.50; 2,000 tasks, 25 users). Pabbly Connect: Free for 100 tasks; Standard $15 a month billed annually for 12,000 tasks. Make: Free (1,000 credits); Core $9 (or ~$12 annual per competitor); Pro $16; Teams $29 a month (from 10,000 credits). IFTTT: Free for two applets; Pro $2.99 a month billed annually.

Is Integrately still worth using?

Integrately remains a reasonable fit for small businesses new to automation. Ready-made one-click automations. Compare it with the alternatives before renewing.

How do I switch from Integrately?

Export a sample of your Integrately data, test an import into the new tool, rebuild automations and integrations, pilot with one team and check your contract’s notice period before cancelling.
