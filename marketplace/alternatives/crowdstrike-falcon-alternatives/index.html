Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to CrowdStrike Falcon?

The main alternatives to CrowdStrike Falcon covered here are SentinelOne Singularity, Microsoft Defender for Business, Sophos Intercept X and Huntress. SentinelOne Singularity may suit teams wanting autonomous EDR with rollback, while Microsoft Defender for Business may suit Microsoft 365 businesses wanting low-cost built-in endpoint security. None is best for everyone: the right endpoint security tool depends on team size, budget and which CrowdStrike Falcon limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond CrowdStrike Falcon for documented reasons: managed SOC costs extra; falcon Go capped at 100 devices. CrowdStrike Falcon remains a reasonable fit for organisations wanting top-tier EDR with an optional managed SOC, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from CrowdStrike Falcon.

Moving off CrowdStrike Falcon means deploying the new agent to every device, removing the old agent cleanly to avoid conflicts, re-creating policies, exclusions and alert routing, and reconnecting SIEM, RMM and identity integrations. Keep devices protected throughout by staging the swap in groups. Deploy each shortlisted tool to a pilot group, run a safe attack simulation and compare detection, alert quality and response time.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your CrowdStrike Falcon data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with CrowdStrike Falcon.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

CrowdStrike Falcon alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to CrowdStrike Falcon?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are SentinelOne Singularity, Microsoft Defender for Business, Sophos Intercept X and Huntress. SentinelOne Singularity may suit teams wanting autonomous EDR with rollback. Microsoft Defender for Business may suit Microsoft 365 businesses wanting low-cost built-in endpoint security. Sophos Intercept X may suit SMBs and mid-market wanting strong anti-ransomware and optional MDR. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to CrowdStrike Falcon?

None of the alternatives covered here documents a permanent free plan. See the Endpoint Security Software category for other free options.

Which CrowdStrike Falcon alternative is best for small teams?

SentinelOne Singularity, Microsoft Defender for Business, Sophos Intercept X and Huntress are documented for small teams. SentinelOne Singularity may suit teams wanting autonomous EDR with rollback. Microsoft Defender for Business may suit Microsoft 365 businesses wanting low-cost built-in endpoint security. Sophos Intercept X may suit SMBs and mid-market wanting strong anti-ransomware and optional MDR.

Which CrowdStrike Falcon alternatives suit larger organisations?

SentinelOne Singularity and Sophos Intercept X are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from CrowdStrike Falcon?

The limitations documented on the CrowdStrike Falcon profile are: Managed SOC costs extra. Falcon Go capped at 100 devices. Higher tiers add up per device. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with CrowdStrike Falcon?
