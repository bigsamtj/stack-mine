Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Synthesia?

The main alternatives to Synthesia covered here are HeyGen, InVideo AI, Descript and Camtasia. HeyGen may suit marketers making avatar videos, while InVideo AI may suit faceless YouTube creators. None is best for everyone: the right video hosting & recording software depends on team size, budget and which Synthesia limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Synthesia for documented reasons: low minute caps on lower plans; translation only on Enterprise. Synthesia remains a reasonable fit for l&D teams making training videos, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Synthesia.

Moving off Synthesia usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Synthesia data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Synthesia.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
