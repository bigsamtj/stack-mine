Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Lark?

The main alternatives to Lark covered here are Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion and Zoho Cliq. Google Workspace may suit small and mid-size businesses that want cloud-first email and collaboration, while Microsoft Teams may suit organisations already using Microsoft 365. None is best for everyone: the right collaboration tool depends on team size, budget and which Lark limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Lark for documented reasons: starter is limited to 20 users, 18 months of message history and 1-on-1 video meetings; starter and Basic restrict video meetings to 1-on-1 sessions, so larger meetings require Pro. Lark remains a reasonable fit for small and mid-size teams that want an all-in-one suite at a low per-user price, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Lark in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Google Workspace

Google · Cloud productivity and collaboration suite (email, docs, meetings, storage)

Google Workspace is Google's subscription suite of business email, video meetings, document editing, shared storage and admin controls, sold per user per month. Business plans are Starter ($7), Standard ($14) and Plus ($22) on a one-year commitment, or $8.40, $16.80 and $26.40 on flexible monthly billing, with custom-priced Enterprise plans. Gemini AI has been included in all plans since January 2025.

Switching

How to switch from Lark.

Moving off Lark usually means exporting messages, pages and files, checking how links, mentions and permissions survive the import, and rebuilding integrations and bots. Agree a cut-over date so conversations do not split across two tools. Move one team and its everyday channels or spaces into each shortlisted tool for two weeks.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Lark data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Lark.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Lark alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Lark?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion and Zoho Cliq. Google Workspace may suit small and mid-size businesses that want cloud-first email and collaboration. Microsoft Teams may suit organisations already using Microsoft 365. Slack may suit teams that want real-time channel-based communication. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Lark?

Yes. Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion and Zoho Cliq list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which Lark alternative is best for small teams?

Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion and Zoho Cliq are documented for small teams. Google Workspace may suit small and mid-size businesses that want cloud-first email and collaboration. Microsoft Teams may suit organisations already using Microsoft 365. Slack may suit teams that want real-time channel-based communication.

Which Lark alternatives suit larger organisations?

Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Notion are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Lark?

The limitations documented on the Lark profile are: Starter is limited to 20 users, 18 months of message history and 1-on-1 video meetings. Starter and Basic restrict video meetings to 1-on-1 sessions, so larger meetings require Pro. Enterprise pricing is not published and advanced security and compliance are limited to Enterprise. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Lark?

Lark may still suit small and mid-size teams that want an all-in-one suite at a low per-user price; multilingual teams that need built-in real-time translation; teams that want chat, docs, wiki and workflow automation in one app.
