The main alternatives to Chatbase covered here are CustomGPT.ai, Botpress, Voiceflow and Landbot. Switching

How to switch from Chatbase.

Moving off Chatbase means exporting conversation flows, knowledge sources and intents, rebuilding them in the new builder, reconnecting channels such as WhatsApp and Instagram, re-creating human handoff rules and retesting answers. Run both bots on a share of traffic before switching fully. Build the same pilot bot covering your top 20 questions in each shortlisted tool and compare accuracy, handoff and cost per conversation.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Chatbase data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Chatbase.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
