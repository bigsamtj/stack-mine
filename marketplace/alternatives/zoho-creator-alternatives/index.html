Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Zoho Creator?

The main alternatives to Zoho Creator covered here are Power Apps, Google AppSheet, Quickbase and Kissflow. Power Apps may suit Microsoft 365 organisations building internal business apps, while Google AppSheet may suit Google Workspace organisations building simple apps. None is best for everyone: the right low-code development platform depends on team size, budget and which Zoho Creator limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Zoho Creator for documented reasons: deluge has a learning curve; advanced templates need HTML and CSS. Zoho Creator remains a reasonable fit for SMBs building internal tools, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Zoho Creator.

Moving off Zoho Creator usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Zoho Creator data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Zoho Creator.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Zoho Creator alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Zoho Creator?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Power Apps, Google AppSheet, Quickbase and Kissflow. Power Apps may suit Microsoft 365 organisations building internal business apps. Google AppSheet may suit Google Workspace organisations building simple apps. Quickbase may suit regulated operations teams needing governed apps. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Zoho Creator?

None of the shortlisted alternatives documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Free tiers usually have user, volume or feature limits, so check them against your needs.

Why do teams switch from Zoho Creator?

Documented reasons include: Deluge has a learning curve. Advanced templates need HTML and CSS. Support is mainly live chat.

Which Zoho Creator alternative is best for small businesses?

Power Apps and Google AppSheet are documented as fitting small teams. Compare free plans, minimums and starting prices in the table above.

Which Zoho Creator alternative suits large organisations?

Power Apps, Google AppSheet, Quickbase and Kissflow are documented as fitting large enterprises. Check security, administration and support terms with each vendor.

How much do Zoho Creator alternatives cost?

Power Apps: Premium $20 per user a month (annual, unlimited apps); $12 per user at 2,000+ seats; Dataverse capacity $40/GB a month; Developer Plan free (not production). Google AppSheet: Starter $5; Core $10 (included in most paid Google Workspace plans); Enterprise Plus $20 per user a month; Publisher Pro $50 per app a month. Quickbase: Team from $35; Business from $55 per user a month (annual), each with an unpublished platform minimum; Enterprise quote. Kissflow: Basic reported at about $2,500 a month on an annual commitment.

Is Zoho Creator still worth using?

Zoho Creator remains a reasonable fit for SMBs building internal tools. Per-user pricing from $8 a month billed annually. Compare it with the alternatives before renewing.

How do I switch from Zoho Creator?

Export a sample of your Zoho Creator data, test an import into the new tool, rebuild automations and integrations, pilot with one team and check your contract’s notice period before cancelling.
