Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Postman?

The main alternatives to Postman covered here are GitHub, Sentry, Bitbucket and CircleCI. GitHub may suit most software teams hosting code and running CI in one place, while Sentry may suit developers who want error tracking and performance monitoring. None is best for everyone: the right developer tool depends on team size, budget and which Postman limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Postman for documented reasons: free plan is limited to one user; team and Enterprise require annual billing. Postman remains a reasonable fit for developers and teams building and testing APIs, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Below, every option is compared with Postman in one table, then described with its strengths, trade-offs and pricing, each taken from its own sourced profile. Anything the sources do not document is marked as unknown.

Switching

How to switch from Postman.

Moving off Postman means migrating repositories, pipelines, configuration, secrets and history, updating integrations and webhooks, and retraining the team. Run both in parallel for at least one release cycle before switching off the old tool. Move one real service or repository end to end to each shortlisted tool and measure setup time, cost and reliability.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Postman data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Postman.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Postman alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Postman?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are GitHub, Sentry, Bitbucket and CircleCI. GitHub may suit most software teams hosting code and running CI in one place. Sentry may suit developers who want error tracking and performance monitoring. Bitbucket may suit teams already using Jira and Atlassian tools. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Postman?

Yes. GitHub, Sentry, Bitbucket and CircleCI list a free plan or free tier in their sourced profiles. Free plans usually cap users, records or usage, so check the limits on each profile before relying on one.

Which Postman alternative is best for small teams?

GitHub, Sentry, Bitbucket and CircleCI are documented for small teams. GitHub may suit most software teams hosting code and running CI in one place. Sentry may suit developers who want error tracking and performance monitoring. Bitbucket may suit teams already using Jira and Atlassian tools.

Which Postman alternatives suit larger organisations?

GitHub, Sentry, Bitbucket and CircleCI are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Postman?

The limitations documented on the Postman profile are: Free plan is limited to one user. Team and Enterprise require annual billing. AI credits are metered. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Postman?

Postman may still suit developers and teams building and testing APIs; QA teams; enterprises standardising API workflows.
