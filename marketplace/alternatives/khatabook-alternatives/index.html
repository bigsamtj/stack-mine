Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Khatabook?

The main alternatives to Khatabook covered here are Vyapar, myBillBook, Zoho Books and TallyPrime. Vyapar may suit Indian micro and small businesses that bill on GST, while myBillBook may suit Indian small businesses billing on GST. None is best for everyone: the right accounting software depends on team size, budget and which Khatabook limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Khatabook for documented reasons: limited reporting; limited customisation. Khatabook remains a reasonable fit for kirana stores tracking credit, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Vyapar

Simply Vyapar Apps Pvt. Ltd. · GST billing, inventory and accounting app (mobile and desktop)

Vyapar is a GST billing and accounting app from Simply Vyapar Apps that lets Indian small businesses create GST invoices, track stock, record expenses and payments, and prepare GST reports on Android, Windows desktop or both, sold as annual Gold and Platinum licences.

Pricing
Desktop Gold reported at INR 3,399 a year; USD mobile licences from about $2.92 a month billed annually.
Free plan
No
Team size

Strengths
  • Annual licences are priced for micro businesses, well below per-user cloud accounting suites.
  • Billing, stock and GST reports sit in one app that runs on a phone.
Trade-offs to weigh against Khatabook
  • No permanent free plan in the sources reviewed.
  • Licences are per platform, so mobile plus desktop costs more.

Switching

How to switch from Khatabook.

Moving off Khatabook usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Khatabook data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Khatabook.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
