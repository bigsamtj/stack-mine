Switching

How to switch from systeme.io.

Moving off systeme.io usually means exporting your core records, rebuilding settings, automations and templates in the new tool, and reconnecting the integrations you rely on. Migrate active work first and archive the rest rather than moving everything. Run one live process with its real users in each shortlisted tool before deciding.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your systeme.io data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real users or volume, including add-ons, onboarding and taxes.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with systeme.io.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
