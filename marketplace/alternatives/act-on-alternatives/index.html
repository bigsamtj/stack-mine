Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Act-On?

The main alternatives to Act-On covered here are Adobe Marketo Engage, Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot), Ortto and CleverTap. Adobe Marketo Engage may suit B2B marketing teams running complex lead management at scale, while Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) may suit B2B teams already running Salesforce Sales Cloud. None is best for everyone: the right marketing automation platform depends on team size, budget and which Act-On limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Act-On for documented reasons: annual contract required; CRM integration is an add-on on Professional. Act-On remains a reasonable fit for mid-size B2B teams wanting simpler automation than Marketo, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Act-On.

Moving off Act-On means exporting contacts, activity history and consent records, rebuilding journeys, scoring models, templates and forms, re-warming sending domains or IPs, and reconnecting your CRM and data sources. Run both platforms in parallel until key journeys are live. Rebuild one live journey (such as onboarding or lead nurture) in each shortlisted platform and compare results.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Act-On data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Act-On.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Act-On alternatives: questions and answers

What is the best alternative to Act-On?

No single alternative is best for everyone. The alternatives covered here are Adobe Marketo Engage, Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot), Ortto and CleverTap. Adobe Marketo Engage may suit B2B marketing teams running complex lead management at scale. Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) may suit B2B teams already running Salesforce Sales Cloud. Ortto may suit SaaS and growth teams wanting automation plus a CDP in one tool. Test two or three against your own process before deciding.

Is there a free alternative to Act-On?

None of the alternatives covered here documents a permanent free plan. CleverTap offers a free trial. See the Marketing Automation Software category for other free options.

Which Act-On alternative is best for small teams?

Ortto and CleverTap are documented for small teams. Ortto may suit SaaS and growth teams wanting automation plus a CDP in one tool. CleverTap may suit consumer app teams that want a self-serve entry plan.

Which Act-On alternatives suit larger organisations?

Adobe Marketo Engage, Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot), Ortto and CleverTap are documented for large enterprises as well as smaller teams. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs and contract terms on each profile.

Why do teams switch from Act-On?

The limitations documented on the Act-On profile are: Annual contract required. CRM integration is an add-on on Professional. Standard support is email-based. Whether they matter depends on your team and budget.

Who should stay with Act-On?

Act-On may still suit mid-size B2B teams wanting simpler automation than Marketo; teams with large but partly inactive databases; non-profits.
