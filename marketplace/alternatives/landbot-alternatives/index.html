Quick answer

What are the best alternatives to Landbot?

The main alternatives to Landbot covered here are ManyChat, Chatbase, Voiceflow and Botpress. ManyChat may suit social- and messenger-first brands automating DMs, while Chatbase may suit teams wanting a fast no-code support bot trained on their docs. None is best for everyone: the right chatbot builder depends on team size, budget and which Landbot limitation you are trying to solve.

Teams usually start looking beyond Landbot for documented reasons: WhatsApp needs a separate plan; tight AI chat caps. Landbot remains a reasonable fit for marketing teams capturing and qualifying leads conversationally, so an alternative is not automatically better, only different.

Switching

How to switch from Landbot.

Moving off Landbot means exporting conversation flows, knowledge sources and intents, rebuilding them in the new builder, reconnecting channels such as WhatsApp and Instagram, re-creating human handoff rules and retesting answers. Run both bots on a share of traffic before switching fully. Build the same pilot bot covering your top 20 questions in each shortlisted tool and compare accuracy, handoff and cost per conversation.

Switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your Landbot data and test an import into the new product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost for your real seat count, including add-ons and onboarding fees.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms, renewal dates and notice periods with Landbot.

General guidance, not specific to any vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
