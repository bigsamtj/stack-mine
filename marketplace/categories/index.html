    FAQ

    Questions about categories

    What is a category on the Stack18 Marketplace?

    A category groups third-party products that do the same job, such as CRM software or AI coding agents. Each category page defines the category, lists who uses it, shows how to evaluate it and links to every sourced profile.

    How are products placed in a category?

    By what the product is documented to do, not by who pays. A product has one primary category and can appear in secondary categories where its documentation supports it.

    Are categories ranked or sponsored?

    No. Listings are alphabetical by default. There are no paid placements, affiliate links, ratings or reviews.

    Why is my category missing?

    Stack18 now publishes 183 category guides across software and AI agents. If a category you need is not listed, contact us and we will consider adding it. Categories without sourced listings yet still have a full guide.

    Can I filter products inside a category?

    Yes. Each category page has search and filters for the attributes that matter in that category, such as free plan, team size, pricing model, deployment and AI features.
