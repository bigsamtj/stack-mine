    What is Customer Support Software?

    Customer support software is a platform for receiving, organising and resolving customer requests. It turns messages from email, chat, social media and phone into tickets, routes them to agents, tracks service levels and offers a help center, increasingly with AI automation.

    Also called Help Desk Software

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 41 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 11 of 41 offer a free plan or program.
    • $14–$29 reported entry price per user per month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Per-agent price and plan limits, Channels, AI pricing model.
    • Sourcing: 8 vendor-sourced and 16 independent-source profiles, each with dated links.

    Customer support software gives teams a shared inbox and ticketing system for handling customer requests, with routing, automation, a help center and reporting. Most modern products add AI agents that resolve routine questions and assistants that help human agents.

    Pricing is usually per agent, but AI is increasingly priced differently, with allowances, sessions or per-resolution fees. Channels such as live chat and phone may sit in higher plans or separate products, so the real cost depends on what you switch on.

    The profiles below summarise independent sources, with dates and caveats. They are research aids, not rankings, and they do not replace a trial with your own tickets.

    The cheapest plan may lack SLAs, chat or AI, so compare like-for-like plans.

    Ignoring AI billing

    Per-resolution or per-session fees can dominate cost at high volume.

    Glossary

    Customer Support Software key terms explained.

    Ticket
    A record of a customer request that is tracked until it is resolved.
    SLA
    A service level agreement: a target for how quickly requests are answered or resolved.
    Omnichannel
    Handling email, chat, social and phone conversations in one workspace.
    Automated resolution
    A conversation an AI agent closes without a human, which some vendors bill per instance.
    Copilot (agent assist)
    An AI tool that helps human agents draft replies and summarise tickets.
    Help center
    A self-service knowledge base customers can search before contacting support.
