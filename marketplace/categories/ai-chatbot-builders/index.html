    Quick answer

    What is AI Chatbot Builders?

    AI chatbot builders let teams create conversational agents for websites, messaging apps and voice. Options range from no-code doc-trained widgets and social DM automation to developer platforms and enterprise conversational AI.

    Also called Chatbot Software, AI Chatbots

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 27 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 12 of 27 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free tiers from Chatbase, Landbot, ManyChat, Botpress, Voiceflow and Yellow.ai; Dialogflow CX has a $600 trial credit. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Usage units (credits, sessions, contacts), Channels needed, No-code vs developer control.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Large language models made chatbots far more capable, and the market now spans quick doc-trained widgets to enterprise agent platforms.

    This category covers no-code and doc-trained builders (Chatbase, CustomGPT.ai, Landbot, ManyChat), design and developer platforms (Voiceflow, Botpress, Dialogflow CX) and enterprise platforms (Ada, Yellow.ai, Kore.ai). Tidio and Intercom have their own profiles.

    Most vendors bill by credits, sessions, contacts or resolutions, so cost depends on volume. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.
