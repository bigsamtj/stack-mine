    Quick answer

    What is Web Hosting?

    Web hosting stores and serves your website. Shared hosting puts many sites on one server at low cost; managed WordPress hosting adds speed, security and backups; managed cloud hosting gives scalable servers without running them yourself.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 15 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 0 of 15 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Intro prices from $1.98 a month (Namecheap) to $2.89-$2.99 (DreamHost, Hostinger); managed WordPress from about $25-$35 a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.

    FAQ

    Web Hosting questions and answers

    What is the best web hosting and managed WordPress hosting for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Bluehost, Cloudways, DreamHost, GreenGeeks, Hosting.com (A2 Hosting), Hostinger, Namecheap Hosting and SiteGround. Bluehost may suit beginners starting a WordPress site. Cloudways may suit developers and agencies wanting scalable managed cloud. DreamHost may suit site owners wanting month-to-month billing and many sites. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which web hosting and managed WordPress hosting suits large enterprises?

    2 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Kinsta and WP Engine. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific web host?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Bluehost alternatives, Hostinger alternatives and SiteGround alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best web hosting?

    Hostinger and Namecheap suit budget sites; SiteGround suits support; Kinsta and WP Engine suit premium WordPress; Cloudways suits scalable cloud. This list is not a ranking.

    Why does hosting renew at a higher price?

    Intro prices require long terms; renewals are often two to six times higher, so compare total cost.

    Which host has the smallest renewal increase?

    Namecheap ($1.98 to $4.48) and DreamHost in one test; Kinsta, WP Engine and Cloudways have little or no renewal jump.

    What happened to A2 Hosting?

    It became Hosting.com after World Host Group acquired it in 2025, per a 2026 review.
