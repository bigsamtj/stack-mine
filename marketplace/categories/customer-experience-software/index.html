Quick answer

What is customer support and experience software?

Customer support and experience software covers the tools businesses use to help customers and understand how they feel. It includes help desk and ticketing, live chat and chatbots, contact centre and phone systems, knowledge bases and self-service portals, customer feedback and experience management platforms.

Category overview

Customer Support & Experience Software, explained.

Problems it solves

Requests arrive by email, chat, phone and social media; without shared tools they get lost, answered twice or left waiting, and nobody sees the patterns behind them.

Why businesses need it

Service quality influences retention, reviews and referrals. Customers expect fast answers on their preferred channel and increasingly prefer to help themselves.

How it works

Conversations from every channel become tickets or threads in a shared workspace, routed to the right team with context. Knowledge bases and bots resolve common questions; feedback tools measure satisfaction and surface issues.

Who should use it

Support and service teams, contact centres, customer experience leaders, ecommerce and SaaS businesses, and IT teams supporting employees.

Where it fits in your stack

Support tools connect the CRM, ecommerce or billing data, product analytics and AI agents that answer or assist.

Buying guide

How to choose Customer Support & Experience Software.

Use these criteria to build a shortlist, then test the finalists with your own data and workflows.

Essential features

Start from your channels and volumes; choose a core help desk or contact centre platform, then add specialist tools.

Pricing models

Pricing is mostly per agent per month, with AI often priced per resolution, conversation or session.

Integrations

Check CRM, ecommerce, billing and identity integrations.

Scalability

Consider agent counts, channel volumes and peaks.

Security and compliance

Review data protection for customer conversations, access controls and certifications.

Implementation

Plan routing, macros and knowledge migration.

Support

Check vendor support quality, ironically important here.

Customisation

Look for custom fields, workflows and apps.

FAQ

Customer Support & Experience Software questions and answers

What is customer support software?

Software that manages customer requests across channels, with ticketing, routing, knowledge and reporting.

What is the difference between customer service and customer experience software?

Customer service tools resolve requests; customer experience tools measure and improve the overall experience across touchpoints.

Do I need a contact centre platform?

If phone is a major channel with queues and many agents, yes. Smaller teams can use help desk telephony add-ons.

How do AI agents fit in?

AI agents answer and resolve routine requests and assist human agents, working on top of help desks and knowledge bases.

What is self-service?

Letting customers find answers or complete tasks themselves through help centres, portals and bots.

Which metrics matter in support?

Response and resolution times, customer satisfaction, first-contact resolution and self-service rate.
