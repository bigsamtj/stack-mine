    Quick answer

    What is Point of Sale (POS) Systems?

    A point of sale (POS) system takes in-person payments and records sales, and usually manages inventory, menus, staff, customers and reporting. Costs combine software fees, card processing and hardware.

    Also called Point of Sale (POS)

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 12 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 5 of 12 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free software from Square, Toast (starter), Loyverse and SumUp (no monthly fee); paid from $29 a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Processing rates, Hardware and contract length, Industry fit (retail vs restaurant).
    • Sourcing: 1 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 9 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Choosing a POS means comparing total cost (software, processing and hardware), not just the monthly plan.

    This category covers general POS (Square, Clover, SumUp, Loyverse), retail (Lightspeed, Shopify POS), restaurants (Toast, TouchBistro, Revel) and India-focused restaurant POS (Petpooja).

    Software ranges from free to quoted multi-year contracts; card processing often costs more than software. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Point of Sale (POS) Systems.

    Comparing only software prices

    Processing fees outweigh subscriptions.

    Signing long contracts blindly

    Some require 36 months.

    Buying locked hardware

    Processor-tied hardware limits switching.

    Ignoring add-on costs

    Online ordering and loyalty add up.

    Skipping offline mode checks

    Internet outages happen.

    Glossary

    Point of Sale (POS) Systems key terms explained.

    Interchange-plus
    Processing priced at card network cost plus a markup.
    KDS
    Kitchen display system.
    Tap to Pay
    Accepting contactless payments on a phone.
    Merchant account
    An account for processing card payments.
    Omnichannel
    Unified online and in-store selling.
    Chargeback
    A disputed card payment reversed.

    FAQ

    Point of Sale (POS) Systems questions and answers

    What is the best free point of sale (POS) systems?

    5 of the 12 point of sale (POS) systems profiles here document a free plan: Loyverse, Shopify POS, Square POS, SumUp and Toast. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best point of sale (POS) systems for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Clover, Loyverse, Shopify POS, Square POS, SumUp and TouchBistro. Clover may suit businesses wanting flexibility through apps. Loyverse may suit very small shops and cafes on a budget. Shopify POS may suit businesses selling both online and in person. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which point of sale (POS) systems suits large enterprises?

    4 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Lightspeed Retail, Petpooja, Revel Systems and Toast. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific POS system?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Clover alternatives, Square POS alternatives and Toast alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free POS system?

    Square and Loyverse offer free software; SumUp has no monthly fee; Toast has a free starter option. This list is not a ranking.

    Square or Toast for restaurants?

    Toast is restaurant-specific ($0-$69 per location); Square for Restaurants runs $0-$149 a month.

    What does a POS cost in total?

    Add software, card processing (often 1.7% to 2.6% plus fees) and hardware; processing usually costs most.

    Which POS suits Indian restaurants?

    Petpooja integrates Swiggy and Zomato, with plans listed in INR.
