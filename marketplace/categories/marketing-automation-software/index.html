    Quick answer

    What is Marketing Automation Software?

    Marketing automation software uses customer data and triggers to send the right message at the right time across email, SMS, push, in-app and WhatsApp. B2B tools focus on lead scoring and CRM sync; B2C engagement platforms focus on real-time, high-volume messaging to app and web users.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 13 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 0 of 13 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: From $85 a month (CleverTap Core) and $100 (Customer.io Essentials) to enterprise contracts above $85,000 a year. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: B2B leads vs B2C users, Pricing unit (contacts, MAU, credits), Channels you need.
    • Sourcing: 5 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 5 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Marketing automation replaces one-off campaigns with rules and journeys that react to what people do: filling in a form, abandoning a cart, going quiet in an app.

    The category splits in two. B2B platforms (Adobe Marketo Engage, Salesforce Account Engagement, Act-On, Ortto) centre on leads, scoring and CRM. Consumer engagement platforms (Braze, Iterable, Customer.io, MoEngage, CleverTap, WebEngage) centre on users, events and mobile channels.

    Most vendors price by contacts, active users or credits, and many publish no prices at all. The profiles below summarise dated sources, label estimates and are research aids, not rankings.

    Evaluation criteria

    Features and criteria to evaluate in Marketing Automation Software.

    01

    B2B or B2C fit

    Lead-scoring suites and real-time app engagement platforms solve different problems.

    02

    Pricing unit

    Compare contacts, active contacts, MAUs, profiles and credits at your real volumes.

    03

    Channels

    Check email, SMS, WhatsApp, push, in-app and web, and which are add-ons.

    04

    Data and integrations

    Native CRM sync, event APIs and warehouse connections decide what you can automate.

    05

    AI and decisioning

    Look at predictive scoring, send-time optimisation and agentic decisioning, and how they are billed.

    06

    Skills and admin effort

    Enterprise suites often need a dedicated administrator.

    How to choose

    How to choose Marketing Automation Software: a step-by-step approach.

    1. 1

      Define your motion

      Decide whether you are nurturing leads for sales or engaging users of a product.

    2. 2

      Size your audience

      Count contacts, active contacts or MAUs and expected message volume.

    3. 3

      List channels and data

      Map required channels and the systems that must feed the platform.

    4. 4

      Get comparable quotes

      Many vendors only quote, so ask for prices at the same volume and term.

    5. 5

      Pilot one journey

      Build a real onboarding or nurture flow before committing.

    6. 6

      Plan ownership

      Assign who will run, maintain and report on the platform.

    Questions to ask before you buy

    • How is pricing affected by contact volume?
    • How deep is CRM sync?
    • Can non-technical marketers build journeys?
    • Which channels beyond email are native?
    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Marketing Automation Software.

    Buying enterprise for a small list

    Suites like Marketo can cost far more than the value at small scale.

    Ignoring the pricing unit

    Contacts, MAUs and credits scale very differently as you grow.

    Forgetting add-on channels

    SMS, WhatsApp and dedicated IPs are often extra.

    Skipping data setup

    Automation is only as good as the events and fields that trigger it.

    No owner

    Platforms without a dedicated owner tend to be under-used.

    FAQ

    Marketing Automation Software questions and answers

    What is the best marketing automation software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Act-On. Act-On may suit mid-size B2B teams wanting simpler automation than Marketo. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which marketing automation software suits large enterprises?

    9 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Adobe Marketo Engage, Braze, CleverTap, Customer.io, Iterable, MoEngage, Ortto, Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) and 1 more. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which marketing automation software has built-in AI features?

    7 of 13 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: Act-On, Adobe Marketo Engage, Braze, CleverTap, MoEngage, Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot) and WebEngage. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    What is the best marketing automation software for India?

    Products built for or documented in India include WebEngage. Indian payroll must handle PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax, so check statutory coverage on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific marketing automation platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Braze alternatives, MoEngage alternatives and Ortto alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the difference between marketing automation and email marketing?

    Email marketing tools focus on campaigns and newsletters; marketing automation adds scoring, multi-step journeys, multiple channels and CRM or product data.

    What is the cheapest marketing automation software here?

    CleverTap Core from $85 a month and Customer.io Essentials from $100 have the lowest published entry prices; Ortto starts at $237 for 10,000 contacts.

    Marketo or Pardot?

    Both are B2B suites. Account Engagement (Pardot) publishes per-org prices and is native to Salesforce; Marketo is quote-based and part of Adobe.

    Is Salesforce buying Braze?

    Yes. Salesforce announced a definitive agreement on 27 October 2026, expected to close in its fiscal 2028.
