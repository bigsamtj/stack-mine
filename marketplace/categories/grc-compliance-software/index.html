    Quick answer

    What is GRC & Compliance Automation Software?

    Compliance automation software connects to cloud, identity and HR systems to collect audit evidence continuously for frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA and GDPR. GRC (governance, risk and compliance) platforms add risk registers, internal audit, SOX and policy management for larger programmes.

    Also called Compliance Management, Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

    Glossary

    GRC & Compliance Automation Software key terms explained.

    SOC 2
    An AICPA attestation on security controls.
    Type I vs Type II
    A point-in-time check versus a 6-12 month observation period.
    ISO 27001
    International standard for information security management.
    GRC
    Governance, risk and compliance.
    Continuous monitoring
    Automated, ongoing control testing.
    SOX
    US Sarbanes-Oxley financial controls requirements.

    FAQ

    GRC & Compliance Automation Software questions and answers

    What is the best GRC and compliance automation software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Scrut Automation, Secureframe, Sprinto and Thoropass. Scrut Automation may suit teams with a GRC function managing many frameworks. Secureframe may suit teams wanting guided remediation. Sprinto may suit startups seeking a fast, affordable first SOC 2. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which GRC and compliance automation software suits large enterprises?

    6 profiles are documented for large enterprises: AuditBoard, Drata, Hyperproof, LogicGate Risk Cloud, OneTrust and Vanta. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific compliance platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Drata alternatives, Hyperproof alternatives and Vanta alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best compliance automation software?

    Vanta and Drata suit broad SOC 2 and multi-framework programmes; Sprinto and Secureframe suit budget-conscious startups; Thoropass bundles the audit; AuditBoard, LogicGate and Hyperproof suit enterprise GRC. This list is not a ranking.

    How much does SOC 2 software cost?

    June 2026 estimates range from about $4,000-$8,000 a year (Sprinto) to $10,000-$55,000+ (Vanta), with auditor fees usually extra.

    Vanta or Drata?

    Both are quote-only; estimates put Drata at $7,500-$15,000 a year plus onboarding and Vanta at $10,000-$12,000 to start, with Vanta known for its integration and auditor network.

    How long does SOC 2 take?

    A competitor guide cites 2-4 months for Type I and a 6-12 month observation period for Type II.
