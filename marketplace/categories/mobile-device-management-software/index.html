    Quick answer

    What is Mobile Device Management Software?

    Mobile device management (MDM) software enrols, configures, secures and updates company laptops, phones and tablets remotely. Unified endpoint management (UEM) extends this across every operating system with patching, app deployment and compliance.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 2 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Mosyle free for 30 devices; Jamf Now free for 3 devices; paid from $1 per device (Mosyle) or $1.98 (Hexnode). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Per user vs per device vs endpoint band, Apple depth vs cross-platform needs, Identity and security integration.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Pricing bases differ: Intune and JumpCloud charge per user, most MDMs charge per device, and ManageEngine prices by endpoint band. Compare on the same basis before comparing totals.

    This category covers Apple specialists (Jamf Pro, Iru, Mosyle, Addigy), cross-platform UEM (Hexnode, Scalefusion, ManageEngine, Omnissa Workspace ONE) and identity-plus-device platforms (Microsoft Intune, JumpCloud).

    Recent changes: Jamf went private in January 2026, Kandji became Iru in October 2025, and Omnissa was spun out of VMware in 2024. Profiles summarise August 2026 guides and are research aids, not rankings.

    Glossary

    Mobile Device Management Software key terms explained.

    MDM
    Mobile device management.
    UEM
    Unified endpoint management across all OSes.
    Zero-touch enrolment
    Devices configure themselves on first boot.

    Apple's free business device programme (replaced Business Manager in April 2026).
    Conditional Access
    Allowing access only from compliant devices.
    EDR
    Endpoint detection and response.

    FAQ

    Mobile Device Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free mobile device management (MDM) and unified endpoint management software?

    2 of the 10 mobile device management (MDM) and unified endpoint management software profiles here document a free plan: Jamf Pro and Mosyle. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best mobile device management (MDM) and unified endpoint management software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Addigy and JumpCloud. Addigy may suit MSPs managing Macs for many clients. JumpCloud may suit SMBs wanting identity and devices in one platform. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which mobile device management (MDM) and unified endpoint management software suits large enterprises?

    8 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Hexnode, Iru (formerly Kandji), Jamf Pro, ManageEngine Endpoint Central, Microsoft Intune, Mosyle, Omnissa Workspace ONE and Scalefusion. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific MDM platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Addigy alternatives, Hexnode alternatives and Microsoft Intune alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best MDM software?

    Jamf Pro and Iru suit deep Apple management; Mosyle suits low-cost Apple; Intune suits Microsoft 365; Hexnode and Scalefusion suit cross-platform fleets. This list is not a ranking.

    Jamf or Mosyle?

    Jamf for Mac is $12.50 per device a month with the deepest Apple features; Mosyle starts at $1 per device and is free for 30 devices.

    Is there free MDM software?

    Mosyle is free for up to 30 devices, Jamf Now for 3, and Apple Business offers free baseline device management.

    What happened to Kandji?

    Kandji rebranded as Iru on 22 October 2025.
