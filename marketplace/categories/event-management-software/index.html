    Quick answer

    What is Event Management Software?

    Event management software helps organisers plan and run events: registration and ticketing, event websites, attendee apps, networking, check-in and badges, virtual sessions and reporting. Enterprise suites add venue sourcing and CRM-connected analytics.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 14 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 2 of 14 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Eventbrite and Eventzilla are free for free events; Swapcard self-service from $610 a year; Eventzilla from $1.50 per registration. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Ticket fees vs subscription, Number of events a year, In-person, virtual or hybrid needs.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Event platforms are priced in very different ways: per-ticket fees, per registration, per user, per event or by enterprise quote. The cheapest option depends on ticket prices, event count and attendee volume.

    This category covers enterprise suites (Cvent, Bizzabo), ticketing and registration (Eventbrite, Eventzilla, Swoogo), networking and event apps (Whova, Swapcard), virtual and hybrid platforms (Airmeet, Goldcast) and all-in-one tools (Accelevents).

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Event Management Software.

    Ignoring ticket fee stacking

    Service and processing fees add up on cheap tickets.

    Buying an enterprise suite too early

    Cvent-class tools suit many events a year.

    Forgetting exhibitor add-ons

    Lead capture and booths can cost extra.

    Underestimating onboarding

    Large platforms take months to master.

    Skipping renewal terms

    Many contracts auto-renew annually.

    Glossary

    Event Management Software key terms explained.

    Registration
    Collecting attendee details and payments.
    Hybrid event
    An event with in-person and online audiences.
    Matchmaking
    Suggesting people or exhibitors to meet.
    Lead capture
    Exhibitors scanning attendee badges.
    Room block
    Hotel rooms reserved for attendees.
    Service fee
    A platform charge per ticket sold.

    Event Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free event management, registration and ticketing software?

    2 of the 14 event management, registration and ticketing software profiles here document a free plan: Eventbrite and Eventzilla. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best event management, registration and ticketing software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Eventzilla. Eventzilla may suit organisers wanting simple, transparent registration fees. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which event management, registration and ticketing software suits large enterprises?

    9 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Accelevents, Airmeet, Bizzabo, Cvent, Eventbrite, Goldcast, Swapcard, Swoogo and 1 more. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific event platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Accelevents alternatives, Bizzabo alternatives and Cvent alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best event management software?

    Cvent and Bizzabo suit enterprise programmes; Eventbrite and Eventzilla suit ticketing; Whova and Swapcard suit networking; Airmeet and Goldcast suit virtual events. This list is not a ranking.

    How much does Eventbrite charge?

    In the US, 3.7% + $1.79 per paid ticket plus 2.9% processing; free events are free.

    Which event platforms publish pricing?

    Bizzabo ($499 per user a month), Swoogo ($11,800 a year), Accelevents (from $7,500) and Eventzilla (from $1.50 per registration).

    What happened to Hubilo?

    Brandlive acquired Hubilo in September 2025 and relaunched it as Virtual PRO in January 2026, according to an August 2026 Rework guide.
