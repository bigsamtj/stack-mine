    Quick answer

    What is Low-Code Development Platforms?

    Low-code platforms let developers and business users build applications with visual tools, prebuilt components and minimal hand-written code. No-code tools target non-developers; enterprise low-code adds governance, DevOps and scale for mission-critical apps.

    Also called Low-Code / No-Code

    Key takeaways

    • 13 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 8 of 13 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Retool (5 users), Bubble, Glide (1 project) and Mendix; Budibase free to self-host; paid from $5 per user (AppSheet Starter). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.

    This category covers internal tool builders (Retool, Budibase), no-code app builders (Bubble, Glide, AppSheet), Microsoft and Google ecosystems (Power Apps, AppSheet), governed operations apps (Quickbase) and enterprise low-code (OutSystems, Mendix, Appian).

    FAQ

    Low-Code Development Platforms questions and answers

    What is the best free low-code and no-code app development platforms?

    8 of the 13 low-code and no-code app development platforms profiles here document a free plan: Appian, Bubble, Budibase, Glide, Mendix, OutSystems, Power Apps and Retool. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best low-code and no-code app development platforms for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Bubble, Budibase and Glide. Bubble may suit founders building customer-facing products without code. Budibase may suit teams wanting self-hosted internal tools with full data control. Glide may suit small teams turning spreadsheets into apps. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which low-code and no-code app development platforms suits large enterprises?

    7 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Appian, Google AppSheet, Mendix, OutSystems, Power Apps, Quickbase and Retool. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific low-code platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Appian alternatives, Google AppSheet alternatives and Power Apps alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best low-code platform?

    Retool suits internal tools for developers; Bubble suits customer products; Power Apps and AppSheet suit Microsoft and Google shops; OutSystems, Mendix and Appian suit enterprise apps. This list is not a ranking.

    Power Apps or AppSheet?

    Power Apps Premium is $20 per user a month (annual); AppSheet Core is $10 and included in most paid Google Workspace plans.

    Which low-code platforms are free?

    Retool (5 users), Bubble, Glide (1 project) and Mendix have free plans; Budibase is free to self-host.

    Which enterprise low-code platforms publish prices?

    Mendix publishes a Standard starting price of €918 a month for one app; OutSystems and Appian are quote-only.
