    Quick answer

    What is PDF Editor Software?

    PDF editor software lets you change text and images in PDFs, convert to and from Office formats, run OCR on scans, build forms, redact and sign. Options range from free browser tools to professional desktop suites.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 11 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 5 of 11 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free: PDFgear, PDF-XChange (free version), Smallpdf and Sejda (limited); paid from $49.99/yr (UPDF) or $62 one-time (PDF-XChange). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Platforms you need, Subscription vs perpetual licence, OCR and redaction depth.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Pricing models vary widely: Acrobat is subscription-only (from $14.99 a month annually), Foxit, PDFelement, UPDF and PDF Expert offer perpetual or lifetime licences, PDF-XChange is one-time from $62, and PDFgear is free.

    This category covers professional suites (Adobe Acrobat, Foxit, Nitro), value desktop editors (PDFelement, UPDF, PDF-XChange Editor), Apple-native PDF Expert and browser tools (Smallpdf, Sejda, PDFgear).

    Watch platforms (PDF Expert is Apple-only, PDF-XChange is Windows-only), introductory discounts that renew higher, and whether online tools process confidential files on servers. Prices come from June-October 2026 guides; profiles are research aids, not rankings.

    PDF Editor Software questions and answers

    What is the best free PDF editor software?

    5 of the 11 PDF editor software profiles here document a free plan: PDF-XChange Editor, PDFgear, Sejda, Smallpdf and UPDF. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best PDF editor software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Foxit PDF Editor, PDF Expert, PDF-XChange Editor, PDFelement, PDFgear, Sejda, Smallpdf and UPDF. Foxit PDF Editor may suit Windows teams wanting a lighter Acrobat alternative. PDF Expert may suit Mac and iPad users who value speed and a clean interface. PDF-XChange Editor may suit Windows power users wanting depth and a perpetual licence. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which PDF editor software suits large enterprises?

    2 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Adobe Acrobat and Nitro PDF. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific PDF editor?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Adobe Acrobat alternatives, PDFelement alternatives and UPDF alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best PDF editor?

    Adobe Acrobat for professional output; Foxit and Nitro as cheaper business alternatives; PDF Expert on Apple; UPDF for cross-device value; PDFgear free. This list is not a ranking.

    Is there a free PDF editor?

    PDFgear is free with no watermark; PDF-XChange has a free version with about 70% of features; Smallpdf and Sejda have limited free plans.

    How much is Adobe Acrobat Pro?

    $19.99 a month on an annual plan or $29.99 month-to-month, per 2026 guides.

    Can I buy a PDF editor outright?

    Yes: PDF-XChange from $62, UPDF lifetime $79.99, PDFelement $139.99, PDF Expert Mac $139.99 and Foxit Editor 14 $209.99.
