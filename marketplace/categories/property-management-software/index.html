    Quick answer

    What is Property Management Software?

    Property management software helps landlords and property managers advertise units, screen tenants, sign leases, collect rent, handle maintenance and keep trust accounting and owner reports. Larger platforms add commercial leases, HOA tools and automation.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 3 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from TurboTenant, Avail and Hemlane (Starter); paid from about $1 per unit (Yardi Breeze) or $62 a month (Buildium). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Number of units and property types, Accounting and owner reporting needs, Transaction fees for rent and screening.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Pricing varies more by portfolio size than almost any other category: some tools are free for DIY landlords, others charge per unit with monthly minimums or require quotes.

    This category covers professional manager platforms (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi Breeze, Rent Manager, DoorLoop), small-portfolio tools (Rentec Direct, TenantCloud) and DIY landlord apps (TurboTenant, Avail, Hemlane).

    Several sources disagree on prices; profiles show both figures. They summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Glossary

    Property Management Software key terms explained.

    Trust accounting
    Tracking owner and tenant funds separately.
    Owner portal
    Where property owners see statements.
    CAM
    Common area maintenance charges in commercial leases.
    Tenant screening
    Credit, criminal and eviction checks.
    ACH
    Bank transfer payments for rent.
    Door
    Industry shorthand for a rental unit.

    FAQ

    Property Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free property management and landlord software?

    3 of the 10 property management and landlord software profiles here document a free plan: Avail, Hemlane and TurboTenant. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best property management and landlord software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Avail, DoorLoop, Hemlane, Rentec Direct, TenantCloud, TurboTenant and Yardi Breeze. Avail may suit DIY landlords wanting state-specific leases and listing syndication. DoorLoop may suit landlords and managers wanting an easy all-in-one tool. Hemlane may suit landlords wanting software plus on-the-ground help. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which property management and landlord software suits large enterprises?

    3 profiles are documented for large enterprises: AppFolio, Buildium and Rent Manager. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific property management platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example AppFolio alternatives, Buildium alternatives and TurboTenant alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best property management software?

    AppFolio and Buildium suit professional managers; Yardi Breeze suits mixed residential and commercial; TurboTenant, Avail and Hemlane suit DIY landlords. This list is not a ranking.

    AppFolio or Buildium?

    AppFolio Core is about $1.49 per unit a month with a $298 minimum; Buildium Essential starts at $62 a month with a 14-day trial.

    Is there free property management software?

    TurboTenant, Avail (Unlimited) and Hemlane (Starter) have free plans.

    Which property software handles commercial leases?

    Yardi Breeze and Rent Manager support commercial properties and CAM.
