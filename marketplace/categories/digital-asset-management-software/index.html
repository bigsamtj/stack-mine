    Quick answer

    What is Digital Asset Management Software?

    Digital asset management (DAM) software stores, organises, finds and shares images, videos, documents and brand files. DAM platforms add metadata, AI tagging, rights management, brand portals and integrations with creative and marketing tools.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 2 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Cloudinary (25 credits) and Air (120 credits); published plans from $100 a month (Pics.io Solo). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Storage and asset volume, Users vs unlimited seats, AI tagging quality on your own files.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Most enterprise DAM vendors are quote-only, so published prices are scarce. A few publish plans (Cloudinary, Pics.io, Air) and others are estimated by third parties.

    This category covers enterprise DAM (Bynder, Aprimo, Acquia DAM, MediaValet), brand management (Brandfolder, Frontify), marketing DAM (Canto, Pics.io), creative DAM (Air) and media APIs (Cloudinary).

    AI tagging can be weaker on your files.

    Ignoring storage growth

    Video grows libraries quickly.

    Skipping taxonomy

    Bad metadata ruins search.

    Overbuying enterprise modules

    Forgetting rights expiry

    Expired licences create legal risk.

    Digital Asset Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free digital asset management (DAM) software?

    2 of the 10 digital asset management (DAM) software profiles here document a free plan: Air and Cloudinary. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best digital asset management (DAM) software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Pics.io. Pics.io may suit teams wanting a DAM on their own Google Drive or S3. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which digital asset management (DAM) software suits large enterprises?

    9 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Acquia DAM, Air, Aprimo, Brandfolder, Bynder, Canto, Cloudinary, Frontify and 1 more. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific DAM platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Brandfolder alternatives, Bynder alternatives and Canto alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best DAM software?

    Bynder and Aprimo suit enterprises; Brandfolder and Frontify suit brand teams; Canto and Pics.io suit marketing teams; Air suits creative teams; Cloudinary suits developers. This list is not a ranking.

    Which DAM tools publish pricing?

    Cloudinary (Plus $89 a month annual), Pics.io (from $100 a month) and Air (Business $900 a month annual).

    Is there free DAM software?

    Cloudinary and Air have free plans.

    Who owns Brandfolder?

    Smartsheet, which acquired it in September 2020.
