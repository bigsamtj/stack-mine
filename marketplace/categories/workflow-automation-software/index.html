    Quick answer

    What is Workflow Automation Software?

    Workflow automation software connects apps and runs multi-step processes automatically when something happens, such as a new lead, form or order. Integration platforms (iPaaS) add enterprise governance, data mapping, APIs and on-premises connectivity.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 26 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 12 of 26 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Zapier (100 tasks), Make (1,000 credits), Pipedream (100 credits) and self-hosted n8n and Activepieces; paid from about $9 a month (Make). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Pricing unit vs your workflow volume, Self-hosting and data residency, Governance and error handling needs.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    The biggest pricing difference is the unit: tasks (Zapier), credits (Make), executions (n8n), active flows (Activepieces), users or bots (Power Automate) or annual enterprise contracts (Workato, Tray.ai, Boomi, Celigo).

    This category covers no-code tools (Zapier, Make, Activepieces), technical and open-source tools (n8n, Pipedream), Microsoft automation and RPA (Power Automate) and enterprise iPaaS (Workato, Tray.ai, Boomi, Celigo).
