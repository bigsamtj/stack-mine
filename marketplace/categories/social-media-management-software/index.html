    Quick answer

    What is Social Media Management Software?

    Social media management software lets teams plan, schedule and publish posts across networks, reply to comments and messages from one inbox, measure performance and collaborate with approvals. Higher tiers add social listening, employee advocacy and enterprise controls.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 12 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 5 of 12 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Buffer, Metricool and Zoho Social; paid from $5 per channel (Buffer) to $399 per user a month (Hootsuite and Sprout Social Advanced). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Pricing unit (users, channels, brands), Inbox and analytics on your plan, Approval workflows.
    • Sourcing: 1 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 9 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Social media management tools replace logging into each network separately: one calendar, one inbox and one place for reports.

    This category covers enterprise platforms (Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse), affordable tools for SMBs and agencies (SocialPilot, Sendible, Zoho Social, Loomly) and creator-friendly schedulers (Buffer, Later, Metricool).

    Pricing varies widely: per user, per channel, per social set or per brand. Free plans have become rarer (Hootsuite and Agorapulse dropped theirs). The profiles below summarise dated sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Evaluation criteria

    Features and criteria to evaluate in Social Media Management Software.

    01

    Pricing unit

    Compare per user, per channel, per social set and per brand pricing at your real size.

    02

    Networks supported

    Check every network you use, including LinkedIn, TikTok and X, and any add-on fees.

    03

    Inbox and engagement

    A unified inbox matters if you answer many comments and DMs.

    04

    Analytics and reports

    Check report depth, competitor tracking and white-label options.

    05

    Collaboration

    Approvals, roles and client access are essential for teams and agencies.

    06

    Listening and AI

    Listening and AI credits are often add-ons or limited by plan.

    How to choose

    How to choose Social Media Management Software: a step-by-step approach.

    1. 1

      Count channels, brands and users

      Your numbers decide which pricing model is cheapest.

    2. 2

      List must-have networks

      Confirm support and add-on fees for each.

    3. 3

      These separate schedulers from full platforms.

    Questions to ask before you buy

    • Which networks and post types are supported?
    • How are approvals handled?
    • Is listening included or extra?
    • How is pricing affected by profiles and users?
    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Social Media Management Software.

    Paying per seat for occasional users

    Per-user plans get expensive when many people need light access.

    Ignoring network add-ons

    Some tools charge extra for X or extra profiles.

    Choosing on scheduling only

    Inbox and reporting often matter more as you grow.

    Overlooking free-plan limits

    Free plans cap channels, posts or scheduling.

    Skipping approval workflows

    Unapproved posts are a common brand risk.

    Glossary

    Social Media Management Software key terms explained.

    Social inbox
    One feed of comments, mentions and messages across networks.
    Social listening
    Tracking brand and keyword mentions across social media.
    Social set
    Later's unit: one profile per supported network.
    Employee advocacy
    Tools that help staff share approved company content.
    White-label reports
    Reports branded with an agency's own logo.
    Approval workflow
    A step where posts must be approved before publishing.

    FAQ

    Social Media Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free social media management software?

    5 of the 12 social media management software profiles here document a free plan: Buffer, Later, Loomly, Metricool and Zoho Social. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best social media management software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Buffer, Later, Loomly, Metricool, SocialPilot and Zoho Social. Buffer may suit creators and small businesses wanting simple, low-cost scheduling. Later may suit creators and brands focused on Instagram, TikTok and visual content. Loomly may suit marketing teams that need collaborative content calendars and approvals. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which social media management software suits large enterprises?

    4 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Agorapulse, Hootsuite, Sendible and Sprout Social. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which social media management software has built-in AI features?

    6 of 12 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: Agorapulse, Hootsuite, Later, SocialPilot, Sprout Social and Zoho Social. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    What is the best social media management software for India?

    Products built for or documented in India include SocialPilot. Indian payroll must handle PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax, so check statutory coverage on each profile.

    Which social media management software supports international teams?

    SocialPilot supports international hiring, HR or payroll. Employer-of-record and global payroll services are priced per employee.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific social media management tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Buffer alternatives, Hootsuite alternatives and SocialPilot alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free social media management tool?

    Buffer (3 channels), Metricool (1 brand) and Zoho Social (publish now only) offer free plans; Later and Loomly have limited free tiers. This list is not a ranking.

    Hootsuite or Sprout Social?

    Both are per-user platforms. Hootsuite lists $99 to $399 per user a month annually; Sprout Social lists $79 to $399 per seat. Compare inbox, reporting and add-ons.

    Which social media tool is best for agencies?

    Sendible, SocialPilot, Agorapulse and Zoho Social's agency plans focus on client workspaces, approvals and reports.

    Does Hootsuite still have a free plan?

    No. It retired the free plan in March 2023; Agorapulse also ended its free plan in July 2026.
