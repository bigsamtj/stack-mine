    Quick answer

    What is Payment Gateways?

    A payment gateway lets a business accept card, wallet and local payments online and settle them to a bank account. Processors charge per transaction; enterprise providers offer Interchange++ pricing; merchants of record also handle sales tax and VAT.

    Also called Payments Software

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 14 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 7 of 14 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: No monthly fee from Stripe, PayPal, Braintree, Razorpay, PayU, Paddle and Mollie; Indian gateways from 1.95%. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Flat rate vs Interchange++, Merchant of record or not, Local methods and settlement countries.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Payment fees are often a business's largest software-related cost, so small rate differences matter at scale.

    This category covers developer platforms (Stripe, Braintree), consumer wallets (PayPal), enterprise processors (Adyen, Checkout.com), a merchant of record for SaaS (Paddle), a European provider (Mollie) and Indian gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU).

    Flat rates commonly run about 2% to 3.5% plus a fixed fee; enterprises negotiate Interchange++. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Key features

    Key features and capabilities of Payment Gateways.

    The capabilities that define the category. Not every product offers all of them, and depth varies a lot between entry-level and enterprise tools.

    • Online card acceptance

      Accept major cards securely on websites and apps.

    • Local payment methods

      Wallets, bank transfers and regional methods customers prefer.

    • Recurring payments

      Store payment details securely for subscriptions and instalments.

    • Fraud screening

      Rules and risk scoring to block suspicious payments.

    • Payouts and settlement

      Funds settled to your bank account on a schedule.

    • Refunds and disputes

      Manage refunds, chargebacks and evidence.

    • Hosted checkout and APIs

      Use a hosted page or build a custom checkout.

    • Reporting and reconciliation

      Match transactions, fees and payouts to the books.

    Questions to ask before you buy

    • What are the total fees, including cross-border, currency and dispute fees?
    • Which local payment methods do you support in our markets?
    • How long does settlement take, and are there reserves?
    • How is PCI DSS scope handled?
    • How does reporting reconcile with our accounting system?
    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Payment Gateways.

    Comparing headline rates only

    FX, international and chargeback fees add up.

    Ignoring tax obligations

    Processors do not file your VAT.

    Single gateway dependency

    Glossary

    Payment Gateways key terms explained.

    MDR
    Merchant discount rate charged per transaction.
    Interchange++
    Card network cost plus scheme fees plus markup.
    Merchant of record
    The legal seller responsible for tax and chargebacks.
    Chargeback
    A disputed payment reversed by the card issuer.
    Settlement
    Paying collected funds to the merchant.
    UPI
    India's Unified Payments Interface.

    FAQ

    Payment Gateways questions and answers

    What is the best free payment gateways and processors?

    7 of the 14 payment gateways and processors profiles here document a free plan: Braintree, Mollie, Paddle, PayPal, PayU, Razorpay and Stripe. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best payment gateways and processors for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Mollie and Paddle. Mollie may suit european SMBs needing local payment methods. Paddle may suit global SaaS and digital product companies (~$100k-$10M ARR). The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which payment gateways and processors suits large enterprises?

    8 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Adyen, Braintree, Cashfree Payments, Checkout.com, PayPal, PayU, Razorpay and Stripe. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    What is the best payment gateways and processors for India?

    Products built for or documented in India include Cashfree Payments, PayU and Razorpay. Indian payroll must handle PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax, so check statutory coverage on each profile.

    Which payment gateways and processors supports international teams?

    Adyen, Braintree, Checkout.com, Paddle, PayPal and Stripe support international hiring, HR or payroll. Employer-of-record and global payroll services are priced per employee.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific payment gateway?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Adyen alternatives, Braintree alternatives and Stripe alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the cheapest payment gateway?

    It depends on volume and region: Indian gateways start around 1.95% to 2%; Stripe EEA cards start at 1.5% + EUR 0.25; enterprises negotiate Interchange++. This list is not a ranking.

    Stripe or Paddle?

    Stripe is a processor at 2.9% + 30 cents (US); Paddle is a merchant of record at 5% + 50 cents that handles tax.

    Razorpay or Cashfree?

    Razorpay is 2% + GST; Cashfree is 1.95% with a reported annual fee, per a 2026 comparison.

    What is Interchange++ pricing?

    Card network interchange plus scheme fees plus the provider markup, used by Adyen and Checkout.com.
