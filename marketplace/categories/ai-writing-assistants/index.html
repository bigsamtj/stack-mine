    Quick answer

    What is AI Writing Assistants?

    AI writing assistants help people draft, rewrite and polish text. Some generate marketing and SEO content with brand controls; others focus on grammar, paraphrasing and rewriting; enterprise platforms add governance and AI agents.

    Also called AI Writing Agents, Content Generation Agents

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 4 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Grammarly, QuillBot, Wordtune and Rytr; Rytr Unlimited $7.50 a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Generation vs editing, Brand and governance needs, Plan changes during 2026.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    General chatbots now write well, so dedicated AI writing tools compete on templates, brand controls, workflows, scoring and integrations.

    This category covers marketing and SEO content (Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic, Anyword, Hypotenuse AI), editing and paraphrasing (Grammarly, QuillBot, Wordtune), budget drafting (Rytr) and enterprise AI (Writer).

    Prices range from free plans to five-figure enterprise contracts, and several vendors changed plans during 2026. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Company information the AI can reference.

    Usage units consumed by AI features.

    FAQ

    AI Writing Assistants questions and answers

    What is the best free AI writing assistants?

    4 of the 10 AI writing assistants profiles here document a free plan: Grammarly, QuillBot, Rytr and Wordtune. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best AI writing assistants for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include QuillBot, Rytr and Wordtune. QuillBot may suit students and writers paraphrasing and polishing text cheaply. Rytr may suit budget users drafting short-form copy. Wordtune may suit writers polishing existing text rather than generating it. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which AI writing assistants suits large enterprises?

    7 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Anyword, Copy.ai, Grammarly, Hypotenuse AI, Jasper, Writer and Writesonic. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific AI writing tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Copy.ai alternatives, Jasper alternatives and Writesonic alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free AI writing tool?

    Grammarly, QuillBot, Wordtune and Rytr have free plans; this list is not a ranking.

    Jasper or Copy.ai?

    Jasper Pro is $59 per seat a month (annual) with Brand Voice; Copy.ai Chat is $29 a month for 5 seats with workflows on Growth at $1,000.

    Grammarly or QuillBot?

    Grammarly Pro is $12 a month (annual) for real-time editing; QuillBot Premium is $8.33 a month with a paraphraser focus.
