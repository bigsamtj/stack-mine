    Quick answer

    What is HR & Payroll Software?

    HR and payroll software pays employees correctly and on time, files payroll taxes, and keeps employee records, leave, attendance and benefits in one place. Some products focus on payroll, some on the HR record (HRIS), and some, such as employer-of-record platforms, let you hire people in countries where you have no entity.

    Also called HR Software, Human Resource Management System (HRMS)

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 20 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 2 of 20 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: From $3 per user a month (Zoho People) and $10 per employee (BambooHR) to custom quotes. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Countries and states you pay in, Payroll included or add-on, Total per-employee cost.
    • Sourcing: 1 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 9 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    HR and payroll systems sit at the centre of every employer's compliance: getting pay, tax withholding and statutory contributions right each cycle, while keeping accurate records for onboarding, leave and exits.

    The category spans US full-service payroll providers such as Gusto, ADP and Paychex, HRIS platforms such as BambooHR, Personio and Zoho People, all-in-one workforce platforms such as Rippling, global employment platforms such as Deel, and Indian HRMS products such as Keka and greytHR built around PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax.

    Pricing ranges from published per-employee rates to custom quotes, and the country you pay people in usually narrows the shortlist fastest. The profiles below summarise dated sources; they are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing HR & Payroll Software.

    Ignoring per-employee growth

    Per-employee fees grow with every hire, so model cost at future headcount.

    Switching payroll mid-year

    Moving mid-year means migrating year-to-date figures accurately.

    Assuming payroll is included

    Several HR platforms sell payroll separately or only in some countries.

    Overlooking contractors

    Paying international contractors needs different tools and compliance from employees.

    Skipping a parallel run

    A single wrong payroll can damage trust and trigger penalties.

    Glossary

    HR & Payroll Software key terms explained.

    Full-service payroll
    Payroll where the provider calculates pay and also files and pays payroll taxes on your behalf.
    HRIS
    Human resources information system: the core employee record for personal data, jobs, documents and leave.
    Employer of record (EOR)
    A provider that legally employs a worker in another country on your behalf.
    PEO
    Professional employer organisation: co-employment that pools benefits and compliance for small US employers.
    PF, ESI and PT
    Indian statutory contributions: Provident Fund, Employees' State Insurance and Professional Tax.
    Per employee per month (PEPM)
    A pricing model that charges a fee for each employee every month.

    FAQ

    HR & Payroll Software questions and answers

    What is the best free HR and payroll software?

    2 of the 20 HR and payroll software profiles here document a free plan: Deel and Zoho People. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best HR and payroll software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include BambooHR, greytHR, Gusto, Keka and Zoho People. BambooHR may suit small and mid-size businesses that want an easy HRIS. greytHR may suit Indian SMEs that want affordable payroll and leave management. Gusto may suit US small businesses that want full-service payroll with simple HR. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which HR and payroll software suits large enterprises?

    5 profiles are documented for large enterprises: ADP Workforce Now, Deel, Paychex Flex, Personio and Rippling. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which HR and payroll software has built-in AI features?

    1 of 20 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: Zoho People. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    What is the best HR and payroll software for India?

    Products built for or documented in India include greytHR, Keka and Zoho People. Indian payroll must handle PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax, so check statutory coverage on each profile.

    Which HR and payroll software supports international teams?

    Deel, Rippling and Zoho People support international hiring, HR or payroll. Employer-of-record and global payroll services are priced per employee.

    Which HR and payroll software includes payroll?

    7 of 20 profiles include payroll rather than selling it separately: ADP Workforce Now, Deel, greytHR, Gusto, Keka, Paychex Flex and Rippling.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific HR and payroll platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example BambooHR alternatives, Gusto alternatives and Rippling alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the difference between HR software and payroll software?

    Payroll software calculates pay and files taxes; HR software (an HRIS) keeps employee records, leave and documents. Many products combine both, while others sell payroll as an add-on.

    Which HR and payroll software suits Indian companies?

    Indian payroll must handle PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax. Keka and greytHR are built for this, and Zoho People covers HR but not payroll.

    How do I pay employees in another country without an entity?

    An employer of record such as Deel employs them on your behalf for a monthly fee per employee, reported at $599 a month for Deel's EOR.

    Why are many HR and payroll prices quote-only?

    Costs depend on headcount, states or countries, pay frequency and modules, so vendors such as ADP, Paychex, Personio and Rippling quote individually.
