    Quick answer

    What is Email & SMS API Software?

    Email and SMS APIs let applications send password resets, receipts, alerts and verification codes programmatically. Email APIs handle delivery, templates and analytics; SMS APIs send texts through carriers and charge per message plus number and carrier fees.

    Glossary

    Email & SMS API Software key terms explained.

    Transactional email
    Triggered messages like receipts and resets.
    SMTP relay
    Sending email via the SMTP protocol.
    DKIM/SPF/DMARC
    Email authentication standards.
    10DLC
    US 10-digit long code for business SMS.
    Short code
    A 5-6 digit number for high-volume SMS.
    Carrier fee
    A surcharge mobile carriers add per message.

    FAQ

    Email & SMS API Software questions and answers

    What is the best free transactional email and SMS API services?

    5 of the 12 transactional email and SMS API services profiles here document a free plan: MailerSend, Mailgun, Mailtrap, Postmark and Resend. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best transactional email and SMS API services for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include MailerSend, Postmark and Resend. MailerSend may suit teams wanting templates and SMS without heavy coding. Postmark may suit apps where transactional delivery speed matters. Resend may suit developers building modern apps with React. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which transactional email and SMS API services suits large enterprises?

    7 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Amazon SES, Mailgun, Mailtrap, Plivo, SendGrid, Sinch and Twilio Programmable Messaging. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific messaging API?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Mailgun alternatives, Resend alternatives and SendGrid alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best transactional email API?

    Postmark suits deliverability; Amazon SES suits lowest cost; Resend suits React developers; Mailgun suits analytics and inbound; SendGrid suits combined transactional and marketing. This list is not a ranking.

    What is the cheapest email API?

    Amazon SES at $0.10 per 1,000 emails pay-as-you-go.

    Twilio or Sinch for SMS?

    Twilio lists US SMS at $0.0083 with a fully published fee schedule; Sinch lists $0.0078 on 10DLC with direct carrier connections.

    Which email APIs have free tiers?

    Mailtrap (4,000 a month), Resend (3,000), MailerSend (500), Mailgun (100 a day) and Postmark (100 a month).
