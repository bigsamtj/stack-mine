    Quick answer

    What is Customer Feedback Software?

    Customer feedback software collects opinions from customers through surveys (NPS, CSAT, CES), in-app prompts, feature-request portals and public reviews, then analyses and routes that feedback so teams can act on it.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 3 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Survicate (25 responses a month), Qualaroo and Trustpilot (50 invitations a month); paid from $19.99 a month (Qualaroo, annual). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Survey vs review vs feature-request focus, Response or invitation limits, Auto-renewal and minimum terms.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Feedback tools differ by who the feedback is for: product teams want in-app surveys and feature requests, CX teams want NPS programmes, and marketing teams want public reviews.

    This category covers enterprise CX (Medallia), feature-request management (UserVoice), multi-channel surveys (Survicate, Qualaroo, Zonka Feedback), NPS for service teams (AskNicely, Retently) and review platforms (Birdeye, Trustpilot Business, Yotpo).

    Delighted, a popular NPS tool, shut down on 30 June 2026, so many buyers are switching. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Customer Feedback Software.

    Ignoring auto-renewal

    Several vendors auto-renew with strict notice periods.

    Over-surveying

    Too many surveys lower response rates.

    Collecting without acting

    Route feedback to owners.

    Underestimating volume

    Response and order caps trigger upgrades.

    Mixing reviews and NPS

    Public reviews and private NPS serve different goals.

    Glossary

    Customer Feedback Software key terms explained.

    NPS
    Net Promoter Score, from the 0-10 likelihood-to-recommend question.
    CSAT
    Customer satisfaction score.
    CES
    Customer effort score.
    Closing the loop
    Following up with customers who gave feedback.
    Nudge survey
    A short targeted on-page or in-app survey.
    UGC
    User-generated content such as photo reviews.

    What is the best free customer feedback, NPS and review management software?

    3 of the 10 customer feedback, NPS and review management software profiles here document a free plan: Qualaroo, Survicate and Trustpilot Business. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best customer feedback, NPS and review management software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Retently. Retently may suit SMBs running relationship and transactional NPS. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which customer feedback, NPS and review management software suits large enterprises?

    9 profiles are documented for large enterprises: AskNicely, Birdeye, Medallia, Qualaroo, Survicate, Trustpilot Business, UserVoice, Yotpo and 1 more. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    What is the best customer feedback software?
