    Quick answer

    What is Form & Survey Software?

    Form and survey software lets you build online forms, surveys and quizzes, collect responses, take payments and send data to other tools. Form builders focus on data collection and workflows; survey platforms add sampling, analysis and research features.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 12 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 8 of 12 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free (Google Forms, Tally, Fillout) to $15 to $39 a month for paid form builders; enterprise research from about $30,000 a year. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Monthly response or submission limits, Branding removal, Payments and compliance needs.
    • Sourcing: 2 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 8 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Forms are how most businesses collect leads, orders, applications, feedback and research data.

    This category covers free and free-first tools (Google Forms, Tally, Fillout), design-led builders (Typeform, Paperform), versatile business form builders (Jotform, Zoho Forms, Formstack) and survey and research platforms (SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics).

    Most tools have free plans; the main differences are response limits, branding, payments, compliance (such as HIPAA) and collaboration. The profiles below summarise dated sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Evaluation criteria

    Features and criteria to evaluate in Form & Survey Software.

    01

    Response limits

    Free and paid plans cap responses or submissions differently; check monthly vs stored limits.

    02

    Branding and design

    Check when you can remove the vendor badge and use custom domains.

    03

    Payments

    Some tools allow payments on free plans; others need paid tiers.

    04

    Compliance

    HIPAA, GDPR and data residency often require higher plans.

    05

    Integrations

    Confirm native links to your CRM, spreadsheets and automation tools.

    06

    Analysis

    Survey platforms add reporting, cross-tabs and text analysis.

    • What response limits apply?
    • How is data stored and secured?
    • Which integrations are native?
    • Does it support conditional logic?
    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Form & Survey Software.

    Ignoring response caps

    Some forms close when monthly limits are reached.

    Paying for research tools for simple forms

    Enterprise survey platforms are overkill for lead forms.

    Overlooking compliance

    Health and personal data may need HIPAA-ready plans.

    Too many questions

    Long forms reduce completion rates.

    Not testing on mobile

    Most respondents use phones.

    Glossary

    Form & Survey Software key terms explained.

    Conditional logic
    Showing or skipping questions based on earlier answers.
    Submission limit
    The maximum responses a plan allows per month or stored.
    Partial submissions
    Capturing answers from people who do not finish.
    Conversational form
    A form that shows one question at a time.
    HIPAA
    US rules for protecting health information.
    NPS
    Net Promoter Score, a common customer loyalty survey.

    FAQ

    Form & Survey Software questions and answers

    What is the best free form and survey software?

    8 of the 12 form and survey software profiles here document a free plan: Fillout, Google Forms, Jotform, Paperform, SurveyMonkey, Tally, Typeform and Zoho Forms. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best form and survey software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Fillout, Paperform, Tally and Zoho Forms. Fillout may suit teams wanting Typeform-style forms with a generous free tier. Paperform may suit small businesses selling products or services through forms. Tally may suit founders and small teams wanting powerful forms for free. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which form and survey software suits large enterprises?

    6 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Formstack, Google Forms, Jotform, Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey and Typeform. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which form and survey software has built-in AI features?

    2 of 12 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: Google Forms and Typeform. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific form builder?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Jotform alternatives, Paperform alternatives and Typeform alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free form builder?

    Google Forms is fully free; Tally offers unlimited forms and submissions under fair use; Fillout includes 1,000 responses a month. This list is not a ranking.

    Typeform or Tally?

    Typeform's free plan allows 10 responses a month and paid plans start at $39; Tally is free for unlimited submissions with Pro at $29.

    Which form builder supports HIPAA?

    Jotform makes HIPAA-enabled features available on Gold; check each vendor's compliance documentation.

    SurveyMonkey or Qualtrics?

    SurveyMonkey has self-serve plans from $300 a year; Qualtrics is quote-only for enterprise research, with a reported median near $30,000 a year.
