    Quick answer

    What is Email Marketing Software?

    Email marketing software lets you collect subscribers, design and send newsletters and promotional campaigns, and automate messages such as welcome series or abandoned cart reminders. Most platforms also handle segmentation, A/B testing, deliverability and reporting, and many add SMS, landing pages or AI writing tools.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 16 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 7 of 16 offer a free plan.
    • $9–$33 reported entry price per month at the smallest paid list size. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Pricing model (contacts vs sends), Automation limits by plan, Deliverability and sending caps.
    • Sourcing: 1 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 9 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Email marketing platforms manage the whole lifecycle of a list: sign-up forms and landing pages to collect subscribers, an editor to design emails, sending infrastructure that protects deliverability, and reports on opens, clicks and revenue.

    The market splits into general-purpose tools for newsletters and small-business campaigns, automation-heavy platforms for behaviour-driven marketing, creator tools built around newsletters and digital products, and ecommerce-first platforms that turn store data into email and SMS flows.

    Pricing models matter more here than in most categories. Some vendors charge by stored contacts, some by subscribers, and some by the number of emails sent, so the cheapest tool depends on the size and engagement of your list. The profiles below summarise dated sources; they are research aids, not rankings.

    Who uses it

    Who uses Email Marketing Software and what problems it solves.

    Small businesses and nonprofits

    Send regular newsletters and promotions and want an easy editor, templates and support without a marketing team.

    Creators and publishers

    Grow an audience, run sequences and sell digital products or paid newsletters from one list.

    Ecommerce brands

    Use store data to trigger abandoned cart, browse and post-purchase flows, often alongside SMS.

    Marketing teams

    Need behaviour-based automation, scoring, segmentation and testing across larger lists and several users.

    Evaluation criteria

    Features and criteria to evaluate in Email Marketing Software.

    01

    Pricing model and list size

    Check whether you pay by stored contacts, subscribers or emails sent, and how unsubscribed or inactive contacts are counted.

    02

    Sending limits

    Look at monthly and daily send caps, overage charges and whether automated emails count toward them.

    03

    Automation depth

    Compare how many automations, steps and triggers each plan allows, and which plan unlocks branching journeys.

    04

    Deliverability

    Ask about authentication setup, shared versus dedicated IPs and the tools offered to keep lists clean.

    05

    Ecommerce and integrations

    Confirm native connections to your store, CRM, forms and analytics, and whether product data drives emails.

    06

    AI and content tools

    Separate AI writing help and send-time optimisation included in plans from features sold as add-ons.

    How to choose

    How to choose Email Marketing Software: a step-by-step approach.

    1. 1

      Size your list honestly

      Count active subscribers, total stored contacts and how often you send. That decides whether contact-based or send-based pricing is cheaper.

    2. 2

      List the emails you need

      Write down newsletters, promotions and automated flows such as welcome series, abandoned cart or re-engagement, then check which plan supports each.

    3. 3

      Price the next two years

      Model cost at your expected list size, including SMS credits, extra users, logo removal and other add-ons.

    4. 4

      Check deliverability basics

      Confirm domain authentication support, sending reputation tools and whether a dedicated IP is available or needed.

    5. 5

      Test with real content

      Build one real campaign and one automation in two or three tools and send test emails to different inbox providers.

    6. 6

      Plan migration and exit

      Check how lists, tags, consent records and templates export, and what is lost when you leave.

    Questions to ask before you buy

    • How is pricing tied to subscribers or sends?
    • What deliverability support is included?
    • Does it integrate with our store or CRM?
    • How is consent recorded?
    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Email Marketing Software.

    Paying for dead contacts

    On contact-based plans every stored contact counts, so unengaged subscribers raise the bill without adding value.

    Choosing on the free plan

    Free plans are often small and change; check what the first paid tier costs at your realistic list size.

    Ignoring send caps

    Monthly and daily limits can stop campaigns mid-send or trigger overage charges.

    Skipping authentication

    Without proper domain authentication, even good emails can land in spam.

    Over-building automation

    Complex journeys are hard to maintain. Start with a few high-value flows and expand.

    Glossary

    Email Marketing Software key terms explained.

    Contact-based pricing
    Billing by the number of contacts stored in your account, whether or not you email them.
    Send-based pricing
    Billing by the number of emails sent each month, regardless of list size.
    Automation (flow or journey)
    A sequence of emails or actions triggered by subscriber behaviour, such as signing up or abandoning a cart.
    Segmentation
    Dividing a list into groups by data or behaviour so each group gets more relevant emails.
    Deliverability
    The share of emails that reach the inbox rather than spam, shaped by authentication, reputation and list quality.
    Dedicated IP
    A sending address used only by your account, giving more control over reputation at higher volumes.

    FAQ

    Email Marketing Software questions and answers

    What is the best free email marketing software?

    7 of the 16 email marketing software profiles here document a free plan: Brevo, GetResponse, Kit (ConvertKit), Klaviyo, Mailchimp, MailerLite and Omnisend. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best email marketing software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Constant Contact, Kit (ConvertKit), Mailchimp, MailerLite and Omnisend. Constant Contact may suit small businesses and nonprofits that want guided, all-in-one email. Kit (ConvertKit) may suit creators and solo businesses building a newsletter. Mailchimp may suit small businesses that want a well-known all-round email tool. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which email marketing software suits large enterprises?

    5 profiles are documented for large enterprises: ActiveCampaign, Brevo, Campaign Monitor, GetResponse and Klaviyo. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which email marketing software has built-in AI features?

    7 of 16 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: ActiveCampaign, Brevo, Campaign Monitor, Constant Contact, GetResponse, Klaviyo and MailerLite. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    What is the best email marketing software for ecommerce?

    Platforms built ecommerce-first include Klaviyo and Omnisend. They use store data for abandoned cart, browse and post-purchase flows. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific email marketing platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Brevo alternatives, Mailchimp alternatives and MailerLite alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    Is contact-based or send-based pricing cheaper?

    It depends on your list. Large lists that are emailed rarely are often cheaper on send-based plans, such as Brevo's, while small, frequently emailed lists can be cheaper on contact-based plans.

    Do I need an ecommerce-specific email platform?

    If most of your email revenue comes from store-triggered flows such as abandoned cart or post-purchase, ecommerce-first tools like Klaviyo or Omnisend are built for that. Newsletter-led businesses usually do not need one.

    Why do email marketing prices differ between sources?

    Vendors price by list size and change plans often; in 2025 and 2026 several, including Brevo, MailerLite and Kit, restructured plans. Confirm current prices on the vendor's own pricing page.

    Are free email marketing plans enough?

    For very small lists, sometimes. Most free plans cap contacts or sends tightly and keep vendor branding, so check the first paid tier before building a list on a free plan.
