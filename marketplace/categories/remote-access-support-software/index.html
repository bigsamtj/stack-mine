    Quick answer

    What is Remote Access & Support Software?

    Remote access software lets you control a computer from another device. Remote support software adds tools for IT teams and help desks to assist users on demand, manage unattended devices and audit sessions.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 3 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free: Chrome Remote Desktop, RustDesk (self-hosted), Zoho Assist free plan, TeamViewer (personal use); paid from $6 a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Access vs support use, Device and technician limits, Commercial-use rules on free plans.

    Remote access tools range from free personal apps to enterprise platforms with auditing and privileged access controls.

    This category covers remote access (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, Splashtop, RemotePC, Chrome Remote Desktop), remote support for IT and MSPs (ScreenConnect, Zoho Assist, GoTo Resolve, BeyondTrust) and self-hosted open source (RustDesk).

    Paid plans range from about $6 a month to enterprise quotes. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Remote Access & Support Software.

    Using personal free plans for business

    Some vendors flag and block commercial use.

    Ignoring device caps

    Entry plans may cover only a few devices.

    Skipping MFA

    Remote access is a common attack path.

    Forgetting session logs

    Audits need recordings or logs.

    Overlooking contract terms

    Some plans auto-renew with annual minimums.

    Glossary

    Remote Access & Support Software key terms explained.

    Unattended access
    Connecting to a device without someone present.
    Attended support
    Helping a user who is at the device.
    Concurrent technician
    A licence shared by technicians not working at the same time.
    Session recording
    Saving a video or log of a remote session.
    RMM
    Remote monitoring and management of endpoints.
    Self-hosted server
    A relay server you run yourself.

    FAQ

    Remote Access & Support Software questions and answers

    What is the best free remote access and remote support software?

    3 of the 10 remote access and remote support software profiles here document a free plan: Chrome Remote Desktop, RustDesk and Zoho Assist. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best remote access and remote support software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include AnyDesk, Chrome Remote Desktop, GoTo Resolve, RemotePC, RustDesk and Zoho Assist. AnyDesk may suit freelancers and small teams wanting lightweight, fast access. Chrome Remote Desktop may suit occasional personal access to your own machines. GoTo Resolve may suit small IT teams wanting remote support plus endpoint management. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which remote access and remote support software suits large enterprises?

    4 profiles are documented for large enterprises: BeyondTrust Remote Support, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, Splashtop and TeamViewer. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific remote access tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example AnyDesk alternatives, ConnectWise ScreenConnect alternatives and TeamViewer alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free remote desktop software?

    Chrome Remote Desktop and RustDesk are free; TeamViewer is free for personal use; Zoho Assist has a free plan. This list is not a ranking.

    TeamViewer or AnyDesk?

    TeamViewer lists $24.90 to $245.90 a month; AnyDesk $28.90 to $111.90, both billed annually.

    What is the cheapest paid remote access?

    Splashtop Solo is listed at $6 a month.

    Which tools suit MSPs?

    ScreenConnect, Zoho Assist and GoTo Resolve focus on remote support for IT and MSPs.
