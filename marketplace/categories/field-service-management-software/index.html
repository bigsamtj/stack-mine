    Quick answer

    What is Field Service Management Software?

    Field service management (FSM) software helps trades and home service businesses book jobs, dispatch technicians, quote, invoice and get paid from the field. Advanced platforms add price books, service agreements, job costing, call tracking and marketing attribution.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 3 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Workiz (2 users), Kickserv (2 users) and ServiceM8 (30 jobs a month); paid from $29 (ServiceM8) or $49 a month (Jobber Core). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Number of technicians and office users, QuickBooks Online vs Desktop, Price book and service agreement needs.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    FSM tools range from free apps for solo operators to enterprise platforms for hundreds of technicians, and pricing models differ: per user, per plan with user limits, unlimited users, job volume or quote.

    This category covers small-business tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro, Kickserv, ServiceM8), call-driven platforms (Workiz), unlimited-user plans (Service Fusion), trade-focused quote-based systems (ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, FieldPulse) and project-heavy contractors (simPRO).

    Glossary

    Field Service Management Software key terms explained.

    Dispatch board
    A schedule view for assigning jobs to technicians.
    Flat-rate price book
    Fixed task prices instead of time and materials.
    Service agreement
    A recurring maintenance contract.
    Job costing
    Tracking labour and material cost per job.
    WIP
    Work in progress on project jobs.
    Call tracking
    Attributing calls to marketing sources.

    Field Service Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free field service management software for trades and home services?

    3 of the 10 field service management software for trades and home services profiles here document a free plan: Kickserv, ServiceM8 and Workiz. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best field service management software for trades and home services for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include FieldEdge, FieldPulse, Housecall Pro, Jobber, Kickserv, Service Fusion, ServiceM8 and Workiz. FieldEdge may suit HVAC and plumbing shops on QuickBooks Desktop. FieldPulse may suit trades teams with many field users and lean offices. Housecall Pro may suit home service companies wanting booking, payments and marketing. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which field service management software for trades and home services suits large enterprises?

    2 profiles are documented for large enterprises: ServiceTitan and simPRO. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific field service platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Jobber alternatives, Service Fusion alternatives and Workiz alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best field service management software?

    Jobber and Housecall Pro suit small home service teams; ServiceTitan and FieldEdge suit larger HVAC and plumbing shops; simPRO suits project-heavy contractors. This list is not a ranking.

    Jobber or Housecall Pro?

    Jobber Core is $49 a month for 1 user; Housecall Pro Basic is $59 a month billed annually. Housecall Pro charges $149 a month for its flat-rate price book.

    Is there free field service software?

    Workiz and Kickserv are free for 2 users and ServiceM8 is free for 30 jobs a month.

    How much does ServiceTitan cost?

    It is quote-only; 2026 guides cite estimates of $245 to $500+ per technician a month plus setup.
