    Quick answer

    What is IT Service Management Software?

    IT service management (ITSM) software runs the IT service desk: logging incidents and requests, managing change and problems, tracking assets and configuration items, and offering self-service portals, usually aligned with ITIL practices.

    Also called IT Service Management (ITSM)

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 13 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 2 of 13 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free self-hosted GLPI; ManageEngine free edition (5 technicians); paid from $13 per technician a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Which tier includes change and problem, Agent definition and licensing, Implementation cost.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    ITSM tools turn IT support into a managed service with tickets, SLAs, change control and asset records.

    This category covers enterprise platforms (ServiceNow, BMC Helix, Ivanti), mid-market ITIL tools (SysAid, HaloITSM, TOPdesk, InvGate, SolarWinds) and budget or open-source options (ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus, GLPI). Jira Service Management and Freshservice have their own profiles.

    Prices range from free self-hosting to quotes averaging six figures a year. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and estimates, and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing IT Service Management Software.

    Buying the wrong tier

    Change and problem are often in higher tiers.

    Underestimating implementation

    Enterprise rollouts cost far more than licences.

    Miscounting agents

    Some vendors count anyone who edits tickets.

    Ignoring the CMDB

    Poor asset data weakens change management.

    Skipping self-service

    Portals and knowledge reduce ticket volume.

    Glossary

    IT Service Management Software key terms explained.

    ITIL
    A widely used framework of IT service management practices.
    Incident
    An unplanned interruption to an IT service.
    Change management
    Controlling changes to reduce risk.
    CMDB
    Configuration management database of IT assets and relationships.
    Fulfiller
    A licensed user who works tickets.
    SLA
    Service level agreement for response and resolution times.

    FAQ

    IT Service Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free IT service management software?

    2 of the 13 IT service management software profiles here document a free plan: GLPI and ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best IT service management software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include SolarWinds Service Desk. SolarWinds Service Desk may suit teams wanting service desk tied to SolarWinds monitoring. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which IT service management software suits large enterprises?

    9 profiles are documented for large enterprises: BMC Helix ITSM, GLPI, HaloITSM, InvGate Service Management, Ivanti Neurons for ITSM, ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus, ServiceNow ITSM, SysAid and 1 more. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific ITSM tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example InvGate Service Management alternatives, ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus alternatives and SysAid alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best ITSM tool?

    It depends on size: ServiceNow and BMC Helix for complex enterprises; SysAid, HaloITSM, TOPdesk and InvGate for mid-market; ManageEngine and GLPI for budgets. This list is not a ranking.

    How much does ServiceNow ITSM cost?

    ServiceNow quotes prices; 2026 estimates run about $70 to $200+ per fulfiller a month by edition.

    Is there free ITSM software?

    GLPI is open source and free to self-host; ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus has a free edition for 5 technicians.

    What changed in ServiceNow packaging in 2026?

    Foundation, Advanced and Prime editions replaced the old tiers on 9 April 2026, per a 2026 guide.
