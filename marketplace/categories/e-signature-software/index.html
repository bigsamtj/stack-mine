    Quick answer

    What is E-signature Software?

    E-signature software lets people sign contracts and forms online with a legally recognised electronic signature, an audit trail and tamper evidence. Plans differ by senders, envelope limits, templates, integrations and API access.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 11 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 4 of 11 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from PandaDoc, Zoho Sign, SignWell and BoldSign; paid from $5 per user (BoldSign) to about $45 (DocuSign Business Pro). Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Envelope limits and overage fees, Per-user vs per-envelope pricing, Compliance needs (HIPAA, eIDAS).
    • Sourcing: 1 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 9 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    E-signatures replace printing, scanning and couriering documents, cutting turnaround from days to minutes.

    This category covers market leaders (DocuSign, Adobe Acrobat Sign, Dropbox Sign), document automation (PandaDoc), affordable and developer-friendly tools (SignNow, BoldSign, SignWell, Signeasy, Zoho Sign) and enterprise platforms for regulated industries (OneSpan Sign).

    Prices range from free plans to about $45 per user a month, and envelope caps matter as much as seat prices. The profiles below summarise dated sources and are research aids, not legal advice or rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing E-signature Software.

    Ignoring envelope caps

    Some plans limit envelopes per user per year.

    Paying for seats occasional senders don't need

    Flat or per-envelope plans may be cheaper.

    Assuming all signatures are equal

    Qualified signatures (eIDAS QES) may be required in some cases.

    Skipping templates

    Templates save time and reduce errors.

    Overlooking ID verification fees

    Identity checks are often charged per attempt.

    Glossary

    E-signature Software key terms explained.

    Envelope
    A package of one or more documents sent for signature.
    Audit trail
    A record of who viewed and signed a document and when.
    ESIGN and UETA
    US laws that recognise electronic signatures.
    eIDAS
    EU regulation defining simple, advanced and qualified electronic signatures.
    Bulk send
    Sending one document to many recipients at once.
    Embedded signing
    Signing inside another application via API.

    FAQ

    E-signature Software questions and answers

    What is the best free e-signature software?

    4 of the 11 e-signature software profiles here document a free plan: BoldSign, PandaDoc, SignWell and Zoho Sign. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best e-signature software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include BoldSign, Dropbox Sign, Signeasy and SignWell. BoldSign may suit developers and SMBs wanting low-cost signing with a strong API. Dropbox Sign may suit teams storing documents in Dropbox. Signeasy may suit small businesses wanting simple unlimited sending. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which e-signature software suits large enterprises?

    6 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Adobe Acrobat Sign, DocuSign, OneSpan Sign, PandaDoc, SignNow and Zoho Sign. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Which e-signature software has built-in AI features?

    1 of 11 profiles document an AI assistant, agents or AI features: DocuSign. Check which plans include AI and whether agent or credit usage is billed separately.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific e-signature tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example DocuSign alternatives, Dropbox Sign alternatives and SignNow alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the cheapest e-signature software?

    BoldSign (free 25 envelopes a month; Growth $5 per user), SignNow ($8 per user) and SignWell ($10 a month unlimited documents) are among the lowest-priced. This list is not a ranking.

    Are e-signatures legally binding?

    In many countries, yes, under laws such as ESIGN and UETA in the US and eIDAS in the EU; some documents have special requirements. This is not legal advice.

    DocuSign or Adobe Acrobat Sign?

    DocuSign lists $25 to $45 per user a month with envelope caps; Acrobat Sign team plans run $16.99 to $29.99 per user with a two-licence minimum and include Acrobat PDF tools.

    Is there a free e-signature tool?

    Yes. PandaDoc, Zoho Sign, SignWell and BoldSign have free plans with document or envelope limits.
