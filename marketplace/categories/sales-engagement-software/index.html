    Quick answer

    What is Sales Engagement Software?

    Sales engagement software automates and tracks the outreach sellers do every day: multi-step sequences across email, phone, LinkedIn and SMS, with tracking, reminders and CRM sync. Cold email tools focus on high-volume sending and deliverability; platforms add calls, deals and forecasting.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 25 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 8 of 25 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: From free (Apollo.io, Mixmax, lemlist lead finding) and $29 a month to enterprise contracts above $45,000 a year. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Per user vs per volume pricing, Deliverability and mailbox limits, CRM integration.
    • Sourcing: 2 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 8 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Sales engagement tools turn prospecting into repeatable sequences, so every lead gets timely follow-up and managers can see what works.

    The category spans enterprise platforms (Outreach, Salesloft), data-plus-engagement tools (Apollo.io), mid-market platforms (Klenty, Reply.io), inbox tools (Mixmax) and cold email senders built for volume and deliverability (Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, Woodpecker).

    Pricing runs from free plans and $29 a month to enterprise contracts above $45,000 a year, priced per user, by contacts or by sends. The profiles below summarise dated sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Sales Engagement Software.

    Buying enterprise seats too early

    Small teams can start with cheaper tools and upgrade later.

    Burning domains

    Sending too much from new domains without warm-up hurts deliverability.

    Ignoring add-on costs

    LinkedIn automation, dialers, data credits and mailboxes often cost extra.

    Skipping CRM sync

    Activity outside the CRM is invisible to managers.

    Over-automating

    Generic messages at scale lower reply rates and damage reputation.

    Glossary

    Sales Engagement Software key terms explained.

    Sequence (cadence)
    A multi-step series of emails, calls and tasks sent over time.
    Warm-up
    Gradually building a mailbox's sending reputation before campaigns.
    Deliverability
    How reliably emails reach the inbox rather than spam.
    Mailbox rotation
    Spreading sends across many inboxes to stay within limits.
    AI SDR
    An AI agent that prospects, writes and replies on a seller's behalf.
    Reply rate
    The share of contacted prospects who reply.
