    Quick answer

    What is Customer Success Software?

    Customer success software helps B2B teams monitor customer health, run onboarding and renewal playbooks, spot churn risk and find expansion opportunities using product usage, support and CRM data.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 10 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 0 of 10 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Akita from $49 a month with a 14-day trial; SmartKarrot Basic $15,000 a year; most others quote-only. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Accounts per CSM and seat limits, Data sources for health scores, Setup effort and CS Ops capacity.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Customer success platforms are mostly quote-only and priced by full users, managed accounts, modules or CSM count. Only a few publish prices.

    This category covers enterprise suites (Gainsight, Totango, Planhat), mid-market platforms (ChurnZero, Vitally, Velaris, SmartKarrot) and SMB tools (Custify, ClientSuccess, Akita).

    Totango's company rebranded as Odie in September 2026. The profiles summarise August 2026 guides and are research aids, not rankings.

    • Which data sources feed the health score?
    • Can we build our own playbooks?
    • How does it integrate with our CRM and product analytics?

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Customer Success Software.

    Buying enterprise suites too early

    Setup can outrun team capacity.

    Health scores without data

    Connect usage data first.

    Ignoring account caps

    Caps can force upgrades.

    Too many playbooks

    Start with one or two.

    No CS Ops owner

    Rules decay without ownership.

    FAQ

    Customer Success Software questions and answers

    What is the best customer success platforms for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Akita, ClientSuccess, Custify, Velaris and Vitally. Akita may suit small CS teams needing published, low pricing. ClientSuccess may suit growing CS teams wanting outcomes tracking. Custify may suit small and mid-size SaaS teams wanting quick value. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which customer success platforms suits large enterprises?

    5 profiles are documented for large enterprises: ChurnZero, Gainsight, Planhat, SmartKarrot and Totango. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific customer success platform?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example ChurnZero alternatives, Totango alternatives and Vitally alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best customer success software?

    Gainsight and Planhat suit enterprises; ChurnZero and Vitally suit mid-market SaaS; Custify, ClientSuccess and Akita suit small teams. This list is not a ranking.

    Gainsight or ChurnZero?

    Both are quote-only; guides describe ChurnZero as lighter to administer and Gainsight as the broadest suite. Vendr reports a ChurnZero median near $44,681 a year.

    Which customer success tools publish prices?

    Akita ($49 and $99 a month) and SmartKarrot ($15,000 a year entry).

    What happened to Totango?

    The company rebranded as Odie in September 2026, keeping Totango and Catalyst as product names.
