    Quick answer

    What is Inventory Management Software?

    Inventory management software tracks stock levels, locations and movements, and manages purchasing, sales orders and fulfilment. Some tools add multichannel listings, warehouse management, landed costs or manufacturing.

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 12 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 5 of 12 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from Zoho Inventory, Sortly, Katana, Odoo (one app) and Ordoro shipping; paid from $29 a month. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Order, user and item caps, Manufacturing or B2B needs, Onboarding and module fees.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Accurate stock data prevents overselling, stockouts and tied-up cash.

    This category covers SMB inventory (Zoho Inventory, inFlow, Sortly), multichannel and ecommerce operations (Cin7, Linnworks, Ordoro), distribution (Unleashed), manufacturing (Fishbowl, Katana) and ERP-based inventory (Odoo).

    Prices range from free plans to over $1,000 a month, often capped by orders, users or items. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing Inventory Management Software.

    Ignoring order caps

    Peak seasons can exceed plan limits.

    Skipping a stocktake

    Bad opening balances cause errors.

    Choosing ecommerce tools for manufacturing

    Production needs BOMs and routing.

    Underbudgeting onboarding

    Several vendors charge setup fees.

    Comparing promo prices

    First-year discounts may expire.

    Glossary

    Inventory Management Software key terms explained.

    SKU
    Stock keeping unit.
    Landed cost
    Product cost plus freight, duty and fees.
    BOM
    Bill of materials for manufactured items.
    Reorder point
    Stock level that triggers replenishment.
    Batch / serial tracking
    Tracing items by lot or unique number.
    MRP
    Material requirements planning.

    FAQ

    Inventory Management Software questions and answers

    What is the best free inventory management software?

    5 of the 12 inventory management software profiles here document a free plan: Katana, Odoo Inventory, Ordoro, Sortly and Zoho Inventory. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best inventory management software for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Cin7, Fishbowl, inFlow Inventory, Katana, Ordoro, Sortly, Unleashed and Zoho Inventory. Cin7 may suit growing wholesalers and multichannel brands. Fishbowl may suit QuickBooks-centric warehouse and manufacturing operations. inFlow Inventory may suit wholesale and B2B teams wanting unlimited order volume. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which inventory management software suits large enterprises?

    2 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Linnworks and Odoo Inventory. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific inventory tool?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Cin7 alternatives, inFlow Inventory alternatives and Zoho Inventory alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best free inventory software?

    Zoho Inventory (50 orders), Sortly (100 items), Katana (30 SKUs) and Odoo (one app) have free options. This list is not a ranking.

    Cin7 or Unleashed?

    Cin7 Core starts at $349 a month for multichannel brands; Unleashed Core at $399 for distributors needing landed costs.

    Which inventory software suits manufacturers?

    Katana, Fishbowl (Advanced Manufacturing) and Odoo suit manufacturers.

    Are Katana's monthly plans still sold?

    An August 2026 guide says older monthly plans were discontinued for new customers.
