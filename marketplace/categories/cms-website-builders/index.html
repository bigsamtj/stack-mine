    Quick answer

    What is CMS & Website Builders?

    A content management system (CMS) stores and publishes website content. Website builders bundle hosting and visual editing, while headless CMS platforms deliver structured content by API to any front end a developer builds.

    Also called Content Management

    Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team

    Key takeaways

    • 16 profiles in this category, listed alphabetically and not ranked.
    • 9 of 16 offer a free plan.
    • Pricing: Free plans from WordPress.com, Webflow, Framer, Contentful, Sanity, Storyblok and HubSpot; free self-hosting with Ghost and Strapi. Confirm current pricing with each vendor.
    • Check first: Builder vs headless, Seat and site pricing, CMS item and API limits.
    • Sourcing: 0 profiles cite the vendor's own pages and 10 rely on independent sources only, all with dated links.

    Choosing a CMS decides who can edit the site, how fast it can change and what it costs as content and teams grow.

    This category covers hosted builders (WordPress.com, Webflow, Framer, Duda), publishing platforms (Ghost), CRM-connected CMS (HubSpot Content Hub) and headless CMS (Contentful, Sanity, Strapi, Storyblok).

    Prices range from free self-hosting to $300+ a month for enterprise headless plans. The profiles below summarise dated 2026 sources and are research aids, not rankings.

    Common mistakes

    Common mistakes when choosing CMS & Website Builders.

    Ignoring seat costs

    Editors can cost more than the site plan.

    Choosing headless without developers

    Headless needs ongoing front-end work.

    Hitting CMS item caps

    Check item and page limits before launch.

    Forgetting redirects

    Migrations without 301s lose search traffic.

    Locking into monthly billing

    Annual plans are often much cheaper.

    Glossary

    CMS & Website Builders key terms explained.

    Headless CMS
    A CMS that delivers content by API without a built-in front end.
    Collection
    A group of structured CMS items, such as blog posts.
    Visual editor
    Editing a page by clicking on it in preview.
    Self-hosting
    Running software on your own servers.
    Static page
    A page not generated from CMS items.
    API request
    A call from a front end to fetch content.

    FAQ

    CMS & Website Builders questions and answers

    What is the best free CMS and website builders?

    9 of the 16 CMS and website builders profiles here document a free plan: Contentful, Framer, Ghost, HubSpot Content Hub, Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi, Webflow and 1 more. Free plans and tiers usually cap users, records or usage, so check each profile for limits. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking.

    What is the best CMS and website builders for small businesses?

    Products documented mainly for small and mid-size teams include Duda, Framer and Ghost. Duda may suit agencies and SaaS companies managing many client sites. Framer may suit designers and startups shipping polished sites fast. Ghost may suit publishers running newsletters and paid memberships. The best fit depends on your sales or work process and budget, so trial two or three.

    Which CMS and website builders suits large enterprises?

    7 profiles are documented for large enterprises: Contentful, HubSpot Content Hub, Sanity, Storyblok, Strapi, Webflow and WordPress.com. Larger rollouts should also check SSO, permissions, audit logs, data residency and contract terms on each profile.

    Where can I find alternatives to a specific CMS?

    Every product in this category has its own alternatives guide, for example Framer alternatives, Webflow alternatives and WordPress.com alternatives. Each guide compares the main competitors on pricing, free plan, features and fit.

    What is the best CMS for a small business?

    Hosted builders such as WordPress.com, Webflow and Framer are quickest to launch; this list is not a ranking.

    Webflow or Framer?

    Webflow lists $15 to $25 a month per site with ecommerce plans; Framer lists $10 to $30 with no ecommerce plan.

    Which headless CMS is free?

    Strapi is free to self-host; Contentful, Sanity and Storyblok have free plans with usage limits.

    What is a headless CMS?

    A CMS that stores content and delivers it by API to any website or app.
