Quick answer

Braze vs Iterable: the short answer

Braze is an enterprise customer engagement platform for cross-channel messaging (push, email, SMS, in-app, WhatsApp), priced by platform edition, monthly active users and flexible message credits. Iterable is a cross-channel customer engagement platform for email, SMS, push and in-app campaigns, sold by custom quote. Both are marketing automation platforms, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Braze is described as: annual contract by edition, active users and credits; Iterable as: annual contract by quote. Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Braze is positioned for consumer brands running real-time cross-channel engagement at scale, and Iterable for consumer brands wanting enterprise cross-channel orchestration.

Considerations

Braze vs Iterable: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Braze: annual contract by edition, active users and credits. Braze publishes no prices; contract figures come from Vendr buyer data. Iterable: annual contract by quote. Averages come from SpendHound, a vendor-management company, based on 160 customers. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Braze profile highlights: edition + active users + credits pricing; no seat fees; pending Salesforce acquisition. The Iterable profile highlights: quote-only pricing; enterprise cross-channel focus; reported contract benchmarks.

Integrations and ecosystem

Braze integrations named in its sources: Braze Currents, Data warehouses and Salesforce (post-acquisition plans). Iterable: Data warehouses and CDPs. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Braze runs on Web and is documented for mid-size teams and large enterprises. Iterable runs on Web and is documented for mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Braze and Iterable.

Moving off Braze or Iterable means exporting contacts, activity history and consent records, rebuilding journeys, scoring models, templates and forms, re-warming sending domains or IPs, and reconnecting your CRM and data sources. Run both platforms in parallel until key journeys are live. Rebuild one live journey (such as onboarding or lead nurture) in each shortlisted platform and compare results.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
