Quick answer

Sage 50 vs QuickBooks Online: the short answer

Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online are two of the best-known accounting products for small businesses. Sage 50 is installed desktop software with cloud connectivity and deeper inventory and job costing; QuickBooks Online is browser-based.

Sage 50 suits businesses that want locally installed software with serialised inventory, job costing and audit trails, and can scale to 40 users on Quantum. QuickBooks Online suits teams that want to work in the browser with a wide app ecosystem and accountant access.

We compare what the dated sources support and mark the rest Unknown. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and how you work.

Considerations

Sage 50 vs QuickBooks Online: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and trials

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Sage 50 free trial: No (30-day refund). QuickBooks Online free trial: Yes (30 days, or 50% off for 3 months). Pilot one real process in each before committing.

Integrations and ecosystem

Sage 50: Microsoft 365 and Excel (reported); Sage add-on marketplace; Payroll and payments add-ons. QuickBooks Online: Payroll add-on (Workforce Payroll); Time tracking add-on; QuickBooks Payments. Check each integration directory for the tools you actually use.

AI capabilities

Sage 50 does not document AI features in the sources reviewed. QuickBooks Online documents AI features on its profile. We did not test output quality, so run both against a real task before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Deployment, security and administration

Sage 50 runs as cloud with self-hosted or on-premise option; QuickBooks Online runs as cloud (SaaS). We did not review certifications or data residency in detail. Request current security documentation from both vendors if you are regulated.

Strengths and limitations

Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online: each option, in balance.

S5

Sage 50

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Inventory, job costing and audit trails go beyond entry-level cloud tools.
  • One subscription scales from a single user to 40 on Quantum.
  • A 30-day refund window reduces purchase risk.
  • Multi-company support from Premium.
Limitations
  • No free trial at present, only a refund window and demos.
  • One-year minimum commitment that auto-renews.
  • Lapsed payments leave data read-only.
  • Prices are high compared with cloud bookkeeping tools for small teams.
QB

QuickBooks Online

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Four plans scale from a single user to 25 users with custom permissions.
  • Plus adds inventory, project profitability, classes and budgets.
  • New customers can choose a 30-day trial or 50% off for three months.
Limitations
  • Prices rose on 1 August 2026, by up to $65 a month on Advanced.
  • Payroll, payments and time tracking cost extra.
  • Inventory and project tracking need Plus at $140 a month.
  • The limited free tier allows one bank account and very few invoices.

Switching

Switching between Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online.

Moving between Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online usually means exporting lists and balances and re-entering opening positions; full transaction history may not transfer cleanly. Ask your accountant about conversion tools and switch at a period end.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Sage 50 vs QuickBooks Online: questions and answers

What is the difference between Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online?

Sage 50 and QuickBooks Online are two of the best-known accounting products for small businesses. Sage 50 is installed desktop software with cloud connectivity and deeper inventory and job costing; QuickBooks Online is browser-based. Sage 50 suits businesses that want locally installed software with serialised inventory, job costing and audit trails, and can scale to 40 users on Quantum.

Is Sage 50 or QuickBooks Online cheaper?

QuickBooks Online is cheaper to start, with plans reported from $38 a month. Sage 50 Pro is $128.67 a month on a one-year commitment, but includes inventory and job management.

Is Sage 50 cloud-based?

Sage 50 is installed locally with cloud-connected options. QuickBooks Online runs fully in the browser.

Do they offer free trials?

Sage 50 currently offers no trial, only a 30-day refund and demos. QuickBooks Online offers a 30-day trial or a discount for three months.

Which is better for small businesses, Sage 50 or QuickBooks Online?

Sage 50 is documented for small teams, mid-size teams; QuickBooks Online for small teams, mid-size teams. Choose Sage 50 if you want installed desktop accounting; choose QuickBooks Online if you want browser-based accounting from anywhere.
