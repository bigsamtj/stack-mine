Quick answer

AWeber vs Mailchimp: the short answer

AWeber and Mailchimp are two of the oldest email marketing platforms for small businesses. Both have free plans and paid tiers priced by list size.

AWeber focuses on newsletters, autoresponders and creator-friendly selling with a free plan for 500 subscribers. Mailchimp offers a broader marketing platform with more templates, segmentation and integrations, and a free plan for 250 contacts.

We compare what the dated sources support and mark the rest Unknown. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and how you work.

Considerations

AWeber vs Mailchimp: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and trials

We did not test setup or migration for either product. AWeber free trial: Yes (14 days, card required). Mailchimp free trial: Yes (14-day trial on paid plans). Pilot one real process in each before committing.

Integrations and ecosystem

AWeber: Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress; PayPal and Stripe; Zapier. Mailchimp: 300+ integrations (reported on all plans); Transactional email add-on; Marketing API. Check each integration directory for the tools you actually use.

AI capabilities

AWeber documents AI features on its profile. Mailchimp does not document AI features in the sources reviewed. We did not test output quality, so run both against a real task before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Deployment, security and administration

AWeber runs as cloud (SaaS); Mailchimp runs as cloud (SaaS). We did not review certifications or data residency in detail. Request current security documentation from both vendors if you are regulated.

Strengths and limitations

AWeber and Mailchimp: each option, in balance.

A

AWeber

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free plan covers up to 500 subscribers.
  • Plus removes limits on automations, pages and users.
  • Built-in commerce lets creators sell without another tool.
  • Annual billing cuts the monthly price noticeably.
Limitations
  • Lite caps automations and landing pages at three each.
  • Automation depth trails newer tools, per reviewers.
  • Upgrades are automatic as lists grow; downgrades are manual.
  • The 14-day trial needs a credit card.
M

Mailchimp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free plan lets very small lists start at no cost.
  • Standard adds full journeys, multivariate testing, dynamic content and send-time optimisation at a low entry price.
  • Premium includes unlimited seats and audiences with phone support.
Limitations
  • The free plan was cut to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, which limits real use.
  • Pricing scales with stored contacts, and billing uses the peak contact total in the cycle.
  • Essentials caps journeys at 4 steps and leaves out advanced segmentation.
  • Premium jumps to about $350 a month, a large step up from Standard.

Switching

Switching between AWeber and Mailchimp.

Moving between AWeber and Mailchimp means exporting subscribers with consent data and tags, rebuilding sign-up forms and automations and warming up sending. Move one list first.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

AWeber vs Mailchimp: questions and answers

What is the difference between AWeber and Mailchimp?

AWeber and Mailchimp are two of the oldest email marketing platforms for small businesses. Both have free plans and paid tiers priced by list size. AWeber focuses on newsletters, autoresponders and creator-friendly selling with a free plan for 500 subscribers.

Is AWeber cheaper than Mailchimp?

At small list sizes, prices are close: AWeber Lite is $12.50 a month annual and Mailchimp Essentials is reported from $13 a month. AWeber’s free plan covers 500 subscribers versus 250 for Mailchimp.

Which has the better free plan?

AWeber’s free plan covers 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month. Mailchimp’s covers 250 contacts.

Do both have trials on paid plans?

AWeber offers a 14-day trial with a card. Mailchimp offers a 14-day trial on paid plans, per its profile.

Which is better for small businesses, AWeber or Mailchimp?

AWeber is documented for solo and freelancers, small teams; Mailchimp for small teams, mid-size teams. Choose AWeber if you want a free plan for up to 500 subscribers; choose Mailchimp if you want a broader marketing platform.

What are the alternatives to AWeber and Mailchimp?

Both are listed in Stack18’s Email Marketing Software directory. See the AWeber alternatives guide and the Mailchimp alternatives guide for sourced shortlists of competitors.
