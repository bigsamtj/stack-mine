Quick answer

Leadpages vs Unbounce: the short answer

Leadpages and Unbounce are landing page builders with different audiences.

Leadpages targets small businesses with simple lead capture and Leadmeter guidance. Unbounce targets campaign marketers with A/B testing and Smart Traffic.

We compare what the dated sources support and mark the rest Unknown. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and how you work.

Considerations

Leadpages vs Unbounce: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and trials

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Leadpages free trial: Yes (14 days). Unbounce free trial: Yes (14 days, no card). Pilot one real process in each before committing.

Integrations and ecosystem

Leadpages: Mailchimp and ActiveCampaign; Stripe; Zapier. Unbounce: HubSpot and Salesforce; Mailchimp; Zapier. Check each integration directory for the tools you actually use.

AI capabilities

Leadpages documents AI features on its profile. Unbounce documents AI features on its profile. We did not test output quality, so run both against a real task before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Deployment, security and administration

Leadpages runs as cloud (SaaS); Unbounce runs as cloud (SaaS). We did not review certifications or data residency in detail. Request current security documentation from both vendors if you are regulated.

Switching

Switching between Leadpages and Unbounce.

Rebuild or import pages, reconnect forms and integrations, point domains or DNS to the new tool, and run a short A/B check on traffic before switching off the old pages.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Leadpages vs Unbounce: questions and answers

What is the difference between Leadpages and Unbounce?

Leadpages and Unbounce are landing page builders with different audiences. Leadpages targets small businesses with simple lead capture and Leadmeter guidance.

Which has A/B testing?

Leadpages on Pro; Unbounce from Experiment.

Which is cheaper to start?

Unbounce Starter at $22 annually, but with 500 visitors.

Do both have trials?

Yes, 14 days.

Which is better for small businesses, Leadpages or Unbounce?

Leadpages is documented for solo and freelancers, small teams; Unbounce for small teams, mid-size teams. Choose Leadpages if you are a small business or coach; choose Unbounce if you run paid campaigns.
