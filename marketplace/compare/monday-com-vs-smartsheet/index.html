Quick answer

monday.com vs Smartsheet: the short answer

monday.com and Smartsheet are both mature work management platforms for operations and project teams. monday.com is a board-based platform sold per seat in a free plan and four paid plans, with a three-seat minimum on paid plans. Smartsheet is built around a spreadsheet-style grid with Gantt, card and calendar views, sold per member in Pro, Business, Enterprise and Advanced Work Management plans, with free viewers.

The sources reviewed describe the trade-off as visual flexibility against structured grid control. monday.com is described as easier to learn and more flexible, with a free plan and built-in time tracking. Smartsheet is described as stronger for formula-driven planning, approvals and governance, and is reported to carry a government compliance edge, but has no free plan for new sign-ups and sells resource management separately.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. One source is written by a monday.com implementation partner, so we treat its verdict as partisan and use it mainly for plan details. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on how your team works with data.

For small teams Smartsheet Pro is the cheapest paid entry, and at the mid tier the two cost the same. Our arithmetic on reported annual prices for 10 people gives about $1,080 a year on Smartsheet Pro ($9 x 10 x 12) against $1,440 on monday.com Standard ($12 x 10 x 12) and $2,280 on monday.com Pro ($19 x 10 x 12) or Smartsheet Business ($19 x 10 x 12). For 25 people, monday.com Standard is about $3,600 a year ($12 x 25 x 12), while monday.com Pro and Smartsheet Business are both about $5,700 ($19 x 25 x 12).

Plan limits matter more than headline prices. Smartsheet Pro is limited to one to ten members, so a larger team moves to Business, and its viewers are free, so many readers cost nothing. monday.com has a three-seat minimum and a free plan for two users, so it suits very small teams that want to start free.

Check what each plan unlocks. Time tracking and resource management are reported inside monday.com plans but as an add-on on Smartsheet, though sources disagree on plan placement. Prices changed on both sides in 2026, so confirm current terms with each vendor.

Considerations

monday.com vs Smartsheet: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Guides describe monday.com as quick to adopt for non-technical teams and Smartsheet as natural for spreadsheet users but sometimes needing administrative support for larger setups. Pilot one real plan in each.

Integrations and ecosystem

Smartsheet is described as strong on Jira, Salesforce and Microsoft Teams connectors and Microsoft Office and Azure integration. monday.com offers native integrations that vary by plan. Integration counts were not independently verified, so check the specific tools you use.

AI capabilities

Both vendors list AI on paid plans. Smartsheet lists AI credits, a Sidekick assistant, formula generation and content summarisation, while monday.com lists AI assistants whose plan placement varies by source. We did not test output quality, so run both on a real project before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Security, deployment and administration

Smartsheet lists enterprise plan management and access controls on Enterprise, and one partner source credits it with a government compliance advantage. monday.com positions Enterprise for governance and security. We did not review certifications or data residency for either product, so request current security documentation.

Strengths and limitations

monday.com and Smartsheet: each option, in balance.

MC

monday.com

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free plan and clear Basic, Standard and Pro steps make it easy to start small.
  • Timeline, calendar and guest access arrive at the mid-priced Standard plan.
  • Automation allowances scale from 250 to 25,000 actions a month by plan.
Limitations
  • Paid plans require a minimum of three seats, which penalises very small teams.
  • Enterprise pricing is quote-based and reported to carry a minimum 12-month term.
  • monday CRM, dev and service are priced separately, so multi-product use costs more.
  • The free plan is limited to two seats and three boards in at least one source.
S

Smartsheet

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A spreadsheet-style grid with Gantt, board, calendar and timeline views lowers the learning curve for teams coming from Excel.
  • Documented AI features cover plain-language formulas, charts, summaries and intelligent form fill.
  • Free viewers, and free guests on Business and Enterprise, let stakeholders follow progress without a paid seat.
  • Integrations with Jira, Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, Slack and Tableau suit enterprise tool stacks.
  • Pro and Business have published per-member prices of $9 and $19 billed annually.
Limitations
  • The free plan is legacy and closed to new sign-ups, so new teams start with a trial rather than a free tier.
  • Pro is limited to one to ten members and Business requires at least three, which constrains plan choice for very small or growing teams.
  • Enterprise and Advanced Work Management are custom-quoted, so enterprise costs are not published.
  • Reviewers note a steeper learning curve for enterprise features, and advanced automation such as Bridge is a separate module.
  • Smartsheet is privately owned by Blackstone and Vista funds since January 2025, so future pricing and packaging may change.

Switching

Switching between monday.com and Smartsheet.

Moving between monday.com and Smartsheet means converting boards and items to sheets and rows (or the reverse), rebuilding formulas, automations and dashboards, and recreating forms. Export a sample project, rebuild it in the other tool, and run one live project in parallel before cutting over.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

monday.com vs Smartsheet: questions and answers

Is monday.com or Smartsheet cheaper?

Smartsheet Pro is the cheaper entry at $9 per member for one to ten members. At the mid tier both are about $19 per seat per month billed annually, and monday.com Standard at $12 is cheaper still.

Does monday.com or Smartsheet have a free plan?

monday.com has a free plan for two users and three boards. Smartsheet has no free plan for new sign-ups; a 30-day trial is reported.

Which is better for Gantt charts?

Both offer Gantt with dependencies. Guides describe Smartsheet as stronger for detailed project tracking and grid-based planning.

Does Smartsheet have time tracking?

Guides report it is not native and sits with the separate Resource Management product, while monday.com includes time tracking. Plan placement differs by source.

Are Smartsheet viewers free?

Yes. Viewers are free, and Business and Enterprise add unlimited free guests.

Which is easier to learn?

Guides describe monday.com as easier and Smartsheet as familiar to spreadsheet users but needing more setup.

Which is better for governance and compliance?

One partner source credits Smartsheet with a government compliance advantage, which we could not verify. Request current security documentation from both.
