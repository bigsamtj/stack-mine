PhonePe lists a Standard plan at 1.99 percent (shown free during an offer) with no setup or AMC. Razorpay charges 2 percent plus GST on domestic cards, net banking and wallets, with 0 percent MDR on UPI plus a platform fee. Rates are close; compare your payment mix.

List prices rarely tell the whole story. Check minimum terms, user or volume minimums, add-ons and taxes such as GST before comparing totals.

Switching

Switching between PhonePe Payment Gateway and Razorpay.

Run both gateways in parallel on part of your traffic, update checkout integrations and webhooks, and switch fully once settlements reconcile.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

PhonePe Payment Gateway vs Razorpay: questions and answers

What is the difference between PhonePe Payment Gateway and Razorpay?

PhonePe Payment Gateway and Razorpay are Indian online payment gateways. PhonePe’s gateway leans on UPI traffic with zero setup and annual fees.

Which is cheaper?

Standard rates are similar: PhonePe 1.99 percent versus Razorpay 2 percent plus GST; check current offers.

Do both support UPI?

Yes.

Are there setup fees?

PhonePe lists zero setup and annual fees; Razorpay has no monthly fee.

Which is better for small businesses, PhonePe Payment Gateway or Razorpay?

PhonePe Payment Gateway is documented for small teams, mid-size teams, large enterprises; Razorpay for small teams, mid-size teams, large enterprises. Choose PhonePe Payment Gateway if you have heavy UPI traffic; choose Razorpay if you want the broadest product suite.

What are the alternatives to PhonePe Payment Gateway and Razorpay?
