Tableau vs Qlik Cloud Analytics: the short answer

Tableau is a visual analytics and BI platform owned by Salesforce, licensed per user by role (Creator, Explorer, Viewer) on Tableau Cloud or Tableau Server, with Standard and Enterprise editions. Qlik Cloud Analytics, built on Qlik Sense, is an analytics platform with associative exploration, AI and automation, priced by plan and data capacity rather than mainly per user. Both are BI tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Tableau is described as: per user per month by role, billed annually; edition affects price; Qlik Cloud Analytics as: annual subscription by plan and data capacity. Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Tableau is positioned for analytics teams that want best-in-class visual exploration, and Qlik Cloud Analytics for teams that want associative analytics priced by data volume.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

Tableau vs Qlik Cloud Analytics: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Tableau: per user per month by role, billed annually; edition affects price. Tableau+ and Cloud+ are quoted. Tableau's own page was not reviewed directly. Qlik Cloud Analytics: annual subscription by plan and data capacity. Prices are "starting at" figures from Qlik's US pricing page. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Tableau profile highlights: role-based per-user licensing; cloud or self-managed server; agentic Tableau Next sold separately. The Qlik Cloud Analytics profile highlights: priced mainly by data capacity; no-cost additional users from Standard; answers Agents and MCP Server.

Integrations and ecosystem

Tableau integrations named in its sources: Salesforce, Tableau Server and Tableau Next. Qlik Cloud Analytics: Qlik Talend Cloud, SAP data extraction (Premium) and MCP Server. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Tableau runs on Web and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. Qlik Cloud Analytics runs on Web and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Tableau and Qlik Cloud Analytics.

Moving off Tableau or Qlik Cloud Analytics usually means rebuilding dashboards and data models (they rarely export cleanly), re-creating permissions and row-level security, reconnecting data sources and retraining users. Run both tools side by side until key dashboards are rebuilt and validated. Rebuild two or three of your most-used dashboards in each shortlisted tool and have real users test them.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Tableau vs Qlik Cloud Analytics: questions and answers

What is the difference between Tableau and Qlik Cloud Analytics?

Tableau is a visual analytics and BI platform owned by Salesforce, licensed per user by role (Creator, Explorer, Viewer) on Tableau Cloud or Tableau Server, with Standard and Enterprise editions. Qlik Cloud Analytics, built on Qlik Sense, is an analytics platform with associative exploration, AI and automation, priced by plan and data capacity rather than mainly per user. Both are BI tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. Tableau is positioned for analytics teams that want best-in-class visual exploration; Qlik Cloud Analytics for teams that want associative analytics priced by data volume.

Is Tableau cheaper than Qlik Cloud Analytics?

It depends on your volume and plan. Tableau: Creator $75, Explorer $42, Viewer $15 per user per month billed annually (Standard). Qlik Cloud Analytics: Starter from $300 a month (10 users, 10 GB), billed annually. Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do Tableau or Qlik Cloud Analytics have a free plan?

Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Tableau free trial: Not documented. Qlik Cloud Analytics free trial: Yes.

Tableau or Qlik Cloud Analytics: which is better for small teams?

Tableau and Qlik Cloud Analytics are both documented for small teams. Tableau suits analytics teams that want best-in-class visual exploration; Qlik Cloud Analytics suits teams that want associative analytics priced by data volume. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of Tableau and Qlik Cloud Analytics?

Tableau: Annual billing only; no monthly option. Qlik Cloud Analytics: Starter data is fixed at 10 GB.

How much does Tableau cost?

On Standard: Creator $75, Explorer $42 and Viewer $15 per user a month billed annually; Enterprise is $115, $70 and $35.

How much does Qlik Cloud Analytics cost?

Qlik lists Starter from $300, Standard from $825 and Premium from $2,750 a month billed annually; Enterprise is quoted.
