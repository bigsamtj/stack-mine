Quick answer

Zoho Projects vs Wrike: the short answer

Zoho Projects and Wrike are both established project management platforms, built for different budgets. Zoho Projects is a low-cost tool in the Zoho suite, sold per user in Free, Premium, Enterprise and Ultimate plans with Gantt charts, time tracking and a messaging app. Wrike is a work management platform for marketing, creative and PMO teams, sold per user in Free, Team, Business, Pinnacle and Apex plans.

The sources reviewed describe the choice as value against depth. Zoho Projects is described as far cheaper, with an Enterprise plan reported at less than half the cost of Wrike's Team plan. Wrike is described as stronger on automation, request forms, time-tracking flexibility and enterprise analytics, with a more generous free plan, but its paid plans are pricier and carry user bands and add-ons.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. Wrike's free-plan limits and Zoho's free-plan caps differ between sources, so we show the range. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on budget and how much workflow control you need.

Zoho Projects is far cheaper at every comparable tier. Our arithmetic on reported annual prices for 10 users gives about $480 a year on Zoho Projects Premium ($4 x 10 x 12) and $1,080 on Enterprise ($9 x 10 x 12), against $1,200 on Wrike Team ($10 x 10 x 12). For 25 users, Wrike Team no longer applies because it is reported for 2 to 15 users, so the comparable Wrike plan is Business at about $7,500 a year ($25 x 25 x 12), against $1,200 on Zoho Premium ($4 x 25 x 12) and $2,700 on Zoho Enterprise ($9 x 25 x 12).

Wrike's free plan is the more generous starting point if its reported unlimited users hold: a team larger than Zoho's three-user free cap can stay free on Wrike, though without automation. Wrike's paid plans then step up quickly and add user bands, while Zoho's price rises gently.

Compare features per dollar rather than plan names. Wrike's advanced automation allowances, request forms and resource management sit on higher tiers, and Zoho's Enterprise adds custom roles and SSO at $9. Prices changed on both sides in 2026, so confirm current terms.

Considerations

Zoho Projects vs Wrike: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Guides describe Zoho Projects as quick to adopt for small teams and especially easy for existing Zoho users, and Wrike as more configurable but heavier for small groups. Both offer free plans or trials, so pilot one real project in each.

Integrations and ecosystem

One guide reports Zoho Projects connects with 800+ apps and Wrike with 400+, and notes Wrike integrates with Slack where Zoho Projects does not. Both integrate with Microsoft Teams. Counts differ between sources and were not verified, so check the specific tools you use.

AI capabilities

Zoho Projects lists Zia Search on Enterprise. Wrike offers AI features with quotas that rise through its higher tiers, with its top AI quotas bundled into custom-quoted Apex contracts. We did not test output quality for either product.

Security, deployment and administration

Zoho Projects lists custom roles, SSO and two-factor authentication on Enterprise at $9. Wrike places advanced analytics, locked spaces and resource management on Pinnacle. We did not review certifications or data residency for either product, so request current security documentation.

Strengths and limitations

Zoho Projects and Wrike: each option, in balance.

ZP

Zoho Projects

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Reviewers report a very low entry price of about $4 per user per month on annual billing.
  • A free plan for a few users lets small teams try Gantt charts and basic project management before paying.
  • Premium is reported to include unlimited projects, project templates, time tracking and blueprints.
  • Support is offered through email, live chat and toll-free phone, according to TechRepublic.
Limitations
  • The free plan is small, and sources disagree on its limits (3 or 5 users, 2 or 3 projects).
  • Premium is reported to cap at 50 users, so larger teams need Enterprise.
  • Custom roles, single sign-on, custom fields and user hierarchy are reported to need Enterprise or above.
  • This profile relies on independent sources, so plan details should be confirmed with Zoho.
W

Wrike

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free plan with unlimited users lets teams evaluate Wrike before paying.
  • Business includes custom workflows, request forms and time tracking at a published $25 per user.
  • Per-user automation allowances are stated for every tier, from 50 to 3,000 actions a month.
  • Pinnacle and Apex provide analytics, resource management and governance for large organisations.
Limitations
  • Business requires at least five licences, so small teams can pay for unused seats.
  • Enterprise was retired for new purchases in January 2026, and above Business pricing is now a sales conversation.
  • Metered AI actions from April 2026, paid action packs and add-ons such as Whiteboard at $15 per user raise real cost.
  • Team is capped at 15 users and Business at 200 for self-serve purchase.
  • Cost reviewers report that integration and automation features common elsewhere need higher tiers or add-ons.

Switching

Switching between Zoho Projects and Wrike.

Moving between Zoho Projects and Wrike means mapping projects, task lists and custom fields, recreating automations and request forms, and reconnecting integrations. Export a sample project, rebuild it in the other tool, and run one live project in parallel before cutting over.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Zoho Projects vs Wrike: questions and answers

Is Zoho Projects cheaper than Wrike?

Yes at every comparable tier: Zoho Projects Premium is $4 per user per month and Enterprise is $9, against $10 for Wrike Team and $25 for Business, billed annually.

Which has the better free plan?

Wrike's free plan is reported for unlimited users, though one guide says it is capped. Zoho Projects' free plan covers up to 3 users with limits on projects and storage.

Does Wrike have a user minimum?

Team is for 2 to 15 users and Business for 5 to 200, and one guide reports a five-seat minimum on the cheapest paid plan.

Which has better automation?

Guides describe Wrike as having advanced automation, with allowances that rise by tier, and Zoho Projects as having basic automation.

Which has more integrations?

One guide reports 800+ for Zoho Projects and 400+ for Wrike, but notes Wrike integrates with Slack and Zoho Projects does not.

Which is better for time tracking?

One guide favours Wrike for flexibility, with manual entries and a background timer. Zoho Projects includes time tracking from Premium.

What changed on Wrike in 2026?

One guide reports that in January 2026 Wrike retired its Enterprise plan for new customers and added Apex as a custom-quoted top tier above Pinnacle.
