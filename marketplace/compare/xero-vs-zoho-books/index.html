Quick answer

Xero vs Zoho Books: the short answer

Xero is cloud accounting software for small businesses and their advisers, sold in the US in Early, Growing and Established plans, with payroll through Gusto as an add-on. Zoho Books is cloud accounting software from Zoho, with a free plan and Standard, Professional, Premium, Elite and Ultimate paid plans that add users, inventory and analytics. Both are accounting platforms, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Xero is described as: monthly subscription by plan, not by user; payroll and Inventory Plus are optional add-ons; Zoho Books as: monthly or annual subscription by plan, with extra users at an added per-user charge. Zoho Books lists a free plan. Xero does not document a permanent free plan. Xero is positioned for small businesses and accountants that want clean, collaborative cloud books, and Zoho Books for small businesses that want low-cost accounting inside the Zoho ecosystem.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

Xero vs Zoho Books: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Xero: monthly subscription by plan, not by user; payroll and Inventory Plus are optional add-ons. The US pricing page advertised $5.40, $11.80 and $19.40 for the first three months under an 80% promotion for new customers. Payroll powered by Gusto costs $36 a month plus $6 per employee or contractor. Zoho Books: monthly or annual subscription by plan, with extra users at an added per-user charge. Prices are US list prices; Zoho prices differ by country. Extra users cost an added per-user fee that was not confirmed. Zoho's own pricing page was not reviewed directly. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Xero profile highlights: plans are not priced per user; cash flow forecasts of 30, 60 or 180 days by plan; free domestic ACH bill payments on every plan. The Zoho Books profile highlights: a free plan with 1,000 invoices a year; six plans scaling to warehouse management and analytics; native connections to the wider Zoho suite.

Integrations and ecosystem

Xero integrations named in its sources: Xero Payroll powered by Gusto ($36 a month + $6 per person), Inventory Plus (optional), Stripe online invoice payments and Domestic ACH bill payments (free). Zoho Books: Zoho CRM and other Zoho apps, Shopify (Elite and above) and API access (from Standard). Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Xero runs on Web, iOS and Android and is documented for small teams and mid-size teams. Zoho Books runs on Web, iOS and Android and is documented for small teams and mid-size teams. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Xero and Zoho Books.

Moving off Xero or Zoho Books usually means choosing a cut-over date, ideally a month, quarter or year end, exporting the chart of accounts, opening balances, customers, suppliers and open invoices, and reconnecting bank feeds, payroll and payment tools. Keep read-only access to Xero or Zoho Books for audit and tax records. Ask your accountant to review a trial file with one month of real transactions before committing.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

Is Xero cheaper than Zoho Books?

It depends on your volume and plan. Xero: US plans from $27 per month (Early); promotional discounts for the first months. Zoho Books: Free plan (1 user); paid plans reported from $15 per month billed annually (Standard). Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.
