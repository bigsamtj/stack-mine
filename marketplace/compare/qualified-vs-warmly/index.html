Quick answer

Qualified (Piper) vs Warmly: the short answer

Qualified (Piper) and Warmly identify website visitors and engage them with AI agents. Qualified sells quoted annual packages reported from about $40,000 a year and is now owned by Salesforce, while Warmly has a free tier and annual products from about $10,000.

Qualified is Salesforce-centric with its Piper AI SDR on your website; Warmly combines de-anonymisation, signals and outbound. We compare what documentation supports and mark everything else Unknown. We declare no overall winner, because the better choice depends on channels, integrations, oversight needs, pricing model and budget.

Switching

Switching between Qualified (Piper) and Warmly.

Moving between Qualified (Piper) and Warmly means re-importing lead lists and suppression lists, reconnecting the CRM and mailboxes, rebuilding messaging and sequences, and re-warming sending domains. Run both on separate segments for a month before committing.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Qualified (Piper) vs Warmly: questions and answers

Is Qualified (Piper) or Warmly better?

The sources reviewed do not support a single ranking. Qualified sells quoted annual packages reported from about $40,000 a year and is now owned by Salesforce, while Warmly has a free tier and annual products from about $10,000. Pilot both on a sample of your own conversations, leads or workflows before choosing.

Does Qualified (Piper) have a free plan?

No free plan.

Does Warmly have a free plan?

Yes. The free plan identifies up to 500 visitors a month.

Which is cheaper, Qualified (Piper) or Warmly?

Qualified (Piper): Quoted annual packages; reported from about $40,000 a year. Warmly: Free tier; annual products from about $10,000 to $30,000 a year; Full GTM custom. The cheaper option depends on seats, usage and which plan features you need.

How do Qualified (Piper) and Warmly differ on integrations?

Qualified (Piper): Salesforce and 20+ GTM integrations. Warmly: MCP on Full GTM, per an independent index.
