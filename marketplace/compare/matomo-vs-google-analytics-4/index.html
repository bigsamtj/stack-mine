Quick answer

Matomo vs Google Analytics 4: the short answer

Matomo is an open-source web analytics platform (GPL) that can be self-hosted for free or used as a hosted cloud service, focused on data ownership and privacy. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's event-based analytics platform for websites and apps. Both are analytics tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Matomo is described as: free self-hosted; cloud subscription by hits; Google Analytics 4 as: free; 360 is a paid monthly subscription by hit volume. Both have a free option. Matomo lists a free option: self-hosted. Google Analytics 4 lists a free plan. Matomo is positioned for organisations that want full data ownership and GA-style reports, and Google Analytics 4 for most websites wanting free traffic and conversion analytics.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

Matomo vs Google Analytics 4: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Matomo: free self-hosted; cloud subscription by hits. Prices checked by the source in July 2026; confirm on matomo.org. Google Analytics 4: free; 360 is a paid monthly subscription by hit volume. Google does not publish 360 prices; the estimate comes from a third-party guide. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Matomo profile highlights: open source (GPL); free self-hosting; GA-style reporting. The Google Analytics 4 profile highlights: free standard version; deep Google Ads and BigQuery integration; enterprise 360 tier with SLAs.

Integrations and ecosystem

Matomo integrations named in its sources: WordPress, Tag managers and Ecommerce platforms. Google Analytics 4: Google Ads, Search Console, BigQuery and Looker Studio. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Matomo runs on Web and Self-hosted and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. Google Analytics 4 runs on Web, iOS and Android and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Matomo and Google Analytics 4.

Moving off Matomo or Google Analytics 4 means re-implementing tracking code or SDKs, mapping event names and properties, rebuilding dashboards, funnels and audiences, and exporting historical data you need (it rarely imports cleanly). Run both tools in parallel and reconcile numbers before switching. Run each shortlisted tool alongside your current one for two to four weeks and compare key numbers.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Matomo vs Google Analytics 4: questions and answers

What is the difference between Matomo and Google Analytics 4?

Matomo is an open-source web analytics platform (GPL) that can be self-hosted for free or used as a hosted cloud service, focused on data ownership and privacy. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's event-based analytics platform for websites and apps. Both are analytics tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. Matomo is positioned for organisations that want full data ownership and GA-style reports; Google Analytics 4 for most websites wanting free traffic and conversion analytics.

Is Matomo cheaper than Google Analytics 4?

It depends on your volume and plan. Matomo: Free self-hosted; Cloud from EUR 29 a month (50K hits). Google Analytics 4: Free; GA4 360 billed monthly by hit volume, estimated at $150,000+ a year. Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do Matomo or Google Analytics 4 have a free plan?

Both have a free option. Matomo lists a free option: self-hosted. Google Analytics 4 lists a free plan. Matomo free trial: Not documented. Google Analytics 4 free trial: Not applicable.

Matomo or Google Analytics 4: which is better for small teams?

Matomo and Google Analytics 4 are both documented for small teams. Matomo suits organisations that want full data ownership and GA-style reports; Google Analytics 4 suits most websites wanting free traffic and conversion analytics. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of Matomo and Google Analytics 4?

Matomo: Self-hosting needs server maintenance. Google Analytics 4: Standard retention is at most 14 months.

Is Matomo free?

Yes, Matomo On-Premise is free to self-host; Matomo Cloud starts at EUR 29 a month.

Is Google Analytics 4 free?

Yes, GA4 Standard is free. Google Analytics 360 is a paid enterprise version.
