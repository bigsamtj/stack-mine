Quick answer

Act-On vs Adobe Marketo Engage: the short answer

Act-On is a B2B marketing automation platform for email, lead scoring, landing pages and account-based marketing, priced by active contacts rather than total database size. Adobe Marketo Engage is a B2B marketing automation platform for lead management, email nurturing, scoring and account-based marketing, sold in four packages priced by contact volume. Both are marketing automation platforms, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Act-On is described as: annual contract priced by active contacts; Adobe Marketo Engage as: annual subscription by contact volume and package, by quote. Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Act-On is positioned for mid-size B2B teams wanting simpler automation than Marketo, and Adobe Marketo Engage for B2B marketing teams running complex lead management at scale.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

Act-On vs Adobe Marketo Engage: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Act-On: annual contract priced by active contacts. Starting price may change with add-ons, configuration and implementation. Adobe Marketo Engage: annual subscription by contact volume and package, by quote. Adobe publishes no prices. Estimates come from a ZoomInfo article. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Act-On profile highlights: active-contact pricing; published entry price; AI predictive lead score included. The Adobe Marketo Engage profile highlights: four packages from Growth to Ultimate; contact-volume pricing; marketo Measure attribution in Ultimate.

Integrations and ecosystem

Act-On integrations named in its sources: Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, SugarCRM, NetSuite and Zapier. Adobe Marketo Engage: Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Adobe Experience Cloud. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Act-On runs on Web and is documented for small teams and mid-size teams. Adobe Marketo Engage runs on Web and is documented for mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Act-On and Adobe Marketo Engage.

Moving off Act-On or Adobe Marketo Engage means exporting contacts, activity history and consent records, rebuilding journeys, scoring models, templates and forms, re-warming sending domains or IPs, and reconnecting your CRM and data sources. Run both platforms in parallel until key journeys are live. Rebuild one live journey (such as onboarding or lead nurture) in each shortlisted platform and compare results.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
