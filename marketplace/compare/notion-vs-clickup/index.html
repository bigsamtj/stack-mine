Quick answer

Notion vs ClickUp: the short answer

Notion and ClickUp both call themselves all-in-one workspaces, but their centres of gravity differ. Notion is docs-first: wikis, documents and flexible databases with a project layer, sold per member from a free plan. ClickUp is task-first: structured project management with docs and whiteboards alongside, sold per member from a free plan with unlimited members.

The sources reviewed agree on the split. Notion is favoured for knowledge management and writing, and ClickUp for delivery work with time tracking, goals and Gantt charts. On AI, Notion bundles full AI into Business, while ClickUp sells Brain as a per-member add-on, though heavier AI usage costs extra on both sides in 2026.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. One source is ClickUp's own help centre, so we treat its claims as vendor-authored. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on whether you think in documents or in tasks.

All ClickUp plans and sources

ClickUp is cheaper at the entry tier. Our arithmetic on reported annual prices for 25 members gives about $2,100 a year on ClickUp Unlimited ($7 x 25 x 12) against $3,000 on Notion Plus at $10 ($10 x 25 x 12). At the upper tier, Notion Business at $18 with AI included is about $5,400 a year ($18 x 25 x 12), while ClickUp Business at $12 plus Brain at $9 is about $6,300 ($21 x 25 x 12). One guide describes Notion as the cheaper option once AI is included.

The comparison therefore flips depending on whether you need AI. Without AI, ClickUp is cheaper at both comparable tiers. With AI, Notion's bundled model can be cheaper, though sources disagree on Notion's exact Plus and Business prices ($10 to $12 and $18 to $24) and note extra credits for heavier AI use on both products.

Seat counting also differs. In Notion every editor is a billed member, so seat counts can grow quickly, while guests are free. Prices changed on both sides in 2026, so confirm current terms with each vendor.

Considerations

Notion vs ClickUp: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Guides describe Notion as quick to start for documentation and wikis but needing design effort for databases, and ClickUp as needing more initial configuration for its many features. Pilot one real project and one wiki in each.

Integrations and ecosystem

Both products connect to common business tools and to Zapier or Make. One guide's integration table shows Figma supported on ClickUp but not on Notion. Integration depth was not otherwise assessed, so check the specific apps you use.

AI capabilities

Notion Business is reported to include a chat agent and AI Meeting Notes, while ClickUp Brain is a paid add-on. Heavier usage, including Notion Custom Agent credits and ClickUp AI credits, costs extra on both sides in 2026. One guide credits Notion with AI search across workspace knowledge. Output quality was not tested here.

Security, deployment and administration

Notion Enterprise adds audit logs and stronger data controls. ClickUp lists Google SSO on Business and advanced permissions and governance on Enterprise. We did not review certifications or data residency for either product, so request current security documentation.

Strengths and limitations

Notion and ClickUp: each option, in balance.

N

Notion

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free plan and free guests make it easy for individuals and small teams to start.
  • Full Notion AI is reported as bundled into Business, rather than a separate add-on.
  • Flexible docs, wikis and databases suit teams that want a wiki with a light project layer.
Limitations
  • Every editor is a billed member, so seat counts can grow quickly.
  • Free and Plus include only a limited AI trial, so meaningful AI use needs Business or above.
  • Guides disagree on prices and on how AI is bundled, so budgeting needs a check of the current page.
  • Some guides note that flexible databases are lighter than purpose-built project tools for heavy operations.
C

ClickUp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • The free plan includes unlimited tasks and members, which is unusually generous.
  • Entry paid pricing of $7 per member is reported among the lowest for a full work management suite.
  • Many functions such as docs, goals and dashboards sit in one workspace.
Limitations
  • Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited.
  • AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost.
  • Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited.
  • API limits of 100 calls a minute on most plans can bottleneck automation-heavy workflows.

Switching

Switching between Notion and ClickUp.

Moving between Notion and ClickUp means converting database pages to tasks (or the reverse), rebuilding properties and views, and recreating automations. Documents move more easily than structured data. Export a sample workspace, rebuild it in the other tool, and run one team in parallel.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Notion vs ClickUp: questions and answers

Is Notion or ClickUp cheaper?

ClickUp is cheaper at the entry tier ($7 against $10 to $12 per member). With AI included, Notion Business (about $18) can undercut ClickUp Business plus Brain (about $21), per one guide.

Is Notion good for project management compared with ClickUp?

Guides describe ClickUp as stronger for structured project management, with time tracking, goals and Gantt charts, and Notion as stronger for docs and knowledge, with a lighter project layer.

Does Notion or ClickUp include AI?

Notion bundles full AI from the Business plan. ClickUp Brain is a paid per-member add-on. Heavier AI usage costs extra on both.

Which has the better free plan?

Both have free plans. ClickUp allows unlimited members with feature limits, while Notion's free plan includes free guests and a limited AI trial.

Which is better for a wiki?

Guides describe Notion as the better wiki and writing tool. ClickUp has native docs but they are described as less refined.

Which has better automation?

ClickUp offers 100+ automation recipes with plan caps. Notion's native automation is described as more limited by ClickUp's own help centre, which is a vendor-authored claim.

Can I self-host Notion or ClickUp?

No. Neither offers self-hosting per the sources reviewed.
