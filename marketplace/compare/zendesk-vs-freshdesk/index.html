Quick answer

Zendesk vs Freshdesk: the short answer

Zendesk and Freshdesk are two of the most widely evaluated help desk platforms. Zendesk sells ticketing and an omnichannel Suite with AI agents billed per automated resolution; Freshworks sells the email-first Freshdesk, a separate multichannel Omni product and a Freddy AI layer priced per agent and per session.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. Both vendors changed pricing in 2026, so verify current figures. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on channels, expected AI volume, team size and governance needs.

All Freshdesk plans and sources

At the entry level the two look similar at about $19 per agent per month, but they buy different things: Zendesk's $19 plan is ticketing only, with omnichannel and AI agents starting at $55, while Freshdesk's Growth adds automation and SLAs with AI on Pro.

At scale, AI billing dominates. Zendesk charges per automated resolution beyond an allowance, and Freshdesk charges per Freddy AI Agent session plus a per-agent Copilot fee. Model your expected AI volume before comparing totals.

Prices and plans change. Confirm current terms with each vendor before you decide.

Considerations

Zendesk vs Freshdesk: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Both offer per-agent plans with annual billing by default, and teams should pilot one queue of real tickets in each.

Integrations and ecosystem

Both belong to wider product families: Zendesk with telephony and Amazon Connect-based add-ons, Freshdesk with Freshworks products and Freddy AI. Third-party integration coverage was not assessed, so check each marketplace for your CRM and order systems.

AI capabilities

Both offer an AI agent that resolves requests and an assistant for human agents. The pricing models differ: per resolution on Zendesk and per session plus per-agent Copilot on Freshdesk. We did not test resolution quality.

Security, deployment and administration

Security documentation was not reviewed for either product. Zendesk lists sandboxes, audit logs and custom roles on Enterprise; Freshdesk reports an audit log on Enterprise. Request current security documentation from each vendor.

Strengths and limitations

Zendesk and Freshdesk: each option, in balance.

Z

Zendesk

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A clear plan ladder from ticketing-only to a full omnichannel Suite.
  • AI agents are included in plans, with an allowance of automated resolutions per agent.
  • Higher tiers add governance features such as sandboxes, audit logs and custom roles.
Limitations
  • Per-agent pricing means costs rise with headcount, and monthly billing is reported about 30 percent higher.
  • AI resolutions beyond the allowance are billed per resolution, so costs vary with volume.
  • Copilot, voice, quality assurance and workforce management are priced as add-ons.
  • Enterprise pricing is quote-only, and older guides mention a Suite Growth plan that no longer appears on the main page.
F

Freshdesk

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Entry paid pricing is lower than many help desks, with automation and SLAs on Growth.
  • A time-limited Free Program lets very small teams try the product.
  • Freddy AI is split into Copilot, AI Agent and Insights, so teams can adopt AI in stages.
Limitations
  • Prices rose across every tier in 2026, so older guides show lower figures.
  • Freddy AI Copilot is only sold on Pro and Enterprise, raising the real per-seat cost.
  • AI Agent sessions are priced per session, and the 500 included sessions are a one-time pool per account.
  • Multichannel support needs the separate, pricier Omni product.

Switching

Switching between Zendesk and Freshdesk.

Moving between help desks involves exporting tickets, macros and knowledge base content, rebuilding triggers and SLAs, and reconnecting channels. Plan a parallel run on one queue, and check that your target plan includes the channels and automation you rely on.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Zendesk vs Freshdesk: questions and answers

Is Zendesk or Freshdesk cheaper?

Entry prices are both around $19 per agent per month, but features differ by plan. Total cost depends on channels, AI volume and add-ons, so price your own scenario.

Which has better AI?

The sources reviewed do not support a quality ranking. They differ in billing: Zendesk per automated resolution, Freshdesk per session plus a per-agent Copilot add-on.

Does either have a free plan?

Freshdesk has a Free Program for one to two agents for six months. No permanent free plan was identified for Zendesk.

Do I need Freshdesk Omni?

If you need live chat, messaging and phone in one product, independent guides say you need Freshdesk Omni rather than Freshdesk, at higher per-agent prices.
