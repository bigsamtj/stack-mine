Quick answer

ClickUp vs Basecamp: the short answer

ClickUp and Basecamp sit at opposite ends of project management. ClickUp is a task-first all-in-one workspace with docs, whiteboards, goals and time tracking, sold per member from a free plan. Basecamp, from 37signals, bundles to-dos, message boards, chat and files into one opinionated product, sold at $15 per user per month or as a flat Pro Unlimited rate for unlimited users.

The sources reviewed describe a trade-off between features and predictability. ClickUp is described as the more powerful and cheaper tool for small teams, with Gantt charts, time tracking and AI. Basecamp is described as simpler and communication-first, with a flat rate that becomes the cheaper option for large teams, but without Gantt charts, native time tracking or AI.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. Basecamp's flat price is reported at $299 on annual billing and $349 to $399 monthly, and one source is written by a vendor of a competing tool. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on team size and feature needs.

ClickUp is cheaper for small teams and Basecamp is cheaper for large ones. Our arithmetic on reported annual prices for 25 users gives about $2,100 a year on ClickUp Unlimited ($7 x 25 x 12), $3,600 on Business ($12 x 25 x 12), $4,500 on Basecamp Plus ($15 x 25 x 12) and $3,588 on Basecamp Pro Unlimited ($299 x 12). For 50 users, ClickUp Unlimited is about $4,200 ($7 x 50 x 12) and Business about $7,200, against $3,588 for Pro Unlimited.

The crossover for ClickUp Unlimited is at about 43 users ($299 / $7), and Pro Unlimited undercuts Business at about 25 users ($299 / $12). Add the extras: ClickUp Brain at about $9 per member would lift 25 members on Unlimited to about $4,800 a year, while Basecamp's Timesheet and admin pack add-ons are reported at $50 a month each.

Check billing terms. Pro Unlimited at $299 is the annual rate, and sources report $349 to $399 month-to-month. Confirm current terms with both vendors.

Considerations

ClickUp vs Basecamp: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Guides describe Basecamp as having a very low learning curve and ClickUp as needing more configuration. Pilot one real project in each.

Integrations and ecosystem

ClickUp connects to many tools through native integrations, Zapier and its API. Basecamp's feature set is intentionally narrow, with fewer native connections. Counts were not verified, so check the specific tools you use.

AI capabilities

ClickUp sells Brain as a per-member add-on. No AI features were identified for Basecamp in the sources reviewed, which is a deliberate product position. We did not test ClickUp Brain output quality.

Security, deployment and administration

ClickUp lists advanced permissions and governance on Enterprise. Basecamp's Pro Unlimited lists SAML SSO and priority support, with no separate enterprise tier per one guide. We did not review certifications or data residency, so request current security documentation.

Strengths and limitations

ClickUp and Basecamp: each option, in balance.

C

ClickUp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • The free plan includes unlimited tasks and members, which is unusually generous.
  • Entry paid pricing of $7 per member is reported among the lowest for a full work management suite.
  • Many functions such as docs, goals and dashboards sit in one workspace.
Limitations
  • Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited.
  • AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost.
  • Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited.
  • API limits of 100 calls a minute on most plans can bottleneck automation-heavy workflows.
B

Basecamp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Flat Pro Unlimited pricing becomes cheaper than per-user plans at roughly 20 users.
  • Every project has the same six tools, which keeps setup and training simple.
  • Automatic check-ins and Hill Charts are distinctive features for asynchronous teams.
  • A free personal tier and a trial let teams evaluate it at no cost.
  • Client access is included, which suits agencies and client-facing teams.
Limitations
  • The flat plan penalises small teams: three users effectively pay about $100 per user per month on Pro Unlimited.
  • There are no Gantt charts or advanced reporting, so planning-heavy teams may find it limiting.
  • Reviewers say there are no AI features, so teams wanting AI summaries or drafts are a poor fit.
  • It is not suited to engineering teams that need GitHub integration, sprints or velocity tracking.
  • Sources disagree on month-to-month Pro Unlimited pricing, so the exact monthly cost needs confirming.

Switching

Switching between ClickUp and Basecamp.

Moving between ClickUp and Basecamp means mapping lists and tasks to to-do lists, and deciding what to do with docs, goals and time entries, which Basecamp does not offer. Message-board history has no direct ClickUp equivalent. Export a sample project and run one live project in parallel.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

ClickUp vs Basecamp: questions and answers

Is ClickUp or Basecamp cheaper?

ClickUp is cheaper for small teams: $7 per member against $15 for Basecamp Plus. Above about 43 users Basecamp's flat Pro Unlimited rate of $299 a month undercuts ClickUp Unlimited, per one guide.

How much does Basecamp Pro Unlimited cost?

A July 2026 guide reports $299 a month billed annually and $399 month-to-month, while others report $349 monthly. Confirm current terms.

Does Basecamp have Gantt charts?

No. Guides describe Basecamp as lacking Gantt charts. ClickUp includes Gantt.

Does Basecamp have time tracking?

Reported as a $50 a month add-on, with sources conflicting on whether Pro Unlimited includes it. ClickUp includes native time tracking.

Do ClickUp and Basecamp have AI?

ClickUp Brain is a paid per-member add-on. No AI features were identified for Basecamp.

Which has a better free plan?

ClickUp's free plan allows unlimited members with caps. Basecamp's free tier is reported at one user or one project, depending on the source.

Which is easier to learn?

Guides describe Basecamp as simpler and ClickUp as more powerful but heavier to configure.
