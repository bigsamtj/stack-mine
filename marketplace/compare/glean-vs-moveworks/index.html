Quick answer

Glean vs Moveworks: the short answer

Glean and Moveworks are enterprise AI assistants with search and agents. Both are custom-priced. Moveworks is now owned by ServiceNow, while Glean is independent.

Glean focuses on enterprise search and work agents; Moveworks focuses on resolving employee IT and HR requests. We compare what documentation supports and mark everything else Unknown. We declare no overall winner, because the better choice depends on channels, integrations, oversight needs, pricing model and budget.

Glean Technologies · Workflow Automation Software

Glean is an enterprise AI platform that indexes a company's apps and documents into a permission-aware knowledge graph, then provides search, an AI assistant and Glean Agents that answer questions and take actions. Agents can be built no-code or with an SDK, using models from several providers.

Switching

Switching between Glean and Moveworks.

Moving between Glean and Moveworks means re-scoping a contract, reconnecting enterprise systems and identity, rebuilding processes or agents, and repeating security review. Pilot one process on each before committing.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
