Quick answer

GitLab vs Bitbucket: the short answer

GitLab is a single DevSecOps platform covering source control, CI/CD, security scanning and planning, available as SaaS or self-managed, in Free, Premium and Ultimate tiers. Bitbucket is Atlassian's Git code hosting and CI/CD (Pipelines) service, tightly integrated with Jira, available in Free, Standard and Premium cloud plans and as Data Center. Both are developer tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, GitLab is described as: per user per month, billed annually; Bitbucket as: per user per month. Both have a free option. GitLab lists a free option: 5 users on SaaS. Bitbucket lists a free option: 5 users. GitLab is positioned for teams wanting source control, CI/CD and security in one platform, and Bitbucket for teams already using Jira and Atlassian tools.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

GitLab vs Bitbucket: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

GitLab: per user per month, billed annually. The source is a third-party SaaS management vendor without a page date. Bitbucket: per user per month. Build minutes for Standard and Premium were not stated on the page when read. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The GitLab profile highlights: single application for DevSecOps; self-managed option; ultimate includes free guest seats. The Bitbucket profile highlights: lowest per-user price among major Git hosts; deep Jira integration; data Center option.

Integrations and ecosystem

GitLab integrations named in its sources: Kubernetes, IDEs and Jira. Bitbucket: Jira, Confluence and Bitbucket Pipelines. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

GitLab runs on Web and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. Bitbucket runs on Web and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between GitLab and Bitbucket.

Moving off GitLab or Bitbucket means migrating repositories, pipelines, configuration, secrets and history, updating integrations and webhooks, and retraining the team. Run both in parallel for at least one release cycle before switching off the old tool. Move one real service or repository end to end to each shortlisted tool and measure setup time, cost and reliability.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

GitLab vs Bitbucket: questions and answers

What is the difference between GitLab and Bitbucket?

GitLab is a single DevSecOps platform covering source control, CI/CD, security scanning and planning, available as SaaS or self-managed, in Free, Premium and Ultimate tiers. Bitbucket is Atlassian's Git code hosting and CI/CD (Pipelines) service, tightly integrated with Jira, available in Free, Standard and Premium cloud plans and as Data Center. Both are developer tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. GitLab is positioned for teams wanting source control, CI/CD and security in one platform; Bitbucket for teams already using Jira and Atlassian tools.

Is GitLab cheaper than Bitbucket?

It depends on your volume and plan. GitLab: Free (5 users); Premium $29 and Ultimate $99 per user per month, billed annually. Bitbucket: Free (5 users); Standard $3.65 and Premium $8 per user per month. Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do GitLab or Bitbucket have a free plan?

Both have a free option. GitLab lists a free option: 5 users on SaaS. Bitbucket lists a free option: 5 users. GitLab free trial: Not documented. Bitbucket free trial: Not documented.

GitLab or Bitbucket: which is better for small teams?

GitLab and Bitbucket are both documented for small teams. GitLab suits teams wanting source control, CI/CD and security in one platform; Bitbucket suits teams already using Jira and Atlassian tools. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of GitLab and Bitbucket?

GitLab: Annual billing only on paid tiers. Bitbucket: Free plan includes only 50 build minutes.

How much does GitLab cost?

Premium is $29 and Ultimate $99 per user a month billed annually; Free covers up to 5 users on SaaS.

How much does Bitbucket cost?

Free for up to 5 users; Standard is $3.65 and Premium $8 per user a month.
