Quick answer

Gusto vs ADP Workforce Now: the short answer

Gusto is a US payroll, benefits and HR platform for small businesses, sold in Simple, Plus and Premium plans priced as a monthly base fee plus a fee per person paid. ADP Workforce Now is a cloud human capital management suite from ADP covering payroll, HR, benefits and time, sold in Select, Plus and Premium plans by custom quote. Both are HR and payroll platforms, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Gusto is described as: monthly base fee plus a fee per person paid; no annual prepay option reported; ADP Workforce Now as: annual contract by custom quote, typically per employee; implementation extra. Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. Gusto is positioned for US small businesses that want full-service payroll with simple HR, and ADP Workforce Now for mid-size US employers that want payroll, benefits and HR from one provider.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 3 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Considerations

Gusto vs ADP Workforce Now: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Gusto: monthly base fee plus a fee per person paid; no annual prepay option reported. The Contractor Only plan ($35 + $6, promoted at $0) closed to new customers on 1 October 2026. Gusto's own pricing page was not reviewed directly. ADP Workforce Now: annual contract by custom quote, typically per employee; implementation extra. The $16,000 median comes from seven third-party buyer reports and is marked limited confidence. ADP's own pages were not reviewed directly. Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Gusto profile highlights: transparent base-plus-per-person pricing; automatic tax filing and payments on every plan; single-state on Simple; multi-state from Plus. The ADP Workforce Now profile highlights: backed by ADP's payroll and tax infrastructure; benefits and ACA tools from Plus; Time, scheduling and labour costing on Premium.

Integrations and ecosystem

Gusto integrations named in its sources: Broker integration (add-on on Premium), Global contractor payments (add-on) and Business Compliance (add-on). ADP Workforce Now: Benefits carrier connections (Plus), Time and attendance (Premium) and Retirement services (separate terms). Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Gusto runs on Web, iOS and Android and is documented for small teams and mid-size teams. ADP Workforce Now runs on Web, iOS and Android and is documented for mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Gusto and ADP Workforce Now.

Moving off Gusto or ADP Workforce Now usually means switching at the start of a tax year or quarter, migrating employee records and year-to-date pay, tax and statutory figures, re-creating pay schedules, leave policies and benefit deductions, and running at least one payroll in parallel before cutting over. Run one full payroll in parallel on each shortlisted product and reconcile every payslip.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
