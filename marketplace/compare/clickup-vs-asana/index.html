Quick answer

ClickUp vs Asana: the short answer

ClickUp and Asana both cover tasks, projects, goals and dashboards, but they are built on different philosophies. ClickUp positions itself as an all-in-one workspace that adds docs, whiteboards, chat and time tracking to task management, sold per member from a free plan with unlimited members. Asana positions itself as a focused work management product with structured projects, portfolios and goals, sold per user from a free Personal plan.

Across the sources reviewed, the practical differences are price, AI and breadth. ClickUp is cheaper per seat and bundles more native tools, but its AI is a separate per-member add-on and several features are capped by usage on lower plans. Asana costs more per user, but several guides report AI included on paid plans and describe it as easier to learn.

We compare what independent 2026 sources support and mark the rest Unknown. Both vendors changed plans during 2026, and sources disagree on several figures, so we show the disagreement. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on how much you want in one tool and how you will use AI.

ClickUp is cheaper per seat at both comparable tiers. Our arithmetic on reported annual prices for 25 people gives about $2,100 a year on ClickUp Unlimited ($7 x 25 x 12) against $3,297 on Asana Starter ($10.99 x 25 x 12), and about $3,600 on ClickUp Business ($12 x 25 x 12) against $7,497 on Asana Advanced ($24.99 x 25 x 12). The gap widens at the upper tier because Asana's goals, portfolios and time tracking sit on Advanced.

AI changes the picture. If Brain is added at the $9 per member rate reported by several guides, ClickUp Business rises to about $6,300 a year for 25 members ($21 x 25 x 12), which narrows the gap with Asana Advanced. Sources describe Asana AI as included in paid plans, but one guide notes credits bundled by tier, so confirm what your plan covers before treating AI as free.

The free plans differ in kind. ClickUp's free plan allows unlimited members with feature caps, while Asana's free plan caps users (sources report 2, 10 or 15). A team larger than the Asana cap that wants to stay free fits only on ClickUp. Prices changed on both sides in 2026, so confirm current terms with each vendor.

Considerations

ClickUp vs Asana: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Several guides describe Asana as easier to adopt and ClickUp as more powerful but heavier to configure, with a steeper learning curve for non-technical teams. Pilot one real project in each, and decide up front which ClickUp features you will actually switch on.

Integrations and ecosystem

Both products connect to common business tools and to Zapier. One guide reports ClickUp has more native integrations, and another reports 100+ for Asana. ClickUp's API limit of about 100 calls a minute on most plans is worth checking if you build custom automations. Integration depth was not assessed for either product, so check the specific tools you use.

AI capabilities

The AI cost models differ most. ClickUp Brain is a paid add-on per member with reported prices of $5 to $9 and, in one guide, $14 to $33 for larger bundles. Several guides report Asana AI included on paid plans. Neither vendor's output quality was tested here, so run both on a real brief before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Security, deployment and administration

ClickUp lists Google SSO on Business and advanced permissions and governance on Enterprise. Asana lists SSO, SCIM and audit controls on higher tiers. We did not review certifications or data residency for either product, so request current security documentation from both vendors.

Strengths and limitations

ClickUp and Asana: each option, in balance.

C

ClickUp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • The free plan includes unlimited tasks and members, which is unusually generous.
  • Entry paid pricing of $7 per member is reported among the lowest for a full work management suite.
  • Many functions such as docs, goals and dashboards sit in one workspace.
Limitations
  • Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited.
  • AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost.
  • Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited.
  • API limits of 100 calls a minute on most plans can bottleneck automation-heavy workflows.
A

Asana

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • A free Personal plan lets small teams start without paying.
  • A clear plan ladder ties specific capabilities, such as timelines, portfolios and goals, to named tiers.
  • Automation and AI allowances scale by plan, with unlimited automations on Enterprise.
  • Enterprise plans add SSO, SCIM and audit logs for organisations with security requirements.
Limitations
  • Paid plans have a seat minimum and no single-user option.
  • Per-user pricing grows quickly with team size, and Advanced costs more than twice Starter.
  • Key reporting, workload and goal features are limited to higher-tier plans.
  • Starter rules are single-trigger, so complex multi-step automation needs Advanced.

Switching

Switching between ClickUp and Asana.

Moving between ClickUp and Asana means mapping spaces and lists to projects, rebuilding custom fields, and recreating automations; docs and whiteboards have no direct Asana equivalent. Export a sample project, rebuild it in the other tool, and run one live project in parallel before cutting over.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

ClickUp vs Asana: questions and answers

Is ClickUp cheaper than Asana?

On list price, yes: $7 and $12 per member per month for ClickUp Unlimited and Business against $10.99 and $24.99 per user for Asana Starter and Advanced, on annual billing. Adding ClickUp Brain at about $9 per member narrows the gap.

Does ClickUp or Asana have the better free plan?

ClickUp's free plan allows unlimited members with feature caps. Asana's free Personal plan caps users at a figure that sources put at 2, 10 or 15. For a larger free team, ClickUp is the roomier option.

Is AI included in ClickUp and Asana?

ClickUp Brain is a paid add-on per member. Several guides report Asana AI included on paid plans, though one notes credits by tier. Confirm what your plan covers.

Which is easier to learn, ClickUp or Asana?

Several guides describe Asana as easier to learn and ClickUp as more powerful but heavier to configure.

Which has better automation?

Allowances differ widely and sources conflict. ClickUp automations are capped by plan, while one guide reports unlimited automations on Asana Starter and above. Check current limits for your volume.

Does Asana have docs and whiteboards like ClickUp?

One guide reports Asana has no native docs or whiteboards, while ClickUp includes both.

Which is better for portfolio management?

Asana lists portfolios and goals on its Advanced plan. ClickUp lists goals and workload on Business. The sources reviewed do not rank them, so test your reporting needs.
