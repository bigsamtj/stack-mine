Quick answer

Nifty vs ClickUp: the short answer

Nifty and ClickUp are all-in-one project management tools. Nifty combines milestones, tasks, docs, chat and time tracking; ClickUp offers a broad work platform with a generous free plan.

Nifty prices flat team plans and is popular with agencies working with clients. ClickUp prices per member with an AI add-on sold separately.

We compare what the dated sources support and mark the rest Unknown. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and how you work.

Nifty is free, with Pro at $79 a month billed annually for 10 members and Business at $124. ClickUp has a Free Forever plan and Unlimited reported at $7 per member a month billed annually. For 10 members, ClickUp Unlimited is about $70 versus Nifty Pro at $79.

List prices rarely tell the whole story. Check minimum terms, user or volume minimums, add-ons and taxes such as GST before comparing totals.

Considerations

Nifty vs ClickUp: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and trials

We did not test setup or migration for either product. Nifty free trial: Not stated. ClickUp free trial: Not documented. Pilot one real process in each before committing.

Integrations and ecosystem

Nifty: Google Drive and Calendar; Slack; GitHub. ClickUp: Integrations on paid plans (unlimited on Unlimited); Enterprise API; MCP access (reported). Check each integration directory for the tools you actually use.

AI capabilities

Nifty documents AI features on its profile. ClickUp documents AI features on its profile. We did not test output quality, so run both against a real task before treating AI as a deciding factor.

Deployment, security and administration

Nifty runs as cloud (SaaS); ClickUp runs as cloud (SaaS). We did not review certifications or data residency in detail. Request current security documentation from both vendors if you are regulated.

Strengths and limitations

Nifty and ClickUp: each option, in balance.

Nifty

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Flat pricing suits growing teams.
  • Docs, chat and time tracking in one tool.
  • Free plan with unlimited members.
  • Client-friendly projects.
Limitations
  • Plan names and prices have changed, causing confusion.
  • Advanced workflows need Business.
  • Storage limits not clearly stated.
  • Time log entries have edit limits.
C

ClickUp

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • The free plan includes unlimited tasks and members, which is unusually generous.
  • Entry paid pricing of $7 per member is reported among the lowest for a full work management suite.
  • Many functions such as docs, goals and dashboards sit in one workspace.
Limitations
  • Many features are capped by number of uses on lower plans rather than being unlimited.
  • AI is a separate add-on billed on every paid member, which raises total cost.
  • Goals, time tracking and workload management need the Business plan, not Unlimited.
  • API limits of 100 calls a minute on most plans can bottleneck automation-heavy workflows.

Switching

Switching between Nifty and ClickUp.

Export tasks and projects to CSV or use built-in importers, then rebuild views, automations and permissions.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Nifty vs ClickUp: questions and answers

What is the difference between Nifty and ClickUp?

Nifty and ClickUp are all-in-one project management tools. Nifty combines milestones, tasks, docs, chat and time tracking; ClickUp offers a broad work platform with a generous free plan. Nifty prices flat team plans and is popular with agencies working with clients.

Which is cheaper for 10 people?

ClickUp Unlimited at about $7 per member is about $70 a month; Nifty Pro is $79 a month for 10 members billed annually.

Do both have free plans?

Yes.

Which suits agencies?

Nifty is often chosen by agencies for client-facing projects.

Which is better for small businesses, Nifty or ClickUp?

Nifty is documented for small teams, mid-size teams; ClickUp for small teams, mid-size teams, large enterprises. Choose Nifty if you run client projects as an agency; choose ClickUp if you want a generous free plan.
