Quick answer

athenahealth (athenaOne) vs eClinicalWorks: the short answer

athenahealth's athenaOne combines a cloud EHR, practice management and managed billing on a shared network for ambulatory practices. eClinicalWorks is EHR and practice management software with one database for clinical, scheduling and billing work, plus ambient AI charting. Both are EHRs, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, athenahealth (athenaOne) is described as: percentage of collections by quote; eClinicalWorks as: per provider per month or % of collections. Neither documents a permanent free plan in the sources reviewed. athenahealth (athenaOne) is positioned for practices wanting managed billing on a shared network, and eClinicalWorks for multi-specialty practices wanting EHR, PM and billing in one database.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 2 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Switching

Switching between athenahealth (athenaOne) and eClinicalWorks.

Moving off athenahealth (athenaOne) or eClinicalWorks means exporting patient demographics, clinical records, documents, appointments, claims and balances in a usable format, mapping templates and codes, re-enrolling with payers and clearinghouses, and keeping read-only access to the old system for records retention. Confirm export rights in the contract. Run a set of test patients through each shortlisted system, from intake and charting to e-prescribing, claim submission and payment posting, and request the BAA before signing.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.
