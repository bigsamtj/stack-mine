Quick answer

MSG91 vs Gupshup: the short answer

MSG91 and Gupshup are Indian messaging platforms. MSG91 is strong in SMS and OTP with a broad CPaaS; Gupshup is strong in WhatsApp and conversational AI at scale.

MSG91 offers SMS, OTP, WhatsApp, email and voice APIs plus the Hello inbox. Gupshup offers WhatsApp, SMS and RCS APIs with enterprise conversational products.

We compare what the dated sources support and mark the rest Unknown. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and how you work.

Switching

Switching between MSG91 and Gupshup.

Switching providers means re-registering sender IDs and templates (DLT and WhatsApp), updating API calls and testing delivery. Run both in parallel for critical OTP traffic.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

MSG91 vs Gupshup: questions and answers

What is the difference between MSG91 and Gupshup?

MSG91 and Gupshup are Indian messaging platforms. MSG91 is strong in SMS and OTP with a broad CPaaS; Gupshup is strong in WhatsApp and conversational AI at scale. MSG91 offers SMS, OTP, WhatsApp, email and voice APIs plus the Hello inbox.

Is MSG91 or Gupshup cheaper for WhatsApp?

Gupshup’s self-serve markup is about $0.001 per message over Meta’s fee. Compare MSG91’s WhatsApp rates for your volume.

Which is better for OTP?

MSG91 is widely used for OTP and SMS in India, with an OTP widget.

Do both handle DLT compliance?

Indian SMS requires DLT registration with either provider.

Which is better for small businesses, MSG91 or Gupshup?

MSG91 is documented for small teams, mid-size teams, large enterprises; Gupshup for mid-size teams, large enterprises. Choose MSG91 if you need OTP and transactional SMS; choose Gupshup if you send high WhatsApp volumes.

What are the alternatives to MSG91 and Gupshup?

Both are listed in Stack18’s Email & SMS API Software directory. See the MSG91 alternatives guide and the Gupshup alternatives guide for sourced shortlists of competitors.
