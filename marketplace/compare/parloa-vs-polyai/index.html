Quick answer

Parloa vs PolyAI: the short answer

Parloa and PolyAI are enterprise voice AI agent platforms for contact centres. Both use consumption pricing by the minute; PolyAI added two free months for self-serve builders in May 2026, while Parloa remains enterprise-only.

Parloa offers its AI Agent Management Platform with simulation and testing; PolyAI is known for natural-sounding voice agents in many languages. We compare what documentation supports and mark everything else Unknown. We declare no overall winner, because the better choice depends on channels, integrations, oversight needs, pricing model and budget.

Switching

Switching between Parloa and PolyAI.

Moving between Parloa and PolyAI means porting phone numbers or SIP trunks, rebuilding call flows and prompts, re-testing latency, voices and languages, and re-checking call recording and consent settings. Pilot the same call type on both before migrating.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Parloa vs PolyAI: questions and answers

Is Parloa or PolyAI better?

The sources reviewed do not support a single ranking. Both use consumption pricing by the minute; PolyAI added two free months for self-serve builders in May 2026, while Parloa remains enterprise-only. Pilot both on a sample of your own conversations, leads or workflows before choosing.

Does Parloa have a free plan?

No free plan.

Does PolyAI have a free plan?

No permanent free plan. Self-serve sign-ups build free for the first two months.

Which is cheaper, Parloa or PolyAI?

Parloa: Custom enterprise pricing, consumption-based (per minute for voice). PolyAI: Per-minute enterprise pricing; two free months for self-serve builders since May 2026. The cheaper option depends on seats, usage and which plan features you need.
