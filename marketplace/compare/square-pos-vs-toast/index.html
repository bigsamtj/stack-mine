Quick answer

Square POS vs Toast: the short answer

Square POS is a point of sale system for retail, restaurants and services, combining free software, card processing, hardware and add-ons such as online stores and loyalty. Toast is a cloud POS and restaurant management platform covering ordering, kitchen display, online ordering, payroll and loyalty. Both are POS systems, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Square POS is described as: free or monthly software plus card processing fees; Toast as: monthly software per location plus processing. Both have a free option. Square POS lists a free plan. Toast lists a free option: starter. Square POS is positioned for most small businesses wanting a free, easy POS, and Toast for restaurants wanting an all-in-one restaurant platform.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 3 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

  • Square POS: Free plan; Plus $49, Premium $149 a month; processing ~2.6% + 15c on the free plan (US).
  • Toast: $0 to $69 a month per location; paid plan processing 2.49% + 15c; add-ons push bills to $150-$500 a month.
  • Free plan: Square POS — Yes; Toast — Yes (starter).
  • Square POS best for: Most small businesses wanting a free, easy POS.
  • Toast best for: Restaurants wanting an all-in-one restaurant platform.
  • No winner: test both against a real scenario before deciding.

Considerations

Square POS vs Toast: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Square POS: free or monthly software plus card processing fees. Dupple (June 2026); UK figures from SumUp (June 2026, competitor). Toast: monthly software per location plus processing. Dupple (June 2026) and CostBench (2026). Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Square POS profile highlights: free plan; integrated payments; industry-specific apps. The Toast profile highlights: restaurant-only; add-on ecosystem; Toast payments.

Integrations and ecosystem

Square POS integrations named in its sources: Square Online, QuickBooks, Xero and DoorDash. Toast: DoorDash, Uber Eats, OpenTable and QuickBooks. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Square POS runs on iOS, Android and Web and is documented for small teams and mid-size teams. Toast runs on iOS, Android and Web and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Square POS and Toast.

Moving off Square POS or Toast means exporting products or menus, customers, gift card balances and sales history, setting up new hardware and payment processing, retraining staff and reconnecting accounting and delivery integrations. Check contract end dates and plan the switch for a quiet trading day. Ring up a typical busy hour of real orders on each shortlisted POS and compare speed, total processing cost and reporting.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Square POS vs Toast: questions and answers

What is the difference between Square POS and Toast?

Square POS is a point of sale system for retail, restaurants and services, combining free software, card processing, hardware and add-ons such as online stores and loyalty. Toast is a cloud POS and restaurant management platform covering ordering, kitchen display, online ordering, payroll and loyalty. Both are POS systems, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. Square POS is positioned for most small businesses wanting a free, easy POS; Toast for restaurants wanting an all-in-one restaurant platform.

Is Square POS cheaper than Toast?

It depends on your volume and plan. Square POS: Free plan; Plus $49, Premium $149 a month; processing ~2.6% + 15c on the free plan (US). Toast: $0 to $69 a month per location; paid plan processing 2.49% + 15c; add-ons push bills to $150-$500 a month. Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do Square POS or Toast have a free plan?

Both have a free option. Square POS lists a free plan. Toast lists a free option: starter. Square POS free trial: Not applicable. Toast free trial: Not applicable.

Square POS or Toast: which is better for small teams?

Square POS and Toast are both documented for small teams. Square POS suits most small businesses wanting a free, easy POS; Toast suits restaurants wanting an all-in-one restaurant platform. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of Square POS and Toast?

Square POS: Processing costs rise with volume. Toast: Add-ons raise total cost.

How much does Square POS cost?

The software has a free plan; Plus is $49 and Premium $149 a month, plus processing fees.

How much does Toast cost?

From $0 to $69 per location a month, plus processing; add-ons can push bills to $150 to $500.
