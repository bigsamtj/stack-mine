Quick answer

Fillout vs Tally: the short answer

Fillout is a form builder with logic, multi-page flows, payments, scheduling and PDF generation, offering a free plan and Starter, Pro and Business tiers. Tally is a form builder with a document-style editor and a generous free plan offering unlimited forms and submissions within fair use, with Pro and Business upgrades. Both are form builders, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Fillout is described as: monthly or yearly by responses; Tally as: free or monthly subscription. Both have a free option. Fillout lists a free option: 1,000 responses. Tally lists a free option: unlimited, fair use. Fillout is positioned for teams wanting Typeform-style forms with a generous free tier, and Tally for founders and small teams wanting powerful forms for free.

Switching

Switching between Fillout and Tally.

Moving off Fillout or Tally means exporting responses (usually CSV), rebuilding forms and logic, reconnecting integrations and webhooks, and updating embeds and links on your site. Keep old forms live until new ones are tested. Rebuild your most important form in each shortlisted tool and test it on mobile with real respondents.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Fillout vs Tally: questions and answers

What is the difference between Fillout and Tally?

Fillout is a form builder with logic, multi-page flows, payments, scheduling and PDF generation, offering a free plan and Starter, Pro and Business tiers. Tally is a form builder with a document-style editor and a generous free plan offering unlimited forms and submissions within fair use, with Pro and Business upgrades. Both are form builders, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. Fillout is positioned for teams wanting Typeform-style forms with a generous free tier; Tally for founders and small teams wanting powerful forms for free.

Is Fillout cheaper than Tally?

It depends on your volume and plan. Fillout: Free (1,000 responses a month); Starter $15/mo yearly; Pro $40; Business $75 yearly. Tally: Free (unlimited forms and submissions); Pro $29 and Business $89 a month. Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do Fillout or Tally have a free plan?

Both have a free option. Fillout lists a free option: 1,000 responses. Tally lists a free option: unlimited, fair use. Fillout free trial: Not documented. Tally free trial: Not documented.

Fillout or Tally: which is better for small teams?

Fillout and Tally are both documented for small teams. Fillout suits teams wanting Typeform-style forms with a generous free tier; Tally suits founders and small teams wanting powerful forms for free. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of Fillout and Tally?

Fillout: Fillout badge on free. Tally: Tally branding until Pro.

How much does Fillout cost?

Starter is $15 a month yearly ($19 monthly); Pro $40 and Business $75 a month yearly.

Is Tally really free?

Yes, with unlimited forms and submissions under fair-use guidelines.
