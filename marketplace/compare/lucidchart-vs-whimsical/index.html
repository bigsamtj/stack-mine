Quick answer

Lucidchart vs Whimsical: the short answer

Lucidchart is a cloud diagramming application for flowcharts, org charts, network and cloud architecture diagrams, ERDs and BPMN, with data linking and enterprise governance. Whimsical is a visual collaboration workspace combining boards (flowcharts, wireframes, mind maps) and docs, priced per editor. Both are design tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus.

On pricing, Lucidchart is described as: per user per month; auto-renewing; Whimsical as: per editor per month or year. Both have a free option. Lucidchart lists a free option: 3 documents. Whimsical lists a free option: limited objects. Lucidchart is positioned for teams needing technical and process diagrams with integrations, and Whimsical for product teams sketching flows and wireframes quickly.

Everything below is drawn from the two sourced profiles and the 3 dated sources listed at the end. Where a source does not document something it is marked Unknown, and third-party estimates are labelled. No winner is declared: the better fit depends on your size, budget and requirements.

Reviewed by the Stack18 editorial team · 3 sources

Key takeaways

  • Lucidchart: Free (3 editable documents); Individual $9; Team $9-$10 per user a month (3-user minimum); Enterprise custom.
  • Whimsical: Free (50 board objects a month); Pro $12, Business $24 per editor a month ($120 / $240 a year).
  • Free plan: Lucidchart — Yes (3 documents); Whimsical — Yes (limited objects).
  • Lucidchart best for: Teams needing technical and process diagrams with integrations.
  • Whimsical best for: Product teams sketching flows and wireframes quickly.
  • No winner: test both against a real scenario before deciding.

Considerations

Lucidchart vs Whimsical: pricing model, ecosystem and fit.

Pricing model and cost drivers

Lucidchart: per user per month; auto-renewing. CostBench (verified July 2026); Team price conflicts on the page. Whimsical: per editor per month or year. Whimsical help centre (modified 2 October 2026). Model costs at your real seat count, usage and contract term before comparing headline prices.

What sets each apart

The Lucidchart profile highlights: broad diagram types; data-linked diagrams; visio import/export. The Whimsical profile highlights: boards plus docs; per-editor pricing; education discount.

Integrations and ecosystem

Lucidchart integrations named in its sources: Confluence, Jira, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Whimsical: Slack, Notion, Jira and GitHub. Check that the connectors you rely on exist on the plan you would buy.

Deployment and team fit

Lucidchart runs on Web, Windows and macOS and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. Whimsical runs on Web, Windows and macOS and is documented for small teams, mid-size teams and large enterprises. We did not test setup or migration.

Switching

Switching between Lucidchart and Whimsical.

Moving off Lucidchart or Whimsical means exporting files in open formats, rebuilding component libraries, templates and brand kits, re-sharing projects with collaborators and checking that fonts and assets carry over. Keep the old workspace read-only until active projects finish. Recreate one recent project in each shortlisted tool and have both an editor and a reviewer test the workflow.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Compare total cost at your real volume, including add-ons and services.
  4. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  5. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

Lucidchart vs Whimsical: questions and answers

What is the difference between Lucidchart and Whimsical?

Lucidchart is a cloud diagramming application for flowcharts, org charts, network and cloud architecture diagrams, ERDs and BPMN, with data linking and enterprise governance. Whimsical is a visual collaboration workspace combining boards (flowcharts, wireframes, mind maps) and docs, priced per editor. Both are design tools, but they differ in pricing model, target team size and focus. Lucidchart is positioned for teams needing technical and process diagrams with integrations; Whimsical for product teams sketching flows and wireframes quickly.

Is Lucidchart cheaper than Whimsical?

It depends on your volume and plan. Lucidchart: Free (3 editable documents); Individual $9; Team $9-$10 per user a month (3-user minimum); Enterprise custom. Whimsical: Free (50 board objects a month); Pro $12, Business $24 per editor a month ($120 / $240 a year). Compare both at your real seat count, usage and contract term.

Do Lucidchart or Whimsical have a free plan?

Both have a free option. Lucidchart lists a free option: 3 documents. Whimsical lists a free option: limited objects. Lucidchart free trial: Not documented. Whimsical free trial: Not documented.

Lucidchart or Whimsical: which is better for small teams?

Lucidchart and Whimsical are both documented for small teams. Lucidchart suits teams needing technical and process diagrams with integrations; Whimsical suits product teams sketching flows and wireframes quickly. Neither is best for everyone.

What are the main limitations of Lucidchart and Whimsical?

Lucidchart: Free plan limited to 3 documents. Whimsical: Free plan object limits.

How much does Lucidchart cost?

Individual is $9 per user a month; Team is $9 to $10 per user with a 3-user minimum; Enterprise is custom.

How much does Whimsical cost?

Pro is $12 and Business $24 per editor a month, or $120 and $240 a year.
