Quick answer

Windsurf vs Zed: the short answer

Windsurf and Zed are AI-native editors with agents built in. Windsurf, now Devin Desktop, bundles quota-based model usage from $20 a month, while Zed is open source, free with your own keys and $10 a month for hosted models.

Zed can host external agents through ACP; Windsurf's Cascade agent is built in. We compare what documentation supports and mark everything else Unknown. We declare no overall winner, because the better choice depends on workflow, model preferences, oversight needs and budget.

Considerations

Windsurf vs Zed: implementation, ecosystem, AI and security.

Ease of implementation and usability

We did not test setup or onboarding effort. Evaluate both tools on a real branch of your own repository, with your own tests and conventions.

Integrations and ecosystem

Windsurf: Claude, GPT and Gemini models, SWE-series models, MCP servers (reported by third-party guides), Devin cloud agent (same vendor). Zed: Agent Client Protocol (Claude Agent, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode), Bring-your-own API keys, MCP servers, Zeta edit prediction model, Real-time collaboration.

AI capabilities

Model choice: Windsurf - SWE models plus Claude, GPT and Gemini. Zed - Hosted Claude, GPT and Gemini, Zeta, or your own keys.

Security, deployment and administration

Approval and admin controls: Windsurf - Teams admin and billing; SSO and compliance on Enterprise. Zed - Business adds AI policies, RBAC and spend visibility; SSO not documented. Check each vendor's security documentation for certifications and data-retention terms.

Strengths and limitations

Windsurf and Zed: each option, in balance.

W

Windsurf

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Tab completions and inline edits are listed as unlimited on every plan, including Free.
  • Daily and weekly quotas spread usage out instead of letting one session drain a monthly pool.
  • Paid plans keep working after the quota, billed at API prices, rather than stopping.
Limitations
  • The product was renamed Devin Desktop in June 2026 and repriced in March 2026, so older guides and docs can be wrong.
  • New Teams plans no longer include SSO; it is Enterprise-only.
  • There is no free trial of paid plans, according to a November 2026 guide.
Z

Zed

Strengths and limitations from the profile

Strengths
  • Personal is free with unlimited use through your own keys or external agents.
  • ACP lets developers run Claude Agent, Codex and other CLI agents inside the editor.
  • Pro costs $10 a month, below most AI editor subscriptions.
Limitations
  • No SSO, SAML or SCIM was documented in one September 2026 profile.
  • Hosted-model usage beyond the included $5 on Pro is billed at list price plus 10%.
  • The extension ecosystem is smaller than VS Code's.

Switching

Switching between Windsurf and Zed.

Moving between Windsurf and Zed means re-creating project instructions and rules, re-adding MCP servers or other tool connections, and adjusting how your team reviews agent output. Trying each on a separate branch before committing is a low-risk way to compare them.

General switching checklist
  1. Export a sample of your data and test an import into the other product.
  2. List the automations, integrations and permissions you would need to rebuild.
  3. Pilot with one team before a full cut-over.
  4. Check contract terms and notice periods with both vendors.

General guidance, not specific to either vendor. Confirm migration options with the vendors.

FAQ

Windsurf vs Zed: questions and answers

Is Windsurf or Zed better?

The sources reviewed do not support a single ranking. Windsurf, now Devin Desktop, bundles quota-based model usage from $20 a month, while Zed is open source, free with your own keys and $10 a month for hosted models. Test both on your own repository or project before choosing.

Does Windsurf have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes a light daily and weekly quota with limited model choice and unlimited Tab completions.

Does Zed have a free plan?

Yes. Personal is free forever with 2,000 accepted edit predictions and unlimited use with your own API keys or external agents.

Which is cheaper, Windsurf or Zed?

Windsurf: Free plan; Pro $20 and Max $200 per month; Teams $40 per seat ($80 minimum); Enterprise custom. Zed: Free Personal plan; Pro $10 per month; Business $30 per seat per month. The cheaper option depends on seats, usage and which plan features you need.

Which supports more models, Windsurf or Zed?

Windsurf: SWE models plus Claude, GPT and Gemini. Zed: Hosted Claude, GPT and Gemini, Zeta, or your own keys.
