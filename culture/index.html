In short

What is the culture at Stack18 like?

Stack18 has an AI-native, ownership-driven culture. People work alongside ten specialised AI agents on every project, own their work from scope to ship, and move across many technology domains, from product engineering and AI to business systems and venture building.

Six values guide what the team chooses when two good things compete: ownership over hierarchy, curiosity over assumptions, execution over excuses, collaboration over silos, continuous learning, and ambition with accountability.

Our values

What we choose when two good things compete.

Values are only useful when they help you decide. Each of ours names the thing we choose, and the thing we choose it over.

01

Ownership over hierarchy

Anyone can pick up a problem. A title does not decide who gets to fix it, and work is assigned to people, not committees.

02

Curiosity over assumptions

We ask, test and check before we decide, and we change our minds when the evidence does.

03

Execution over excuses

Working software at the end of every sprint is the standard we hold ourselves to.

04

Collaboration over silos

Engineers, designers and strategists share one backlog and one outcome.

05

Continuous learning

No finished skill sets. Time spent learning is part of the work, not a reward for finishing it.

06

Ambition with accountability

Aim high, and own the result either way.

AI-native by default

Less repetitive work. More decisions.

Every delivery team works with ten specialised AI agents across seven lifecycle stages. Using AI well is the job here, not a side experiment.

What the agents do

  • Draft specifications and user stories
  • Generate code, APIs and tests
  • Run functional, regression and security checks
  • Keep documentation up to date
  • Monitor systems and flag regressions

What you do

  • Understand the customer and the real problem
  • Direct the agents and review their output
  • Make the calls at governance gates
  • Own the result from scope to ship
  • Decide what to improve next
Every sprintPlan together

Two-week sprints start with a shared goal and one backlog across disciplines.

Every weekShow, don't report

A demo someone can click. Progress is shown, not written up in a status report.

AlwaysDecide close to the work

Whoever scopes the work stands behind it, and is trusted to make the calls.

When it shipsMark the moment

Learning days, team activities and celebrations when something goes live.

FAQ

Culture: common questions

What are Stack18's values?

Ownership over hierarchy, curiosity over assumptions, execution over excuses, collaboration over silos, continuous learning, and ambition with accountability.

How do people work with AI at Stack18?

Every delivery team works with ten specialised AI agents across seven lifecycle stages. Agents draft specifications, code, tests and documentation; people direct them, review their output and make the decisions at governance gates.

Where is the team based?

Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, where engineering, product development and business operations are based.

Is Stack18 hiring?

There are no advertised roles at the moment, but Stack18 is always open to exceptional people. You can share your profile and register interest in internships on the Careers page.

Does Stack18 offer internships?

Yes, across technology, AI, marketing, design and business.

Sound like your kind of place?

Share your profile with the Stack18 team. Great people are always welcome, even when no role is advertised.
