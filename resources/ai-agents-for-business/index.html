Quick answer

What is an AI agent for business?

An AI agent is software that uses AI to carry out a task or workflow, such as answering support queries, drafting documents or reconciling invoices, using your data and tools. Unlike a basic chatbot it can take steps. Good deployments keep people in charge of critical decisions through approval gates and audit trails.

Agents compared

How an AI agent differs from the tools you already know.

The difference is how much work the system does on its own, and how much oversight it needs.

ToolWhat it doesOversight needed
ChatbotAnswers questions in a conversation.Low, but it does not complete work.
Automation or RPARepeats fixed steps exactly as scripted.Low, but it breaks when inputs change.
AI assistantHelps one person draft, summarise or search.The person reviews each output.
AI agentPlans and carries out steps across systems toward a goal.Approval gates, permissions and an audit trail.

FAQ

Common questions.

Ask us anything
What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that uses AI to carry out a task or workflow, using your data and tools, and taking steps toward a goal rather than only answering questions.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot converses. An agent can plan and carry out steps across systems, which is why it needs permissions, approvals and an audit trail.

Are AI agents safe for business data?

They can be, when permissions, approvals and audit trails are built in and data stays in an environment you control. Stack18’s AI Enterprise OS provides private AI with governance built in.

Where should we start with AI agents?

With one repetitive, measurable, low-risk workflow, such as support queries or invoice matching. Prove the value, then expand to the next department.

How much does AI Enterprise OS cost?

Stack18 AI Enterprise OS is priced once, in rupees and excluding GST: ₹3.2L for Launch, ₹7.9L for Grow and ₹12.5L for Scale.

Will agents replace our team?

Stack18’s approach is AI-native and human-governed: agents do repetitive work and people approve the decisions that matter.

How long does it take to deploy a first AI agent?

With the AI Enterprise OS Launch edition, a first agent typically goes live in 4 to 5 weeks.

Can AI agents work in Hindi and on WhatsApp?

Yes. Stack18 builds agents for WhatsApp and web that work in Hindi and English, with human handover where needed.
