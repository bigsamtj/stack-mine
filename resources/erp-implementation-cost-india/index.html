Quick answer

How much does ERP implementation cost in India?

ERP cost has five parts: licence or build, setup, data migration, training and running costs such as hosting and support. Per-user pricing grows with headcount every year. Stack18 Business OS is priced once, in rupees and excluding GST, at ₹2.8L (Launch), ₹5.7L (Grow) and ₹9.95L (Scale), with no per-user fees.

Key takeaways

The short version.

FAQ

Common questions.

Ask us anything
How much does an ERP cost in India?

It varies with the licence model, number of users, modules and setup. Stack18 Business OS is priced once, excluding GST: ₹2.8L for Launch, ₹5.7L for Grow and ₹9.95L for Scale, with no per-user fees.

How long does ERP implementation take?

For Stack18 Business OS, Launch typically goes live in 3 to 4 weeks, Grow in 6 to 8 weeks and Scale in 10 to 12 weeks, depending on modules and integrations.

Does ERP pricing include GST compliance?

Business OS includes GST accounting, e-invoicing with IRN and QR, e-way bills and TDS, with GSTR-ready reports. Always check whether other vendors charge for these separately.

Can we migrate from Tally?

Yes. Business OS includes Tally Migration and Sync in the Launch edition, bringing masters, opening balances and history across, with ongoing sync if your CA prefers to file from Tally.

What costs are not in the one-time price?

Cloud hosting on your own account and third-party usage such as WhatsApp or payment gateway charges are paid separately. Prices exclude GST.

Can we buy only some modules?

Yes. Individual modules start from ₹20,000, and the Launch, Grow and Scale editions bundle modules at 10%, 15% and 20% below their combined price.

How do I calculate the three-year cost of an ERP?

Count everyone who will log in within three years, add every line item (licence, setup, migration, training, hosting, support and add-ons), check what is billed separately and ask how you would take your data with you if you leave.

Where does Business OS run?

On your own cloud account or servers, not a shared multi-tenant system. Hosting is paid to your cloud provider.
