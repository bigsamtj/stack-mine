How much does it cost to build a mobile app in India?

App cost depends on features, platforms, integrations, offline needs and design depth, so a fair price needs a scope. What moves the price

Seven features and choices that change the estimate.

Use this list to compare quotes and decide what belongs in version one.

DriverWhy it changes the costWay to control it
Number of screens and flowsEach journey needs design, build and testing.Ship the core journey first.
PlatformsAndroid only, iOS only, or both from one codebase.One cross-platform codebase covers both stores.
Offline modeField and low-network use needs data sync and conflict handling.Make only the essential flows work offline.
Payments and loginUPI, saved payments and OTP login add integration work.Use standard providers rather than custom flows.
Backend and adminThe APIs, database and admin panel behind the app.Reuse existing systems where you can.
IntegrationsERP, CRM, WhatsApp and push notifications.List must-have integrations before scoping.
Design depthA tested prototype costs more than a simple template, and saves rework.Test the prototype before building it.

FAQ

Common questions.

Ask us anything
How much does it cost to build a mobile app in India?

It depends on features, platforms, integrations, offline needs and design depth. Stack18 starts with a fixed-price App Discovery of 2 weeks, then a fixed-price App Build of 8 to 14 weeks.

How long does mobile app development take?

A typical Stack18 App Build takes 8 to 14 weeks to store launch, after a 2 week fixed-price discovery. Growth work then continues as a monthly team.

Do I need separate Android and iOS apps?

Not necessarily. Stack18 builds Android and iOS apps from one codebase, which reduces cost and keeps both in step.

Can the app work offline?

Yes. Stack18 designs apps for Indian networks and devices, with offline mode for field and low-network use.

What happens after the app is live?

Stack18 monitors crashes and usage and ships updates, either as a one-off or through a monthly App Growth Pod.

Can I start with a prototype first?

Yes. App Discovery is a 2 week fixed-price step that produces user journeys, a tested prototype and a fixed-price quote.

Should I build a native or a cross-platform app?

For most business apps, one cross-platform codebase covers both Android and iOS and keeps cost down. Native builds make sense when you need deep device features or platform-specific performance.

What does an app cost to run after launch?

Plan for hosting, app store fees, third-party usage such as SMS, WhatsApp or payments, and ongoing support. Stack18’s optional Care plan starts from 15% of build value per year.
