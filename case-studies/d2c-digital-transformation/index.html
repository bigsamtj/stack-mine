Quick answer

What did Stack18 deliver for the ethnic wear brand?

A nine-member Stack18 team delivered a new website, a shopping app and an ERP on one connected foundation for a Jaipur-based ethnic wear brand with more than ₹100 crore in revenue. The project ran for three months from kickoff to launch.

The brief

A fast-growing brand needed its technology to keep pace.

Ethnic wear is one of the most demanding categories in D2C. At ₹100 crore and beyond, the website, the app and the back office all have to hold up together.

01

Large, variant-heavy catalogues

Ethnic wear ranges run to thousands of styles across sizes, colours, fabrics and collections, and every variant has to be right on screen and in stock.

02

Festive and wedding-season peaks

Demand surges around festivals and the wedding season, so the whole stack has to stay fast when traffic and order volume spike.

03

One view of the business

Storefront, app, inventory and operations need to share one source of truth, so stock, orders and customers match everywhere.

Our approach

From first workshop to launch in three months.

1

Discover

We mapped the brand’s catalogue, channels and operations, then agreed what had to go live first.

2

Design

Customer journeys for web and app, and the ERP workflows behind them, designed together.

3

Build

Nine specialists working in two-week sprints, with a working demo at the end of each one.

4

Launch

Go-live across website, app and ERP, with the team supported through the launch.

What we built

Three platforms, one connected foundation.

The website, the app and the ERP share one catalogue and one record of stock and orders.

01

Website

A fast, search-friendly storefront designed for a large catalogue.

  • Mobile-first design
  • Search and filters built for large ranges
  • Streamlined cart and checkout
  • SEO-ready structure
02

Mobile app

A shopping app that runs on the same catalogue as the website.

  • Shared catalogue, cart and customer accounts
  • Browse and order in a few taps
  • Order tracking and notifications
03

ERP

One back office connected to every sale.

  • Inventory management across locations
  • Order management from website and app
  • Purchasing and supplier management
  • Finance and reporting
The outcome

What the project delivered.

One connected system

Website, app and ERP work from one catalogue and one record of stock and orders, so what a customer sees matches what the business holds.

Built for the next stage

Designed for a brand already past ₹100 crore, so the foundation is ready for the growth that follows.

Lessons for similar projects

What other teams can take from this.

Practical points from the brief and the way the project was run, for anyone planning something similar.

  • Model the catalogue before the screensIn variant-heavy categories, sizes, colours, fabrics and collections need a data model that the website, app and ERP all share.
  • Plan for the peak, not the averageFestive and wedding-season traffic decides whether a stack holds up; test for it before launch.
  • One source of truth for stockWhen web, app and back office read one record of stock and orders, overselling and mismatches disappear.
  • Build together, launch togetherDesigning front end and back office in the same programme avoids integrating three vendors later.
