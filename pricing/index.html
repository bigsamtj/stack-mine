FAQ

Pricing questions.

Ask about pricing
How is pricing calculated?

Each module has a one-time build and setup price in rupees, excluding GST. Editions bundle modules at 10–20% below their combined price. Bigger modules include the smaller ones they contain, so nothing is charged twice.

Are there monthly or per-user fees?

No licence or per-user fees. You pay for cloud hosting on your own account and any third-party usage such as WhatsApp, payment gateway or AI model charges. The Stack18 Care plan for updates and support is optional, from 15% of build value per year.

How much does a custom project cost?

It depends on scope, so we fix the price after a short discovery of 1 to 4 weeks. For comparison, typical Indian agency quotes for comparable custom builds are often ₹5–20 lakh or more.

How long does it take to go live?

Launch editions typically go live in 3–5 weeks. Grow and Scale editions are delivered in phases over 6–14 weeks, depending on modules and integrations.

Do prices include GST?

No. All prices on this page are in rupees and exclude GST.

Can we combine solutions?

Yes. All six share the same foundation, so customers, products, stock and money flow between them without duplicate entry. Many businesses start with one and add another within a year.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute consultation, and an indicative estimate follows after a short call.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with teams in India and abroad, across time zones, with English as the working language. Prices here are in rupees. Tell us where you are and we will confirm how we price and invoice for your region.
