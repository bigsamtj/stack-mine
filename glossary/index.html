A–Z

Every definition.

Select a term to read the full definition and its operational context.

ABCDEFGIMNOPRSTVZ

A

AI Security
Protecting AI systems and using AI to strengthen security operations.
Attack Surface
The set of exposed systems, services, identities and interfaces an adversary could attempt to exploit.

B

Behavioural Analysis
The study of how threat actors and defenders actually behave, to explain intent and anticipate action.

C

Chain of Custody
The documented record of who handled evidence, when, how and why, from acquisition to presentation.
Cloud Security
The protection of cloud identities, configurations, workloads, data and services under a shared responsibility model.
Cybercrime Attribution
The assessed identification of the actors responsible for a cyber operation, with stated confidence.
Cybersecurity
The protection of digital systems, data and operations against unauthorised access, disruption and harm.

D

Dark Ecosystem Mapping
Mapping the actors, markets and supply chains of the criminal underground to anticipate threats.
Dark Web
Overlay networks and services that are not indexed by standard search engines, including criminal marketplaces.
Digital Evidence
Information stored or transmitted in digital form that may be relied upon in an investigation or proceeding.
Digital Forensics
The preservation, analysis and reporting of digital evidence to establish what happened and why.

E

Encryption
The transformation of data so that only authorised parties can read it, using cryptographic keys.
Entity Relationship Mapping
Visualising and analysing links between people, organisations, infrastructure, accounts and events.
Exploit
A technique or tool that takes advantage of a vulnerability to cause unintended behaviour.

F

Forensic Readiness
Designing systems and procedures so that an investigation is possible when an incident occurs.

G

Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)
The integrated approach to directing, managing and assuring an organisation's risk and obligations.

I

Incident Response
The structured process of detecting, containing, eradicating and recovering from a security incident.
Intelligence Cycle
The structured process of planning, collecting, processing, analysing and disseminating intelligence.

M

Malware Analysis
The examination of malicious software to understand its behaviour, capability and indicators.

N

Natural Language Processing (NLP)
The field of AI concerned with enabling machines to understand, analyse and generate human language.

O

Operational Technology (OT)
Hardware and software that controls or monitors physical processes, often in critical infrastructure.
OSINT
Open-source intelligence: intelligence produced from publicly available information through lawful collection and analysis.

P

Penetration Testing
Authorised adversarial testing of systems to identify and validate exploitable weaknesses.
Post-Quantum Cryptography
Cryptographic algorithms designed to resist attacks from both classical and quantum computers.

R

Red Team
An authorised adversary-simulation team that tests an organisation's people, process and technology together.

S

Security Operations Centre (SOC)
The function that monitors, detects, triages and responds to security events.
Social Engineering
Manipulating people into actions or disclosures that undermine security, often through trust or urgency.

T

Threat Actor
An individual or group that poses a threat through malicious cyber activity.
Threat Hunting
Hypothesis-driven investigation to find threats that existing detections do not catch.
Threat Intelligence
Analysed information about threats that supports decisions, not merely data about indicators.
Threat Prediction
Estimating likely threat developments using evidence, patterns and analytical judgement.

V

Vulnerability
A weakness that can be exploited to cause harm or unintended behaviour in a system.

Z

Zero Trust
A security model that removes implicit trust and verifies access explicitly, continuously and least-privilege.
