Artificial Intelligence & AI Agents

What Is Agentic AI?

Definition

Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can independently plan, make decisions, use tools and carry out multi-step tasks to achieve a goal. Unlike AI that only responds to a prompt, agentic AI takes actions in software, checks the results and adapts its approach, usually within limits and approval points set by people.

Jump to FAQs

Also known as agentic artificial intelligence, autonomous AI, AI agents, agentic systems

  • CategoryAI & AI Agents
  • Reading time4 min

What does agentic AI mean in simple terms?

Most people first met AI as something you ask and it answers. Agentic AI is AI you give a job to. Instead of writing one email when asked, an agentic system can run the whole follow-up process: decide who needs a reminder, write each message, send it, watch for replies and update the records.

The word agentic comes from agency, the ability to act. In business settings that agency is deliberately bounded. People decide what the AI may do alone, what needs sign-off and how every action is recorded.

How does agentic AI work?

Agentic AI systems combine several building blocks:

  1. 1

    A reasoning model

    A large language model interprets the goal, plans steps and decides what to do next.

  2. 2

    Tools and integrations

    Connections to APIs, databases, documents and business applications let the system act, not just talk.

  3. 3

    Memory and context

    The system keeps track of what it has done and retrieves relevant knowledge, often with retrieval-augmented generation.

  4. 4

    Orchestration

    Work is structured as an agentic workflow, sometimes with several specialised agents handing tasks to each other.

  5. 5

    Governance

    Permissions, approval gates, monitoring and audit logs keep autonomy within agreed boundaries.

What are real-world examples of agentic AI?

Procure-to-pay

Agents read purchase requests, compare supplier quotes, prepare purchase orders for approval and reconcile invoices when goods arrive.

Software delivery

Specialised agents draft requirements, generate code and tests, and prepare releases, while engineers approve each stage. Stack18's Agentic SDLC works this way.

Customer service

An agentic system resolves refunds end to end within policy limits, and routes anything above the limit to a supervisor.

Sales operations

Agents enrich leads, personalise outreach, schedule meetings and keep the CRM up to date.

Why does agentic AI matter for businesses?

Agentic AI is where AI starts to change how work is organised, not just how fast documents are written. Processes with many handoffs between systems and people are the strongest candidates.

It also raises the stakes. An enterprise adopting agentic AI needs clear policies on autonomy, security reviews of every tool an agent can use, and metrics that show the work is being done correctly.

What are the benefits and challenges of agentic AI?

Key benefits

  • Automates complete processes rather than single steps
  • Handles variation and exceptions better than fixed scripts
  • Lets small teams operate at much larger scale
  • Shortens cycle times for multi-step work such as approvals
  • Keeps people focused on decisions, with AI doing the execution

Challenges and limitations

  • Requires strong governance: permissions, approvals and audit trails
  • Harder to test because behaviour depends on context
  • Depends on clean data and reliable integrations
  • New security risks, including prompt injection and misuse of tools
  • Organisational change: roles and processes must adapt to supervising AI

How is agentic AI different from related terms?

Agentic AI vs Generative AI

Generative AI creates content in response to a request. Agentic AI uses generative models to pursue goals and take actions across systems.

Agentic AI vs AI Agent

An AI agent is a single system that acts. Agentic AI describes the overall approach, which may involve one agent or many working together.

Frequently asked questions about agentic AI

What is agentic AI in simple terms?

Agentic AI is AI that can do a job for you, not just answer a question. It plans the steps, uses software tools, checks its own progress and asks a person when it needs approval.

How is agentic AI different from generative AI?

Generative AI produces content such as text or images when prompted. Agentic AI uses generative models as its reasoning engine but goes further: it decides on actions, uses tools and completes multi-step tasks.

What are examples of agentic AI?

Examples include agents that process invoices from receipt to payment, resolve customer support cases end to end, qualify and follow up sales leads, and carry out stages of software development under human review.

How do businesses use agentic AI safely?

By starting with well-defined processes, giving agents the minimum access they need, requiring human approval for sensitive actions, logging everything and measuring outcomes before expanding autonomy.
