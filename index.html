FAQ

Questions, answered.

Ask us anything

What does Stack18 do?

Stack18 helps companies build, modernise and run technology. We offer an AI-native delivery platform (Agentic SDLC), six ready-made business systems, technology services, enterprise AI transformation through dedicated Transformation Pods and Stack Ventures, which builds companies in-house and spins them off. We also run the Stack18 Marketplace, a research directory of third-party software and AI agents.

Where is Stack18 based?

Stack18 is headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It works with businesses across India and abroad.

Is Stack18 a software development company in Jaipur?

Yes. Stack18 is an AI-native software company headquartered in Jaipur that builds custom and enterprise software, web and mobile apps, AI agents, ERP and CRM systems. See software development in Jaipur.

How is solution pricing calculated?

Each module has a one-time build and setup price in Indian rupees, excluding GST, and each solution comes in Launch, Grow and Scale editions at 10–20% below their combined module price. Bigger modules include the smaller ones they contain, so nothing is charged twice.

How do service engagements start?

With a free 30-minute consultation, then a short fixed-price first step such as a discovery sprint or assessment, before any larger commitment.

What is the Enterprise Transformation Pod?

A dedicated multidisciplinary Stack18 team on a 12, 24 or 36 month annual contract, in Foundation (6–8 specialists), Scale (12–20) or Enterprise (25+) sizes. It combines strategy, engineering, AI, data and operations and is accountable for a shared transformation scorecard. Many enterprises start with a free 60-minute leadership briefing and a focused first engagement.

How does Stack18 govern AI work?

AI agents execute work on the Agentic SDLC, and your people approve requirements, architecture, releases and production changes at five governance gates. Every decision is recorded in an audit trail, your code, data and deliverables stay yours, and your data is not used to train models without your prior written permission.

What is the Stack18 Marketplace?

A research directory of third-party software and AI agents. Profiles show what a product does, who it suits, how it is priced and where the evidence comes from, with dated sources, comparisons, alternatives and discussions. There is no paid placement, and Stack18’s own ready-made systems are listed under Solutions.

What is Stack Ventures?

Stack18’s company-building arm. It builds new companies inside Stack18, takes them to product-market fit and ₹1 crore in revenue, hires a dedicated CEO and spins each one off as a separate company.

How long does a ready-made system take to go live?

A Launch edition typically goes live in 3 to 5 weeks, depending on the solution. Grow and Scale editions take longer because they include more modules. See solutions.

How much does custom software cost in India?

It depends on scope. Stack18 fixes the price after a short 1–4 week discovery, so you see the plan, team and price before committing. The software development cost guide explains what drives the number.

Which industries does Stack18 work with?

Retail and D2C, manufacturing, distribution and trading, BFSI and fintech, healthcare, education, real estate, logistics, SaaS and startups, and enterprises and groups.

Does Stack18 work with clients outside India?

Yes. Stack18 works with businesses in India and abroad, with global connections in San Francisco, London and Dubai.

What else does Stack18 run besides client work?

Stack School for practical learning, Stack Connect for its community, events, a partner programme, Stack Gives for giving back, and Stack Ventures, which builds Stack18’s own companies.

How do I contact Stack18?

Book a free 30-minute consultation through the contact page or email hello@stack18.com. A senior specialist replies within one business day.
